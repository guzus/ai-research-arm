Privacy Policy — ARA Daily (iOS)

Effective 2026-08-28

ARA Daily is a reader for AI Research Arm, an open-source pipeline that publishes an AI news digest, a live-news feed and research articles at ara.guzus.xyz.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not collect, store or transmit personal information.

Network requests

The app downloads publicly published content from ara.guzus.xyz and audio files from s3.guzus.xyz. These servers receive the standard technical information any web request carries (your IP address, request time and the file requested), which is used only to serve the content and for ordinary server logs.

On-device data

Your language and notification preferences, and which editions you have already seen, are stored only on your device and never leave it. Notifications are generated on your device from published content; no push server receives information about you.

Third parties

Links inside articles open in your browser and are subject to those sites' policies.

Questions: github.com/guzus/ai-research-arm/issues
