## Breaking News

**OpenAI ships GPT-6 Astra.** Posted September 3, OpenAI calls Astra its most capable broadly deployed model and the first to hit **Critical** cybersecurity under the Preparedness Framework (High biological/chemical; below High on self-improvement). Access starts with Daybreak organizations today; ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise plus the OpenAI API and AWS are scheduled over the coming days. The API id is `gpt-6-astra` at **$10 / $50** per million input/output tokens; Fast mode is 2× that price for up to 2.5× speed. Pro/Business/Enterprise get a “GPT-6 Astra Pro” SKU (Enterprise off by default until an admin enables it). Company tables: AutomationBench 41.4% vs GPT-5.6 Sol 18.1%; Terminal-Bench 4.0 57.7% vs Sol 37.3%; DeepSWE v1.1 74.1%; BrowseComp 91.5%; GPQA Diamond 96.0%; FrontierMath Tier 4 v2 97.6%; ExploitBench 100%; ARC-AGI-3 99.9% on OpenAI’s Provider Adapter harness vs 62.7–63% on the default harness. Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index is **61.2**, behind Claude Fable 5.1 (65.7). President Greg Brockman said there is no contractual AGI trigger and left the label to the reader; he closed the briefing with “Welcome to the AGI era.” The system card says Astra “is more capable of controlling its own CoT than GPT 5.6-Sol” (60.9% vs 16.1% on matched traces) and found two zero-days now in disclosure; Daybreak Blue is the defender unlock. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/03/openai-launches-astra-its-powerful-and-controversial-new-model/), [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/gpt-6-astra-is-the-first-model-making-openai-willing-to-declare-the-agi-era/), [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/3/gpt6-astra/), [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-astra/), [system card](https://deploymentsafety.openai.com/gpt-6-astra/gpt-6-astra.pdf))

Hugging Face hosts more than 3 million models, 500,000 datasets, and 1 million applications for 18 million developers and 200,000 companies. ([NVIDIA Blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-to-acquire-hugging-face/), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/03/nvidia-confirms-it-will-buy-hugging-face-for-12-9-billion/), [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/985474/nvidia-buying-hugging-face-deal))

**Google DeepMind and Google Research ship WeatherNext 3 into Search, Gemini, and Maps.** Officially posted September 3, the flagship weather model trains on live geostationary satellite mosaics and station observations instead of a six-hour NWP lag, emitting hourly forecasts at 5 km (temperature/moisture), 10 km (other surface), and 25 km (atmosphere) — Google says about 5× sharper than WeatherNext 2’s 25 km / 6-hour grid. Vendor evals: precipitation CRPS improved up to 60% vs IMERG, 30% vs MRMS, 10% vs gauges at early leads; Search/Maps users are promised up to 50% more accurate day-ahead rain. Brightband’s Operational WeatherBench ranks it ahead of other AI and traditional NWS/ECMWF forecasts on temperature, wind, and humidity. The model is 2.4× larger than WeatherNext 2 and adds 100-meter wind plus solar radiation for clean-energy planning. Data is on BigQuery, Earth Engine, and GCS starting today. ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/introducing-weathernext-3/), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/03/googles-latest-ai-weather-model-gives-you-no-excuse-to-forget-your-umbrella/))

**Meta ships Muse Spark 1.3 in Muse Code and the Meta Model API.** Officially posted September 2, Meta says the closed-weights model improves long-horizon agentic work, instruction following, and multitasking, with previously available reasoning modes live now and **max reasoning held for additional safety testing**. Artificial Analysis, dated the same day, scores the public **xhigh** SKU at Intelligence Index 61 (+4 vs 1.2) and a partner-preview **max** at 62 — behind Claude Fable 5.1 (66) and Opus 5 max (63), tied with GPT-5.6 Sol (max) and Grok 4.6 (high). **Hugging Face releases funes, a local memory layer for coding agents.** Posted September 3, funes is a single binary that indexes existing session traces from Claude Code, Codex, pi, and Hermes, then exposes `recall` / `get` tools so a later agent can retrieve raw turns with provenance. Embedding and reranking run on-device (Lance + BM25 + cross-encoder); optional sync publishes a redacted copy to a private Hugging Face dataset the user owns. Hugging Face's own handoff-vs-recall bench says recall was 4–8× cheaper than a written handoff on two tasks that required prior-session knowledge. ([Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/blog/funes))

## Research Papers

**Nemotron-3-Ultra-CC is the first reported AI system to outscore the top human at IOI** (`2609.02849`, submitted September 2). NVIDIA authors train Nemotron-3-Nano-CC (30B-A3B) with SFT+RL and Ultra-CC (550B-A55B) with SFT on 22k curated problems, then apply GenCorrect (iterative generate–evaluate–refine). On IOI 2025, Nano-CC goes 130 → 291 after post-training and 468 with GenCorrect (gold line 438.3); Ultra-CC reaches 502. A competition-specific Ultra-CC system then ran *prospectively* during IOI 2026 under human time, internet, and submission rules and scored **535.4 / 600**, above gold (361.12) and the top official contestant (498.27). The authors note the run was unofficial and unsupervised by IOI. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02849))

**SolarWM: open data and scalable training for long-horizon video world models** (`2609.02886`, September 2). A multi-source engine turns 1.43M clips from 10 datasets into a frame-aligned contract; a backbone-native adapter instantiates four 5B–33B models on Wan2.2, LTX-2.5, and MiniMax-H3. A three-stage recipe (bidirectional adaptation → teacher-forced AR init → distribution-matching distillation) yields causal models that support minute-to-hour interactive rollouts after training on 5-second sequences. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02886))

**Cliff: process rewards from the first mistake** (`2609.02817`, September 2). After a reasoning trace first goes wrong, later tokens are conditioned on an invalid prefix. Cliff uses an off-the-shelf teacher only to mark that first error, then writes token-level advantages (positive on the correct prefix, negative after). Across 12 settings it beats on-policy distillation by 15% and GRPO by 7%, without a specialized process-reward model. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02817))

**oHC: orthogonal Hyper-Connections on SO(4) via quaternions** (`2609.02672`, September 2). Unconstrained residual-stream mixers compound and blow up; mHC projects onto doubly-stochastic matrices, which caps amplification but lets streams collapse. oHC restricts the mixer to SO(*n*); at *n*=4 a pair of unit quaternions parameterizes the group with no extra parameters. The authors report it beats the single-stream residual, mHC, and identity-fixed iHC on a downstream suite. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02672))

**Linguistic illegibility implies CoT monitors cannot be completely sound** (`2609.02852`, September 2). James Mickens argues that once internals are math over activations and language is only a lossy bookend, CoT monitors, constitutional self-critique, and linguistically defined activation probes can never be fully sound. The proposed floor is a sandbox whose guarantees do not read the model's linguistic state: a priori taint-tracking over system state the model must never influence, plus virtualization and third-party audit of the sandbox config. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02852))

**Google Research and HHMI Janelia publish the complete male fruit-fly CNS connectome.** Posted September 3 and published in *Cell*, the proofread map covers 166,000 neurons and 125 million synapses — the largest brain map by neuron count — including the ventral nerve cord. Flood-filling networks produced the reconstruction; it is open in Neuroglancer. A companion *Nature* paper this week maps an elephantnose-fish cerebellum-like circuit. ([Google Research](https://research.google/blog/a-connectomics-milestone-mapping-the-complete-male-fruit-fly-brain/))

## Regulatory Updates

**Protect Democracy sues four federal agencies to force disclosure of the White House frontier-model safety-review framework.** Ars Technica reported September 2 that the nonpartisan group filed after FOIA requests to the Office of the National Cyber Director, OSTP, Treasury, and Commerce produced no records (ONCD denied expedition). The complaint seeks the unclassified framework text, participation terms, participant identities, and access criteria by September 30, plus an injunction against improper withholding. The group says the August 3 “voluntary” pre-release review and the GOLD EAGLE clearinghouse have been shared only with “trusted partners,” and that Congress is being asked to extend CISA 2015 liability protections that the administration has said sustain GOLD EAGLE without seeing the program's terms. A supporting declaration from California Sen. Josh Becker contrasts the secrecy with the public process around California's SB 813. ([Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/09/trump-may-be-forced-to-reveal-secret-rules-feds-use-for-ai-safety-testing/))
