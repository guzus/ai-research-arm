How the two largest private labs raise money is the pressure gauge for the
[[ai-capex]] buildout. The 2026-11-01 digest carries two data points that move
in the same direction: **less capital, at lower marks, on weaker credit terms**,
during an AI-stock selloff.

## OpenAI: the round shrinks (FT, WSJ, TechCrunch, The Decoder)

- The FT reports the target fell from **$30B at $1.4T** to roughly **$20B at
  $1.25T**, with UAE investors including MGX expected to lead.
- Some backers trimmed allocations during the selloff. A WSJ-sourced report
  says only about **$11B** has come in so far, with the rest expected from an
  offering to wealthy individuals.
- Context: [[openai]] is also reported as meeting about 50 Samsung executives
  in Korea (Bloomberg) to deepen memory and data-center ties; neither Samsung
  nor SK hynix ([[sk-hynix]]) has commented.

## Anthropic: junk-rating expectation (FT via @DailyStackHQ, TechCrunch)

- **Blackstone**, the anchor investor in [[anthropic]]'s **$63B chip-financing
  deal**, now expects a **double-B (sub-investment-grade) rating** after the
  November listing, down from an earlier investment-grade view.
- **No agency has published a rating yet**; this is an investor's expectation.
- The S-1 shows **H1 2026 revenue of $16.3B** and **$6.2B of debt at June 30,
  all convertible notes held by [[amazon]]**.

## Reading across

Both labs lean on structured or backstopped capital rather than plain equity or
investment-grade debt (compare [[neocloud]] and [[coreweave]] credit stress).
Treat the figures as press-reported and still moving; none is an issuer filing
except the S-1 numbers attributed above.
