How the two largest private labs raise money is the pressure gauge for the
[[ai-capex]] buildout. The 2026-11-01 digest carries two data points that move
in the same direction: **less capital, at lower marks, on weaker credit terms**,
during an AI-stock selloff. The 2026-11-03 digest adds the first full look at
[[anthropic]]'s books from its amended S-1.

## Anthropic's amended S-1 (2026-11-03; TechCrunch, The Decoder, X relays)

- **Q3 revenue $16.96B (+1,127% YoY), but a $567M operating loss.** The swing
  is attributed to about **$5B of short-term compute bought above the price of
  its long-term contracts**.
- **Compute commitments total $895B**, the figure the original S-1 left blank:
  **$447B due by end-2030** and a Google minimum raised to **$121B through July
  2033**. Liquidity was **$147B at Sept 30**.
- **Risk-factor language (page 29):** internal estimates suggest Anthropic may
  "imminently" meet its RSP threshold of one year delivering two years of
  2018–2024-pace progress. This is hedged legal text on Anthropic's own
  definition, not a capability announcement.
- **Unreconciled headline:** The Decoder headlines a "$23 billion loss", which
  the operating figures reported on X do not reconcile with. Hold the loss
  figure at the operating-line number until reconciled.
- Community reaction was skeptical (HN: "The SEC Should Block Anthropic's IPO",
  "Anthropic's growth just flattened"); these are opinion pieces.

## OpenAI: the round shrinks (FT, WSJ, TechCrunch, The Decoder)

- The FT reports the target fell from **$30B at $1.4T** to roughly **$20B at
  $1.25T**, with UAE investors including MGX expected to lead.
- Some backers trimmed allocations during the selloff. A WSJ-sourced report
  says only about **$11B** has come in so far, with the rest expected from an
  offering to wealthy individuals.
- Context: [[openai]] is also reported as meeting about 50 Samsung executives
  in Korea (Bloomberg) to deepen memory and data-center ties; neither Samsung
  nor SK hynix ([[sk-hynix]]) has commented.

## Anthropic: junk-rating expectation (FT via @DailyStackHQ, TechCrunch)

- **Blackstone**, the anchor investor in [[anthropic]]'s **$63B chip-financing
  deal**, now expects a **double-B (sub-investment-grade) rating** after the
  November listing, down from an earlier investment-grade view.
- **No agency has published a rating yet**; this is an investor's expectation.
- The S-1 shows **H1 2026 revenue of $16.3B** and **$6.2B of debt at June 30,
  all convertible notes held by [[amazon]]**.

## Reading across

Both labs lean on structured or backstopped capital rather than plain equity or
investment-grade debt (compare [[neocloud]] and [[coreweave]] credit stress).
The $895B commitment stack against a first operating loss makes the S-1/A the
sharpest public statement of the buildout's fixed-cost risk. Treat the figures
as press-reported and still moving; none is an issuer filing except the S-1
numbers attributed above.
