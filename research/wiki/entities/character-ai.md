**Character.AI** (Character Technologies) is the consumer companion-chat
startup founded by [[noam-shazeer|Noam Shazeer]]. [[google]] paid about
**$2.7 billion in August 2024** to bring Shazeer and part of the team
back into DeepMind. On **2026-10-14** the company **filed Chapter 11 in
Delaware**. On **2026-10-19** TechCrunch reported that **Disney hired
most of the remaining workforce**, named former CEO **Karandeep Anand**
its first CTO, and had agreed to buy some assets for **$20 million**.
The Chapter 11 case remains; the digest does not say the consumer app
survives (TechCrunch, The Verge, The Decoder, Ars Technica; ARA daily
digests 2026-10-14 and 2026-10-19).

## Why it matters

- **A $2.7 billion acqui-hire left a consumer product that could not
  stand alone.** Google bought the team, not a durable business. The
  leftover company entered Chapter 11 four years after the consumer
  companion boom, and two years after Shazeer left Google for
  [[openai]]. See [[noam-shazeer]] and [[google]].
- **Disney took the people, not a going concern.** Hiring most of the
  workforce and installing Anand as Disney's first CTO is an
  acqui-hire. The ~**$20 million** asset figure matches the
  stalking-horse IP-license bid already on this page. The digest does
  not say Disney is keeping the consumer app alive.
- **The platform's fate is still the open question.** Chapter 11
  continues. If no buyer takes the live product, it goes dark even
  after the workforce has moved.

## 41 attorneys general target user data in the sale (2026-10-15)

NY AG Letitia James and **40** others filed to constrain consumer-data
transfers in the Chapter 11 sale. That is a data-transfer fight on top of
Disney's stalking-horse bid, not a competing bid for the live product. See
[[federal-ai-policy]] (The Verge; ARA daily digest 2026-10-15).

## Disney hires most of the workforce; Anand becomes Disney CTO (2026-10-19)

- **Disney hired most of Character.AI's workforce.** Former CEO
  **Karandeep Anand** is now Disney's first CTO, and Disney had
  agreed to buy some assets for **$20 million**. That converts
  this week's stalking-horse auction into an acqui-hire: the
  people go to Disney, the remaining company stays in Chapter 11,
  and the ~$20 million asset figure matches the IP-license bid
  already on this page. The digest does not say the consumer app
  survives. See [[noam-shazeer]] and [[google]] (TechCrunch; ARA
  daily digest 2026-10-19).

## Open questions
