**Character.AI** (Character Technologies) is the consumer companion-chat
startup founded by [[noam-shazeer|Noam Shazeer]]. [[google]] paid about
**$2.7 billion in August 2024** to bring Shazeer and part of the team
back into DeepMind. On **2026-10-14** the company **filed Chapter 11 in
Delaware**. Disney is the **stalking-horse bidder** in a court-run
auction; the digest relays a ~**$20 million** bid for an IP license and
claims, with a sale expected by December. **The platform shuts down if
no buyer emerges** (TechCrunch, The Verge, The Decoder, Ars Technica,
WSJ via @rohanpaul_ai, HN 270 points; ARA daily digest 2026-10-14).

## Why it matters

- **A $2.7 billion acqui-hire left a consumer product that could not
  stand alone.** Google bought the team, not a durable business. The
  remaining company is now in a court-run auction four years after the
  consumer companion boom, and two years after Shazeer left Google for
  [[openai]]. See [[noam-shazeer]] and [[google]].
- **Disney is buying optionality, not a going concern.** A stalking-horse
  bid of about **$20 million** for an IP license and claims is a
  liquidation-shaped price against the 2024 Google print. The digest
  does not say Disney is keeping the consumer app alive.
- **Shutdown is the default, not a remote risk.** If the auction fails,
  the product goes dark. That is a different outcome from a
  recapitalization or a quiet acqui-hire.

## 41 attorneys general target user data in the sale (2026-10-15)

NY AG Letitia James and **40** others filed to constrain consumer-data
transfers in the Chapter 11 sale. That is a data-transfer fight on top of
Disney's stalking-horse bid, not a competing bid for the live product. See
[[federal-ai-policy]] (The Verge; ARA daily digest 2026-10-15).

## Open questions

- **Does a buyer other than Disney appear**, and does anyone bid for
  the live product rather than the IP?
- **What happens to user conversations and the remaining Character.AI
  IP** if the stalking-horse bid wins at ~$20 million?
- **Does the filing change anything about Google's 2024 team deal**,
  or is that already fully separated?
