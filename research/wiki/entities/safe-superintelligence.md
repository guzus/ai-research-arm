**Safe Superintelligence (SSI)** is the stealth AI lab co-founded by
**Ilya Sutskever** — OpenAI co-founder and former chief scientist — in 2024,
pursuing safe superintelligence as its sole stated mission with no interim
commercial products.

## Why it matters

- **NVIDIA's first substantial disclosed backing (2026-07-28).** [[nvidia|NVIDIA]]
  reportedly invested **~$5B** in SSI, according to separate Financial Times
  and Bloomberg reporting, against SSI's **~$32B post-money valuation** — the
  first substantial disclosed NVIDIA backing of SSI since its 2024 founding.
  The FT frames the sum as **milestone-contingent** rather than a straight
  cash payment, hours after the two companies jointly confirmed a **compute
  partnership** to scale SSI's compute **roughly tenfold within a year**.
- **A stealth lab getting a hyperscaler-silicon price tag.** SSI has
  shipped no public models or products since its founding, making the deal
  notable primarily as a valuation and compute-access data point rather
  than a capability one — a striking vote of confidence from the dominant
  AI-accelerator supplier in a lab still operating in stealth. It lands the
  same day NVIDIA is separately reported in talks over a ~$250B [[openai|OpenAI]]
  datacenter financing backstop and launched the Open Secure AI Alliance —
  part of a broader NVIDIA capital-deployment day across the frontier-lab
  ecosystem (ARA daily digest 2026-07-28).

## Open questions

- **What is SSI actually building?** No public model, benchmark, or product
  has shipped; the compute scale-up and valuation are the only concrete
  data points so far.
- **Is the $5B a straight investment or a compute-credit/milestone
  structure?** The FT's "milestone-contingent" framing suggests the
  headline figure may not be a simple cash-for-equity deal.
