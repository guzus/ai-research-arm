**MiniMax M3.1 Flash** is MiniMax's 2026-10-19
coding-and-agent Fast SKU: a **428B-total /
~23B-active MoE** with **1M context** and
text, image and video input. It is the named
form of the anonymous OpenRouter listing
tracked as **Space Bunny**. List is **$0.15 /
$0.60** per million tokens ($0.015 cached).
Weights are promised for **23 October**; that
date is disputed. A larger Pro sibling is
said to be coming. It succeeds
[[minimax-m3|M3]] as the lab's cheap
long-context coding line, distinct from
[[minimax-h3|H3]]'s video bet.

## Why it matters

- **The cheap Fast card is now a named
  product.** MiniMax's own post (2026-10-19)
  said "Welcome back. Space Bunny" and named
  **MiniMax-M3.1-Flash**. Secondary accounts
  put pay-as-you-go at **$0.15 / $0.60**,
  with guides to run it inside Claude Code
  and Codex. Free promotional windows ran on
  [[openrouter|OpenRouter]] (72h), Vercel
  (to 20 October) and OpenCode Zen. Thinking
  must stay on; one Codex guide recommends a
  **512K** working context out of 1M
  (@MiniMax_AI, @cdiamond, MarkTechPost, The
  Decoder; ARA daily digest 2026-10-20 and
  ticket `minimax-m3-1-flash-2026-10`).
- **MiniMax's own chart trails
  [[deepseek-v4-1-flash|DeepSeek V4.1 Flash]]
  on four of six shared benches.**
  Vendor scores: DeepSWE **73.7%** (V4.1
  Flash **77.0%**, [[claude-opus-5|Opus 5]]
  **74.0%**), Automation-Bench **62.2**,
  Terminal Bench 4.0 **38.1** vs Opus 5's
  **51.8**. The Decoder reports **+13 points
  to 42** on the Artificial Analysis
  Intelligence Index at half the prior cost.
  HN commenters called it "pretty
  benchmaxed" and said they prefer V4.1
  Flash and [[zhipu-glm-5-3|GLM 5.3 Flash]]
  (MiniMax, The Decoder, HN 279 pts / 110
  comments; ARA daily digest 2026-10-20).
- **Weights are a date, not a drop.** The
  digest records **23 October** against "no
  date yet." Until weights land, this is an
  API SKU inside the [[open-weights]] wave,
  not a downloadable successor to
  [[minimax-m3]]. The earlier Space Bunny
  ticket had closed as stale; MiniMax's
  naming reopened it as a released product
  with still-partial verification.
