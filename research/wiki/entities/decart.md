**Decart** is a chip-efficiency and
world-model startup. Until this
weekend it sat on the wiki only as
the target [[anthropic]] walked away
from after diligence. On Sunday
evening three wires independently
described [[amazon|Amazon]] talks at
about **$7 billion**. Nothing is
signed, and neither company has
commented.

## Why it matters

- **The reported price jumped twice
  in five months.** A May round of
  about **$300 million** marked the
  company near **$4 billion**, with
  [[nvidia]] on the cap table. The
  September Anthropic bid that died
  after diligence on **8 September**
  sat near **$6 billion** and, per
  Bloomberg then, was aimed at the
  **inference-optimization stack**,
  not the world models. Sunday's
  ~$7 billion is roughly **75%**
  above the May mark and **$1
  billion** above the Anthropic
  walk-away. Treat $7 billion as
  the reported number, not a closed
  multiple (WSJ, Bloomberg, Reuters
  via X; ARA daily digest
  2026-10-12).
- **Amazon would be buying a
  capability it does not already
  own as a first-party product.**
  The hyperscaler is already
  Anthropic's largest investor and
  a Bedrock distributor; a Decart
  close would add a chip-efficiency
  / world-model stack beside
  Trainium and the reported
  Qualcomm inference book. See
  [[amazon]] and [[ai-capex]].
- **Consideration, exclusivity, and
  whether Nvidia's preferred rights
  can block a sale are not
  stated.** Those are the three
  facts that would turn talks into
  a process. Until they appear,
  this is a three-outlet exclusive
  cluster, not a signed deal.

## Open questions

- **Does a term sheet exist**, or
  is $7 billion a banker-circulated
  ask?
- **Do Nvidia preferred rights
  actually constrain a sale**, and
  would Nvidia exercise them?
- **Would a close keep Decart's
  world-model work intact**, or
  fold it into Amazon's Nova /
  Bedrock stack the way the
  September diligence framed the
  inference side?
