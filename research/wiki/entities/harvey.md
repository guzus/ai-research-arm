**Harvey** is a legal-AI company. Until this ingest it
appeared only as a distribution surface for
[[claude-fable-5|Fable 5]] (GitHub Copilot, Amazon
Bedrock, and Harvey). The 2026-09-10 raise makes it a
company node of its own: a vertical that is now
training in-house on an open-weight Chinese flagship.

## Why it matters

- **$550 million at $15.5B (2026-09-10).** Co-led by
  Diffusion and Lightspeed, after **$200M at $11B in
  March**. Cumulative disclosed capital is now more
  than **$1.55B**. First-party plus TechCrunch (ARA
  daily digest 2026-09-10).
- **Harvey Tenet is its first in-house model**,
  post-trained from open-weight
  [[moonshot-kimi-k3|Kimi K3]]. A legal vertical
  shipping a named model on a Chinese open-weight
  base is a different posture from remaining a
  Claude reseller. See [[open-weights]] (Harvey,
  TechCrunch; ARA daily digest 2026-09-10).
- **The same digest hour carried Listen Labs walking
  away from a signed $1.5B Menlo Ventures Series C
  for Salesforce talks.** That is a same-hour
  TechCrunch exclusive at the 00:00 UTC close;
  contested until Salesforce or Listen confirms, and
  not a Harvey fact except as the contrasting
  enterprise-AI financing print (TechCrunch; ARA
  daily digest 2026-09-10).

## Open questions

- **How much of Tenet is Kimi K3 versus Harvey
  post-training?** The digest states the base, not
  the delta.
- **Does $15.5B price legal AI as a frontier-adjacent
  vertical**, or as a professional-services multiple
  that will not travel to other agent startups?

## Astra for Law arrives as a lab SKU, not a Harvey bake-off (2026-09-18)

[[openai]] shipped **[[astra|Astra for Law]]**, a
GPT-6 Astra configuration with a
**230-million-URL** U.S. legal index that scored
**54.0%** on Vals' private Legal Research Bench
versus **38.7%** for web-search Astra. The digest
is explicit that the lift is against OpenAI's own
web-search baseline, **not Harvey or Claude for
Legal**. 54% remains a failing grade on a
vendor-chosen private set. Harvey's $15.5B mark
and Tenet model are unchanged; the new fact is
that a frontier lab now sells a named legal SKU
into the same buyer set (OpenAI, HN; ARA daily
digest 2026-09-18).
