**Gemini 4 Carbon** is [[google]]'s next Gemini 4 frontier SKU after
[[gemini-4-argon|Argon]], released about **15 days** later. Access is
limited to the same **Fairwind** cyber-defender cohort that first
gated Argon and [[gemini-3-8-flash|3.8 Flash Cyber]]. Introductory
API pricing is **$2 / $10 per Mtok**, matching Argon's intro card.
There is **no GA date**. Google pitches it for coding, cyber, and
long-horizon work, and says it can "work through complex tasks for
days" (Google DeepMind, Logan Kilpatrick, HN, The Verge, Ars
Technica; ARA daily digest 2026-10-29).

The October 10–13 "Carbon checkpoint" notes on the Argon ticket and
wiki page are this artifact. Keep Argon on [[gemini-4-argon]]; this
page is the shipped Carbon SKU.

## Why it matters

- **Independent composite lead, hours after an index reset.**
  Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index **v5** scores Carbon
  **59**, overtaking [[claude-opus-5-5|Claude Opus 5.5]] and
  [[gpt-6|GPT-6 Astra]], which had been narrowly ordered that
  way at v5 launch. Text Arena ranks it **#1 at 1528**. v5 is
  **60% private or held-out**; GDPval-AA is dropped. Open-weight
  models measure further behind. Mollick says take it with a
  grain of salt; Lambert says the old index "did flatter" open
  models, while private sets give up community bug-squashing
  (Artificial Analysis, Bluesky, Interconnects, The Decoder;
  ARA daily digest 2026-10-29). See [[open-weights]].
- **Vendor cyber table is first-party.** Google claims
  **SEC-bench Pro 88.2%**, **ExploitGym 54.8%**, **CWE-bench
  71.0%**, and **0.0% Terminal-Bench cheat success**. Arena
  still ranks Opus 5.5 ahead on Agent and WebDev. Treat the
  vendor cyber numbers as un reproduced (Google DeepMind,
  @testingcatalog, HN 610 pts; ARA daily digest 2026-10-29).
  See [[agentic-ai-security]] and [[claude-opus-5-5]].
- **Same gate, same intro price, no public date.** Fairwind
  scarcity is the product. HN (610 / 509) mostly mocked the
  atomic-mass naming or the "cyber defenders first" delay, and
  noted the launch landed on Alphabet earnings day. Commenters
  say **Barium, Argon and Carbon** are checkpoints of one
  ongoing run; that is HN commentary, not a Google statement.
  @scaling01 warns that Argon was a "hallucination machine" on
  cyber tasks — a prior-SKU caveat, not a Carbon eval (HN;
  ARA daily digest 2026-10-29). See [[google]] and [[ai-capex]].

## Open questions

- **When does Fairwind lift?** Argon reached paid API / Pro /
  Ultra about two weeks after its partner preview. Carbon has
  no GA date in today's files.
- **Do the vendor cyber scores hold** once anyone can run them,
  given the Argon hallucination caveat on similar tasks?
- **Is Carbon a later checkpoint of the same Gemini 4 run as
  Argon**, or a separately trained SKU? Google has not said.
