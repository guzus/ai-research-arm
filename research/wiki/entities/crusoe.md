**Crusoe** is a US AI-infrastructure builder — a
[[neocloud]] that develops power and GPU capacity
for training and inference. It had no dedicated
wiki page before this ingest; earlier $3B / $30B
financing chatter was skipped on 2026-09-05 as
budget. The 2026-09-18 Series F is the first
closed, multi-outlet print large enough to mint
the node.

## Why it matters

- **$3.9B Series F at $30.9B post-money
  (2026-09-18).** Co-led by Atreides, Mubadala
  Capital and Valor Equity Partners, with
  [[nvidia|Nvidia]], Founders Fund and QIA
  participating. The company cites **$140B+ in
  contracted value**. Reuters and TechCrunch
  both carry the close — the infrastructure
  print of the day's cycle (Reuters, TechCrunch;
  ARA daily digest 2026-09-18).
- **Peer set.** Crusoe now sits next to
  [[coreweave]] and [[nebius]] as a named
  independent GPU-cloud / energy-plus-compute
  builder inside the [[ai-capex]] supercycle.
  Nvidia participating in the round is the
  familiar supplier-as-investor pattern this
  theme already tracks on CoreWeave and other
  neoclouds.
- **What this is not.** The digest does not
  print revenue, backlog mix, power megawatts,
  or a customer list. **$140B+ contracted
  value** is a company-cited figure, not a
  filed RPO. Treat the valuation as a priced
  round and the contracted-value line as
  vendor math until an IR disclosure lands.

## Open questions

- **How much of the $140B+ book is take-or-pay
  versus optionality?** [[coreweave]]'s public
  RPO is the benchmark; Crusoe has not shown
  the same split.
- **Energy origin.** Crusoe's historical
  stranded-gas / flare-mitigation story is not
  restated in today's files. Does the $30.9B
  mark still price that origin, or only GPU
  capacity?
