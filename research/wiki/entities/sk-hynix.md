**SK Hynix** is the South Korean memory-chip maker whose **HBM
(high-bandwidth memory)** underpins [[nvidia]]'s AI accelerator supply
chain. On 2026-07-11 it raised **$26.5 billion** through a **Nasdaq
listing** — reported as the **largest US share sale ever by a foreign
company** — with shares reportedly climbing roughly 14% on debut.

## Why it matters

HBM supply is a direct bottleneck on AI accelerator production. A raise
of this scale by a top-two HBM supplier is a concrete data point in the
memory/HBM supply crunch feeding AI datacenter buildouts, alongside
[[micron]] (Anthropic's primary memory supplier) on the US side. It also
lands the same week as reports that **Samsung** moved up its first Yongin
semiconductor fab by two years (now targeting October 2029), underscoring
how memory capacity is becoming a capex-supercycle chokepoint — see
[[ai-capex]].

Single-account sourcing (@WesRoth) in the ingest window, detailed and
specific, but no primary financial-press corroboration yet captured —
`verification: partial`.

- **KOSPI selloff erases a month of gains (2026-07-29).** SK Hynix fell
  **more than 12%** as the KOSPI posted its worst session since March
  (down as much as 10.7%, Korea's eighth circuit-breaker halt of the
  year), **erasing nearly $600B of SK Hynix value in a month**, as
  investors weighed China's advancing domestic chip tooling against
  doubts about AI-capex durability — a sharp reversal from the bullish
  Nasdaq-listing narrative that opened this page. See [[ai-capex]] (ARA
  daily digest 2026-07-29).
- **A KRW 40 trillion buyback-and-cancellation — the largest in Korean
  corporate history (2026-08-19/20).** SK Hynix's board approved a
  **KRW 40 trillion (~$29B) buyback-and-cancellation** covering about
  **3.3% of shares** — the **largest cancellation ever by a Korean-listed
  company**, funded from **KRW 69 trillion of net cash** — and raised its
  **2025–27 shareholder-return floor from "up to 50%" to "at least 50%"**
  of free cash flow. It is a capital-returns pivot for the memory supplier
  that raised $26.5B in the Nasdaq listing less than six weeks earlier,
  and a counterweight to the July KOSPI selloff that erased nearly $600B
  of its value (Twitter; ARA daily digest 2026-08-20). See [[ai-capex]]
