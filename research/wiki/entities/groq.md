**Groq** is an AI-inference-chip startup known for its **LPU**
(language processing unit) architecture and its fast, low-latency inference
offering.

## Why it matters

- **A $350M raise at a $3.5B valuation — roughly half its peak (2026-08-18).**
  Groq raised **$350M, reportedly Series A, at a $3.5B valuation** — about
  **half its $6.9B September 2025 mark**. Groq's framing is that the number
  prices *what remains* after **NVIDIA licensed its LPU architecture and
  absorbed founder Jonathan Ross and senior technical staff** — the 
  [[model-specific-silicon]] storyline this wiki tracks. The "Series A" label on
  a company that last priced at $6.9B is **recapitalization nomenclature**.
  Some reporting claims **NVIDIA is expected to participate**; if the licensor
  is also funding the licensee at half its former price, the number is not an
  arm's-length print (TechCrunch via @rohanpaul_ai; ARA daily digest
  2026-08-18).
- **The licensing-vs-acquisition tell.** NVIDIA "[absorbed] founder Jonathan
  Ross and senior technical staff" while licensing, not buying, the LPU
  architecture — a structure this wiki tracks in [[model-specific-silicon]],
  alongside [[amd|AMD]]'s [[taalas]] acquisition, as the incumbent GPU vendors
  buying-or-absorbing the fixed-function-inference threat to their core product.

## Open questions

- **What is Groq actually selling now?** Post the NVIDIA LPU license and the
  senior-staff departure, the residual valuation is a bet on the remaining
  team and IP — but the product direction is unclear.
- **Is NVIDIA participating, and at what terms?** Licensor-funding-licensee at
  a markdown would confirm the recapitalization read rather than a market
  print.
