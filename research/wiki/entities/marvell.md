**Marvell Technology** is a US data-infrastructure chipmaker. On 2026-11-02 its
8-K (EX-99.1) announced an **all-stock acquisition of Lumentum**, the optical
components supplier (this page also answers to Lumentum's name until it earns
its own page).

## The deal (Marvell 8-K; traders on X)

- **Terms as reported by traders:** about **4.5 Marvell shares per Lumentum
  share**, roughly **$115B** at announcement. The 8-K's own terms were not
  captured in the digest, so treat the exchange ratio and value as relayed.
- **Pitch:** CEO Matt Murphy — *"AI infrastructure is undergoing a fundamental
  architectural shift toward optical connectivity, creating an opportunity that
  comes along once in a generation."* Lumentum's CEO cites a **$550B market
  opportunity**.
- **Synergies:** the deck claims line of sight to **$1B+ run-rate synergies**,
  mainly in cost of goods, with accretion to non-GAAP EPS in year one.
- **Timing:** reported close is **2028**.
- **Market reaction:** both stocks fell sharply on the news.

## Why it matters

The deal is a bet that the [[ai-capex]] bottleneck is moving from compute to
the links between accelerators, the same layer [[broadcom]] and [[nvidia]]
compete for. It is also the largest all-stock combination in the AI hardware
supply chain in this wiki's coverage, and the sell-off in both names suggests
investors doubt the 2028 close and the price.

## Open questions

- Regulatory path and the length of a 2028 close.
- Whether the $115B figure survives against the 8-K's actual ratio.
