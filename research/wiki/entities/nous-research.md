**Nous Research** is the lab behind Hermes
Agent. On 2026-10-07 it closed a **$90M
Series B at a $1.5B valuation** led by Robot
Ventures, with [[nvidia|Nvidia]], Samsung,
[[microsoft|Microsoft]]'s M12, USV, Menlo,
and Y Combinator in the round. Total funding
is now **$158M**. Hermes is the product being
pushed into enterprises (TechCrunch, WSJ,
@NousResearch; ARA daily digest 2026-10-08).

## Why it matters

- **The raise prices an open-weights agent
  shop as a $1.5B company.** That is a
  mid-2026 enterprise-agent print, not a
  frontier-pretrain megaround. The company
  claims Hermes has been cloned more than
  **24 million** times and is about **2.5%**
  of global AI token usage. WSJ says
  annualized revenue was about **$36M** by
  mid-September and is expected to pass
  **$100M** by year-end. Those usage and
  revenue figures are company or Journal
  estimates, not third-party measurements
  (TechCrunch, WSJ; ARA daily digest
  2026-10-08).
- **It sits on the open-weights agent
  stack, not the closed frontier.** Hermes
  already appears as a named client of
  [[google|Google]] Home MCP and as a
  host for [[zhipu-glm-5-3|GLM-5.3]]
  FlashX via [[openrouter|OpenRouter]].
  See [[open-weights]] (ARA daily digest
  2026-10-08).
