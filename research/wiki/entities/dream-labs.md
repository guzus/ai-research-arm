**Dream Labs** launched a $90 million
seed around **Dream-1**, a robotics
world-action model API. Dream-1
controls unseen bimanual robots
zero-shot at **$3/hour** ($2/hour for
Flash). The launch held on Hacker
News (134 points / 65 comments).

## Why it matters

- **The price claim is per hour,
  not per token.** $3/hour is a
  robot-hour card, not an LLM
  token card. An independent
  RoboDojo-RC report puts Dream-1
  **16× cheaper per attempt** than
  [[gemini-4-argon|Gemini 4 Argon]].
  That is a third-party robotics
  bench, not a first-party Google
  number (Dream Labs, Hacker News;
  ARA daily digest 2026-10-25).
- **Zero-shot on unseen bimanual
  robots** is the product claim.
  It sits next to
  [[figure-ai|Figure]]'s Helix
  household stack and
  [[mistral-robostral-navigate]],
  but as an API rather than a
  first-party humanoid. Treat the
  "unseen" claim as vendor copy
  until a public eval set is
  named (Dream Labs; ARA daily
  digest 2026-10-25).
- **A $399 hardware cite landed
  in the same digest window.**
  Dream Labs separately cited
  **10,000+ units in four days
  at $399**. [[hugging-face]]
  Microduck is the other
  voice-commanded toy-robot
  print the same day (TechCrunch
  20,000 sold after a Jensen
  Huang demo). The digest does
  not say the $399 units are
  Microducks; keep the two
  counts apart (TechCrunch,
  Hacker News; ARA daily digest
  2026-10-25).
