**Poolside** is an AI software company focused on model-development technology.
It entered the wiki on **2026-08-21** as the counterparty to the cycle's largest
apparent team-plus-technology licence.

## Why it matters

- **NVIDIA's $6B licence + $1B investment (2026-08-21).** [[nvidia|NVIDIA]]
  agreed to a **non-exclusive licence to Poolside's model-development
  technology**, a **$1B investment at a $12B pre-money valuation**, and **job
  offers to 109 Poolside staff**. Both companies insist it is **not an
  acquisition**, but the disclosed terms — a **fixed $76.20-per-share investor
  payout by end-2027** — read like one (Newcomer via relays, investor letter;
  ARA daily digest 2026-08-21).
- **The template.** The deal **resembles Microsoft/Inflection, Amazon/Adept and
  Google/Character.AI** — structures that let a buyer absorb a team and
  technology while avoiding a reviewable change of control. The **licensed
  technology was not disclosed**, and the licence is **non-exclusive**, so
  Poolside's technology remains available to other buyers/partners.

## Open questions

- **What exactly was licensed?** "Model-development technology" is undisclosed —
  training data, RL tooling, agent harnesses, or something narrower.
- **Does the $76.20/share payout amount to an acquisition in substance?** The
  fixed per-share investor payout by end-2027 reads as a locked-in exit,
  undercutting the "not an acquisition" framing even if the licence keeps the
  corporate shell alive.
