**Claude Ultra** is the new name for [[anthropic]]'s $200 **Max 20x** subscription tier, announced 2026-10-31 (@alexalbert__, @claudeai via @theo).

## What changed

- **5-hour window lifted for "verified" accounts.** The rollout is staged, and newer accounts keep a temporary limit.
- **Weekly cap unchanged.** Anthropic confirmed the weekly cap stays at the old 10x-Pro level, so the change affects how usage is paced, not how much is available.
- **Messaging walk-back.** Anthropic's subscriptions lead apologized that "20x" was read as applying to both limits.

## Context

Earlier subscription-limit friction includes a June federal class action over Claude Max usage limits, logged on [[anthropic]]. The model line sold on these plans is covered on [[claude-fable-5]], [[claude-opus-5-5]] and [[claude-sonnet-5-5]].
