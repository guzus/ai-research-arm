  > Multiverse Computing's paper ("Efficient Knowledge Distillation for LLMs: Offline Top-K Logits and a Fused Chunked KL Loss") caches the teacher's top-K logits once so it never sits in memory during training, and a fused chunked KL loss avoids materializing the full vocabulary×sequence matrix — cutting a GPT-OSS 20B distillation at 32K context from four GPU nodes to one (~5× faster step time). Implementation open-sourced at github.com/CompactifAI/Full-Chunked-KL-Loss.
