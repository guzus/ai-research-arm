## Breaking News

- **OpenAI’s autonomous cyber pipeline validated 1,724 Linux-kernel bugs and produced 948 local privilege-escalation exploits in one mid-October run** (OpenAI, 2026-11-04). An upcoming “cyber model” scanned 5,034 audit targets (~2.8 million lines, about 7% of the kernel), produced 2,308 candidate findings, dynamically reproduced **1,724** of them (~74.7%), and generated **291** information-leak proofs of concept plus **948** working LPE exploits on a stock desktop OS with SMEP/SMAP/KASLR on. Total API cost: **$1,213,430**, or **$1,279 per LPE**. OpenAI compares that with historical Pwn2Own / TyphoonPWN Linux-kernel LPE bounties of **$20,000 / $70,000**, a hypothetical **$18.9M–$66.3M** (about **16–55×** the run). On a hardened kernelCTF-style target the same pipeline got **21** LPEs for **$32,936** (~**45×** if those were accepted). Agents used at least **10** novel Linux exploitation techniques, per the company; only **3.1%** of candidates were incorrect analysis. Hundreds of critical reports and patches are already going to maintainers via Patch the Planet. OpenAI’s conclusion: a low-privileged account on an unpatched OS should be treated as root for a well-resourced attacker. Source: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/a-thousand-fewer-paths-to-root/).

- **Meta acquired Conductor, the multi-agent coding workspace** (TechCrunch 2026-11-04 13:10 PT; The Verge 22:04 UTC). Founder-CEO Charlie Holtz posted that Conductor is joining Meta and teasing “the next interface” for early 2027; he said he will work with chief AI officer Alexandr Wang and Superintelligence Labs product head Nat Friedman. Meta confirmed the deal to TechCrunch and would not discuss terms. Conductor launched last summer; its agents come from Anthropic and OpenAI rather than in-house models. **Conductor Cloud goes read-only on November 13 and shuts down on November 20**, after which cloud data is deleted; local Conductor keeps models and critical fixes until early 2027. Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/04/meta-acquires-ai-coding-startup-conductor/), [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1026915/meta-conductor-acquisition).

- **Uber’s Halloween week: 320 million trips and orders, four rides booked by an AI agent** (TechCrunch 2026-11-04 13:56 PT, from the Q3 earnings call). CFO Balaji Krishnamurthy said **500** Uber Eats orders that week were placed by an agent. CEO Dara Khosrowshahi: “It is very, very early and very limited traction that we are seeing so far.” He noted a ride takes about **50 seconds** in the Uber app and a favorite food reorder about **90 seconds**, so an agent is not obviously faster. DoorDash said last week it has only seen a few hundred agent orders. Khosrowshahi still called agents “another opportunity to reach our platform” and said “every single AI system company is talking to us.” Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/04/almost-no-one-uses-ai-agents-to-book-their-uber-company-says/).

- **OpenAI shipped Images 2.6 with native GIFs and a 50% speed-up** (OpenAI 2026-11-04; The Verge 18:00 UTC). ChatGPT, Codex, and the API now generate looping GIFs from a prompt or photo, with playback-speed and per-frame edits. The company says users create **4 billion images per week** across those surfaces (more than half a billion a day); The Verge notes that is up from **3 billion** a week in September. API SKUs are **GPT-Image-2.6 Flare** (as little as **six seconds** at low quality; better poster/product layout) and **GPT-Image-2.6 Sunburst** (higher fidelity and edit consistency at medium/high). Markup editing comes to the API; SVG export and a new `complexity` parameter are live. The model is on all ChatGPT/Codex tiers globally; API pricing is on OpenAI’s pricing page (not restated here). Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-images-2-6/), [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1026474/openai-chatgpt-animated-gifs-images-2-6).

## Industry Moves

- **DuckDuckGo is pitching K-12 as the no-AI-required Search alternative** (TechCrunch 2026-11-04 11:29 PT). After Google said a full AI restriction also turns off Search, CEO Gabriel Weinberg pointed schools at DuckDuckGo’s default-search docs; public-affairs chief Kamyl Bazbaz called the coupling “how a monopolist behaves.” DuckDuckGo says AI features can be disabled in search and the browser, and it runs a dedicated [noai.duckduckgo.com](https://noai.duckduckgo.com) surface. Google’s @NewsFromGoogle clarification: Gemini, Gemini Notebook, and Search will be **off by default**, and schools can enable them one by one — but Search now includes AI Overviews / AI Mode, so “Search without AI” is still not on offer. Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/04/duckduckgo-pitches-itself-to-schools-amid-backlash-over-googles-ai-policy/).

- **OpenAI’s Chris Lehane published a 2028-election policy essay** (TechCrunch 2026-11-04 13:25 PT). The chief global affairs officer analogizes AI to FDR’s New Deal, asks politicians for a “compelling third path,” and lists worker training, capital access, employee ownership, child-safety/education, data-center buildout, and enforceable safety standards with a kill switch. He compares a data-center moratorium to the 2002 Iraq-war authorization. AI Now’s Kate Brennan called the New Deal frame “ahistorical at best.” OpenAI did not give TechCrunch more specifics. Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/04/openai-is-already-trying-to-shape-the-2028-election/).

- **Anthropic loosened Claude Code’s game-clone rule in 2.1.317** (The Verge 2026-11-04 18:53 UTC). A short Opus 5.5 prompt rule added to block recreations of named games was also refusing genre references. The update: name a game to describe mechanics and Claude should build an original game in that genre; a request for a copy is offered a similar-but-original alternative. The Nov 2 clampdown itself is outside this window. Source: [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1026672/anthropic-tweaks-how-claude-code-handles-requests-about-making-games).
