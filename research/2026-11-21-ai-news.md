## Breaking News

- **Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge says he and Claude proved the Riemann Hypothesis, and Stanford rushed out a competing all-human GRH draft the same day** (The Verge 2026-11-20 16:34 UTC; TechCrunch 19:32 UTC; The Decoder 2026-11-21 11:19 UTC). Alpöge posted a note plus Lean-formalized arguments, writing that the method “easily extends” to the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis and, with more time, the Extended Riemann Hypothesis. He texted CEO Dario Amodei “late Sunday night” and held the post until after Anthropic’s Thursday IPO; Anthropic has not commented and is not a party to the announcement. The claim is not Clay-accepted. Hours later, Stanford’s Ravi Vakil (AMS president) posted Alessandro Masullo’s independent GRH draft: “It is all human, with no AI.” Vakil said Brian Conrad had been reviewing it for months and that they had hoped to bring Masullo to Stanford for a year before going public. TechCrunch later updated that Alpöge’s Lean artifact has been checked by independent sources; The Decoder reports that about eight hours after Vakil’s post, people contacted him with AI-assisted problem reports on the Masullo draft. Neither result is independently verified as a completed Millennium Prize proof. Sources: [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1010051/anthropic-riemann-hypothesis-millennium-prize-problem), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/20/ai-claimed-a-solution-to-the-167-year-old-riemann-hypothesis-and-a-human-did-too/), [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/hours-after-anthropic-claims-a-claude-proof-of-the-riemann-hypothesis-stanford-releases-an-all-human-competing-draft/).

- **Signal is blocking screenshots so Gemini Intelligence and Siri AI cannot read chats** (Signal 2026-11-20; The Verge 12:30 UTC; The Decoder 19:50 UTC). President Meredith Whittaker calls OS-level on-screen agents an “existential threat” to Signal’s ability to independently guarantee privacy. The app now blocks screenshots on iOS 27 and when it detects an AI assistant capturing the screen on supported Android builds — the same class of defense Signal used against Windows Recall. Users can turn the protection off in settings; doing so, Whittaker says, can expose that user’s and contacts’ content and break Signal’s privacy guarantees. The fix is incomplete: filesystem and notification access remain, and macOS still has no reliable screenshot block. Signal asks Apple, Google, and Microsoft for an app-level way to opt out of OS agents. Sources: [Signal](https://signal.org/blog/protecting-your-privacy/), [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1038390/signal-blocking-ai-agent-screenshots-mobile-app).

- **OpenAI cut protected-reasoning extraction on GPT-6.2 Astra from 54.0% to 0.2%** (OpenAI, 2026-11-20). In an internal adaptive black-box red-team, attackers tried to recover chain-of-thought the model had generated on an earlier prompt. Average attack success fell from **54.0%** on GPT-6 Astra with original safeguards to **0.2%** on GPT-6.2 Astra with updated ones; extraction from the *current* request fell **22.9% → 0.9%**. Defenses mix training, input/output filters, API blocks that stop older models from reading protected reasoning, and weekly bans of thousands to tens of thousands of accounts on coordinated campaigns. OpenAI is bringing selected improvements back to GPT-6 Astra. The post frames unauthorized distillation as a dual-use safety issue (cyber and biology), not only IP. Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/combatting-adversarial-distillation), [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/openai-makes-it-much-harder-for-attackers-to-extract-how-its-latest-ai-models-think/).

## New Model Releases

- **Thinking Machines Lab shipped Inkling 2 Mini** (Thinking Machines, 2026-11-20; Hugging Face blog 22:55 UTC). Open-weight looped MoE: **26.4B total / 3.4B active** (5.4B weight activations per token with looping), ~**20GB** NVFP4/MXFP8 footprint, text+image in / text out. Architecture: 24 stored layers; the 12 middle layers run three times, for 48 layer visits per token. Vendor benches for models under 100B total include DeepSWE v1.1 **48.2%**, Vals Index v2.2 **46.5%**, SWE-bench Verified (bash only) **79.2%** (best in that row vs Qwen3.8-27B 77.8%), and a tie with Qwen3.8-27B on AA Index v5.0 at **20.1**. A research variable-loop checkpoint is also out; no-CoT time horizon rose from **16s** at one pass to **37s** at three. Hosted on Tinker; weights on Hugging Face. Sources: [Thinking Machines](https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/inkling-2-mini), [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/blog/inkling2mini), [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/11/21/thinking-machines-inkling-2-mini-looped-transformer/).

- **OpenAI put `gpt-live-1.1` in the API** (MarkTechPost, 2026-11-21 11:46 UTC, citing OpenAI’s forum/docs). Full-duplex voice SKU over WebRTC/WebSocket, 128k default context. Standby mode bills idle WebRTC time at **$0.02/min** versus **$0.05/min** active (60% lower). OpenAI lists better accents, transcription, letter/number handling, and task completion versus GPT-Live-1; MarkTechPost notes **zero published benchmark scores**. Image, video, and structured outputs are not supported. No matching `openai.com/index/…` post was fetchable here. Source: [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/11/21/openai-gpt-live-1-1-api-standby-mode/).

- **RadixArk released DaisyRose-27B** (MarkTechPost, 2026-11-20 17:42 UTC). Apache-2.0 agentic coder post-trained from Qwen3.8-27B with **RL only** (no SFT or distillation). Terminal Bench 4.1 pass@1 **5.6 → 12.0**. Dense ~27.8B; BF16 ~52GB / FP8 ~29GB, billed as one-GPU. Vendor numbers. Source: [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/11/20/radixark-daisyrose-27b-agentic-model/).

- **Reflection’s Beam technical report landed on arXiv** (submitted 2026-11-19; Friday mailing). Sparse MoE **501.3B total / 23.4B active**, pretrained on **23.8T** tokens, 1M-token context, then four weeks of asynchronous RL: **100M** rollouts on **10.5K** GB300 GPUs. First-party: frontier-level reasoning at **3–4×** less inference compute, plus **88.9%** refuse/pivot on harmful requests vs **81.5%** compliance on harmless ones. A 1.93B speculative drafter (DFlash 2) plus FP8/NVFP4 co-design is the serving stack. This is a model report, not a third-party eval. Source: [arXiv 2611.26358](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26358).

## Research Papers

arXiv’s 2026-11-20 announcement (IDs through `2611.26629`; `published` 2026-11-19) is the dated drop for this window. Saturday has no new mailing; the 2026-11-21 papers file continues the same Thursday window. Highlights with specific findings:

- **Beam Model Technical Report** ([2611.26358](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26358), Reflection Team, submitted 2026-11-19) — see New Model Releases.

- **RL Post-Training in Diffusion Transformers Is Activation Steering** ([2611.26627](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26627), Meral, Oktay, Yanardag, Chau et al.). A *fixed* set of steering vectors on frozen DiT block outputs recovers **90.0–96.3%** of the reward gains of six public RL-tuned image models (and 89.8% / 111.9% on two video models). On SD3.5-M the vectors are **0.29M** parameters vs **18.8M** LoRA (**65×** smaller).

- **TideCache** ([2611.26619](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26619), Luo, Yao, Yang, Zhijian Liu, Song Han, Hu). Hybrid KV serving in SGLang: up to **2.04×** as many agent turns and **20–59%** lower overall P50 per-token latency vs SGLang+HiCache.

- **One-text author unmasking** ([2611.26216](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26216), Desai, Emi, Gonen, Daumé, Wieting, Karpinska, Iyyer et al.). One sample and a zero-shot prompt: Opus 5.5 names the correct lab on **98%** of Anthropic/OpenAI/Google texts; Fable 5.2 names the author of **39%** of public Oct–Nov 2026 peer reviews (**78%** on a Nature subset) and the username behind **69%** of active Redditors.

- **TwinLab-Bench** ([2611.26248](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26248), Wan, Zhu). 52 self-designed lab experiments graded against hidden real-lab records. Claude Fable 5.2 averages **45.0**, Opus 5.5 **40.2**, GPT-6.1-sol **31.7**.

- **Awaken-HI** ([2611.26420](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26420), EngineAI). ~50,000 hours of egocentric stereo + whole-body motion; after T800 adaptation, mean success **93.75%** on eight tasks, beating GR00T N1.7 and π₀.₅ on the authors’ evals.

The Alpöge Lean dump is not an arXiv paper.
