## Breaking News

**Anthropic warns infostealer malware is hijacking Claude sessions to drain usage.** Anthropic disclosed that common infostealer malware — including Vidar, LummaC2, StealC, RedLine and Acreed on Windows, plus Atomic Stealer (AMOS) on a small number of Macs — is stealing already-authenticated Claude session cookies from infected computers, letting attackers replay victims' sessions and burn through paid usage without a password, bypassing 2FA/SSO entirely. Anthropic is signing out affected users, removing saved payment methods, and refunding unauthorized charges, though signing out doesn't remove the malware itself, so a still-infected machine can be compromised again on the next login. ([BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/anthropic-warns-infostealer-malware-is-hijacking-claude-sessions-to-drain-usage/), [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/08/31/claude-accounts-compromised-through-infostealer/))

**OpenAI cuts off model access to SpaceX-owned Cursor; Anthropic moves to fill the gap.** OpenAI said it will end Cursor's access to its models on November 12, citing distrust after SpaceX's $60B all-stock acquisition of Cursor-maker Anysphere (completed August 14) — pointing to Elon Musk's companies' history of contract violations, including X breaking a data-licensing deal and Musk's admitted xAI distillation of OpenAI models. Cursor CEO Michael Truell said OpenAI models account for only about 5% of Cursor's traffic and that the companies are still in discussions; users will still be able to bring their own OpenAI API keys. Anthropic co-founder Tom Brown said Anthropic will instead increase Claude compute supply to Cursor, a notable move given Anthropic has reportedly been buying compute from SpaceX's Colossus cluster since May. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/29/openai-cursor-spacex-model-access.html), [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/openai-cuts-off-cursor-after-spacex-acquisition-citing-musks-history-of-breaking-contracts/), [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/jonmarkman/2026/08/31/openai-cuts-off-cursor-after-spacexs-60-billion-takeover/))

## Industry Moves

**OpenAI retires the DALL·E GPT from ChatGPT.** As of August 30, the standalone official DALL·E GPT is gone from ChatGPT; OpenAI is pointing all users to ChatGPT Images, built on the newer `gpt-image-1` / `gpt-image-1-mini` models. User-built GPTs with image generation enabled are unaffected. Users were told to download any images they wanted to keep before the cutover. ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/DALL-E-leaves-ChatGPT-on-August-30-download-your-images-first.1360522.0.html), [CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/openai-removes-dall-e-chatgpt/))

**Caterpillar details its enterprise AI rollout, drawing on autonomous-mining experience.** Speaking to TechCrunch on the sidelines of the Ai4 conference in Las Vegas (published August 30), CTO Jaime Mineart said Caterpillar is applying lessons from its autonomous mining-truck fleet (11B+ tons moved, 385M+ autonomous km) to broader jobsite and construction AI deployments, and plans to spend $100 million over five years training its 118,000-person workforce in AI, autonomy and robotics. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/30/caterpillar-is-bringing-to-ai-deployment-what-it-learned-from-automating-mining/))

**Google rolls out in-room Gemini note-taking control for Meet hardware.** Starting August 31, Google Meet Hardware touch controllers (Early Preview devices first) can start, stop, and manage the "Take notes for me" Gemini feature directly, without requiring a laptop joined in Companion mode — including pausing notes for off-the-record moments. ([Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/08/control-take-notes-for-me-directly-from-Google-Meet-hardware-touch-controllers.html))

**OpenAI reportedly buys tens of thousands of Macs to train computer-use AI agents.** Per The Information (reported August 31), OpenAI has purchased tens of thousands of display-less/keyboard-less Mac mini and Mac Studio units — likely including Apple's new M6-based models refreshed August 25 — to run reinforcement-learning workloads for computer-use agents, a departure from its usual GPU-cloud reliance. Apple's unified-memory architecture, which pools RAM across CPU and GPU on one chip, is reportedly a better fit for the memory-bound, parallelism-light nature of RL loops that repeatedly observe a screen, act, and get feedback. Anthropic is separately reported to be renting Mac minis via AWS for similar workloads. ([TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-openai-mac-mini-mac-studio-ai-agents/), [Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/08/31/openai-buys-apple-macs-ai-training/))

## Regulatory Updates

**California's 2026 legislative session adjourns with a wave of AI bills passed.** The session ended at midnight August 31, with lawmakers racing roughly two dozen AI-related bills to Governor Newsom's desk. The legislature overwhelmingly passed SB 813 (authored by Sen. Jerry McNerney), creating the first U.S. state framework for independent verification organizations to test AI safety claims and measure risk, and gave final approval to AB 1883, which limits employers' use of AI to monitor workers' nervous systems and emotional states. Newsom must now sign or veto the bills covering companion chatbots, algorithmic pricing, deepfakes, and automated decision-making. ([AI Journal](https://aijourn.com/california-legislature-overwhelmingly-passes-fathom-sponsored-bill-to-spur-independent-verification-of-ai-safety/), [HR Dive](https://www.hrdive.com/news/california-ban-on-workplace-ai-emotion-surveillance-heads-to-newsom/829207/))
