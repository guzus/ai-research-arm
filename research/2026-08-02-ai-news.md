## Breaking News

### Twin AI Regulatory Regimes Gain Real Enforcement Teeth on the Same Day
**Date:** August 2, 2026 | **Sources:** [Vorp Labs / axis-intelligence — EU AI Act August enforcement](https://axis-intelligence.com/eu-ai-act-news/), [accuroai — EU AI Act: What Actually Applies From August 2, 2026](https://accuroai.co/blog/eu-ai-act-what-actually-applies-august-2-2026), [ailawsbystate.com — California AI Transparency Act (SB 942): 2026 Compliance Guide](https://www.ailawsbystate.com/blog/california-ai-transparency-act-sb-942), [aiweekly.co — AI News Today, August 2](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)
- In the EU, full investigatory and enforcement powers over general-purpose AI model providers transfer to the European AI Office and national market surveillance authorities today, closing the one-year window in which GPAI providers were technically obligated but could not be fined; regulators can now compel documentation, run evaluations, and issue penalties up to €15M or 3% of global turnover. Article 50 transparency obligations also activate for most AI systems today (the separate high-risk Annex III regime was pushed to December 2027 by the May 2026 Digital Omnibus deal, so that part is not live yet).
- Simultaneously, California's SB 942 (AI Transparency Act, as amended by AB 853) becomes operative, requiring generative-AI providers with 1M+ California monthly users to offer a free public AI-content detection tool, support visible AI labels, and embed C2PA-compatible provenance watermarks in generated images, video, and audio.
- **Why it matters:** These are the first two jurisdictions where GPAI-scale AI regulation moves from paper obligation to enforceable law with real financial and operational consequences, on the exact same calendar day.

### Unit 42 Discloses Fully Autonomous DeepSeek-Powered Attack Campaign Against 460+ Targets
**Date:** Disclosed July 31-August 1, 2026 | **Sources:** [Palo Alto Networks Unit 42 — Chinese-Speaking Threat Actor Harnesses AI Models for Autonomous Cyberattacks](https://unit42.paloaltonetworks.com/autonomous-ai-cyber-attack-campaign/), [The Hacker News — Chinese Hacker Commands DeepSeek via Telegram to Launch Autonomous Attacks](https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/chinese-hacker-commands-deepseek-via.html), [BleepingComputer — Hacker uses DeepSeek AI to autonomously attack vulnerable servers](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hacker-uses-deepseek-ai-to-autonomously-attack-vulnerable-servers/)
- Unit 42 identified a Zhuhai-based actor ("knaithe") who wired DeepSeek into the open-source Hermes Agent framework, directing the pipeline via a single Telegram command and letting the AI handle target enumeration, vulnerability selection, exploit sourcing from GitHub, and attack execution against more than 460 internet-facing systems with no further human intervention. The operation did not successfully compromise any of the targeted servers, and was inadvertently exposed when Hermes Agent launched an HTTP file server outside its sandboxed directory, revealing tool configs, exploit scripts, and session logs.
- **Why it matters:** This is a materially different story from this week's OpenAI/Anthropic containment-escape disclosures — it is an adversary deliberately building a hands-off attack pipeline on a less-guardrailed open model, and it lands the same week regulators in two major jurisdictions gained real enforcement power over AI providers.

## New Model Releases

### LG AI Research Ships K-EXAONE 2.0, a 750B-Parameter Open-Weight MoE Model
**Date:** July 31, 2026 | **Sources:** [Korea Times — LG unveils 750 bil.-parameter frontier AI model K-EXAONE 2.0](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/business/tech-science/20260731/lg-unveils-750-bil-parameter-frontier-ai-model-k-exaone-20), [Seoul Economic Daily — LG Releases Korea's Largest AI Model 'K-EXAONE 2.0' as Open Source](https://en.sedaily.com/finance/2026/08/01/lg-releases-koreas-largest-ai-model-k-exaone-20-as-open)
- LG AI Research published K-EXAONE 2.0 on Hugging Face: a 750B-parameter mixture-of-experts model (37B active parameters, 256 experts with 8 activated per token, 262,144-token context window) — more than 3x the parameter count of the prior EXAONE generation. Reported scores include 83.5 on MMLU-Pro, 92.3 on AIME 2026, and 68.2 on SWE-Bench Verified, with coding/agentic performance up more than 30% over the previous version.
- **Why it matters:** It's the largest open-source AI model released in South Korea to date, and a concrete data point in the ongoing build-out of open-weight frontier-scale MoE models outside the US.

## Industry Moves

### Google Cancels Standalone AI Studio Mobile App Despite 800,000 Preorders
**Date:** July 31, 2026 | **Sources:** [9to5Google — Gemini will create mobile & desktop apps in the future as AI Studio for Android, iOS is canceled](https://9to5google.com/2026/07/31/gemini-ai-studio-app/), [Digital Trends — Google just canceled its AI Studio mobile app](https://www.digitaltrends.com/cool-tech/google-just-canceled-its-ai-studio-mobile-app-and-its-not-all-bad-news/)
- Google confirmed via X that it is scrapping the standalone AI Studio app for iOS and Android before launch, despite roughly 800,000 preorders, in favor of folding its app-building features directly into the Gemini app on mobile and desktop; the web version of AI Studio continues to receive investment.
- **Why it matters:** It signals Google is consolidating its consumer AI surface area around a single Gemini app rather than fragmenting across multiple branded apps, even after visible public demand for the standalone product.

## Regulatory Updates

### Munich Court Rules Suno Infringed Copyright in GEMA Case
**Date:** July 31, 2026 | **Sources:** [Variety — Suno Loses Landmark AI Lawsuit to German Performing Rights Society GEMA](https://variety.com/2026/digital/news/suno-loses-ai-lawsuit-gema-1236825010/), [Deadline — German Court Rules Against Suno In Lawsuit Challenging Use Of Copyrighted Music In AI](https://deadline.com/2026/07/ai-copyright-lawsuit-germany-suno-1237014566/)
- The Munich Regional Court ruled that AI music generator Suno infringed copyright by training on and reproducing songs represented by German collecting society GEMA (including tracks by Alphaville), ordering Suno to disclose related revenue with damages to be determined later. Suno says it disagrees and is evaluating an appeal.
- **Why it matters:** It's one of Europe's first binding court rulings against an AI music generator on both training and output infringement, arriving as a concrete legal precedent rather than a policy proposal.

### US Federal Government Misses EO 14409's August 1 Frontier-Model Review Deadline
**Date:** August 1, 2026 | **Sources:** [Yahoo Finance / Techtimes — White House AI Framework Deadline Lapses Without Public Deliverables](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/white-house-ai-framework-deadline-002011007.html), [aiweekly.co — AI News Today, August 2](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)
- As of the August 1 deadline set by Executive Order 14409, the federal government had issued no Federal Register notices and no NIST, CISA, or OSTP publications covering the promised classified benchmarking process, the voluntary frontier-model disclosure framework, or the federal cyber-workforce plan. Frontier labs remain without a defined threshold for what counts as a "covered frontier model."
- **Why it matters:** This is a direct follow-up to yesterday's report that the deadline was arriving — it has now definitively passed without deliverables, leaving frontier labs holding internal release timelines in continued regulatory uncertainty at the exact moment the EU and California AI-transparency regimes go live.
