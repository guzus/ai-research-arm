## Breaking News

### Alibaba Launches Qwen3.8-Max, a 2.4-Trillion-Parameter Model Aimed Squarely at OpenAI and Anthropic
**Date:** August 3, 2026 | **Sources:** [The Decoder — Alibaba's open-weight Qwen3.8-Max takes on long-horizon AI tasks with 2.4 trillion parameters](https://the-decoder.com/alibabas-open-weight-qwen3-8-max-takes-on-long-horizon-ai-tasks-with-2-4-trillion-parameters/), [SiliconANGLE — Alibaba debuts Qwen3.8-Max model with 2.4T parameters](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/03/alibaba-debuts-qwen3-8-max-model-2-4t-parameters/), [InfoWorld — Alibaba takes aim at OpenAI and Anthropic with Qwen3.8-Max launch](https://www.infoworld.com/article/4204415/alibaba-takes-aim-at-openai-and-anthropic-with-qwen3-8-max-launch.html)
- Alibaba introduced Qwen3.8-Max, its largest AI model to date and the Qwen family's first multimodal model above 1 trillion parameters: a 2.4-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model that activates roughly 95 billion parameters per inference pass, with a 1-million-token context window and text/image/video/document input. It is priced at $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens. Open weights for Qwen3.8-Max and a smaller Qwen3.8-27B checkpoint are scheduled to land on Hugging Face next week via Alibaba Cloud's Model Studio — not yet published as of this report.
- **Why it matters:** It is explicitly positioned against closed frontier offerings from OpenAI and Anthropic on price and context length, and would be the first Max-class Qwen model ever released open-weight if the promised weights ship on schedule.

### Palantir Posts Record Quarter, Then Its CEO Calls the AI Industry "Marxist"
**Date:** August 3, 2026 | **Sources:** [TechCrunch — After killer quarter, Palantir CEO Alex Karp calls AI industry 'Marxist'](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/03/after-killer-quarter-palantir-ceo-alex-karp-calls-ai-industry-marxist/), [CNBC — Palantir (PLTR) earnings Q2 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/palantir-pltr-earnings-q2-2026.html)
- Palantir reported Q2 2026 revenue of $1.935 billion, up 93% year-over-year, with U.S. commercial revenue up 149% and GAAP net income of roughly $1.07 billion. In the shareholder letter accompanying the results, CEO Alex Karp wrote "There are Marxist overtones and undertones to our business" about frontier AI labs, arguing that companies building large language models intend, "knowingly or otherwise, to capture the means of production of their purported partners" — a swipe at labs he says are too untrustworthy for enterprises to work with directly.
- **Why it matters:** It's a pointed competitive framing from one of the largest AI-adjacent enterprise vendors, timed to land in the same news cycle as its strongest quarter on record.

## Regulatory Updates

### White House Reviews Completed Voluntary AI Testing Framework with OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google
**Date:** August 3, 2026 | **Sources:** [CNBC — White House to host AI companies Tuesday to review new model-testing framework](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/white-house-ai-companies-voluntary-framework-meeting.html), [CNN Business — White House to meet with OpenAI, Anthropic and other top AI companies in first big regulation push](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/03/tech/white-house-meet-with-top-ai-companies-big-regulation-push)
- The White House met with staff from OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic to review a completed voluntary framework for testing the cybersecurity capabilities of frontier AI models, stemming from a June 2026 executive order. Per reporting, the framework would give federal agencies up to a 30-day pre-release review window on covered frontier models but explicitly cannot be used to create a mandatory licensing or preclearance system; the specific capability benchmarks and thresholds that trigger review remain classified, and no framework text has been published.
- **Why it matters:** It's the clearest sign yet of the shape U.S. frontier-model oversight will take — a voluntary, pre-release access channel rather than a rulemaking — landing within two weeks of the EU AI Act's enforceable transparency obligations taking effect.
