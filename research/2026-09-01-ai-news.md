## Breaking News

**Pentagon adds OpenAI's ChatGPT Mil and xAI's Grok for Government to its GenAI.mil portal.** The Department of Defense launched custom military versions of ChatGPT and Grok on the GenAI.mil platform on August 31, joining Google Gemini as the third and fourth models available to the department's roughly 3 million personnel (1.7 million of whom are already onboarded to the portal). ChatGPT Mil is scoped to chat, files, projects, and custom GPTs for document-heavy unclassified work — administrative tasks, logistics, planning, and policy — and is exempt from the consumer-product data collection that applies to ordinary ChatGPT. GenAI.mil launched last December with Gemini alone. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/31/the-pentagon-now-has-its-own-version-of-chatgpt-and-grok/), [DefenseScoop](https://defensescoop.com/2026/08/31/grok-chatgpt-added-to-genai-mil/), [Military Times](https://www.militarytimes.com/industry/techwatch/2026/08/31/the-militarys-chatgpt-is-now-live-via-the-pentagons-genai-platform/))

## New Model Releases

**Moonshot AI retires Kimi K2.5 and the moonshot-v1 series, leaving Kimi K3 as its sole flagship.** As of August 31, calls to the `kimi-k2.5` and `moonshot-v1` model endpoints return a 404 error; Moonshot is directing all users to `kimi-k3`, the 2.8-trillion-parameter, native-vision, 1,048,576-token-context MoE model it shipped July 16, now the only current flagship on its official platform. ([BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/87dc9670-9bba-43d7-83ef-6b3aff4af97c))

## Funding & Acquisitions

**Nvidia invests $3.5B in MediaTek to deepen AI computing partnership.** Announced August 31, Nvidia is buying $3.5 billion in MediaTek convertible bonds as the companies expand collaboration across three areas: MediaTek adopting Nvidia's NVLink Fusion platform so hyperscalers and frontier-model developers can build custom XPUs into NVLink-connected rack-scale AI factories; continued joint development of RTX Spark and DGX Spark chips for consumer/enterprise PCs; and AI-powered automotive platforms built on MediaTek's Dimensity Auto line. ([NVIDIA Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-and-mediatek-deepen-long-standing-partnership-to-build-ai-edge-to-cloud-computing-platforms), [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/31/3353306/0/en/nvidia-and-mediatek-deepen-long-standing-partnership-to-build-ai-edge-to-cloud-computing-platforms.html))

**AI sales/marketing platform Clay raises new round at $7B pre-money, led by Wellington Management.** Reported August 31 by Axios, the deal marks a jump from the $5 billion valuation set in Clay's January 2026 employee tender offer (led by DST Global), which itself followed an August 2025 Series C at a $3.1 billion valuation. ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/pro/all-deals/2026/08/31/clay-7-billion-pre-money-valuation))

## Industry Moves

**John Ternus becomes Apple's CEO, succeeding Tim Cook, effective September 1.** Cook moves to executive chairman after roughly 15 years as CEO; Ternus, who spent 24 years leading Apple's hardware engineering — including the Apple silicon and on-device Apple Intelligence roadmap — also joins Apple's board effective today. It is Apple's first CEO transition since Cook succeeded Steve Jobs in 2011, and comes as Apple Intelligence and Siri's generative overhaul are central to Apple's product strategy. ([Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/04/tim-cook-to-become-apple-executive-chairman-john-ternus-to-become-apple-ceo/), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/20/apple-names-john-ternus-ceo-replacing-tim-cook-who-becomes-chairman.html))
