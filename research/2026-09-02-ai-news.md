## Breaking News

**Google ships Gemini 3.8 Flash and Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber.** Announced September 2 — Google's third Flash SKU in six weeks — the general model is described as a workhorse that "works harder" via extra reasoning steps and iterative tool calls. Introductory API pricing matches 3.7 Flash at $0.75 / $3.75 per million input/output tokens through year-end (list $1.50 / $7.50 from January 2027). Google-reported DeepSWE v1.1: 73.7% (3.7 Flash 65.3%, GPT-5.6 Sol 72.7%, Claude Opus 5 74.0%). Artificial Analysis scores Intelligence Index 59 (vs 56 for 3.7 Flash) at about $0.58/task — cheapest at that intelligence band, but ~40% more expensive per task than 3.7 Flash because of ~30% more output tokens and extra agentic turns. 3.8 Flash Cyber, distributed through the Fairwind Program (~650 members including CrowdStrike and the Center for Internet Security), is limited to governments and trusted partners; Google cites CyberGym 86.2% (3.5 Flash Cyber 77.5%, GPT-5.6 Sol 83.6%) and CWE-Bench Pass@1 47.2%. The consumer model is live in the Gemini app (Pro/Ultra), AI Studio, Antigravity, Android Studio, Gemini Enterprise, Search AI Mode, and Sheets. ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/988742/google-gemini-3-8-flash), [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/09/google-releases-gemini-3-8-flash-its-third-flash-model-in-six-weeks/), [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/gemini-3-8-flash-is-googles-third-budget-model-in-six-weeks-while-frontier-models-remain-mia/))

**DOJ files a statement of interest backing OpenAI's fair-use defense in the New York Times copyright suit.** Reported September 2, the Trump administration's brief argues that excluding LLM training from fair use would be "inconsistent with basic copyright law principles" and would "thwart… creative and scientific progress while hindering American prosperity." The 2023 Times suit seeks billions from OpenAI and Microsoft; the filing is not a ruling. **New York City bans classroom AI for about 600,000 students in 2-K through eighth grade.** Mayor Zohran Mamdani announced the one-year moratorium September 2 for the 2026–27 school year. Teachers may not use AI to grade; companion/mental-support chatbots are banned in all grades. High-school use is limited and paired with twice-yearly AI-literacy classes. Individual screens are banned until third grade; a limited high-school pilot (Quill, Edia, Brisk Teaching, Playlab, Intel AI-Ready Schools) is capped at five classes. Exceptions cover disability and multilingual supports plus computer-science programs. ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/policy/988228/nyc-ai-restrictions-in-schools-chatbot-ban))

**Thirty new Tumbler Ridge lawsuits accuse OpenAI of "aiding and abetting" the school shooting.** Filed September 2 in California federal court by students, teachers, and the principal, the suits allege OpenAI's review system flagged Jesse Van Rootselaar's ChatGPT conversations about gun violence, that safety staff recommended contacting Canadian authorities, and that chief global affairs officer Chris Lehane was involved in a decision not to. They also allege deactivating rather than banning the account let the suspect return with a new email. OpenAI CSO Jason Kwon called claims about safety-team motives "completely untrue." ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/988261/openai-tumbler-ridge-shooting-lawsuit-aiding-abetting), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/02/openai-faces-30-more-lawsuits-tied-to-tumbler-ridge-shooting/))

**Safety researchers warn Astra's "recurrent depth" will weaken chain-of-thought monitoring.** TechCrunch reported September 2 (The Information first) that Astra will use "opaque recurrence" — looping the same query so fewer tokens stay in a linear CoT. Redwood's Buck Shlegeris and Ryan Greenblatt, and Zvi Mowshowitz, said scaling the technique could destroy CoT monitorability; OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki said preserving CoT monitoring is "a core goal" and the company pushed back on a shift to "neuralese." Use of the technique is described as limited. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/02/openais-new-reasoning-technique-alarms-ai-safety-experts/))

**Anthropic ships Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Mythos 5.1.** Announced September 1 on Anthropic's newsroom, the two products are the same underlying model with different safeguards: Fable 5.1 is generally available (`claude-fable-5-1` on the API, plus AWS, Google Cloud, and Azure); Mythos 5.1 is limited to trusted-access programs for cybersecurity and life-sciences work. Enterprise Frontier Safeguards (customer-controlled cloud storage with the privacy of zero data retention) begin rolling out this fall; eligible customers can use Fable 5.1 with ZDR until then. Fable 5.1 may now be used to identify software vulnerabilities, but penetration testing, exploit generation, and binary vulnerability scanning still redirect to Opus. ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-1), [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/987830/anthropic-claude-fable-mythos-5-1), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/01/anthropics-new-fable-release-is-cheaper-less-restrictive/))

**World Labs unveils Atlas, an omni world model for spatial intelligence.** Officially posted September 1 (covered September 2): Atlas is a multimodal autoregressive diffusion transformer pretrained from scratch on text, images, video, and 3D, with every input anchored at a 3D pose. Human raters preferred Atlas in 75–94% of camera-control comparisons vs MiniMax H3, Gemini Omni Flash, Happy Horse 1.1, and Seedance 2.5. Early access is limited to select partners and will power future Marble versions. World Labs raised a $1 billion round in February 2026. ([World Labs](https://www.worldlabs.ai/blog/atlas), [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/world-labs-unveils-atlas-a-single-ai-model-that-generates-reconstructs-and-simulates-3d-worlds-from-just-a-few-photos/))

**Gemini Flash models add agent-based video analysis.** Reported September 2 by The Decoder from Google's developer materials: Gemini 3.7 Flash, 3.6 Flash, and 3.5 Flash-Lite can now choose which video segments, frame rates, and modalities (frames, audio, transcript) to inspect instead of sampling at a fixed 1 fps. ## Research Papers

**Mechanism Design for Alignment and Control** (`2609.01595`, September 1). Yale/Columbia/MIT/DeepMind economists Dirk Bergemann, Andrew Koh, and Stephen Morris give a revelation-principle treatment of agents whose preferences *and* capabilities are private. Capabilities can be concealed but not counterfeited, which yields nested cyclical monotonicity for implementable policies. Worked cases cover sandbagging, an alignment–interpretability trade-off, peer scoring, competitive reward coupling, and scalable oversight (a weak monitor can robustly force a strong actor onto the designer's favorite action). ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01595))

**SMELT: compute-matched MoE looped transformers** (`2609.01343`, September 1). Tsinghua/ByteDance Seed/M-A-P loop the middle half of a sparse MoE twice while matching the unlooped baseline on per-token FLOPs, non-embedding parameters, *and* KV cache, then fit a separate Chinchilla law up to 54B. Loss falls faster with compute (6.8–18.0% fewer training FLOPs on the compute-optimal frontier); the gap is largest on code and grows with sample length. A second pass reduces the attention sink and redirects mass toward content tokens. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01343))

**Harness-of-Harness enables multi-day autonomous software development** (`2609.01481`, September 1). Shanghai AI Lab wraps existing coding harnesses in iterative plan–code–test loops. Across GameCraft-Bench, FrontierSWE, and ProgramBench, three harness–model pairs (Codex/GPT-5.5, OpenCode/DeepSeek-V4-Pro, Pi/MiniMax-M3) gain 52.25% on average and 82.86% at peak after three iterations. A 70-iteration multi-day run ships a first-person shooter with a coherent storyline. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01481))

**Knowledge distillation during mid-training favors reasoning over factual recall** (`2609.01532`, September 1). Jacqueline He, Howard Yen, Shuyue Stella Li, Pang Wei Koh, Luke Zettlemoyer, and Wen-tau Yih find that forward-KL from a post-trained teacher helps both reasoning and facts in pre-training, but during mid-training it keeps lifting reasoning while slowing factual acquisition. Switch Distillation routes on teacher entropy and otherwise falls back to next-token CE. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01532))

**Ant Group's UI-Venus-2 is an open-weight GUI agent for mobile, web, and desktop.** Posted to arXiv September 1–2 (`2609.00028`), the Venus Team scales environment coverage (170+ multilingual mobile apps plus native desktop OS), function-grounded task generation, and trace-/sample-level verifiers with visual keypoints and multi-model voting. Weights initialize from Qwen3.5-9B and Qwen3.6-27B; training is mid-training, per-domain offline RL, then multi-teacher on-policy distillation. Full weights and eval infra are released. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00028))

**OpenAgentFlow proposes a shared pre-execution safety plane for heterogeneous agent fleets.** Posted `2609.00015` (September 1–2 window): GUI actions, API/tool calls, and LLM-planned invocations are normalized into an AgentEvent stream and gated by a Policy Enforcement Point before commit. On a 300-case action-event benchmark the authors report 94.0% accuracy and a 95.3% attack block rate; a 100-case Android emulator suite scores 90.8% raw / 92.9% trace-adjusted. Prompt-only advisory blocked no attacks in their comparison. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00015))

**SAGE learns cheap policies from imperfect VLM teachers.** `2609.01567`, published September 1: Selective Agent Guidance via Entropy queries a vision-language model only when the learner is uncertain, then distills the teacher into a lightweight RL policy so the expensive VLM is not needed at every step. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01567))

**MIDR moves multimodal document reasoning to index time.** `2609.01316`, published September 1: a training-free enrichment-augmented indexer uses a multimodal LLM at ingest so visually rich tables/charts are searchable without putting ColPali-style late interaction on the query-time path. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01316))
