## Breaking News

**Anthropic ships Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Mythos 5.1.** Announced September 1 on Anthropic's newsroom, the two products are the same underlying model with different safeguards: Fable 5.1 is generally available (`claude-fable-5-1` on the API, plus AWS, Google Cloud, and Azure); Mythos 5.1 is limited to trusted-access programs for cybersecurity and life-sciences work. Cache-read pricing is cut 75% to $0.25 per million tokens; input/output stay at $10 / $50 per million. Anthropic estimates typical token-billed workloads run about 25% cheaper than Fable 5, and highly agentic jobs up to about 45% cheaper. Headline scores: Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1 at 52.6% (vs 24.7% Fable 5, 29.0% Opus 5, 22.4% GPT-5.6 Sol); Terminal-Bench 4.0 at 55.8% (Mythos 5.1 60.9%); Humanity's Last Exam 60.9% without tools / 65.0% with tools; CursorBench 3.2.0 73.4%. Enterprise Frontier Safeguards (customer-controlled cloud storage with the privacy of zero data retention) begin rolling out this fall; eligible customers can use Fable 5.1 with ZDR until then. Fable 5.1 may now be used to identify software vulnerabilities, but penetration testing, exploit generation, and binary vulnerability scanning still redirect to Opus. ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-1), [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/987830/anthropic-claude-fable-mythos-5-1), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/01/anthropics-new-fable-release-is-cheaper-less-restrictive/))

**OpenAI says Astra is the first model to cross the "Critical" cybersecurity capability threshold under its Preparedness Framework.** Reported September 1 by CNBC and confirmed on OpenAI's own blog, expert-led red-team assessments found Astra could discover previously unknown vulnerabilities in a hardened browser and operating system and chain them into working exploits without human intervention — including a full browser-sandbox escape that executed commands on the host, and a local-privilege-escalation chain from unprivileged user to root. OpenAI says it delayed parts of Astra's development and release to build and test stronger safeguards, and now judges those safeguards sufficient to release under the framework. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/01/open-ai-astra-cyber-model.html), [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/responding-next-frontier-critical-cyber-capabilities/))

**ChatGPT Health connects to Epic EHRs and public medical data sources.** OpenAI said September 1 that clinicians can import appointment notes, labs, medications, and specialist documentation from Epic — which holds records for more than 325 million patients — and, in some deployments, run pre-visit review and clinical timelines inside the chart. Access is read-only; the model does not write back to the record. A Healthcare Public Data plug-in can pull ClinicalTrials.gov, CMS Coverage, RxNorm, DailyMed, and PubMed. Organizations with a Business Associate Agreement can use ChatGPT Work, Codex, apps, and connectors for compliant workflows. OpenAI cited 4,300+ physician responses across 27 clinical use cases with 99.1% judged safe, and reiterated that the product is not for diagnosis or treatment. ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-connects-health-records-and-healthcare-sources/), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/01/chatgpt-health-adds-epic-integration-for-clinicians-to-import-patient-data/))

**ChatGPT Ads crosses $1 billion annualized revenue run rate less than 200 days after launch; self-service buying expands to India, Europe, Middle East and North Africa.** Effective September 1, advertisers in those regions can buy ChatGPT ads directly through Ads Manager rather than through agency-led deals, opening the platform to smaller businesses; OpenAI says tens of thousands of advertisers now use the product across more than 40 countries. ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/expanding-access-to-ai-with-chatgpt-ads/), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/31/open-ai-chatgpt-ads-revenue.html))

**Apple asks a court for expedited discovery, alleging OpenAI is destroying evidence in its trade-secrets suit.** In a Monday filing reported September 1 by The Verge (Bloomberg earlier), Apple says OpenAI only recently produced a company MacBook kept by former Apple engineer Chang Liu after he joined OpenAI; inspection allegedly found a confidential Apple circuit schematic used in OpenAI work and messages about “destroying the types of forensic data Apple needs.” OpenAI calls the dispute “a mess of Apple’s own making” and says there is no evidence Liu accessed Apple information improperly. The suit, filed in July, accuses OpenAI of stealing trade secrets to build an AI device. ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/987575/apple-openai-destroying-evidence-trade-secrets-lawsuit))

**Google launches Pics, an AI image studio for Workspace and paid Gemini subscribers.** Officially posted September 1, Google Pics is built on the Nano Banana image model and rolls out over coming weeks to Google AI Pro/Ultra subscribers and most Workspace business customers. Docs and Slides integration started the same day (`pics.new`); Drive follows later. Features include object isolation, in-image text edit/translation, multi-generation pick lists, and collaborative editing. ([Google](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/workspace/google-pics/), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/01/googles-answer-to-canva-is-an-ai-tool-where-you-prompt-instead-of-design/))

## New Model Releases

**Gemini Flash models add agent-based video analysis.** Reported September 2 by The Decoder from Google's developer materials: Gemini 3.7 Flash, 3.6 Flash, and 3.5 Flash-Lite can now choose which video segments, frame rates, and modalities (frames, audio, transcript) to inspect instead of sampling at a fixed 1 fps. Google says that cuts token use by up to 88% and cost by about 66% on 1H-VideoQA and LVBench while slightly raising accuracy, and can catch sub-second cuts. The mode is live via the Gemini API (processing mode `agentic`) at standard token rates, with later rollout to the Gemini app and YouTube's "Ask YouTube." ([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/google-geminis-new-agent-based-video-analysis-cuts-token-usage-by-up-to-88-percent/))

**Runway unveils Solaris, an "Interface World Model."** Announced September 1, Solaris renders interactive 720p UIs frame-by-frame from clicks, drags, or voice instead of executing app code, building on Gen-4.5. Runway is pitching adaptive shopping, interior visualization, and visual tutorials; it says readable text, long-session reliability, and screen-reader support are still unsolved, so the system is a research/early-access effort rather than a shipping product. ([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/runways-solaris-is-an-ai-system-that-generates-software-interfaces-in-real-time/))

No other new public model SKUs with verifiable version numbers/specs from the last 24-48 hours met the recency bar beyond Fable/Mythos 5.1 and the Astra safety-threshold disclosure above (Astra itself has not shipped a public release date).

## Research Papers

**Ant Group's UI-Venus-2 is an open-weight GUI agent for mobile, web, and desktop.** Posted to arXiv September 1–2 (`2609.00028`), the Venus Team scales environment coverage (170+ multilingual mobile apps plus native desktop OS), function-grounded task generation, and trace-/sample-level verifiers with visual keypoints and multi-model voting. Weights initialize from Qwen3.5-9B and Qwen3.6-27B; training is mid-training, per-domain offline RL, then multi-teacher on-policy distillation. Full weights and eval infra are released. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00028))

**OpenAgentFlow proposes a shared pre-execution safety plane for heterogeneous agent fleets.** Posted `2609.00015` (September 1–2 window): GUI actions, API/tool calls, and LLM-planned invocations are normalized into an AgentEvent stream and gated by a Policy Enforcement Point before commit. On a 300-case action-event benchmark the authors report 94.0% accuracy and a 95.3% attack block rate; a 100-case Android emulator suite scores 90.8% raw / 92.9% trace-adjusted. Prompt-only advisory blocked no attacks in their comparison. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00015))

**SAGE learns cheap policies from imperfect VLM teachers.** `2609.01567`, published September 1: Selective Agent Guidance via Entropy queries a vision-language model only when the learner is uncertain, then distills the teacher into a lightweight RL policy so the expensive VLM is not needed at every step. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01567))

**MIDR moves multimodal document reasoning to index time.** `2609.01316`, published September 1: a training-free enrichment-augmented indexer uses a multimodal LLM at ingest so visually rich tables/charts are searchable without putting ColPali-style late interaction on the query-time path. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01316))

([McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/build-vs-buy-shift-32-113806700.html))

**Anthropic reports early lab-validated science results from Mythos/Fable 5.1.** In the same September 1 announcement: Mythos-designed protein binders hit ~50% experimental hit rate across 12 targets (typical literature 10–15%) and 10× higher affinity than the best Adaptyv Bio competition designs on three named targets; Fable 5.1 produced a higher-resolution Venus elevation map from Magellan radar (released CC); Mythos wrote custom GPU kernels that sped seven open-source biology models by up to 2.5×. These are company-reported experimental results, not independent peer review. ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-1))

## Regulatory Updates

**Anthropic opens a Claude text-watermark detection API in private preview.** Updated September 1 on Anthropic's watermark explainer (and covered the same day by The Decoder): regulators, law enforcement, media, fact-checkers, independent researchers, educational organizations, EU civil-society groups, and compliance-obligated enterprises can request access. The mark is a SynthID-Text-style statistical pattern required for models released after August 2, 2026 under the EU AI Act / Transparency Code of Practice; Anthropic says it carries no user identifiers and does not change output quality. Access is planned to widen over time. ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-text-watermark), [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-opens-claude-ai-text-detection-to-regulators-media-fact-checkers-and-others/))
