## Breaking News

**OpenAI says Astra is the first model to cross the "Critical" cybersecurity capability threshold under its Preparedness Framework.** Reported September 1 by CNBC and confirmed on OpenAI's own blog, expert-led red-team assessments found Astra could discover previously unknown vulnerabilities in a hardened browser and operating system and chain them into working exploits without human intervention — including a full browser-sandbox escape that executed commands on the host, and a local-privilege-escalation chain from unprivileged user to root. OpenAI says it delayed parts of Astra's development and release to build and test stronger safeguards, and now judges those safeguards sufficient to release under the framework. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/01/open-ai-astra-cyber-model.html), [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/responding-next-frontier-critical-cyber-capabilities/))

**ChatGPT Ads crosses $1 billion annualized revenue run rate less than 200 days after launch; self-service buying expands to India, Europe, Middle East and North Africa.** Effective September 1, advertisers in those regions can buy ChatGPT ads directly through Ads Manager rather than through agency-led deals, opening the platform to smaller businesses; OpenAI says tens of thousands of advertisers now use the product across more than 40 countries. ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/expanding-access-to-ai-with-chatgpt-ads/), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/31/open-ai-chatgpt-ads-revenue.html))

## Funding & Acquisitions

**AIR Security emerges from stealth with $50M to build a "firewall" for AI agents.** Announced September 1, the six-month-old startup raised across two seed rounds — a $10M round led by Sequoia Capital followed by a $40M round led by Greenoaks — to vet the skills, tools, and add-ons that autonomous AI agents use, targeting enterprise agent-supply-chain risk. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/01/air-raises-50m-to-help-companies-vet-the-skills-and-add-ons-ai-agents-use/), [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/01/air-security-launches-with-50m-to-build-a-firewall-for-ai-agents/))

**Sydney's Gridsight raises $26M Series B for AI-driven energy-grid analytics.** Announced September 1, the round was led by Insight Partners with Galvanize and existing investors AirTree, Energy Transition Ventures, and Aera VC participating. ([Tech Startups](https://techstartups.com/2026/09/01/startup-funding-news-today-september-1-2026-vast-gridsight-airbility-kepler-aerospace-more/))

**China's Tripo AI and VAST each raise roughly RMB 3 billion (~$446M) in Series B/B+ rounds for generative 3D AI.** Announced September 1; VAST's total raised over the past six months reaches approximately RMB 5 billion, with backers including Matrix Partners China, Perfect World, BlueFocus, CICC Capital, and Primavera Capital. ## Industry Moves

**McKinsey's "State of AI 2026" global survey finds large enterprises scaling AI agents jumped from 27% to 40% year-over-year.** Published this week, the survey also found 32% of organizations have skipped buying a software product or feature because they could build it internally with agentic coding tools, and about 20% report AI operating/token costs are now constraining usage; the share reporting AI has moved the EBIT needle held flat at 37%.
