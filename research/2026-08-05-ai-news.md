## Breaking News

### Ninth Circuit Vacates Amazon's Injunction Against Perplexity's Comet Shopping Agent
**Date:** August 4, 2026 | **Sources:** [Bloomberg Law — Perplexity Overturns Amazon Ban on AI Shopping Bot on Appeal](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/us-law-week/perplexity-overturns-amazon-ban-on-ai-shopping-bot-on-appeal), [EFF — Appeals Court Agrees with EFF that Building a Web Browser Doesn't Violate the CFAA](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/08/appeals-court-agrees-eff-building-web-browser-doesnt-violate-cfaa), [Engadget — Perplexity has successfully overturned Amazon's injunction on its AI shopping bot](https://www.engadget.com/2230471/perplexity-has-successfully-overturned-amazon-injunction-on-its-ai-shopping-bot/)
- A Ninth Circuit panel vacated the preliminary injunction (originally granted March 9, 2026 by U.S. District Judge Maxine Chesney) that had blocked Perplexity's Comet browser from operating on logged-in Amazon account pages. In a 21-page opinion, Circuit Judge Milan Smith held that it is users — not Perplexity — who "access" Amazon's servers under the federal Computer Fraud and Abuse Act, applying the rule of lenity to construe the statute's ambiguity against liability. The court explicitly noted there is "little to no existing caselaw" on how to assign CFAA responsibility for AI agents. The case returns to district court; Amazon's trademark and state-law claims survive.
- **Why it matters:** It's the first appellate-level guidance on how the CFAA applies to AI browser agents acting on a user's behalf, directly shaping whether Amazon and other retailers can use anti-hacking law to block agentic shopping tools.

**Date:** August 4, 2026 | **Sources:** [Anthropic — Tino Cuéllar joins Anthropic as Chief Global Affairs Officer](https://www.anthropic.com/news/tino-cuellar), [CNBC — Anthropic names global affairs chief as Trump tensions persist](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/04/anthropic-names-global-affairs-chief-as-trump-tensions-persist.html), [The Harvard Crimson — Harvard Corporation Member Tino Cuéllar Named Anthropic's First Global Affairs Chief](https://www.thecrimson.com/article/2026/8/4/cuellar-anthropic-global-affairs/)

- **Why it matters:** The hire signals Anthropic is building out dedicated policy and diplomacy leadership as it navigates escalating tension with the Trump administration over frontier-model oversight.

## New Model Releases

### Microsoft Research Open-Sources Orchard, an Agent-Training Framework Rivaling Larger Proprietary Systems
**Date:** August 4, 2026 | **Sources:** [Microsoft Research — Orchard: An open framework for scalable agentic AI](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/orchard-an-open-framework-for-scalable-agentic-ai/), [GitHub — microsoft/Orchard](https://github.com/microsoft/Orchard), [AIBusiness — Microsoft Framework to Cut AI Agent Training Costs](https://aibusiness.com/agentic-ai/microsoft-framework-cut-ai-agent-training-costs)
- Microsoft Research released Orchard, an open-source framework that separates agent training from execution via "Orchard Env," a lightweight Kubernetes-based environment service supporting software-engineering, web-navigation, and personal-assistant agent domains without custom infrastructure per task. Its Orchard-SWE recipe reaches 67.5% on SWE-bench Verified after supervised fine-tuning plus reinforcement learning — a reported state of the art among open-source models of comparable size — using roughly 3 billion active parameters, while Orchard-GUI posts 74.1%/67.0%/64.0% success on WebVoyager, Online-Mind2Web, and DeepShop respectively.
- **Why it matters:** It gives outside labs a reusable, much cheaper path to train competitive coding and browser-navigation agents without the bespoke RL infrastructure that previously kept that capability concentrated at a handful of frontier labs.

## Funding & Acquisitions

### Anaconda Acquires AI-Security Startup Enkrypt AI
**Date:** August 4, 2026 | **Sources:** [Anaconda — Anaconda Acquires Enkrypt AI for AI Security](https://www.anaconda.com/blog/anaconda-acquires-enkrypt-ai), [HPCwire/AIwire — Anaconda Acquires Enkrypt AI to Secure the Trillion-Token Enterprise](https://www.hpcwire.com/aiwire/2026/08/04/anaconda-acquires-enkrypt-ai-to-secure-the-trillion-token-enterprise/)
- Anaconda acquired Enkrypt AI, folding its pre-deployment red-teaming (covering 300+ attack categories), runtime guardrails, and NIST/EU AI Act compliance automation into the Anaconda Platform, which now spans governance from a developer's first prompt through a deployed AI-native application, including MCP servers. Anaconda cited Enkrypt's own scan data from the two months before the deal closed: more than 268,000 tools scanned across 25,000 MCP servers, turning up over 143,000 vulnerabilities affecting 73% of those servers. Deal terms were not disclosed.
- **Why it matters:** It's a concrete data point — 73% of scanned MCP servers carrying vulnerabilities — on how exposed the rapidly growing agent-tooling ecosystem is, and shows established data-science platform vendors moving to absorb AI-security compliance rather than build it in-house.
