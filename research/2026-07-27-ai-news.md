## Breaking News

### Nvidia in Talks to Backstop $250 Billion of OpenAI's Ohio Data Center Lease, Plus Up to $350 Billion in Chip Financing

**Date:** Reported July 26, 2026 | **Sources:** [Bloomberg — Nvidia in Talks to Back OpenAI Lease of $500 Billion Data Center](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-26/nvidia-in-talks-on-250-billion-backing-for-openai-hub-wsj-says), [Business Recorder — Nvidia in talks with OpenAI to guarantee $250 billion financing for data center, WSJ reports](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40431959/nvidia-in-talks-with-openai-to-guarantee-250-billion-financing-for-data-center-wsj-reports), [Benzinga — Nvidia Reportedly Moves to Backstop $250 Billion in OpenAI Data Center Financing](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/07/60686864/nvidia-openai-data-center-financing-michael-burry)
- The Wall Street Journal reported Nvidia is negotiating to guarantee roughly $250 billion in lease/debt financing so OpenAI can take control of a 10-gigawatt, ~$500 billion data-center hub in Piketon, Ohio being developed by SoftBank's SB Energy — a step toward OpenAI owning infrastructure rather than renting compute from Microsoft, Amazon, and Oracle. Nvidia is separately discussing financing up to $350 billion of the chip purchases for the same site; talks are early-stage and could still collapse, and neither company has confirmed the terms publicly.
- **Why it matters:** It would make Nvidia both OpenAI's chip supplier and its infrastructure financier, deepening the circular-financing structure already drawing scrutiny (per Michael Burry and others) across the industry's compute buildout, on the heels of South Korea's $950B chip-supply deals reported the day before.

## New Model Releases

### Moonshot AI Publishes Kimi K3's Full Open Weights on Hugging Face — Confirmed Live at 00:00 UTC

**Date:** July 27, 2026, 00:00 UTC | **Sources:** [Hugging Face — moonshotai/Kimi-K3](https://huggingface.co/moonshotai/Kimi-K3), [Hugging Face Blog — Kimi K3 Model Overview: 2.8T Parameters, MXFP4 Quantization](https://huggingface.co/blog/ResterChed/kimi-k3-model-overview-mxfp4-quantization-open-wei)
- Following the "scheduled for July 27" reporting covered in [2026-07-26](2026-07-26-ai-news.md), Moonshot AI's 2.8-trillion-parameter Kimi K3 weights are now confirmed live on Hugging Face under a Modified MIT license — an MXFP4-quantized download of roughly 594GB-1.4TB depending on packaging, making it the first open model in the 3-trillion-parameter class. The mixture-of-experts architecture (built on "Kimi Delta Attention" and "Attention Residuals") activates a small fraction of experts per token and targets long-horizon coding, knowledge work, and native agentic use.
- **Why it matters:** This moves the model from an announced release to an actually-downloadable artifact, letting enterprises with data-sovereignty requirements self-host a frontier-class open model for the first time at this scale — the concrete event previous reports could only describe as upcoming.
