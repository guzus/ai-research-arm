## Breaking News

- **Nvidia reports Q2 FY2027 earnings today after market close** — Nvidia is scheduled to report fiscal second-quarter results (period ended July 26, 2026) after the U.S. market close on August 26, widely viewed as the single biggest catalyst for AI/semiconductor stocks this quarter. Bloomberg consensus calls for adjusted EPS of $2.09 on revenue of $92.2 billion (+97% year-over-year), with Data Center revenue expected to top $85.4 billion; Nvidia itself guided to roughly $91.0 billion ±2%. Results were not yet public at the time of this report — 58 of 61 tracked analysts rate the stock Buy/Strong Buy heading in, though NVDA has fallen after six of its last eight earnings reports, including the last four in a row, making post-earnings guidance the more closely watched number than the headline beat. ([The Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/08/25/nvidia-could-grow-revenue-97-and-still-disappoint/), [Kiplinger](https://www.kiplinger.com/investing/live/nvidia-earnings-live-updates-and-commentary-august-2026))

GAAP/non-GAAP EPS came in at $2.46/$2.22, beating the $2.09 estimate by roughly 6%. Gross margin held at 75.0% (GAAP and non-GAAP), though Nvidia guided that margin will decline and bottom out at 71–72% in Q4 FY2027, partly on rising memory costs. Guidance for the current quarter is ~$91 billion ±2%, and CEO Jensen Huang forecast roughly 70% revenue growth for fiscal 2028 — well above prior Street estimates — citing accelerating AI demand. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/26/nvidia-nvda-earnings-report-q2-2027-live-updates.html), [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/26/nvidia-results-q2-earnings/), [NVIDIA Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/aws-and-nvidia-to-deliver-2-million-additional-gpus-and-next-generation-infrastructure-for-agentic-and-physical-ai))
- **OpenAI's "Jalapeño" custom inference chip beats Nvidia Blackwell in first published benchmarks.** Built with Broadcom, OpenAI presented Jalapeño results at the Hot Chips conference on August 25. Measured on SemiAnalysis's public InferenceX benchmark against an Nvidia Blackwell system, Jalapeño delivered 1.5–1.9x more work per watt and cut end-to-end latency by 1.7–3.6x; on chatty, low-latency ChatGPT-style traffic specifically, it ran 2.1–4.1x faster. The chip is targeting only low-volume production in late 2026 and has not yet been benchmarked against Nvidia's upcoming Vera Rubin generation; OpenAI says it will keep buying from Nvidia regardless. Analysts told CNBC this adds a new margin threat to Nvidia's inference business even as its data-center GPU demand remains enormous (see Nvidia earnings update above). ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/26/openai-jalapeno-ai-chip-nvidia.html), [SemiAnalysis](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/openai-jalapeno-better-than-nvidia), [24/7 Wall St.](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/08/26/openais-custom-chip-embarrasses-nvidia-while-company-vows-to-keep-buying-from-it/))
- **Meta agrees to pay up to $16.68 billion to settle a 29-state teen-safety lawsuit.** Disclosed in an August 26 court filing amid a federal trial in Oakland (which included testimony from Instagram chief Adam Mosseri), the settlement resolves claims that Meta engineered Facebook and Instagram to be addictive to children, misrepresented platform safety, and unlawfully collected minors' data. Meta must roll out a default overnight lockout (midnight–6am local time) and a default two-hour daily cumulative use cap for teen accounts (messaging and long-form video excluded), plus strengthened age verification and expanded parental controls; the lockout and cap tighten further (10pm–7am, 60 minutes/app) if rival platforms like TikTok, YouTube, or Snapchat adopt matching commitments. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/meta-settles-us-states-over-130318028.html), [France 24](https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20260826-meta-us-states-agree-16-7-bn-settlement-in-landmark-teen-safety-case), [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/social-media/meta-settles-social-media-addiction-suit-16-billion-rcna594492))
- **OpenAI expands ChatGPT Ads to 31 European markets** — OpenAI began rolling out ChatGPT Ads to 31 additional European countries on August 24–25, including Germany, France, Spain, Italy, Sweden, Norway, Denmark, the Netherlands, and Austria — six months after starting U.S. tests. This is OpenAI's largest single ad-market expansion to date, bringing total ad availability to 35 countries. Ads will show only to Free and Go tier users; Plus, Pro, and Enterprise remain ad-free. Advertiser access starts through OpenAI's Ads Solutions team and agency/technology partners, with self-service Ads Manager access following later this summer. ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-ads-expands-across-europe/), [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/417446/openai-expands-advertising-pilot-in-31-european-ma.html), [Neowin](https://www.neowin.net/news/openai-expands-chatgpt-ads-to-31-european-markets/))

## Research Papers

- **Prime Intellect releases "Prime Agent," an open-source self-improving RLM harness** — Posted to arXiv August 25 (arXiv:2608.23552) by Seth Karten, Alex L. Zhang, and collaborators at Prime Intellect, "Prime Agent" is an open-source harness for long-horizon coding and research agents built on a persistent IPython REPL and the "Recursive Language Model" abstraction, paired with a "Continual Harness" that preserves memories, skills, and subagent specs across trajectories. The paper reports raising ARC-AGI-3 RHAE Best@1 from 30% to 95.5%, and matching or exceeding existing harnesses on long-context coding, GPU-kernel generation, emulator construction, and autonomous nanoGPT speedrun tasks. Code is public on GitHub. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23552), [Prime Intellect](https://www.primeintellect.ai/blog/prime-agent), [GitHub](https://github.com/PrimeIntellect-ai/prime-agent))

Available in preview, it ships with legal-specific pre-built agents and connectors into firms' existing document-management, e-discovery, and legal-research systems for contract review, diligence, regulatory monitoring, and privacy-request workflows, inheriting firms' ethical walls automatically and keeping client data inside the firm's private perimeter. A parallel financial-services edition launched alongside it, with healthcare/life-sciences versions described as upcoming. ([Google Cloud](https://www.googlecloudpresscorner.com/2026-08-25-Google-Cloud-Launches-Gemini-Enterprise-for-Legal), [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/google-takes-gemini-enterprise-into-big-law-with-legal-specific-agents/))
- **Amazon is shutting down Mechanical Turk after 21 years.** Amazon confirmed it will close AWS Mechanical Turk — the crowdsourced "human intelligence task" marketplace Jeff Bezos once called "artificial artificial intelligence" — effective September 30, 2026; new customer signups already stopped a month earlier. Launched in 2005 to connect businesses with people doing small tasks computers couldn't handle (labeling, transcription, surveys), the platform is being wound down as AI capabilities have advanced and competing data-labeling platforms have expanded; a 2023 study had already found up to 46% of MTurk workers were using AI tools to complete the very tasks meant to train it. ([TechStartups](https://techstartups.com/2026/08/26/amazon-is-shutting-down-mechanical-turk-after-21-years-as-ai-reshapes-crowdsourced-work/), [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/amazon/2026/amazon-sunsets-crowd-sourced-work-platform-mturk/))
