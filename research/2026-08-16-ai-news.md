### Oracle Prepares Fresh Round of Layoffs to Fund AI Data Center Buildout
**Date:** August 12-14, 2026 | **Sources:** [Yahoo Finance — Oracle planning new round of layoffs in August 2026](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/oracle-planning-round-layoffs-august-134527039.html), [Quartz — Oracle planning new round of layoffs in August 2026](https://qz.com/oracle-layoffs-august-ai-debt-081226), [Telecom Reseller — Oracle Reportedly Prepares More Layoffs as AI Spending Surges](https://telecomreseller.com/2026/08/14/oracle-reported-layoffs/)
- Oracle has reportedly asked managers to identify employees for cuts that could reach double-digit percentages on some teams, aiming to reduce payroll before its second fiscal quarter starts September 1. The move follows a fiscal-2026 capital expenditure bill of $55.7 billion (versus $21.2 billion the prior year), a $23.7 billion cash shortfall, and $43 billion tapped from debt markets plus $5 billion from stock sales to fund AI infrastructure; Oracle's headcount already shrank 13% (21,000 people) over the fiscal year ended May 31.
- **Why it matters:** it's one of the starkest examples yet of a company financing an AI infrastructure buildout by cutting its own workforce, and ties into the broader 2026 pattern of AI-cited layoffs (over 205,000 workers year-to-date per tracking firms).
