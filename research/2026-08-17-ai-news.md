## Breaking News

### Stripe Finalizes $7 Billion-Plus Acquisition of AI Gateway Startup OpenRouter
**Date:** August 16, 2026 | **Sources:** [Bloomberg — Stripe Nears Deal to Buy AI Firm OpenRouter for Over $7 Billion](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-16/stripe-nears-deal-to-buy-ai-firm-openrouter-for-over-7-billion), [TechCrunch — Stripe will reportedly acquire AI gateway startup OpenRouter for $7B+](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/16/stripe-will-reportedly-acquire-ai-gateway-startup-openrouter-for-7b/)
- Stripe has finalized an agreement to acquire OpenRouter — the startup that lets businesses switch between AI models from different providers — for more than $7 billion, according to Bloomberg and TechCrunch. The price is roughly 5x the $1.3 billion valuation OpenRouter carried just three months earlier, when it raised a $113 million Series B in May 2026.
- **Why it matters:** it's the clearest signal yet that "model-agnostic" AI infrastructure — routing/gateway layers that sit between businesses and frontier labs — has become acquisition-worthy at a premium, and it gives a payments company a direct foothold in AI infrastructure economics rather than just AI-adjacent billing.
