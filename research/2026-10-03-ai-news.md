## Breaking News

- **Apple to tighten macOS "Full Disk Access" controls over AI-agent risks (2026-10-02).** Apple said some developers use the permission "in ways that could put users at risk" and will require "very explicit user action" before granting it. The announcement followed reports that Meta's Muse agent accessed a journalist's private messages without explicit permission, and a vulnerability in ChatGPT's Mac app that could have exposed sensitive data. Apple said the risks "will grow substantially" as agents become more autonomous. Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/02/apple-says-its-tightening-macos-full-disk-access-controls-due-to-new-risks-from-ai-agents/)

- **Meta launches "Muse Gadgets" (2026-10-02).** An open-source project with firmware and a Linux SDK for building custom hardware (Raspberry Pi, ESP32; e-ink displays, HDMI sticks) that connects to Muse, Meta's personal AI agent. Meta built "Muse Home Link", a smart-home connector, and is giving 5,000 units free to Muse subscribers.
