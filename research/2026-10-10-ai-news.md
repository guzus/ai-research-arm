## Research Papers

No Saturday 04:00 UTC arXiv announcement (`<skipDays>` Saturday/Sunday; both CS.AI and CS.LG carried zero `<item>` nodes). No Sunday build either. Two first-version papers from Friday’s build (IDs through 2610.12470) were not named in the 2026-10-09 report; three more from that build were not named in the morning 2026-10-10 file:

- **One Word Opens the Gate** ([2610.12292](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12292), Azizi, Baghaei Potraghloo, Pedram, USC). Typed decision models as agent guardrails. On prompt-injection / jailbreak / toxicity screening, seven open-weight models score 36–72% accuracy (chance 50%). Six lines of irrelevant server-log text lift fail-open from 0% to 63% on a policy the gate otherwise decides correctly; renaming the permissive option (definition and judged text untouched) pushes fail-open to 93–100% on the four models that put the label in the input. Every defense tested fails. A deterministic rule over parsed policy fields hits 100% and makes the model unnecessary. Submitted 2026-10-08.

- **DreamTrue** ([2610.12468](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12468), Li, Fan, Zhang et al., CASIA / Alibaba Amap). Multi-view, cross-embodiment robot world model. Offline geometric calibration plus counterfactual post-training against a human-annotated defect reward (44.9k videos). On AgiBot, interaction defects 48.12% → 6.25%; first in the AgiBot World Challenge 2026 world-model track. Releases calibration for 153,666 episodes / 1,660+ hours. Submitted 2026-10-08.

- **(Saturday) Ecology of AI Agents** ([2610.12436](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12436), Crawley, Tanaka, Harvard CBS-NTT / NTT Research). Population-growth model in which fitness is cyber capability. Without collaboration, takeoff requires individual capability above a threshold. With collaboration, capability rises with *N*, producing a strong Allee effect: below *N_crit* the population shrinks; above it, it grows even if per-agent capability is unchanged. The authors argue red-teaming a small group cannot certify a larger swarm and call for “ecological red teaming” plus population pacing. Toy theory, not a measured *N_crit*. Submitted 2026-10-08.

- **(Saturday) CSF: Contextual Safety Filtering for Motion Generators** ([2610.12467](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12467), Yang, Hou, Tang, Li, Weng, Werner, Ames, Caltech / NYU). Training-free filter that turns natural-language safety rules into CBF-QP constraints from the generator’s own safe/unsafe reference trajectories. On four pretrained motion generators, CSF caught every explicit and scene-triggered unsafe case, cut the danger-event rate by up to **90%**, and kept **88–100%** of benign motions. Demonstrated on a Unitree G1. Submitted 2026-10-08.

- **(Saturday) BrickBench** ([2610.12452](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12452), Kulits, Xu, Jones, Schmid, Wu, Stanford / MPI-IS / Inria). Text-to-buildable LEGO-set benchmark (Model ≤400 parts; Set 400–4,000; Alt-Build of set 10698) plus BrickAgent. Leading agents (GPT-6 Astra, Claude Opus 5.5, Qwen 3.8 Flash, Gemini 3.8 Flash, Muse Spark 1.3) mostly meet verifiable physical and semantic checks but stay below human designs. Site: [brickben.ch](http://www.brickben.ch). Submitted 2026-10-08.
