## Breaking News

### Anthropic in Talks to Buy Israeli AI Startup Decart for $6 Billion
**Date:** August 13, 2026 | **Sources:** [Bloomberg — Anthropic Said in Talks to Buy AI Startup Decart for $6 Billion](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/anthropic-said-in-talks-to-buy-ai-startup-decart-for-6-billion), [Fortune — Anthropic said in talks to buy startup Decart for $6 billion](https://fortune.com/2026/08/13/anthropic-said-in-talks-to-buy-startup-decart-for-6-billion/)
- Anthropic is negotiating to acquire Decart AI, an Israeli startup founded in 2023 by Dean and Orian Leitersdorf and Moshe Shalev that builds software to make AI chip training and inference more efficient, alongside consumer-facing generative-video and world-model products. The deal, still unfinalized, would be Anthropic's largest known acquisition; Decart was valued at $4 billion in a May round backed by Nvidia and Toyota, so $6 billion would mark a roughly 50% valuation jump in under three months.
- **Why it matters:** it signals Anthropic using M&A to shore up compute efficiency ahead of surging Claude demand and a hotly anticipated IPO, rather than relying solely on external chip-efficiency vendors.

## Industry Moves

### Anthropic Makes Claude Code's Auto Mode the Default for Paid Users Today
**Date:** August 14, 2026 (rollout date) | **Sources:** [TechCrunch — Anthropic is turning Claude Code's auto mode on by default](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/09/anthropic-is-turning-claude-codes-auto-mode-on-by-default/), [InfoWorld — Anthropic makes Claude Code's auto mode default for paid users](https://www.infoworld.com/article/4207959/anthropic-makes-claude-codes-auto-mode-default-for-paid-users.html)
- August 14 is the date Anthropic set for auto mode to become the default permission setting for new Claude Code sessions on Pro, Max, and Team plans: instead of prompting for approval at each step, Claude Code now proceeds automatically unless it judges an action "irreversible, destructive, or aimed outside your environment." Anthropic cites a controlled study finding humans catch planted dangerous commands only 13.6% of the time versus 89% for auto mode's own checks. It remains optional (not defaulted) on Enterprise, the API, Bedrock, Google Cloud's Agent Platform, and Microsoft Foundry.
- **Why it matters:** it's a default-behavior change landing today for a widely used coding-agent product, shifting the baseline trust model from human-in-the-loop approval to automated risk judgment for a large existing user base.

## Regulatory Updates

### California Legislature Holds Make-or-Break Votes on ~30 AI Bills
**Date:** August 13, 2026 | **Sources:** [Tech Times — California AI Bills Face Final Vote Today: Chatbot Safety, Copyright, US-First Commission](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/324338/20260813/california-ai-bills-face-final-vote-today-chatbot-safety-copyright-us-first-commission.htm)
- Roughly 30 AI-related bills went through simultaneous Assembly and Senate suspense-committee hearings on August 13, deciding which measures survive to floor votes before the legislature adjourns August 31. The package spans chatbot safety for minors, AI-copyright transparency for creative works, algorithmic management protections for workers, healthcare AI transparency, and a proposed first-in-nation AI safety certification commission. Bills that failed to clear suspense die without a recorded vote.
- **Why it matters:** California continues to act as the de facto lead U.S. AI regulator in the absence of comprehensive federal law, and this single hearing day will determine which child-safety, copyright, and labor AI protections move toward enactment this year.
