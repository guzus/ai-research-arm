## Breaking News

### OpenAI Cuts GPT-5.6 Sol API Pricing by More Than 20%
**Date:** August 21, 2026 | **Sources:** [OpenAI (X/Twitter) — official announcement](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2090885187634905500), [Business Standard — OpenAI cuts developer pricing for GPT-5.6 Sol model by more than 20%](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/openai-cuts-developer-pricing-for-gpt-5-6-sol-model-by-more-than-20-126082200107_1.html), [Finimize — OpenAI Cut GPT-5.6 Sol API Prices To Keep Developers Close](https://finimize.com/content/openai-cut-gpt-56-sol-api-prices-to-keep-developers-close)
- OpenAI announced it is cutting API and credit pricing for its frontier model GPT-5.6 Sol by over 20% for the next three months: input tokens drop from $5 to $4 per million tokens and output tokens from $30 to $20 per million in standard short-context use. The cut also applies to eligible credit-based usage on ChatGPT Work and Codex, runs at least through November 21, 2026, and leaves Pro/Plus/Business subscription prices unchanged. It is OpenAI's second pricing cut in under a month, following late-July reductions on its Terra and Luna tiers.
- **Why it matters:** it's a concrete, dated escalation of API price competition aimed squarely at Anthropic and Chinese model providers, arriving as OpenAI works to keep developers on its platform ahead of its planned IPO.

### Nevada Approves Up to 8,000 Robotaxis for Tesla, Uber and Waymo in Las Vegas
**Date:** August 20-21, 2026 | **Sources:** [Dataconomy — Nevada Approves Permits For Tesla, Uber, And Waymo's Robotaxi Services](https://dataconomy.com/2026/08/21/nevada-approves-permits-for-tesla-uber-and-waymos-robotaxi/), [Tesla Oracle — Tesla (TSLA) obtains approval for Robotaxi Service in Nevada (including Las Vegas)](https://www.teslaoracle.com/2026/08/21/tesla-tsla-obtains-approval-for-robotaxi-service-in-nevada-including-las-vegas/)
- The Nevada Transportation Authority unanimously replaced its interim order — which had capped Tesla at just 10 vehicles on a narrow Las Vegas Strip corridor — with a full permit structure allowing up to 8,000 combined robotaxis in Clark County over the next year: 5,000 for Tesla, 1,000 each for Waymo and Uber.
- **Why it matters:** it's a concrete, dated regulatory green light that moves autonomous-vehicle AI from a tightly geofenced pilot to city-scale deployment in one of the largest US robotaxi markets, with three separate operators authorized to scale simultaneously.

## Research Papers

### Anthropic's Claude Autonomously Designs Protein Binders, Wet-Lab Validated at More Than Double Industry Hit Rate
**Date:** August 20, 2026 | **Sources:** [Anthropic — How Claude is accelerating protein design and analytical chemistry](https://www.anthropic.com/research/Claude-accelerates-protein-design), [Dataconomy — Anthropic Says Claude Designed Protein Binders For 14 Of 15 Targets](https://dataconomy.com/2026/08/20/claude-ai-protein-binders-14-of-15-targets/), [Adaptyv Bio — Case study: Benchmarking Claude's protein designs in the wet lab](https://www.adaptyvbio.com/blog/anthropic-1)
- Anthropic had Claude models (including a "Mythos Preview" variant and Opus 4.8) run autonomous protein-binder design campaigns against 15 clinically relevant targets — including PD-L1, TREM2, TNFα, and EGFR — with independent wet-lab synthesis and testing by Adaptyv Bio and Twist Bioscience. Claude produced confirmed binders for 14 of the 15 targets, with 354 confirmed binders from 1,320 designs; overall hit rates of 22.6-26.7% across models, more than double the reported 10-15% industry baseline, rising to 35.1% when Mythos Preview focused on single targets in isolation. It failed completely against maltose-binding protein (0 of 90 designs) and scored modestly on a synthetic hard-benchmark protein.
- **Why it matters:** it's a concrete, independently wet-lab-verified data point on frontier LLMs doing autonomous scientific design work with real, physically-confirmed success rates rather than benchmark-only claims — while the two clean failure cases show the capability is target-dependent, not universal.

## Industry Moves

### Perplexity's "Computer" Agent Now Runs Tasks Directly From Email
**Date:** August 21, 2026 | **Sources:** [AlternativeTo — Perplexity launches Computer in Email to give its AI agent instructions from your inbox](https://alternativeto.net/news/2026/8/perplexity-launches-computer-in-email-to-give-its-ai-agent-instructions-from-your-inbox/), [Perplexity Help Center — Computer for Email](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/20260715-computer-for-email)
- Perplexity extended its "Computer" agent platform so users can send, forward, or cc `computer@perplexity.com` to launch a full agentic task without leaving their inbox; Computer reads the thread and any attachments, then returns deliverables (spreadsheets, PDFs, presentations) in the same email thread. Each request creates a full session reviewable on web or mobile with the same audit trail as in-app tasks, and draws on Perplexity's persistent "Brain" memory/context layer.
- **Why it matters:** it's a concrete, dated move to make agentic AI accessible through the lowest-friction enterprise interface — email — rather than requiring a dedicated app or browser session.

### Apple Music to Introduce Mandatory "Made With AI" Labels
**Date:** August 20-21, 2026 | **Sources:** [MacRumors — Apple Music to Label AI-Generated Songs](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/20/apple-music-to-label-ai-generated-songs/), [9to5Mac — Apple Music will soon get visible labels for AI-generated content](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/20/apple-music-will-soon-get-visible-labels-for-ai-generated-content/), [MacDailyNews — Apple Music to introduce 'Made With AI' labels for AI-generated music later this year](https://macdailynews.com/2026/08/21/apple-music-to-introduce-made-with-ai-labels-for-ai-generated-music-later-this-year/)
- Apple Music confirmed it will roll out mandatory "Made With AI" labels later this year, requiring record labels and distributors to tag any track where AI materially generated a portion of the content, distinct from AI-assisted production tools like mastering or EQ. Apple Music VP Oliver Schusser said more than a third of tracks currently uploaded to the platform are entirely AI-generated, with AI involved in roughly 40% of July releases.
- **Why it matters:** it's a concrete, dated move by one of the largest streaming platforms to make AI-generated content disclosure mandatory rather than optional, as competing platforms like Deezer report AI tracks approaching half of daily uploads.
