### SpaceX Commits Exclusively to Nvidia, Previews Orbital AI Compute
**Date:** August 5, 2026 | **Sources:** [Tom's Hardware — Elon Musk says SpaceX will exclusively use Nvidia GPUs](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/elon-musk-says-spacex-will-exclusively-use-nvidia-gpus-because-they-are-the-best-says-optimized-vera-rubin-nvl72-will-be-launched-into-space-next-year), [HotHardware — NVIDIA Wins Exclusive SpaceX Pact For Space-Based AI Servers](https://hothardware.com/news/nvidia-wins-exclusive-spacex-pact-for-space-based-ai-servers), [Benzinga — Musk Praises Vera Rubin Platform on SpaceX Earnings Call, Nvidia Stock Climbs](https://www.benzinga.com/trading-ideas/movers/26/08/60935776/musk-praises-vera-rubin-platform-on-spacex-earnings-call-nvidia-stock-climbs)
- On SpaceX's earnings call, Elon Musk said the company has decided to "build exclusively on Nvidia, because we think the Vera Rubin architecture is the best architecture," committing to Nvidia's rack-scale Vera Rubin NVL72 platform (codenamed Kyber) for SpaceX's AI data centers. SpaceX is targeting 2 gigawatts of compute capacity by the end of 2026, scaling to 10 gigawatts by late 2027, and Musk said an optimized version of the NVL72 system, branded Starmind, is planned to launch into orbit next year as the first step toward space-based AI compute powered by onboard solar arrays. Nvidia shares rose more than 4% on the announcement.
- **Why it matters:** It's a high-profile exclusivity commitment from one of the world's most-watched infrastructure builders, reinforcing Nvidia's dominance in AI silicon just as rivals push custom-chip alternatives, and it puts a concrete timeline on Musk's long-teased orbital AI compute concept.

### Meta Enters the AI Coding Agent Race with Muse Code and Muse Spark 1.2
**Date:** August 5, 2026 | **Sources:** [VentureBeat — Meta enters the AI coding wars with Muse Spark 1.2 and Muse Code](https://venturebeat.com/orchestration/meta-enters-the-ai-coding-wars-with-muse-spark-1-2-and-muse-code-with-persistent-async-background-agents), [9to5Mac — Meta launches Muse Code AI coding agent for macOS and Linux](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/05/meta-launches-muse-code-ai-coding-agent-for-macos-and-linux/), [MarkTechPost — Meta AI Releases Muse Code (Beta)](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/08/05/meta-superintelligence-labs-releases-muse-code/)
- Meta released Muse Code, a terminal-only AI coding agent now in public beta for macOS and Linux, powered by a new coding-focused model, Muse Spark 1.2. Muse Code runs persistent background agents that retain context across a session rather than restarting per task, and fans large jobs out to sub-agents working in parallel across isolated git worktrees. Pricing on the pay-as-you-go API matches Muse Spark 1.1 ($1.25/M input, $4.25/M output tokens), while a contributor tier — where developers opt to help improve the model — costs over 10x less.
- **Why it matters:** It puts Meta in direct competition with Anthropic's Claude Code and OpenAI's Codex in the agentic coding-tool category for the first time, signaling that terminal-based coding agents have become a battleground all three major U.S. labs now contest.

## New Model Releases

### Hark Launches Handoff, a Low-Cost Browser-Use Agent
**Date:** August 5, 2026 | **Sources:** [VentureBeat — AI startup Hark unveils first product: Hark Handoff](https://venturebeat.com/technology/ai-startup-hark-unveils-first-product-an-affordable-fast-computer-use-agent-hark-handoff), [TechCrunch — Hark previews its browser use agent for completing tasks](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/05/hark-previews-its-browser-use-agent-for-completing-tasks/), [Business Wire — Hark Announces Handoff](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260805041028/en/Hark-Announces-Handoff-the-Worlds-Best-Web-Browsing-AI-Agent)
- Hark, the robotics-and-AI startup founded by Brett Adcock that raised a $700M Series A in May, unveiled Handoff, a browser-use agent that navigates ordinary websites without official APIs — ordering food on DoorDash, booking flights on United and Delta, messaging candidates on LinkedIn — by interpreting page structure and visual cues. Hark says Handoff posted the top-ever score on the OM2W web-navigation benchmark at under one-tenth the token cost of rivals, though its published comparisons ran against prior-generation models (GPT-5.5, GPT-5.4, Opus 4.8, Gemini 2.5 Pro) rather than the current-generation GPT-5.6 or Claude Opus 5. Public sign-ups opened at hark.com, with wider availability planned later this month.
- **Why it matters:** It's another well-funded entrant racing to own the "computer-use agent" category that OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google are also building toward, though the benchmark gap against current frontier models is a real caveat on the "world's best" claim.
