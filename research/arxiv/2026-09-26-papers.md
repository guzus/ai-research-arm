## Highlights (Top 5 Must-Read)

### 1. WanPE: Towards Cinematic Prompt Enhancement for Modern Text-to-Video Generation
**Authors**: Yubo Zhu, Yawen Shao, Yu Liu, Wei Tong et al. (Wan / Tongyi video)
**arXiv**: [2609.30221](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30221)
**TL;DR**: Video models now follow 30-second multi-shot scripts; the prompt is the director. WanPE is a 397B enhancer trained on 1.05M real videos. Shot plans are reverse-constructed from video, then Semantic-Consistency GRPO keeps user intent across shots. WanPEval is a 5–30s human testbed (~11k blind pairs). On Wan3.0, WanPE lifts preference +10.7–18.8 points at 5–15s and +50.9 at 30s — ahead of commercial enhancers at short horizon, competitive with Seedance 2.5 at long.
**Why it matters**: The bottleneck for long T2V is no longer the denoiser — it is a cinematic planner the size of a frontier LLM.

### 2. Canopy: Exploiting Piecewise Smooth Tree Priors for Multi-Fidelity Bandits
**Authors**: Michael Jerge, Suman Jana
**arXiv**: [2609.30017](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30017)
**TL;DR**: Routing, prefix-cache, prompt trim, and test-time search are all trees: cheap biased internal nodes, expensive accurate leaves. Hierarchical bandits need a smoothness schedule you do not have. CANOPY learns where the prior is valid — random-path probes certify local aggregation bias, then spend leaf evals on cells that violate smoothness. Extra cost is additive in the number of discontinuities. Same budget: 2.9× top-10 recall on a 1,000-model pool, 1.6× more SWE-bench Verified than best-of-N, 3.6× lower median TTFT with prefix cache.
**Why it matters**: Inference search is a piecewise-smooth tree, not a flat bandit — and the certificate tells you where the prior lies.

### 3. World Action Agent: Harnessing VLMs for Robot Manipulation via World Action Rehearsal
**Authors**: Yehang Zhang, Haojian Huang, Ying-Cong Chen, Zexi Li et al.
**arXiv**: [2609.29964](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29964)
**TL;DR**: General VLMs usually write programs or predict constraints. WAA puts them in a visual action workspace: auto-selected contact views, editable action rehearsal (preview/revise before execute), and in-view correction of residual offsets. Skills evolve from expert video and human teaching; traces fine-tune smaller pilots. With skills from LIBERO-90 only: 75.6% on LIBERO-Pro, SOTA vs VLAs, code-as-policy, and a same-backbone visual harness. Same skills transfer to robosuite. Qwen3.5-9B OOD success 1.7% → 43.3% on harness traces.
**Why it matters**: Give the VLM a world to rehearse in, not a camera feed to narrate.

### 4. Minimally Invasive Steering of Language Models
**Authors**: Taha Entesari, Jingyu Zhang, Daniel Khashabi, Mahyar Fazlyab
**arXiv**: [2609.30218](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30218)
**TL;DR**: Pre-logit steering adds vectors to a frozen model's last hidden states. Unregularized reward optimization wrecks the output distribution. MISVO penalizes the intervention with the local KL / Fisher geometry of the token distribution; the gradient is analytic through the frozen LM head. Sequence-level KL splits into a Fisher term plus a suffix score-function term that is second-order in steering size. No weight updates. On ~1B–14B preference and code tasks, highest mean reward in 6/7 settings, diversity/coherence near Best-of-N.
**Why it matters**: Test-time steering that is actually small in distribution space, not just small in vector norm.

### 5. Synthetic Hospital: An Open, Verifiable, Physician-Validated Longitudinal EHR Benchmark
**Authors**: Christine Park, Valerie Chen, Tim Dettmers
**arXiv**: [2609.30027](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30027)
**TL;DR**: Real EHRs cannot be shared and have no verifiable ground truth. Synthetic Hospital is fully synthetic, fact-grounded, no PHI: 1,268 longitudinal patients, 5,602 encounters, every diagnosis/finding/temporal relation in ICD-10-CM / SNOMED / LOINC with a provenance chain to public medical-education sources. Served through a fake hospital stack (interop APIs, RBAC, function calling). Blinded physicians vs real charts: 53% (near chance). Best of 10 frontier/open models: severity-weighted problem-list F1 0.73 (matches mean of 7 MDs, well below the best at 0.89) and misses ~half of clinically relevant findings in a chart summary.
**Why it matters**: An open clinical bench that is hard enough that frontier models still lose to the best physician — and that anyone can run.
