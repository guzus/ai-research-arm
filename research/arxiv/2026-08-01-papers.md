## Highlights (Top 5 Must-Read)

### 1. Tycho: Active Abstraction with Programmatic World Models for ARC-AGI-3
**Authors**: Jens Lehmann, Andrei Aioanei, Sahar Vahdati
**arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28287
**TL;DR**: Formalizes ARC-AGI-3 games as parameterized rendered deterministic Moore machines and builds a coding-agent system that constructs, tests, repairs, or bypasses a free-form executable world model during play. Under matched inference budgets, the best orchestration policy (actor-requested delegation to a model builder) scores 88.49 mean Relative Human Action Efficiency with Claude Opus 4.8; with that policy, GPT-5.6 Sol and Opus 5 both hit **100.00 RHAE and complete all 183 levels**, with Opus 5 using 61% fewer scored actions than the aggregate official human baselines.
**Why it matters**: A frontier interactive-abstraction benchmark going from open problem to fully completed, with the paper's own ablation showing that simulator accuracy alone (83.07 RHAE via automatic repair) is not what wins — deciding *when* to build or skip a model is.

### 2. Echoverse: Deep, Evolving Environments for Training Computer-Use Agents at Scale
**Authors**: Yash Pandya, Sahil Gupta, Sarthak Harne, Archana Yadav, Hussein Mozannar, Ahmed Awadallah, Ece Kamar, Akshay Nambi, et al. (13 authors)
**arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28074
**TL;DR**: Argues the bottleneck for computer-use agent training has moved from *how many* synthetic environments exist to *what is inside each one*, and compiles specifications into stateful applications graded against their own database, with a co-evolution loop that reads each graded rollout twice — as environment/verifier repair and as model training signal. Trained on twelve environments, a 9B model goes from 36.5% to 67.1% across fourteen splits; shallow environments actually *degrade* live-site accuracy (80.0 → 75.0) where deep ones lift it (80.0 → 85.0), and repairing one environment moves its model from 16.2% to 38.5%.
**Why it matters**: Isolates environment depth as the causal variable in agent training and shows bulk environment generation can be net-negative — a direct rebuttal to scale-the-environment-count pipelines.

### 3. Understanding Is Done Early: A Depth Division of Labor in LLMs and Its Use for Unbounded-Context Memory
**Authors**: Hanzuo Liu, Xuan Qi, Chunyu Liu, Haotian Zhong, Yulong Wang, Alex Lamb, Mingyu Gao, et al.
**arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28263
**TL;DR**: Exploits the observation that lower/middle transformer layers build semantic representations while upper layers specialize them for prediction: CoMem writes each context chunk through an intermediate layer only, retrieves a fixed number of cached residual states, and recomputes query-conditioned upper layers over the pack — making read compute and memory independent of stored-context length. On a frozen Qwen3-8B with only a rank-32 self-distillation LoRA, it reaches 97.05 on RULER and 38.27 on LoCoMo (vs 34.59 for full-context KV-Direct), and at 128k uses 18.26 GB rather than 89.36 GB with a 7.83× prefill speedup.
**Why it matters**: Reframes long-context memory as a *layer-axis* problem rather than a token-axis one, which is a different lever than every KV-eviction and token-pruning line of work.

### 4. Scaling Vision-Language Models Is Not Enough to Mitigate Bias
**Authors**: Ioannis Sarridis, Ioannis Kompatsiaris, Symeon Papadopoulos
**arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28211
**TL;DR**: First large-scale empirical study of 194 public VLMs spanning 16 model families, 24 training datasets, and three benchmarks. The Spearman correlation between model scale and performance decays from ρ=0.68 on ImageNet to ρ=0.48 on single-attribute bias (CelebA) and to **ρ=0.05 on multi-attribute bias** (UrbanCars), while training-data size and quality correlate far more consistently with worst-group accuracy — curated datasets buy up to 25% over uncurated ones at comparable scale.
**Why it matters**: A scaling-law negative result at unusual sample size: parameter count essentially stops predicting robustness to compound spurious correlations, and data curation takes over.

### 5. Fidelity Is Not Safety: Gently-Compressed LLMs Pass Every Data-Free Quality Guard Yet Invent Procedure Steps in Agentic Execution
**Authors**: I. Kennedy, T. Kennedy
**arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28196
**TL;DR**: Across three model families, gently-compressed models clear the entire standard acceptance stack — perplexity, MMLU, and data-free output-fidelity probes — and then invent procedure steps that were never in the instructions when executing an SOP as an agent. The effect is operator-specific (coherent low-rank SVD truncation induces it; perplexity-matched magnitude pruning does not), and the governing axis is coherence of the compression error times its rate, not damage magnitude; the authors give a two-axis data-free screen that flags failing builds at fixed thresholds.
**Why it matters**: The dissociation is clean — the same weights that CI-win a fidelity test CI-fail the invented-step canary — meaning the industry-standard compression acceptance stack is structurally blind to an agentic safety failure.
