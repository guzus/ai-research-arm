## Highlights (Top 5 Must-Read)

### 1. NCP-ArchPreview: Moving towards Latent Space Language Models through Next Concept Prediction
**Authors**: Intern-NCP Team, Jiaqi Cao, … Dahua Lin, Zhouhan Lin, Bowen Zhou
**arXiv**: [2609.10715](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10715)
**TL;DR**: Autoregressive pretraining that is not only next-token prediction. Next Concept Prediction builds a product-quantized concept vocabulary from hidden states, predicts multi-token concepts with a dedicated Concept Module, and feeds them back to guide token generation — NTP and NCP trained jointly. Scaled to 8.9B on 5.73T Dolma-3 tokens (largest latent-space LM to date). Hits OLMo-3-7B's final pretrain loss on 51.3% of the tokens; after full pretrain, +2.45 macro / +5.99 GSM8K. 85% of the compute of a matched 8.9B NTP baseline approaches its loss. Post-pretrain, a 17M VQ update is a domain-adaptation interface; injecting concepts into a DFlash2 drafter lifts mean accepted length 4.17%.
**Why it matters**: Concept-level latent prediction is no longer a toy — it is a 5T-token architecture that buys loss and a cheap adaptation knob.

### 2. An Open Recipe for IMO Gold: Training Nemotron for Olympiad Mathematics
**Authors**: Ivan Moshkov, Stephen Ge, George Armstrong, Wei Du, Sadegh Mahdavi, Igor Gitman
**arXiv**: [2609.10712](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10712)
**TL;DR**: From Nemotron 3 Ultra, two specialist checkpoints (SFT + RL) plus a test-time pipeline that stays in natural language — no formal prover, no tools, no web. Three Ultra variants generate, verify, and refine proofs; a high-compute stage picks the submission. The system scored 30/42 at IMO 2026, the gold threshold. Released: both specialists, training data, train/infer code, submitted solutions, and Nemotron-IMO-Bench (200 novel olympiad problems).
**Why it matters**: An open, tool-free NL proof stack that actually clears IMO gold — the recipe is the paper.

### 3. Phase-Decoupled, Model-Calibrated Power Control for Disaggregated LLM Serving
**Authors**: Jae Gon Kim, Donghoon Yoo, Hanyul Ryu, Sungho Ha, Juyeon Lee, Soojung Ryu
**arXiv**: [2609.11133](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11133)
**TL;DR**: Prefill/decode disaggregation plus NVIDIA Max-Q on B200: only +8.6% tokens/J at +5.2% mean e2e, one setting on two opposite hardware regimes. The paper's controller gives prefill an SM-clock floor (latency by construction) and decode a calibrated power cap just above the measured cliff. On 8×B200 serving Qwen3-Coder-480B FP8 under agentic load: +20.4% tokens/J at +3.5% e2e. On Qwen3-235B-A22B NVFP4 every mode meets ITL-p99; both vendor profiles miss. A three-day run saves 32.3% of a lane pair's electricity. Dense recovers ~5× less — claims scoped to MoE.
**Why it matters**: Serving power is a (model, quant, engine, hardware, phase) tuple, not a GPU-class recipe.

### 4. Mr.LHDR: A Benchmark for Multimodal Real-World Long-Horizon Deep Research Agents
**Authors**: Minghao Guo, Meng Cao, … Xiaodan Liang, Xiaojun Chang
**arXiv**: [2609.11318](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11318)
**TL;DR**: Deep-research benches still score medium-horizon browse. Mr.LHDR builds each question from a hidden Node-Relation graph: mean 12.1 necessary intermediate conclusions, dependency depth 10.4, plus at least one image/map/PDF/logo/chart/table/video frame that changes the state. Grades final answers *and* intermediate conclusions under annotated dependencies (OA / SA / CS / DACS). Strongest system: 43.1% OA, 34.3% SA. Strip images: DACS −12.6. SA falls as chains lengthen.
**Why it matters**: Final-answer accuracy is flattering — the bottleneck is dependency-consistent multimodal evidence, not another search tool.

### 5. Routing by Reasoning Need: Trajectory-Aware Decoding Control for Diffusion Vision-Language Models
**Authors**: Yixiang Liu, Zhongxing Xu, Zhonghua Wang, Xiaoying Tang
**arXiv**: [2609.11315](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11315)
**TL;DR**: Diffusion VLMs expose an answer trajectory you can inspect. A universal generation length still mismatches reasoning need: closed visual questions get harmed by extra refinement; CoT-sensitive ones get harmed by early commit. Training-free controller on LLaDA-V routes each example to early commitment, baseline, or reasoning-supportive decoding from closure, commitment evidence, and representation-revision pressure — no gold answers. Beats fixed-long, pure-short, and single-rule interventions on answer-focused, mixed, and CoT-sensitive benches. EMNLP 2026 Findings.
**Why it matters**: Diffusion VLM test-time compute should follow the observed trajectory, not a global step count.
