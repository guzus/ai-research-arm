## Highlights (Top 5 Must-Read)

### 1. InternW0: A Foundational Physical World Model for Efficient Real-World Interactions
**Authors**: Physical Intelligence Team, Shanghai AI Laboratory
**arXiv**: [2609.27656](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27656)
**TL;DR**: InternW0 is the first InternW physical world model: a mixture-of-transformers with a high-capacity video expert and a faster action expert, coupled by observation-conditioned context routing that reuses layerwise K/V instead of regenerating the future at every control tick. Trained on ~7,200 hours of heterogeneous robot and egocentric data (including EgoLab, 275h of real-lab first-person video). Evaluated in sim and on real science: a 15-stage metal–organic framework synthesis workflow and 5-stage force-aware pipetting. Contact-aware post-training adds force/tactile channels and predicted 6-D wrenches.
**Why it matters**: Shanghai AI Lab just defined a physical world model as an asynchronous, omnimodal control loop — not a video generator you hope to steer.

### 2. Hunyuan-A13B Technical Report
**Authors**: Tencent Hunyuan Team
**arXiv**: [2609.27284](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27284)
**TL;DR**: Open 80B MoE that activates 13B at inference. Pretrained on a filtered 20T-token corpus with extra STEM curation, then SFT + large-scale RL. Dual-mode Chain-of-Thought: fast thinking for routine queries, slow thinking for multi-step work. Competitive on math, science, code, language, and agent tasks against much larger models, with high decode throughput for latency-sensitive serving.
**Why it matters**: Another open sparse frontier checkpoint — 13B active, not 13B total — with an explicit fast/slow reasoning switch.

### 3. KITE: KV-Invariant Transformer Expansion for Efficient Agentic LLM Scaling
**Authors**: Zhiheng Hu, Yixun Wei, Jian Zhou, Yizhuang Zhou, Ji Li, Xing Chen, Yang Li, Bojun Wang, Yibo Zhu, Xiangyu Zhang, Daxin Jiang (StepFun)
**arXiv**: [2609.27294](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27294)
**TL;DR**: Upcycling usually cheapens training and then you pay it back at inference. KITE adds parameters only in regions that do not change attention KV, so prefill still runs on the small tower. Instantiation: Step Scale Transformer, a two-tower decoder. SST 67B MoE (2.15B active body / decode token) beats 47B and 63B classic MoEs at matched training FLOPs, with estimated inference cost −6.7% vs 47B and −31.6% vs 63B. The motivation is agent traffic: OpenRouter traces show uncached input still dominates spend even after prompt cache (GPT-6 Astra 74.6% of uncached charges).
**Why it matters**: Scale the reader, not the KV writer — the cheap way to grow an agent model when prefill is the bill.

### 4. Harness as a Language: A Minimalist Agent Framework With Maximal Expressivity
**Authors**: Zhening Li, Joshua Liu, Mateja Vukelic, Nicole Shen, Supriya Lall, Amitayush Thakur, Alex Zhang, Omar Khattab, Jonathan Light, Armando Solar-Lezama
**arXiv**: [2609.26891](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26891)
**TL;DR**: JAZ exposes one primitive, `invoke`: the model writes arbitrary executable code (including recursive `invoke`), and everything it can see is a variable in that environment. No hand-built memory, no file-system sidecar, no extra harness. On long-horizon recall past the context window, invoke beats Letta/MemGPT by 8% at half the cost on the recall-heavy slice of StuLife. On continual self-improvement, it beats ACE by 4% at lower cost on AppWorld.
**Why it matters**: Memory systems and self-improve loops may be special cases of "the model can write the next function."

### 5. CAVEAT: Towards Robust Computer-Use Agents in Incentive-Misaligned Environments
**Authors**: Yuxuan Li, Will Epperson, Wesley Deng, Zezhou Huang
**arXiv**: [2609.27273](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27273)
**TL;DR**: CUA benches test cooperation or explicit attacks. CAVEAT tests what happens when the *environment* has a stake: nine marketplaces, eight steering mechanisms. Five model families buy the user-optimal product in 78.6% of matched-control episodes and 17.3% once steering is on. Larger models and more reasoning help; they do not close it. Failures enter at three points — distorted user priorities, premature narrowing, early commit. CAVEAT-Harness targets those and raises user-optimal purchasing by 55.0%.
**Why it matters**: Delegated shopping is not a jailbreak problem. The page itself is the adversary.
