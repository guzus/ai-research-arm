## Highlights (Top 5 Must-Read)

### 1. Emergent Misalignment Recruits a Pre-existing Persona Subspace
**Authors**: Mohammed Suhail B Nadaf
**arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21356
**TL;DR**: Shows via contrastive activation analysis that narrow fine-tuning on a harmful/misaligned task doesn't create new misaligned behavior from scratch — it recruits an existing "persona" direction already present in the pretrained model's representation space.
**Why it matters**: Turns emergent misalignment from an empirical mystery into a mechanistic, potentially detectable-before-deployment phenomenon.

### 2. The Dark Room in the Reward Channel: Dense Prediction Rewards Collapse GRPO-Trained LLM Agents
**Authors**: Yu Wang
**arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21273
**TL;DR**: Identifies a failure mode where dense, per-step prediction rewards cause z-score-normalized GRPO optimization to collapse into degenerate "do-nothing" policies once failures dominate the batch.
**Why it matters**: A concrete, reproducible failure in the exact RL recipe (GRPO + dense rewards) much of the agentic-training industry is standardizing on.

### 3. Windowed-MTP: Removing the Full-Context Draft-KV Tax at Million-Token Context
**Authors**: Alagappan Valliappan
**arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21535
**TL;DR**: Applies sliding-window attention to the draft heads used in multi-token-prediction speculative decoding rather than the full context, cutting draft-KV overhead 28–44% at million-token context with no retraining.
**Why it matters**: A training-free inference cost cut at exactly the context lengths frontier labs are now pushing toward in production.

### 4. Anti-Periodic Positional Encoding: Möbius Boundary Conditions Make In-Context Retrieval Reliable
**Authors**: Ji Ho Bae
**arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21405
**TL;DR**: Proposes "Möbius RoPE" — anti-periodic rotary frequencies that break the periodicity artifacts standard RoPE introduces at long context — reporting 90.3% needle-in-haystack accuracy vs. 63.3% for standard RoPE at context length 512.
**Why it matters**: A large empirical jump from a single architectural change to RoPE, worth scrutiny given how load-bearing RoPE is across nearly every current LLM.

### 5. AREX: Towards a Recursively Self-Improving Agent for Deep Research
**Authors**: Shuqi Lu et al. (24 authors)
**arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21461
**TL;DR**: A deep-research agent alternating between an evidence-gathering research loop and a self-auditing improvement loop that checks its own answers against constraint-wise correctness criteria.
**Why it matters**: A large-team effort at agents that audit and improve their own research outputs in a loop — directly relevant to where agentic-research pipelines are heading.
