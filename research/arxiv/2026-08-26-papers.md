# arXiv AI Research - 2026-08-26

## Highlights (Top 5 Must-Read)

### 1. Effective Learning Rate Governs Loss Dynamics in Language Model Pretraining
**Authors**: Zihan Liu; Ruiheng Zheng; Shaobo Zhang; Changxin Tian; Kunlong Chen; Zhiqiang Zhang
**arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24814
**TL;DR**: Learning rate and parameter norm shape pretraining loss dynamics almost entirely through their ratio — the effective learning rate (ELR). When ELR is matched, loss trajectories collapse across different LRs/norms, optimizers, architectures, datasets, and scales (errors of ~1e-3, below seed noise), and weight decay/Hyperball act mostly via induced ELR schedules. ELR-based functional scaling laws transfer across norm-control methods and explain delayed acceleration.
**Why it matters**: Establishes ELR as a common coordinate unifying LR scheduling and norm control, giving the field a single lever for reproducible, predictable pretraining dynamics.

### 2. Meta$^n$: Recursive Self-Improvement through Emergent Depth
**Authors**: Zae Myung Kim; Young-Jun Lee; Seungyeon Jwa; Dongyeop Kang
**arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24735
**TL;DR**: Keeps the meta-operation fixed and recurses on its own products: each layer reads the solver stack's traces plus the code that produced them, then writes the next layer as a strategic pre-process and helper library. Depth is set by convergence, and an evolutionary archive searches over layer chains; across two backbones it outperforms all prior self-improving agents on eight benchmark families, and is the only method scoring above zero on ARC-AGI-2.
**Why it matters**: A stable, architecture-free recipe for recursive self-improvement that does not destabilize its own editing machinery — the sharpest demonstration on a memorization-resistant benchmark.

### 3. LAION-BVD: A 10-Million-Hour Open Video Dataset for Multimodal Pre-training
**Authors**: Andreas Hochlehnert; Marianna Nezhurina; Mehdi Cherti; Andrej Radonjic; Thaddäus Wiedemer; Christoph Schuhmann
**arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24845
**TL;DR**: From 1.3B CommonCrawl video URLs, the authors download 80M videos totaling 10M hours, extract scene-detected clips with synthetic video and audio captions, and release it for multimodal pre-training. Models trained on it are competitive on video-text and audio-text benchmarks with consistent scaling gains; extracted scene-changing frames also yield a distinct, strong image-text corpus.
**Why it matters**: An order-of-magnitude expansion of open access to multimodal video — a core resource bottleneck for video/audio foundation models.

### 4. Recuris: Recursive Experiential-Working Memory Evolution for Long-Horizon Agent Harnesses
**Authors**: Zhaochen Yu; Yingcheng Wu; Zhenfei Yin; Kaiyuan Chen; Zhe Zhao; Mengdi Wang
**arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24876
**TL;DR**: A working-memory/experiential-memory architecture grounds skill selection in current task state while a fixed meta-agent converts execution evidence into validation-gated skill updates, forming a bounded recursive memory-evolution loop. Across 4 benchmarks and 10 models it improves success in 35/37 model-benchmark pairs, lifting Claude Opus 5 to 87.9% on tau-bench and widening gains to +32.2 points on the longest horizons.
**Why it matters**: Positions recursively evolving memory as a scalable, evidence-grounded foundation for long-horizon agents and recursive self-improvement.

### 5. TurboT2VA: Fast Large-Scale Text-to-Video-Audio Generation via Score-Regularized Consistency Distillation
**Authors**: Xiaoda Yang; Yuxiang Liu; Kaiwen Zheng; Yuan Liu; Yibo Lai; Shengpeng Ji
**arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24674
**TL;DR**: A distillation plus inference-stack framework for a 19B-parameter joint text-to-video-audio model: per-modality normalization and a progressive consistency curriculum (warm-up → continuous refinement → joint matching) plus W8A8/fused/padded-text/sparse-attention kernels. Four-step distillation cuts generator latency 50.52s→2.51s at 512x768 (20.1x) and 318.74s→5.83s at 1024x1792 (54.67x) on one H20.
**Why it matters**: Brings joint video-audio generation from minutes to seconds, removing a major inference-cost barrier for large multimodal generative models.
