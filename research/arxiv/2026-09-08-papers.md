# arXiv AI Research - 2026-09-08

## Highlights (Top 5 Must-Read)

### 1. Molecular Déjà Vu: Digit-Level Retrieval of Published Values in Frontier Language Models
**Authors**: Matthias Busch, Marius Tacke, … Roland C. Aydin, Christian Feiler
**arXiv**: [2609.05381](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05381)
**TL;DR**: Accuracy on molecular-property regression cannot tell a predictor from a lookup. Across 22 frontier models and 12 benchmarks, verbatim digit-level retrieval is widespread but dataset-specific: on five sets, more than half the models retrieve published values. Raising the reasoning level, same molecules and prompt, flags retrieval 89% more often. In the worst cells, the strongest models still recognize transformed SMILES paired with the original labels; suppress retrieval and the models' error gaps shrink — the ranking was partly a memorization ranking.
**Why it matters**: A science bench that a model can pass by reciting the paper is not a capability measurement.

### 2. τ^τ-Bench: An Environment for End-To-End, Realistic Agent Construction
**Authors**: Quan Shi, Keshav Dhandhania, Karthik Narasimhan, Victor Barres
**arXiv**: [2609.04611](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04611)
**TL;DR**: Coding agents now *build* production agents; existing benches never score that job. τ^τ-bench (hyper-tau-bench) gives a developer agent the records, a client, a production API, an inherited codebase, and a serving-cost cap, then grades the shipped customer-service agent on held-out users. 53 tasks × 4 domains: Claude Opus 5 under Claude Code passes 23.9% of simulations; an expert-authored ceiling is 82.2%. Failures match the human ones — shallow record queries, almost no client talk, first-design-that-runs.
**Why it matters**: The scarce skill is no longer tool use. It is delivering an agent under a real engagement's constraints.

### 3. Beneath the Surface of Chains-of-Thought: A Mechanistic Interpretation of Reasoning Operations in LLMs
**Authors**: Seogyeong Jeong, Jaehui Hwang, … Alice Oh, Taekyung Kim
**arXiv**: [2609.04753](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04753)
**TL;DR**: Formulation, decomposition, and deduction are distinct in the *text* of a CoT. They are also distinct in the residual stream. Held-out operation classifiers separate them, peaking in middle layers, and the split is not lexical or positional. Alignment spreads over the span; the same surface token sits in a different place depending on the surrounding operation; attention-masking shows chunk-onset geometry depends on prior reasoning context. EMNLP 2026 Main.
**Why it matters**: CoT is not just a narrative the model emits — the named operations have a corresponding geometry.

### 4. Optimizer Memory Schedules for Outscaling the Overtraining Axis
**Authors**: Katie Everett, Shikai Qiu
**arXiv**: [2609.04577](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04577)
**TL;DR**: Which optimizer wins is a function of *how far past Chinchilla you train*. Across 51M–253M models and overtraining factors 1×–256×, with a learning-rate sweep at every point: the preferred LR schedule can reverse, best weight decay scales ~√OT, and longer horizons want longer fixed memory. ADANA's exponent edge over AdamW survives per-horizon memory tuning and matches DANA theory on power-law random features; Muon and SOAP buy a roughly constant token-efficiency gap. ADANA starts behind both and closes, passing Muon and meeting SOAP at the highest OT.
**Why it matters**: Optimizer bake-offs at 1× Chinchilla do not predict the overtrained regime that frontier training actually uses.

### 5. Locating and Steering Refusal Beyond Attention
**Authors**: Preethi Carmel Bosco, Gopalakrishnan Srinivasan
**arXiv**: [2609.04721](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04721)
**TL;DR**: Transformer refusal is one residual direction. SSMs share no token mixer, so the question is whether the same representation exists. It does: a single rigid rotation aligns the spaces; a transformer harm probe flags an SSM's harmful inputs; removing the aligned direction answers attacks a random direction of equal size does not. Architecture-specific is the *read site* — harm is clean at the layer write, before residual add — not the steer site. A detector-gated intervention drops jailbreak success on SSM, transformer, recurrent, and hybrid families.
**Why it matters**: Safety tooling built on a refusal direction ports by re-estimating at the new write site, not by starting over.
