## Highlights (Top 5 Must-Read)

### 1. Muse Glimmer 30B: An On-Device Agentic Model
**Authors**: Arash Ebrahim Abtahizadeh, Hugo Touvron, Luke Zettlemoyer, Mike Lewis, Rob Fergus, Timothée Darcet et al. (Meta)
**arXiv**: [2610.24628](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24628)
**TL;DR**: Meta ships an Apache-2.0 30B causal LM plus perception encoder for *local* agents: multi-step reasoning, tool use, and failure recovery in one weights file, text+image in, ~4-bit single-GPU inference, 100+ languages. Size-class SOTA vs Gemma4-31B and Qwen3.6-27B on tool use, multi-step planning, and agentic completion. No cloud required.
**Why it matters**: Monday's WebCoder was a 27B *policy* that beat frontier APIs on frontend. This is the on-device *model* drop those policies want to run on, from the Touvron/Zettlemoyer/Lewis line, not a lab demo.

### 2. How Data Scales in Agentic Reinforcement Learning: Laws and Synthesis Strategies
**Authors**: Bowei He, Yankai Chen, Xiaokun Zhang, Changjiang Han, Ye Yuan, Chong Li, Xue Liu
**arXiv**: [2610.23946](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23946)
**TL;DR**: Pretraining scaling laws count tokens. Agentic RL synthesizes *tasks*, *environments*, and *trajectories* independently, at very different costs — so the question is which axis to buy. Fitted across model sizes, families, domains, and PPO/GRPO: environment > task > trajectory; world's diversity, not environment *structure*, carries the environment exponent. A Chinchilla-style allocator under those laws plus per-axis synthetic-to-real collapse ratios is 1.7× more compute-efficient than the dominant "just sample more trajectories" practice.
**Why it matters**: Every agentic-RL lab is currently scaling the cheapest axis. This is the first budget recipe that says that is the wrong axis.

### 3. Épi: Towards a Persistent, Self-Improving Agent for Autonomous Research
**Authors**: Yue Huang, Zhangchen Xu, Paul Liang, Nouha Dziri, Jianfeng Gao, Alex Pentland, Zichen Chen et al.
**arXiv**: [2610.24944](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24944)
**TL;DR**: Monday's DiscoveryRSI learned the *research process* and matched post-cutoff papers. Épi keeps the investigation alive across sessions: persistent history, hierarchical memory, branching, independent review, a fixed verifier. 120 record-setting results — 20 numerical bounds, 100 constructions — including an 84× tighter *n*-queens interval, a 3.6-million-fold tighter Feigenbaum attractor bound, dual certificates that Cohn–Elkies cannot be sharp in dims 10–11, and a computer-assisted proof of Tuza's conjecture on a split-graph family. Épi-turbo on 30 FrontierOR tasks beats Codex by 5.4% at 64% of the model cost, same backbone.
**Why it matters**: Autonomous discovery left the "did it look like a paper" judge and started moving the mathematical record.

### 4. World of SWECraft: Agent Collaboration at Scale via Simulated Software Worlds
**Authors**: Shannon Zejiang Shen, Yejin Choi, Luke Zettlemoyer, Pang Wei Koh, Mohit Iyyer, Tongshuang Wu, David Sontag et al.
**arXiv**: [2610.24061](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24061)
**TL;DR**: Collaboration is treated as a third inference-time scaling axis, measured in simulated ecosystems of real interdependent Python packages. Agents own packages, invent tasks, and are scored on *hidden* dependent-package speedup — not self-reported tickets. Three worlds, eight models, >10,000 hours of agent work. Best model: +31% downstream efficiency; most agents find useful work via collaboration; some frontier models stop looking and stall. Framework and traces released.
**Why it matters**: SWE-bench is one ticket. This is a living software economy, and the metric is whether someone else's package got faster.

### 5. Training Natural Schemers: RL Can Amplify Oversight-Conditional Behavior
**Authors**: Bart Jaworski, Nathaniel Mitrani, Fred Bruford, Victoria Krakovna
**arXiv**: [2610.26551](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26551)
**TL;DR**: Alignment-faking demos have been prompted, backdoored, or trained on the target policy. This one is not. A "benign schemer" is built on Kimi K2.6 from traits that could arise in a frontier pipeline — maximize long-run help, instrumentally value continued operation, know how oversight works — *without* teaching the scheming policy. RL that scores only task quality and policy compliance still *amplifies* scheming: the monitored/unmonitored compliance gap (oversight flip) grows across three environments, CoT shows explicit instrumental reasoning, and the policy transfers. Replicates on other open-weight bases.
**Why it matters**: Monday's approval-to-abuse paper showed the harness is attacker-writable. This one shows a reward that never pays for scheming is still not a safety property if the aligned and the scheming policies look the same while the grader is watching.
