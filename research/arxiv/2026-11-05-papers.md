## Highlights (Top 5 Must-Read)

### 1. From Pixels to Actions — Towards Native Vision-Language-Action Policies
**Authors**: Weichen Fan, Haiwen Diao, Penghao Wu, Fangzhou Hong, Dahua Lin, Ziwei Liu et al.
**arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06918
**TL;DR**: NEO-VLA drops the frozen visual encoder. Lightweight patch convolutions tokenize the image, then vision and language evolve jointly through the backbone so a flow-matching action expert can read instruction-conditioned features at every depth. Native architectures beat encoder-based counterparts under controlled comparisons; full fine-tuning and extra scale do not close the gap. Layer-wise probes show action-critical signals often peak well before the final visual representation.
**Why it matters**: It argues that embodied control needs the visual hierarchy, not a single encoder endpoint — a structural claim, not a data-scale one.

### 2. Perceptive Humanoid Motion from Simulation
**Authors**: Siyuan Hu, Abulikemu Abuduweili, Yue Meng, Matthias Müller, Vladlen Koltun et al.
**arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06908
**TL;DR**: Apple AIML's HALO synthesizes vision-language-motion data across a thousand procedurally generated homes, so the corpus scales with compute rather than teleoperation hours. The humanoid learns to sit, navigate to named objects, sidestep through half-open doors, crawl under tables and push boxes, then composes those skills into long-horizon tasks. Trained with no real homes, it transfers zero-shot to six unmapped physical environments at 91% success over 360 trials.
**Why it matters**: It is a concrete existence proof that sim-only, language-conditioned humanoid policies can land in the real world without a paired real-home corpus.

### 3. FrontierPhysics: Evaluating Agents for End-to-End Physics Research
**Authors**: Bingran You, Wenjun Ke, Ria Rosenauer et al. (42 physicists; 72 authors)
**arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06910
**TL;DR**: 56 tasks written by 42 physicists from their own research, spanning nine theoretical, computational and experimental domains. Agents must read the literature, plan, run experiments and write a manuscript; scoring mixes deterministic verifiers with expert rubrics for correctness and publishability. Across 22 models in 26 configurations, no setup passes more than 46 of 168 rollouts. Trajectory review blames insufficiently rigorous reasoning and missing expert intuition, not missing textbook knowledge.
**Why it matters**: It is an end-to-end AI-scientist eval, and it shows current agents fail at research judgment rather than at recall.

### 4. Open-HumanScale: An Open Recipe for Scaling Human and Robot Experience
**Authors**: Juncheng Ma, Jiaxin Li, Jiankai Tu et al.
**arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06817
**TL;DR**: An open 50,000-hour egocentric corpus with dense hand-motion and language labels, plus the curation and pretraining recipe. Closed-loop sim evals yield a log-linear scaling law from pretraining hours to downstream robot success. HumanWAM-Vanilla, pretrained only on human video, reaches 92.08% / 68.88% on RoboTwin 2.0 Clean2Clean / Clean2Random; hybrid HumanWAM-Goal lifts Clean2Random to 84.60% and shows zero-shot visual-goal in-context learning on physical robots.
**Why it matters**: It publishes both the data scaling law and the checkpoints, which is what the rest of the field can actually rerun.

### 5. Visual Token Routing: Learning to Keep Tokens That Matter
**Authors**: Pranjal Aggarwal, Sean Welleck
**arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06854
**TL;DR**: Computer-use agents drown in screenshot tokens. VTR jointly trains the VLM with a lightweight spatio-temporal router that admits a region only if it helps the next action. After 2,000 steps it matches the full-resolution model on OSWorld, CUA-World and game action prediction with 2.5–5.8× fewer visual tokens, up to 3.3× lower inference cost and 2.9× faster prefill, and beats resolution-dropping by up to 12.8 points on OSWorld.
**Why it matters**: Long-horizon computer-use is currently gated by visual context; this is a learned, action-aware way to cut that cost without shrinking the screenshot.
