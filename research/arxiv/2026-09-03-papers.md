# arXiv AI Research - 2026-09-03

## Highlights (Top 5 Must-Read)

### 1. Post-Training Language Models for Gold-Medal Performance in Coding Competitions
**Authors**: Aleksander Ficek, Sean Narenthiran, Mehrzad Samadi, Somshubra Majumdar, Boris Ginsburg
**arXiv**: [2609.02849](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02849)
**TL;DR**: An end-to-end specialization stack — 22k curated problems, synthetic traces, SFT, and RL — produces Nemotron-3-Nano-CC (30B-A3B) and Ultra-CC (550B-A55B). On IOI 2025, Nano-CC rises 130 → 291 after post-training and 468 with GenCorrect (iterative generate–evaluate–refine), clearing the 438.3 gold line; Ultra-CC reaches 502. A competition-specific Ultra-CC system then ran *prospectively* at IOI 2026 under human time, internet, and submission rules and scored 535.4 / 600, above gold (361.12) and the top human (498.27).
**Why it matters**: The first reported AI system to outscore the highest-scoring human on an IOI problem set, under contest constraints rather than a retrospective dump.

### 2. SolarWM: Open Data and Scalable Training for Long-Horizon Video World Models
**Authors**: Junchao Huang, Guian Fang, Shengju Qian, … Mike Zheng Shou, Li Jiang
**arXiv**: [2609.02886](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02886)
**TL;DR**: A fully open stack for interactive video world models: a multi-source engine turns 1.43M clips from 10 datasets into a frame-aligned contract (pixels, metric cameras, captions, quality, provenance), then a backbone-native adapter instantiates four 5B–33B models on Wan2.2, LTX-2.5, and MiniMax-H3 without rewriting their native objectives. A three-stage recipe (bidirectional adaptation → teacher-forced AR init → distribution-matching distillation) yields causal models that support real-time interaction over minute-to-hour rollouts after training on 5-second sequences.
**Why it matters**: World-model research has been locked to closed data and one-off backbones; SolarWM ships the data contract, recipes, and weights so long-horizon interaction can be reproduced.

### 3. Cliff: Learning Process Rewards from the First Mistake
**Authors**: Peixuan Han, Runhui Wang, Ketan Ramaneti, Jie Hao, Gerald Friedland, Chris Kong
**arXiv**: [2609.02817](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02817)
**TL;DR**: Once a reasoning trace first goes wrong, later tokens are conditioned on an invalid prefix, so scoring them adds little. Cliff uses an off-the-shelf teacher only to mark that first error, then writes token-level advantages: positive on the correct prefix, negative after. Across 12 settings it beats on-policy distillation by 15% and GRPO by 7%, even with modest teachers — no specialized process-reward model and no assumption that teacher and student reason alike.
**Why it matters**: Process supervision without a process-reward model: the first-mistake cut is enough to turn outcome RLVR into fine-grained credit.

### 4. oHC: Orthogonal Hyper-Connections on SO(4) via Quaternions
**Authors**: Haoqiang Guo, Xuyi Chen, Bo Ke, … Shikun Feng, Wenhan Luo
**arXiv**: [2609.02672](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02672)
**TL;DR**: Hyper-Connections mix *n* residual streams with a learned *n×n* matrix; unconstrained mixing compounds and blows up. mHC projects onto doubly-stochastic matrices, which caps amplification at 1 but still lets inter-stream differences shrink with depth. oHC instead restricts the mixer to SO(*n*). At the usual *n*=4 they parameterize the group with a pair of unit quaternions — no extra parameters, a fixed pattern of signed adds instead of iterative projection — so mixing can neither amplify nor attenuate any direction. Beats the single-stream residual, mHC, and identity-fixed iHC on a broad downstream suite.
**Why it matters**: The residual-mixer design space now has a closed-form rotation that keeps streams diverse without the stability tax of unconstrained HC.

### 5. The Implications of Linguistic Illegibility for LLM Security
**Authors**: James Mickens
**arXiv**: [2609.02852](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02852)
**TL;DR**: “Linguistic illegibility” names the case where a model’s externalized text or probed linguistic features fail to represent how it actually computes — unavoidable once internals are math over activations and language is only a lossy bookend. CoT monitors, constitutional self-critique, and linguistically defined activation probes can therefore never be completely sound. The proposed floor is a sandbox whose guarantees do not read the model’s linguistic state at all: a priori taint-tracking policies over system state the model must never influence, plus robust virtualization and third-party audit of the sandbox config.
**Why it matters**: A security argument, not a benchmark: if language is not the computation, reading the model’s words cannot be the last line of defense.
