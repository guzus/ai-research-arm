# arXiv AI Research - 2026-08-22

## Highlights (Top 5 Must-Read)

### 1. AI4AI-Bench: Benchmarking LLM Agents in Algorithmic Design for Recursive Self-Improvement
**Authors**: Yizhe Chi, Wenyi Li, Deyao Hong, Xiaoqiu Wang, Mingju Gao, et al.
**arXiv**: http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20318
**TL;DR**: A benchmark that isolates whether an agent can redesign a training algorithm itself (the "compute-capability exchange rate") across 10 frozen research repositories spanning 10 algorithm families, with a hidden fixed evaluator and hard 4h/12h time budgets. Across 29 configurations of 6 systems, the mean score is 0.166 and the best reaches 0.250 — even the strongest closes under a fifth of the distance to the task optimum.
**Why it matters**: It measures not what models know but whether they can improve the machinery that produces them, finding that reasoning effort reallocates agents toward actually editing learning rules (8% → 64% of submissions) — a direct probe of the recursive self-improvement question.

### 2. Swift-Image: Exploring the Performance Frontier of Compact Unified Image Generation Models
**Authors**: Taihang Hu, Zhao Wang, Zuan Gao, Tao Liu, Hao Yan, et al.
**arXiv**: http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20334
**TL;DR**: A compact 6B single-stream DiT for unified text-to-image, single-image and multi-image editing, trained with a progressive curriculum, parallel-expert RL, multi-teacher on-policy distillation, a decoupled Prompt Enhancer, and structural pruning. It leads evaluated open-source models with only 243K GPU-hours; the pruned 3B variant loses nearly nothing and few-step distillation further improves editing.
**Why it matters**: It maps how far a small generator can be pushed through training-engineering alone — direct evidence on parameter efficiency, compression, and the practical gap between compact and frontier-scale generation.

### 3. 4DAnyone: Create Anyone in 4D from a Casual Monocular Video
**Authors**: Yudong Jin, Tao Xie, Qihang Zhang, Zehong Shen, et al.
**arXiv**: http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20335
**TL;DR**: Recovers 4D humans from uncalibrated monocular video by generating reconstruction-grade multiview videos and lifting them into 4D Gaussian Splatting. It diagnoses DiT multi-view failure as a bounded-attention-context problem and fixes it with O(1) Reference Context Packing plus Target Context Routing that rotates view groupings across denoising steps.
**Why it matters**: It breaks the view-count ceiling on video-diffusion conditioning, enabling direct monocular video → 4D reconstruction that outperforms prior methods on DNA-Rendering and DyMVHumans.

### 4. WithEveryone: Unified Planning and Identity Grounding for Group Image Generation
**Authors**: Hengyuan Xu, Qixun Wang, Yiji Cheng, Miles Yang, et al.
**arXiv**: http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20336
**TL;DR**: A unified framework for generating group images with up to ten identities: each identity is injected as an addressed token, a structured identity-layout plan is predicted and rendered as a visual condition, and a Layout-Grounded ID Loss supervises identities directly on annotated face regions. It improves face similarity from 0.462 (GPT-Image-2) to 0.499 while cutting copy-paste artifacts from 0.169 to 0.055.
**Why it matters**: Explicit identity-layout grounding lets identity-preserving generation scale to large groups without reference-face copying — a persistent failure mode of image models.

### 5. Phantom Gains: Auditing Self-Improvement Against a Measured Null
**Authors**: Cheng Xu, Nan Yan, Liming Chen, M-Tahar Kechadi
**arXiv**: http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20290
**TL;DR**: Auditing three rounds of LoRA self-training on Qwen3-8B against a frozen control run through the identical pipeline, it identifies seven measurement failures — each capable of inverting a reported finding when the control is absent. It replaces fragile thresholds with a per-problem exact test under FDR control, which detects nothing on held-out replicates, and shows external distillation gains are not attributable to self-training (p < 10⁻⁸).
**Why it matters**: It supplies the missing methodology — a separately measured null for every transition statistic — that self-improvement claims need before they can be believed.
