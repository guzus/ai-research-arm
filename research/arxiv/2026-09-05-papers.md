# arXiv AI Research - 2026-09-05

## Highlights (Top 5 Must-Read)

### 1. Interface-Induced Trajectory Censoring
**Authors**: Wenbo Wang
**arXiv**: [2609.03966](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03966)
**TL;DR**: Agent leaderboards report a tool-call rate read off the serving stack. That number can be zero while the model is already emitting well-formed calls — the interface censors the trajectory before anything downstream sees it. On BFCL v4, holding weights, cases, decoding, and seeds fixed and changing only the serving adapter, the same model scores 0.00 or 0.96 / 0.19. A 2×2 over chat template and parser puts the entire effect in the *interaction*: neither side is broken, and repairing one buys nothing. On τ-bench the same swap moves server-parsed calls from 0 to 636. The mismatch reaches inside training (verl AgentLoop at 7B: 45/115 complete calls, 0 accepted). A 98-line preflight catches every silent failure in the study.
**Why it matters**: The observed tool-call rate is a property of the model–interface stack, not the model — a large class of agent evals and RL loops are measuring the adapter.

### 2. ESPO: Error-Structured Prompt Optimization via Diagnose, Diversify, and Stabilize
**Authors**: Lihao Liu, Peng Tang, Kunwar Yashraj Singh, Shabnam Ghadar
**arXiv**: [2609.04197](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04197)
**TL;DR**: Evolutionary prompt optimizers such as GEPA bloat: each iteration appends rules until the prompt is 3× longer and no more accurate. ESPO diagnoses all training errors into structural patterns in one pass, proposes candidates from four complementary strategies, and selects with bootstrap stability. On seven NLP benches it beats GEPA by +3.76 pp (74.67 vs 70.91) with prompts 47% shorter, and the gain transfers across Gemma 3 12B, Mistral 14B, Qwen3 32B, and Claude Haiku 4.5 — Qwen3 GSM8K jumps 15.00 → 91.40. Adding diversity without the bootstrap actually hurts (−1.20%). EMNLP 2026.
**Why it matters**: Prompt search was being scored on length-as-progress; structured error coverage plus stability selection is the actual lever.

### 3. Scal3R: Learning Efficient Multi-Relative Pose Query for Scalable Online 3D Reconstruction
**Authors**: Chin-Yang Lin, Yang-Che Sun, Cheng Sun, Fu-En Yang, Min-Hung Chen, Yen-Yu Lin, Wei-Chen Chiu, Yu-Lun Liu
**arXiv**: [2609.04201](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04201)
**TL;DR**: Online 3D reconstruction dies on long video because poses are regressed against a fixed first-frame anchor and drift compounds into geometric collapse — while per-frame depth stays intact. Scal3R freezes the backbone, injects ~1% learnable tokens that query poses relative to multiple past keyframes, and closes loops with an online pose graph. It converges in 8 hours on one GPU, cuts average ATE by over 60% on KITTI versus the online baseline, and is SOTA on Virtual KITTI, Sintel, TUM-Dynamic, ScanNet, and 7-Scenes. ECCV 2026.
**Why it matters**: The failure was the pose head, not the geometry — a 1% query layer plus loop closure is enough to make online reconstruction scale.

### 4. Alignment-Free Text-Audiobox for Voice Dubbing and Full-Duplex Dialogue Synthesis
**Authors**: Sanyuan Chen, Min-Jae Hwang, Sho Inoue, … Juan Pino, Wei-Ning Hsu
**arXiv**: [2609.03992](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03992)
**TL;DR**: Text-AB is a 3B Diffusion Transformer trained with flow matching on DAC-VAE latents (48 kHz → 25 Hz, >10× denser than EnCodec, better resynthesis). It drops forced alignment and duration prediction: raw text goes through an off-the-shelf encoder and cross-attention learns the map. Pretrained on 480k hours, then fine-tuned for cross-lingual dubbing, full-duplex dialogue, and emotional full-duplex. One-shot generation covers ~1 min; multi-diffusion covers arbitrary length. On a real dubbing bench it is a step-change over the prior internal system; on long-form dialogue it models turn-taking, back-channels, and emotion natively.
**Why it matters**: Speech generation that no longer needs a duration model — and a single stack that does dubbing *and* overlapping conversation.

### 5. Speak for Me: Giving LLMs the Situational Awareness to Participate in a Meeting
**Authors**: Muneeb Khan, Frederic Kirstein, Terry Ruas, Bela Gipp
**arXiv**: [2609.03923](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03923)
**TL;DR**: Prompt-only meeting delegates stay silent on 51.4% of the absent participant’s talking opportunities on AMI. CAPA keeps a structured meeting state (stances, coverage, floor), predicts the next turn, decides whether to speak and which proposition to surface, then generates in the participant’s style; two judges plus a recalibrator close the loop. On 137 AMI meetings, silence falls 51.4% → 2.5%, credited recovery doubles (26.1 → 52.2), hallucination stays at 0.6%. The leftover errors are selection, not omission, and each traces to a named module. Schema-constrained judges agree with humans at κ = 0.71. EMNLP 2026 Main.
**Why it matters**: Delegation agents were missing the *when*, not the *what* — a meeting state, not a longer context window, is what closes the recognition gap.
