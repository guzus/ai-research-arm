## Highlights (Top 5 Must-Read)

### 1. On the Difficulty of Sequence-Level Supervised Learning for Autoregressive Language Models
**Authors**: Mathieu Blondel, Michaël E. Sander, Germain Vivier-Ardisson, Tianlin Liu, Vincent Roulet
**arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16736
**TL;DR**: Teacher forcing is blamed for exposure bias, so a natural fix is a sequence-level loss that folds in greedy decoding or autoregressive sampling. Both attempts are unbounded below; the sampling loss is the Gumbel-perturbed expectation of the greedy one. Stopping the gradient through the sampler yields a moment-matching direction that is a valid function-space descent for autoregressive NLL, but it has high variance. The authors conclude that teacher forcing may well be all you need for supervised AR training.
**Why it matters**: It closes a long-open “just train on the decoded sequence” path with a hardness result, not a heuristic.

### 2. daVinci-Prover: Agent-Native Mid-Training for Formal Theorem Proving
**Authors**: Wenhong Zhu, Xuefeng Li, Jiashuo Zhang, Yu Chen, Tiantian Mi, Qipeng Guo, Pengfei Liu
**arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16433
**TL;DR**: Agentic Lean provers need math priors *and* long-horizon tool trajectories, not SFT on proofs alone. The authors synthesize a 33.7B-token mid-training corpus (decompose, search, self-verify, revise) plus ~36K verifier-grounded trajectories collected in an isolated Lean workspace. The recipe gains 13.7 points over a matched Qwen3-8B SFT-only baseline on MiniF2F, FATE-M and ProofNet, and near-saturates MiniF2F on Qwen3.5 with memory compaction.
**Why it matters**: It is a transparent data recipe for interactive theorem-proving agents, not another opaque proof-only SFT run.

### 3. Experience-Handoff Reinforcement Learning for Recursive Self-Improvement
**Authors**: Doyoung Kim, Byeongguk Jeon, Kailin Zhang, Sherry Yang
**arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16409
**TL;DR**: Scalar-reward agent RL throws away error logs and other trial debris; ACE-style harnesses hand that debris forward as playbooks, but the playbook writer is untrained. ACE-RL keeps the ACE scaffold and co-trains actor, reflector and curator, scoring each candidate handoff by its effect on *future* actor rollouts. Held-out agentic scores beat scalar-only and unlearned-handoff baselines: the thing future episodes inherit has to be optimized, not just the current action.
**Why it matters**: It turns recursive self-improvement from a prompt wrapper into a gradient objective on the memory that the next trial reads.

### 4. Teach with Blinks: Breaking Linguistic Momentum in On-Policy Distillation for Fine-Grained Multimodal Perception
**Authors**: Tangzheng Lian, Hang Zhang, Guanyu Hu, Renjiu Hu, Zhensong Zhang, Jiankang Deng, Jifei Song
**arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16402
**TL;DR**: Vanilla OPD lets the teacher continue the student’s wrong prefix, so the correction is weak. BlinkOPD has the teacher read every prefix twice — eyes open and eyes closed — and a first-glance gain upweights rollouts that conflict with the teacher. With 500 images (12× less data than prior OPD) it is SOTA on six fine-grained benches and transfers zero-shot to ten held-out perception, hallucination and reasoning suites.
**Why it matters**: It names a real failure mode of on-policy VLM distillation and fixes it with a two-pass teacher, not more zoom labels.

### 5. STEALTH: Steganographic Text Encoding Agnostic to Low-Level Tensor Hardware
**Authors**: William Fleshman
**arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16928
**TL;DR**: Provably secure linguistic steganography assumes the receiver can recompute the sender’s token probabilities bit-for-bit. Across three model families that assumption dies: change precision, quantization, GPU, or even batched vs sequential scoring on the *same* box, and bit-exact schemes decode almost nothing. STEALTH uses a keyed exponential race plus a rateless spinal code so the two sides never share a distribution; it recovered every tested payload across precision, GPU architecture, and even different vocabularies, at higher payload-per-token than prior robust schemes, with steganalyzers at chance.
**Why it matters**: It invalidates the hardware contract behind nearly every “provably secure” LM covert channel, and replaces it with one that actually survives deployment.
