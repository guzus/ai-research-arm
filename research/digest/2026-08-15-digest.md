## Model Releases & Updates

- **Qwen3.8-27B (Alibaba)** — Apache 2.0, native-multimodal dense architecture, 262K native context extendable to 1M via YaRN. Unsloth Dynamic GGUFs and NVFP4 quants run it in 17GB. Being dense rather than MoE, it runs poorly on unified-memory boxes and wants discrete 3090/5090/RTX PRO 6000 cards. Simon Willison's laptop run produced his best pelican-on-a-bicycle from any laptop-runnable model, but took nearly 21 minutes and 22,276 reasoning tokens for 3,223 tokens of output; a separate tester reports multi-turn agentic work truncating at 6 of 15 turns.
- **GLM-5.3 (Z.ai)** — Same 743B base as GLM-5.2, all gains from scaled post-training: Terminal-Bench 3.0 4.6 → 28.3, CyberGym 77.2% → 84.5%, ExploitBench 24.4% → 54.4%, DeepSWE 46.2 → 66.9. API access and open weights staged behind a stated ~two-week safety review, with sensitive cyber functions limited to verified users. SemiAnalysis called it a decisive win over every American open model.
- **Gemini 3.7 Flash (Google)** — Completed rollout to Gemini web and mobile for all Pro and Ultra subscribers across 160+ countries, with 1M context and 64K output. Artificial Analysis measures it at 56 on its intelligence index, one point behind GPT-5.6 Terra, at ~340 tokens/sec; the measured gain over 3.6 Flash is 4 index points, narrower than the launch table implied. Cognition put Devin on it at 50% off through 27 August.
- **dots3-note Preview (RedNote)** — Open-sourced 280B multimodal MoE, 16B active parameters, 512K context, built around TEMPO, a reinforcement-learning method that has the agent critique its own progress and update memory mid-task.
- **Grok 4.6 (SpaceXAI)** — ARC Prize published third-party-verified results: 87.5% on ARC-AGI-1 at $0.30/task and 67.1% on ARC-AGI-2 at $0.76/task. The separate vendor claim that it ranks #1 on "CursorBench" has no published leaderboard, methodology or independent relay behind it.
