## Research Highlights

From the 2026-08-05 arXiv submission batch (~180 entries screened across cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.CL, cs.CV, cs.NE). The day's centre of gravity was **agent training infrastructure** — three independent groups attacking sparse reward on long-horizon rollouts — and **audit papers**, whose contribution is showing an accepted benchmark is broken.

- **[Argus: A General-Purpose Agentic Runtime for Long-Horizon Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05144)** — self-evolving runtime splitting work across Manager/Planner/Engineer roles with persistent state, gating each self-modification behind verification. Reports 78% on SWE-Bench Pro; the gain comes from the harness, not the model.
- **[SciCode-Verified](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04975)** — an audit finding 263 defects across 65 SciCode problems; on the corrected set models reach 84–98% subproblem accuracy. The benchmark, not the models, was the ceiling.
- **[ABSeeker](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05102)** — backtracks from the final answer to convert one sparse trajectory outcome into dense step-level rewards, reporting 55.3% on BrowseComp. Dense credit assignment without human step labels is the lever everyone is reaching for.
- **[Gradient Immunity: Null-Space Resistance to Malicious Fine-Tuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05045)** — a pre-release "unidirectional safety gate" blocks harmful gradient directions so downstream fine-tuning cannot strip refusal behaviour. Tamper-resistant open weights is the unsolved precondition for defensible open releases.
- **[Towards Physics of Multimodal Pretraining](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05000)** — systematic sweep mapping knowledge flow between modalities, when they are genuinely synergistic, and why early unification pays. Rare attempt to give multimodal recipes scaling-law-style structure.
- **[Item Response Theory for AI Safety](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05086)** — psychometrics over 8 safety benchmarks × 192 models resolving into three interpretable factors: refusal strictness, truthfulness, contextual harm.
- **Measurement-validity note:** [What We Observe as LLM Behavior Can Be a Side-effect of Inference Backend](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04714) bears directly on every benchmark claim above — worth carrying forward as a standing caveat.
