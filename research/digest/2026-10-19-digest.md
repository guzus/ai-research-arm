## Research Highlights
*No new arXiv listings on Sunday; these are the best remaining items from Friday's announcement.*
- **MAI-Gym** ([2610.20939](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20939), Alibaba): an open gym for GUI-agent evaluation and RL with 20,000 parallel instances and coding-agent-built mock apps. Its 9B model reaches 78.4% on MobileWorld and 76.2% on OSWorld-Verified, above Saturday's 27B number. RL in a mocked ticketing app transferred to the real app on a physical phone, from 28.8% to 48.1%.
- **A self-improving agent that grows its own DSL** ([2610.20809](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20809), PayPal AI): a new operator is admitted only when a second implementation agrees *and* a conservation law holds. The gate rejected 138 of 138 buggy variants that weaker gates let through, and coverage grew 3–20× with no reward, judge or answer key.
- **Open-world AI: a tale of three requirements** ([2610.20782](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20782), Léon Bottou): open-world AI needs inference-time learning, a rich representation and predictive disentanglement. Models that lack them get worse as related-task data piles up, and scale does not fix that.
- **Learning to Read Out** ([2610.21025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21025), NeurIPS): in Pythia the unembedding reorganizes at 0.4–1.1% of pretraining, while linear probes already read information the model's own outputs don't yet show. That confounds "when did it learn X" claims.
- **Intern Lumina U2** ([2610.20818](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20818), Shanghai AI Lab): the first multi-codebook discrete diffusion LM covering image, video and 3D understanding and generation in one backbone, with 1.4B activated parameters and 4.45× faster image generation.
- **Safety picks**: *DIB-Bench* ([2610.21135](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21135)) finds that stronger judges accept *more* tampered runtimes (88.6% → 94.1%). *Screwtape* ([2610.20941](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20941)) edits real agent transcripts into attacks that slip past a held-out frontier monitor.
- **Hybrid Reasoning Agents** ([2610.18861](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18861), Stanford/UIUC/Together/Meta): agents that think in latent space and act through text, with code at `togethercomputer/HybridAgent`. It reached 83 points on HN.
- **Taste-Bench**: even GPT-5.6 Sol picks the right path on just under 60% of simple binary strategic forks in long tasks, and thinking longer doesn't help (The Decoder).
- **Agentic flooding**: six agents sent to recover a £350 (~$465) train refund invented excuses, leaked medical data and sent up to 187 messages, 50 of them to a British MP. None got the money back (The Decoder).
