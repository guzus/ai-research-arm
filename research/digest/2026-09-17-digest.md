## Research Highlights

Wednesday's arXiv build is still current (Thursday's 04:00 UTC listing had not landed at press time): 72 new cs.AI and 98 new cs.LG announcements. Curated picks:

- **LimiX-2** ([2609.17488](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17488)) — a tabular foundation model that shifts in-context learning from target-centric \(p(y \mid x, D)\) to mechanism-oriented joint modeling \(p(x, y \mid D)\). On TabArena, TALENT and BCCO it beats dataset-specific models and current tabular FMs; feature attention recovers a causal skeleton.
- **ScienceBuddy** ([2609.17523](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17523)) — recursive-in-recursive self-improvement as a product: inner loop evolves the harness with the model fixed, outer loop trains the model under the improved harness. Workspace, code and the RSI recipe are released.
- **Coding Agents Have Converged** ([2609.17394](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17394)) — audit of 254 SWE-bench submissions. On Verified the leading two each resolve 396 of 500; McNemar tests separate *none* of 29 adjacent top-thirty pairs. Small leaderboard gaps are not a ranking.
- **Belief State Geometry** ([2609.17376](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17376)) — six open-source LLMs prompted with 40 HMMs carry linearly decodable Bayesian posteriors in the residual stream (probe \(R^2\) 0.83–0.99). Patching the probe subspace leaves prediction intact; controls collapse it.
- **Dream-RSI** ([2609.14858](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14858)) — the coding agent stays frozen; what self-improves is the exploration policy. Replay over realized search trees scores thousands of candidates at zero new executions. Up to 1.7× fewer agent calls than fixed exploration and 162× fewer than SimpleTES on Lasso — useful, and not weight-updating RSI (HN 171 pts).
- **How good are frontier models at physics?** ([2609.13009](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13009)) — Yale-led faculty re-grade of six benches: of 250 audited "failures," only 12 were model errors. GPT-5.6 Sol's HLE-Physics mean@4 rises 47.3% → 78.7% after questions and graders are fixed; the authors' agents still solved none of the open theoretical-physics problems they tried.
- **Breaking the 1.58-bit Barrier** ([2609.16338](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16338)) — Intel's BITCOS: 29 ternary checkpoints are not equiprobable \(\{-1,0,+1\}\); zeros reach 51.5%. Bitmap-plus-compacted-signs packing beats five-trit on 26 of 29 models, down to 1.485 bits/weight, with up to 1.27× decode on Xe2.
- Also notable: **Verifiable Social Reasoning / Fuse** ([2609.17496](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17496)) grades social advice against a hidden motive; **Salesforce DarwinX** (VentureBeat recap) moved a frozen GPT-5.5 from 43.5% to 93% on WebArena-Infinity by evolving the harness, not the weights; **OPEN-1B** ([2609.17380](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17380)) is a bitwise-auditable 1B training run; and **CoSQ** ([2609.17516](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17516)) cut unsupported wrong answers on TruthfulQA from 13.1% to 8.9% with a prompt-only abstention rule.
