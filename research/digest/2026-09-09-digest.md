## Research Highlights

From the 2026-09-08 arXiv build (44 papers curated; note the 2026-09-08 04:00 UTC RSS build was empty, so the RSS lane carried no papers):

- **Molecular Déjà Vu** ([2609.05381](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05381)) — across 22 frontier models and 12 benchmarks, verbatim digit-level retrieval of published molecular-property values is widespread; on five datasets more than half the models retrieve rather than predict. Suppress retrieval and the error gaps shrink: the ranking was partly a memorization ranking.
- **τ^τ-Bench** ([2609.04611](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04611)) — grades a coding agent on *building* a production customer-service agent under a serving-cost cap. Claude Opus 5 under Claude Code passes 23.9% of simulations against an expert ceiling of 82.2%; failures mirror human ones (shallow record queries, no client conversation).
- **Beneath the Surface of Chains-of-Thought** ([2609.04753](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04753)) — formulation, decomposition and deduction are separable in the residual stream, not just in CoT text, peaking in middle layers and not reducible to lexical or positional cues. EMNLP 2026 Main.
- **Optimizer Memory Schedules** ([2609.04577](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04577)) — which optimizer wins depends on how far past Chinchilla you train. Across 51M–253M models at 1×–256× overtraining, preferred LR schedules can reverse and best weight decay scales ~√OT; ADANA starts behind Muon and SOAP and closes at the highest overtraining factors.
- **Locating and Steering Refusal Beyond Attention** ([2609.04721](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04721)) — a single rigid rotation aligns refusal representations across transformers and SSMs; what is architecture-specific is the read site (the layer write, before residual add), not the steer site. Detector-gated intervention cuts jailbreak success across four model families.
- Also notable: **TruthInsightBench** ([2609.05079](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05079)) finds four coding agents plateau at 58.4–60.3/100 on blind discovery tasks — they execute, they do not establish a claim; and **Discovery Loop** ([2609.05093](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05093)) reports LLM-evolved solvers taking 10 Packomania circle-packing records (N=101–114) for $27.72 in compute.

## Quote of the Day

> "The 50% is modeled cost rather than throughput; per-chip speed is only about 5% ahead."

— @a2ycapitalinc, decomposing SemiAnalysis's claim that Google's Ironwood TPU delivers 50% better performance per dollar than Blackwell Ultra. The reading puts the realistic range at 19–34% and argues the headline number materializes only once SemiAnalysis substitutes its own hourly TPU cost assumption.
