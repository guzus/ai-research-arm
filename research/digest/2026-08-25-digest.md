## Research Highlights

Curated from the 2026-08-24 arXiv batch (announcement build of 2026-08-21):

- **WA-JEPA: Video JEPA for World-Action Modeling in Autonomous Driving** ([2608.20974](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20974)) — recasts prediction as conditional flow matching over latent futures and denoises scene tokens and ego trajectories jointly; 91.7 EPDMS on NAVSIM-v2 and best closed-loop HUGSIM HD-Score (0.4462) without task-specific fine-tuning.
- **E²-TTT: Rethinking Expressivity and Efficiency in Test-Time Training** ([2608.21308](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21308)) — a closed-form chunk transition exactly reproducing per-token TTT recurrence, enabling parallel chunk-level training; retains >90% accuracy at 8× the training context on Needle-in-a-Haystack at 1.3B parameters.
- **Scaling Muon for Diffusion Transformers** ([2608.20818](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20818)) — Periodic Row-wise Muon preserves a 12.9–19.1% generative-quality edge over AdamW while cutting optimizer time 46.9–54.3% and step time 15.7–24.3%, from 1.3B to 15B parameters.
- **CLEAR: Continuous Latent Adapter Routing for Safety Alignment** ([2608.21278](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21278)) — a hidden-state gate modulating safety-LoRA strength drops HarmBench ASR from 32.3% to 0.5% on Llama-3-8B-Instruct while gaining up to 7.1 points on GSM8K over global SFT/LoRA.
- **OmniAssistBench** ([2608.21360](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21360)) — first interactive benchmark for omni-modal assistants, built from 1,000+ expert-hours of reverse-engineered video; Gemini-3-Pro scores 66.4/100 and Qwen3-Omni-Instruct 51.2, with failures concentrated in visual-prompt use, multi-turn context and response timing.

Also notable: **Certified Multi-Turn Robustness (MTCR)** lifts multi-turn jailbreak certification from p^k to beta^k degradation ([2608.20820](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20820)); **BackDFL** finds decentralized-FL robustness overestimated, with SOTA Byzantine-robust methods failing at as low as 15% malicious participation ([2608.21137](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21137)); **Specification Portability Across LLM Development Agents** shows cross-agent spec transfer degrading sharply (Token F1 0.035 worst case), meaning specs are not agent-neutral artifacts ([2608.21208](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21208)); and **The Logic of Machine Self-Preservation** separates instrumental-convergence evidence from claims about instincts ([2608.20940](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20940)). A circulating agent-skills result argued the same way: on ContinualSkillBench, pure in-context learning scored 0.605 against 0.602 with explicit skill maintenance, implying most of the benefit is carried context rather than reusable abstracted skills ([2608.03874](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03874)).
