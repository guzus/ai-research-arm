On 8×B200 serving Qwen3-Coder-480B FP8, +20.4% tokens/J at +3.5% mean e2e versus NVIDIA Max-Q’s +8.6% / +5.2%. A three-day run saves 32.3% of a lane pair’s electricity; claims scoped to MoE.

- **Routing by Reasoning Need** ([2609.11315](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11315)) — training-free controller for diffusion VLMs that routes each example to early commit, baseline, or extra refinement from the observed trajectory. Beats fixed-long and pure-short decoding; EMNLP 2026 Findings.
