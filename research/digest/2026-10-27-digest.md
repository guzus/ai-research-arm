## Research Highlights
- **Kolibri (Aleph Alpha)**: a 78.1B-total / 3.46B-active English–German MoE under Apache 2.0, trained on 24T tokens. German makes up more than 20% of the data by bytes. Among the open models evaluated, it sits on the quality-vs-serving-cost Pareto frontier in both languages, which suits sovereign deployments ([2610.31945](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31945)).
- **HyScale**: makes training learned sparse attention at 1M tokens cheaper by computing exact top-k without building the full score table and by reusing the forward pass's selected indices during recompute. It reports 2.07× over Megatron-LM for GLM-5.1 on 512 H20s ([2610.32057](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32057)).
- **Scaling laws for synthetic rewrites (Liang et al.)**: a Chinchilla-style law that weighs the compute spent rewriting data against pretraining compute. When compute is the limit, the optimal rewrite fraction rises with compute from under 10% ([2610.31867](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31867)).
- **Model or harness, who's to blame?**: across 52k trajectories, four general harnesses and four models, model rankings swap depending on the harness, and valid-completion rate does not track task score ([2610.32483](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32483)).
- **Ψ-0.6**: a single vision-language world model that switches between image, text and explicit geometry "streams of thought." Zero-shot, it beats leading open-weight models on multi-view spatial reasoning ([2610.32177](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32177)).
- **Safety picks**: SWE-Shift finds GPT-6 Astra falls from 67.35 to 24.79 when the workspace changes mid-run ([2610.32196](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32196)). A compaction study finds the first context compaction already drops about a third of ProgramBench's anti-cheat rules ([2610.31778](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31778)).
