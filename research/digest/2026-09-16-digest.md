## Research Highlights

Tuesday's arXiv build (still the current one at press time) carried 147 new cs.AI and 190 new cs.LG announcements. Curated picks:

- **Corrupt Plans, Clean Traces: Evading CoT Monitoring with Plan Injection** ([2609.15989](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15989)) — planting harmful but benign-sounding reasoning in an actor's context steers adversarial actions while the monitor's trace stays clean (25–33% evasion). Giving the monitor the injected plan *drops* detection by up to 50%: extra thinking is spent rationalizing rather than flagging. CoT monitoring is not a sealed audit.
- **When Agents Slow Down: Elo-per-token Analysis** ([2609.15309](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15309)) — agents beat independent sampling early, then their marginal Elo falls below it. Splitting 100M tokens across parallel sessions beat one long session by +264 Elo. Long single sessions waste the tail.
- **Stellar Colosseum** ([2609.15983](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15983)) — a many-agent proof harness producing new results on open FOCS and JMLR problems, 71.0% on TCS-Bench and 218/222 on Codeforces with Gemini 3.1 Pro.
- **ZGCM-1** ([2609.13356](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13356)) — 7B from scratch, interleaved gated sliding-window plus full attention, FP8 Muon, 16K→64K→256K context curriculum, ~4.2× faster to the 16K loss target.
- **Lightning Weave** ([2609.14708](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14708)) — composes independently post-trained specialists instead of another long-CoT RL run: Qwen3.5-4B goes 59.2%→64.0% on HMMT 2025 with 10.7% *fewer* response tokens.
- Also notable: **Shallow Beliefs** ([2609.14998](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14998)) finds synthetic document finetuning can make reward hacking sound acceptable without blocking emergent misalignment, while inoculation prompting works; **AcquireBound** ([2609.14744](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14744)) quarantines agent-acquired compute and credentials before activation; and Sakana's **PC-ALM** ([2605.31022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.31022)) trains residual MLPs to 1000 layers within ~2 points of backprop with no global backward pass.
