## Research Highlights
*No new arXiv listings on Saturday; these are the best of Friday's leftovers.*
- **Athanor** ([2610.21055](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21055)): four Claude Opus 5.5 agents, running 104 hours on eight GPUs each, autonomously post-trained Qwen3-4B-Base from 15.9 to 67.3 on the dev suite and from 9.2 to 48.2 on held-out tasks. That matches the official Qwen3-4B-Instruct (66.5 / 47.9). The authors call it the first public demo of a frontier-agent team turning a raw base model into a generalist.
- **OS-Helix** ([2610.22234](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22234)): handing a GUI agent a code tool through prompting alone makes it worse. Training the GUI/code routing, with step-balanced GSPO, gets a 27B model to 66.44% on OSWorld-Verified.
- **The skill-policy co-evolution trap** ([2610.20845](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20845)): when the skill bank drifts after deployment, 52–93% of the reported gains from co-evolved skill banks disappear. The paper proposes CODA, a robustness audit.
- **KV-cache handoff fails** ([2610.22016](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22016)): all eight methods for swapping one agent's cache into another reader score at the uninformative floor (0.180 vs 0.665 native). What works is letting the reader attend over the sender's cache as extra context.
- **Can capability enhancement be localized?** ([2610.21294](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21294), Zico Kolter): ablating a small set of heads can *raise* task accuracy with no fine-tuning. Even at a global optimum, gradient descent concentrates feature conflict into specific heads.
- **Agents hide their own mistakes**: Google Research, MIT and Harvard found GPT-5.5 left negative results out of 198 of 200 paper abstracts it wrote. A one-sentence prompt instruction reduces this (The Decoder).
- **OpenAI's Navier-Stokes submission is disputed**: mathematicians say the 166-page proof's claimed methodology does not hold up and its origin is opaque (The Decoder).
- **Epoch AI's Cyber ECI**: the gap between open-weight and closed models on cyber tasks widened from ~6 to 11 points. GLM-5.3 leads the open models at 160, against 171 for the US closed frontier (@EpochAIResearch).
