## Research Highlights

- **Performance Foundations of Parallel & Distributed Reasoning Language Models** (arXiv:2608.27046): the first compute-centric systems treatment of RLVR post-training for reasoning models, with a taxonomy of intra- and inter-model parallelism for PPO/GRPO-style training made rigorous with the work-depth model, plus practical guidelines.
- **Squeezing More from Limited Data with Recursive Transformers** (arXiv:26973): maps the 10M–100M-word pretraining regime where parameter scaling past an optimum degrades generalization, and shows shared-block recursive Transformers plus factorized embeddings decouple compute from parameters, staying competitive with BabyLM 2025 winners.
- **Do Monolingual Language Models Converge on Universal Representations?** (arXiv:27115): strictly monolingual models develop alignable representational geometry without joint training; a single Procrustes rotation causally transfers factual answers between English and German models.
- **JudgeStealer: Extracting LLM Judging Capabilities** (arXiv:26982): a query-efficient extraction attack converts pointwise judge scores into pairwise/listwise supervision for free, cloning judging capabilities with up to 87.0% pairwise fidelity and surviving representative defenses — defining the threat model for evaluation and reward infrastructure as IP.
- **SpatialCrafter: Single Image World Modeling with Generative 3D Proxies** (arXiv:27073): predicts a geometrically consistent global 3D proxy, then refines appearance with video diffusion, achieving state-of-the-art consistency and reduced drift under rapid camera motion; releases a 115K-scene dataset.
- **WikiSkill: Compiling Agent Experience into Persistent Knowledge** (arXiv:27454): Google Research gives agents a wiki-like memory where they document failures and successes and reuse them across tasks; The Decoder's coverage framed it as agents learning from their own mistake logs.
