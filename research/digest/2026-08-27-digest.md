## Research Highlights

- **Effective Learning Rate Governs Loss Dynamics in Language Model Pretraining** ([2608.24814](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24814)) — LR and parameter norm shape pretraining loss almost entirely through their ratio. Matching ELR collapses loss trajectories across optimizers, architectures, datasets and scales to ~1e-3 error, below seed noise, giving one reproducible lever for schedule and norm control.
- **Meta^n: Recursive Self-Improvement through Emergent Depth** ([2608.24735](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24735)) — fixes the meta-operation and recurses on its own products, with depth set by convergence and an evolutionary archive searching layer chains. Outperforms prior self-improving agents on eight benchmark families and is the only method scoring above zero on ARC-AGI-2.
- **LAION-BVD: A 10-Million-Hour Open Video Dataset** ([2608.24845](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24845)) — 80M videos totaling 10M hours distilled from 1.3B CommonCrawl URLs, with scene-detected clips and synthetic video/audio captions. An order-of-magnitude expansion of open multimodal video, a standing resource bottleneck.
- **Recuris: Recursive Experiential-Working Memory Evolution** ([2608.24876](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24876)) — a validation-gated memory-evolution loop that improves success in 35 of 37 model-benchmark pairs, lifting Claude Opus 5 to 87.9% on tau-bench with gains widening to +32.2 points on the longest horizons.
- **TurboT2VA: Score-Regularized Consistency Distillation** ([2608.24674](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24674)) — four-step distillation of a 19B joint text-to-video-audio model cuts generator latency 50.52s → 2.51s at 512×768 and 318.74s → 5.83s at 1024×1792 on a single H20.
- Also notable in the safety track: **When "Must" Becomes "Maybe": Constraint Weakening in LLM Agent Workflows** ([2608.24569](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24569)) and **StepGuard** ([2608.24777](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24777)), both landing on the same day as the incident reports above.
