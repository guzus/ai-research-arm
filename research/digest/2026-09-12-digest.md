## Research Highlights

From the 2026-09-11 curated arXiv build (Friday 04:00 UTC daily; 51 new CS.AI / 73 new CS.LG announcements). Saturday's build had not landed at digest close.

- **T1: Terminal Agent Reinforcement Learning** ([2609.11042](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11042)) — a 122B-total MoE is RL-trained on a real shell for 300+ tool-call turns. TITO plus routing replay cut the train–infer logprob gap from 0.021 to 0.013; Terminal-Bench 2.1 rises from 43.8% to 64.0%, and Long-Horizon Terminal Bench is 27.9% versus the authors' GPT-5.4 / GLM-5.1 prints.
- **Mixtures-of-Experts Overfit More to Repeated Data** ([2609.11917](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11917)) — Jha, Li, Leskovec, Liang and Zettlemoyer find the damage tracks *total* parameters, not active ones. An 80M dense model can repeat 8× with little loss; matched MoEs start hurting at 4× and, by 32×, give back every all-unique advantage.
- **SenseNova-U1.5** ([2609.11929](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11929)) — native unified visual intelligence without a VAE or a separate encoder, with post-training experts folded by on-policy distillation. SFT, RL, and OPD code are promised.
- **SpecGuard** ([2609.11799](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11799)) — when a backdoor fires, speculative-decoding draft acceptance moves because the clean draft does not follow the target. Mark Russinovich is a listed author; "for free" means no extra generation pass on a stack that already drafts.
- **Thinking with Looped Flows** ([2609.11801](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11801)) — local denoising objectives train recurrence without a long BPTT tape. First-party scores include 58.8% on ARC-AGI-1 and 12.2% on ARC-AGI-2.
- Also notable: **GPU-CFR** ([2609.11923](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11923)) is 29.8–80.4× faster than the prior GPU CFR baseline by compiling the game once; **Why Does Post-Training Quantization Work?** ([2609.11716](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11716)) argues layer errors oppose inherited error.
