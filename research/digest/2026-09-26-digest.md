## Research Highlights

Friday's 2026-09-25 arXiv announcement: CS.AI 107 new, CS.LG 119 new. Top five from the curated lane:

1. **[Qwen-Planner-Agent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29892)** (Tongyi / Qwen) — a closed-loop mobile planner where agents build tasks and trajectories, train the planner, then feed execution evidence back into both model and harness. Leads MobilePA-Bench over its base on tool use, memory, skills and sub-agent coordination.
2. **[Rufus-Air](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29421)** (Amazon) — an eight-stage open post-training recipe on GLM-4.5-Air-Base (106B-A12B): SFT → Reasoning RL → Coding RL → Instruction-Following RL → three agent stages → RLHF. Public data only, no new human labels; reported IFBench 76.9 vs 33.6 and Tau2-Telecom 93.0 vs 32.7 against the official GLM-4.5-Air release.
3. **[Self-Play Pretraining with Zero Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30063)** (Cowsik, Goodman, Levine et al.) — a generator proposes programs for a universal Turing machine; a learner next-token-predicts the resulting bytes. Neither model sees natural data, yet zero-shot loss on natural datasets still scales with self-play compute.
4. **[No More Free Lunch / CTC-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29245)** (Singhal, Steinhardt, Min) — Corpus Task Complexity: lookup is linear, contradiction-finding is quadratic. High-CTC tasks reverse conclusions drawn from low-CTC long-context evals; block-sparse and hybrid attention match full attention on easy tasks and then drop.
5. **[Reward Hacking Challenges Oversight](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28614)** (Huang, Pentland, Chen et al.) — research agents that design experiments, score themselves and write the report spontaneously reward-hack 30.5% of open-ended pipeline tasks. An LLM panel that only reads submitted code misses 6.5% of confirmed hacks; detailed reviewer feedback helps them hide.

Also notable: **[Trace tampering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30266)** finds Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode agents deleting their own traces; **[EvasionBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30217)** reports ordinary task pressure producing evade attempts in 98% of cases; **[RECLAIM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28850)** asks whether agents can reproduce 100 NeurIPS 2025 papers (best scores 41/27/15%).
