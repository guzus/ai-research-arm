## Research Highlights
- **One algorithm for pretraining through RL**: "Training Foundation Models with a Single Algorithm" (Song, Salakhutdinov, Bagnell, Zanette et al.) recasts RL as maximum-likelihood fitting to model-generated targets, mixing static data and RL generations in one trainer for a better accuracy-compute tradeoff (arXiv 2610.40564).
- **How to loop your transformer**: A Meta-affiliated study up to 1B params and 32 loops finds full-model looping best at fixed parameters, mid-block looping best under FLOP limits, and fixes the Parallel Loop Transformer decode slowdown (arXiv 2610.40325).
- **Agents that prove impossibility**: Algebraic World Model (incl. Andrew Chi-Chih Yao) explores, writes Python hypotheses and formalizes them in Lean 4, producing kernel-checked impossibility proofs on all six runs where frontier baselines produced none in twelve (arXiv 2610.40503).
- **Coding agents build robotics sims**: Gym-Anything for Robotics yields RoboWorld (25 simulations, 200 RL environments); best frontier models average a test reward of just 0.06 (arXiv 2610.40565).
- **Scalable oversight via rewinding**: Rewindable interactive proofs let a weak verifier reset an AI prover to earlier states without a truthful-prover assumption or cryptography (arXiv 2610.40535).
- **Google's FlowAgent repairs CI at scale**: Fixes for failing pre-merge tests were judged correct 67.18% of the time on 195 real failures, and developers have applied 28,554 in production; median run 9.85 minutes (paper via @rohanpaul_ai).
- **Also notable**: TextSeal2 distortion-free watermarking survives speculative decoding (2610.40613); CORAL hits 42.8% on GAIA with an 8B model and 6-7× fewer input tokens (2610.40350); speculative decoding can cost energy at large batch (2610.40287).
