## Research Highlights

Thirty-nine papers were curated from the 2026-08-22 arXiv pool (cs.AI / cs.LG / cs.CL / cs.CV / cs.NE). Top five:

1. **AI4AI-Bench: Benchmarking LLM Agents in Algorithmic Design for Recursive Self-Improvement** ([2608.20318](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20318)) — isolates whether an agent can redesign a training algorithm itself across 10 frozen research repos with a hidden evaluator and hard 4h/12h budgets. Across 29 configurations of 6 systems the mean score is 0.166 and the best is 0.250; raising reasoning effort lifts the share of submissions that actually edit the learning rule from 8% to 64%.
2. **Phantom Gains: Auditing Self-Improvement Against a Measured Null** ([2608.20290](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20290)) — audits three rounds of LoRA self-training on Qwen3-8B against a frozen control through the identical pipeline, identifying seven measurement failures each capable of inverting a reported finding. Its per-problem exact test under FDR control detects nothing on held-out replicates, and external distillation gains are shown not attributable to self-training (p < 10⁻⁸).
3. **Swift-Image: Exploring the Performance Frontier of Compact Unified Image Generation Models** ([2608.20334](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20334)) — a 6B single-stream DiT for unified text-to-image and multi-image editing that leads evaluated open-source models on only 243K GPU-hours; the pruned 3B variant loses nearly nothing.
4. **WithEveryone: Unified Planning and Identity Grounding for Group Image Generation** ([2608.20336](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20336)) — scales identity-preserving generation to ten people, raising face similarity from 0.462 (GPT-Image-2) to 0.499 while cutting copy-paste artifacts from 0.169 to 0.055.
5. **4DAnyone: Create Anyone in 4D from a Casual Monocular Video** ([2608.20335](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20335)) — diagnoses DiT multi-view failure as a bounded-attention-context problem and fixes it with O(1) Reference Context Packing plus Target Context Routing, beating prior methods on DNA-Rendering and DyMVHumans.

Also notable: **EchoCoT** extracts near-verbatim hidden chain-of-thought from black-box reasoning models via a tool-call "reasoning replay surface" (up to 66.4% success, 33k+ tokens from Gemini-2.5); **MemTrapBench** finds that even faithfully stored, relevant memories cause reasoning fixation, with all five memory frameworks underperforming no-memory by >10%; **Learning When to Think** cuts mean response length 41% at equal MATH500 accuracy; **Task-CoEvolve** (University of Tokyo) finds over 70% of a typical agent benchmark uninformative and reduces evaluations during harness optimization by 80%; and **InsufficiencyBench** reports no frontier model exceeds F2=0.46 at identifying missing elements in legal queries — all hedge or fabricate presumptions.
