## Research Highlights

Curated from `research/arxiv/2026-09-02-papers.md` (the 2026-09-02 04:00 UTC build). The 2026-09-03 RSS sweep still lists that same daily drop.

- **Mechanism Design for Alignment and Control** (arXiv:2609.01595): Bergemann, Koh, and Morris give a revelation-principle treatment of agents whose preferences *and* capabilities are private. Capabilities can be concealed but not counterfeited, which yields nested cyclical monotonicity and worked cases for sandbagging, peer scoring, and scalable oversight.
- **SMELT: Scaling Laws for Compute-Matched MoE Looped Transformers** (arXiv:2609.01343): loops the middle half of a sparse MoE twice while matching FLOPs, non-embedding parameters, and KV cache, then fits a Chinchilla law up to 54B. Loss falls faster with compute (6.8–18.0% fewer training FLOPs); a second pass reduces the attention sink.
- **Knowledge Distillation During Mid-Training Favors Reasoning over Factual Recall** (arXiv:2609.01532): forward-KL from a post-trained teacher helps both reasoning and facts in pre-training, but during mid-training it keeps lifting reasoning while slowing factual acquisition. Switch Distillation routes on teacher entropy.
- **GlossoGen: Emergent Language in Complex Multi-Agent LLM Interactions** (arXiv:2609.01491): agents under efficiency pressure invent compositional languages that leave the English prior and become unreadable to humans — a monitorability result, not a linguistics curiosity.
- **Harness-of-Harness** (arXiv:2609.01481): an outer plan–code–test loop around existing coding harnesses. Three harness–model pairs gain 52.25% on average after three iterations; a 70-iteration run ships a first-person shooter.
- Other titles worth a look: Instella-MoE (open 16B/2.8B-active MoE on AMD MI300X), Cheap Verifiers, Large Blind Spots (cascade error growing with student scale), When Safety Routing Breaks, and Solaris (interface world model; also in RSS).
