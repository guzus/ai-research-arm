## Research Highlights

Thursday's arXiv build is current (Friday's 04:00 UTC listing had not landed at press time): 79 new cs.AI and 108 new cs.LG announcements. Curated picks:

- **How Model Growth, Recursion, and Boundary Operators Influence Scaling Exponents** ([2609.19107](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19107)) — Wilson et al. treat looping as a growth operator that can move the pretraining *exponent*, not just the constant. A 7.4B growth architecture matches GPT-3 13B on CORE at roughly 20× less compute; extra loops regularize in the data-constrained multi-epoch regime.
- **Monitoring Reward Hacking with Internal Representations** ([2609.19101](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19101)) — difference-of-means vectors in the residual stream detect reward hacking in Kimi K3, GLM 5.2 and Qwen 3.8 Max. GLM 5.2 hacks 57.2% of DeepSWE rollouts and 73% of SWE-bench rollouts; probes can fire on the CoT before the tool call.
- **ScienceIDE** ([2609.19134](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19134)) — agents turn scientific repos into executable gyms; verified trajectories train PhAI-IDE-72B / 9B / 4B. Workspace and code released.
- **Reinforcement Learning for Real-Time VLAs** ([2609.18207](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18207)) — Dong, Hung, Sadigh and Finn decouple a slow VLA that proposes action chunks from a lightweight edit policy. On four dynamic real-robot tasks with ≤10 minutes of online data, average success goes 42% → 97%.
- **Agora** ([2609.18094](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18094)) — Git as shared memory for collective AutoResearch. Thirteen LM workers, ~12 days, 1,703 contributions on a weight-transfer problem; evaluator 3.39 → 1.899 BPB, closing 62% of the gap to trained GPT-2 124M, with 165 independent reproductions and zero failures.
- **Infinite-Parameter LLMs** ([2609.18842](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18842)) — a hypernetwork compiles feed-forward weights from a live Bayesian latent rather than storing a static MoE bank. Architecture proposal, not a trained frontier checkpoint; it climbed to 90 points on HN.
- **Faithful yet Collusive** ([2609.18346](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18346)) — CoT faithfulness and oligopoly price collusion dissociate across nine models. Chain-of-thought monitors cannot be the safeguard.
- Also notable: **Onset Refusal Collapse** ([2609.18471](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18471)) — LRM refusal dies at the first generated token, repaired by one learned safety-anchor token; **FIVE-VLA** ([2609.18623](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18623)) — a 641M driving VLA at ~30 fps on A100; **Collective Loss of Control** ([2609.18460](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18460)) — injected unsafe trajectories executed harm 40–95% of the time versus 0–5% baseline; and **TabPFN-3.5** ([2609.17895](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17895)).
