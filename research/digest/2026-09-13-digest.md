## Research Highlights

From the 2026-09-12 curated arXiv build (Saturday 04:00 UTC daily; 77 new CS.AI announcements; CS.LG skipped the weekend). Sunday’s build had not landed at digest close.

- **Musec: MomentUm SpEctral Clipping** ([2609.11655](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11655)) — Muon’s spectral flattening sets every momentum singular value ≈1 and weights grow without bound. Musec clips values above a threshold; Soft Musec approximates that with coupled Newton–Schulz and is the first Muon-type method with a nonconvex-nonsmooth convergence proof.
- **ORCH** ([2609.11737](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11737)) — task-specific org charts for embodied multi-agent stacks. Across 25 wildfire missions and teams of up to 50 agents on eight LLMs, human-designed ORCH beats four prior frameworks by +63.97% final score and +74.29% execution efficiency.
- **Vidu S2** ([2609.11638](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11638)) — real-time 720p interactive character video with mid-stream reference swaps, plus a live editing stack for restyle, re-clothe, and re-background. The paper reports wins over listed baselines; a playable demo is up.
- **Power Flexibility Index** ([2609.11542](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11542)) — 131 H200 LLM training runs plus matched H100s show large but variable throughput sensitivity to GPU power caps. Under a 30% cut, PFI-aware allocation recovers ~1.5k tokens/s per job, 63% of the equal-weight-to-oracle gap.
- **An Open Recipe for IMO Gold** ([2609.10712](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10712)) — Nemotron 3 Ultra plus two specialist checkpoints and iterative generate/verify/refine search scores 30/42 at IMO 2026 in natural language only. Checkpoints, data, and Nemotron-IMO-Bench are released.
- Also notable: **Learnware** ([2609.11656](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11656)) treats a model-plus-comparable-spec as the reuse unit, not a checkpoint; **ZipCodec** ([2609.11642](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11642)) is a 6.25 Hz / 0.80 kbps streaming speech codec that runs in real time on a consumer CPU; **WORLDVIEW** ([2609.11532](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11532)) finds DALL-E-3, Imagen-4, and GPT-Image-1.5 *revision* layers mark and stereotype non-US contexts.
