## Research Highlights

arXiv ran no weekend announcement cycle (2026-08-01 was a Saturday, `totalResults=0`),
so the papers below come from the uncovered tail of the 2026-07-30 batch.

1. **[Tycho: Active Abstraction with Programmatic World Models for ARC-AGI-3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28287)** — formalizes ARC-AGI-3 games as rendered deterministic Moore machines. With actor-requested delegation to a model builder, GPT-5.6 Sol and Opus 5 both hit 100.00 RHAE and complete all 183 levels; the ablation shows deciding *when* to build a world model beats simulator accuracy alone.
2. **[Echoverse: Deep, Evolving Environments for Computer-Use Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28074)** — environment *depth*, not count, is the causal variable: a 9B model goes 36.5% → 67.1% across fourteen splits, and shallow environments actively degrade live-site accuracy (80.0 → 75.0).
3. **[Understanding Is Done Early](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28263)** — CoMem writes context chunks through an intermediate layer only, making read compute independent of stored-context length: 97.05 RULER on a frozen Qwen3-8B, 18.26 GB at 128k rather than 89.36 GB, 7.83× prefill speedup.
4. **[Scaling VLMs Is Not Enough to Mitigate Bias](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28211)** — across 194 public VLMs, the scale/performance correlation decays from ρ=0.68 on ImageNet to ρ=0.05 on multi-attribute bias; curated training data buys up to 25% over uncurated at comparable scale.
5. **[Fidelity Is Not Safety](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28196)** — gently-compressed models pass perplexity, MMLU and data-free fidelity probes, then invent procedure steps when executing an SOP as an agent. The standard compression acceptance stack is structurally blind to the failure.

Also notable from the same batch: [Old Tricks, New Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28187) (grayscale and color inversion alone bypass all three commercial multimodal moderation APIs), [MemHarness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28272) (agents reconstruct retrieved experience rather than replaying it), and [Numerical State in Sparse MoE Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28097) (mathematically equivalent expert aggregations produce divergent continuations in DeepSeek-V4-Flash).
