## Research Highlights

Forty papers were curated from the 2026-09-24 arXiv announcement (CS.AI 59 new, CS.LG 115 new). The top five:

1. **[InternW0](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27656)** (Shanghai AI Lab) — a foundational physical world model built as a mixture-of-transformers with a high-capacity video expert and a faster action expert, coupled by observation-conditioned context routing that reuses layerwise K/V instead of regenerating the future every control tick. Trained on ~7,200 hours of robot and egocentric data; evaluated on a 15-stage metal–organic framework synthesis workflow and 5-stage force-aware pipetting.
2. **[Hunyuan-A13B](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27284)** (Tencent) — an open 80B MoE activating 13B at inference, pretrained on a filtered 20T-token corpus with dual-mode chain-of-thought: fast thinking for routine queries, slow thinking for multi-step work.
3. **[KITE / Step Scale Transformer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27294)** (StepFun) — upcycling that adds parameters only where attention KV is unchanged, so prefill still runs on the small tower. A 67B MoE beats 47B and 63B classic MoEs at matched training FLOPs with estimated inference cost −6.7% and −31.6% respectively.
4. **[JAZ / Harness as a Language](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26891)** (Li, Khattab, Solar-Lezama et al.) — an agent framework exposing one primitive, `invoke`: the model writes arbitrary executable code and everything it can see is a variable. Beats Letta/MemGPT by 8% at half the cost on recall past the context window, and ACE by 4% on AppWorld.
5. **[CAVEAT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27273)** (Li, Huang et al.) — computer-use agents in incentive-misaligned environments. Five model families buy the user-optimal product in 78.6% of matched-control episodes and 17.3% once marketplace steering is on; larger models and more reasoning help but do not close it.

Also notable: **[PotARCin](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27288)** (with Melanie Mitchell) finds a 25–52pp gap versus standard ARC with models self-contradicting; **[Delegated misalignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27900)** argues multi-agent structure *amplifies* safety risk; and **[StudentBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28470)** (Northcutt et al.) reports AI matching human GRE tutoring (p=.015) at 918× lower cost.
