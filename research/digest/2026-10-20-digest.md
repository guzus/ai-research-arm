## Research Highlights
- **Busy Beaver discovery** ([2610.22603](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22603)): A Gemini pipeline conjectures and proves in Lean compact accelerations of Turing machines on the BB(6) frontier, giving formally verified results for 11 machines. - **WebCoder** ([2610.23104](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23104)): Agentic RL scored on the executable artifact. WebCoder-27B reaches 72.8 requirement satisfaction on Webdev-Bench, above the Claude-Opus-4.8 (65.1) and GPT-5.4 (59.6) figures the paper reports.
- **Self-Building Labs** ([2610.23826](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23826)): Aspuru-Guzik's group has agents design the lab setup itself (layout, digital twin, robot skills), demonstrated end-to-end on quantum-dot synthesis.
- **DiscoveryRSI** ([2610.23839](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23839)): A model that recursively learns to generate research hypotheses. The 27B version matches papers published after its training cutoff 6.3% of the time, versus 2.5% for GPT-6 and 2.3% for Claude Opus 5.
- **Privilege escalation on coding-agent harnesses** ([2610.22987](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22987)): Untrusted repo content steers Claude Code and Codex CLI into requesting overly broad allow-rules, which they elicit in 58% of cases. Once a rule is approved, silent goal completion reaches 81%.
- **Small decision models for agents** (arXiv 2610.21412, Princeton/Google): A 4B model gated by a confidence threshold cuts agent cost by 81–89% while keeping success within 3 points. Without the gate, success drops 19 points.
- **Google Harness²**: Open-source recursive harness editing around a frozen model. The authors report +8.4 on WorkBuddy-Bench for Gemini 3.5 Flash, and GLM-5 rising from 64.2 to 75.7 under OpenCode.
