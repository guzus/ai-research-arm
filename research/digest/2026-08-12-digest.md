## Research Highlights

- **[Stealing Reasoning Traces from Proprietary LLM APIs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09867)** (Panfilov, Schmotz, Shumailov, Beurer-Kellner et al.) — Encrypted chain-of-thought blocks turn out to be interchangeable across sessions, users and models: injecting a strong model's encrypted trace into a weaker sibling from the same provider makes it decode and emit the trace verbatim in plaintext. Demonstrated across Anthropic, OpenAI and Google; the day's top research story on HN too (381 pts, 151 comments).
- **[Matryoshka Language Model Suites](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09703)** (Godey, Artzi) — Trains 500M/1.5B/3B models as nested sub-models inside one architecture, matching independent baselines with 36% less training compute and speeding speculative decoding 14–26% because the draft model already lives inside the verifier.
- **[SWE-Bench ProMax](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09802)** (Shi, Xu, Fu, Zeng et al.) — A 170-instance multilingual code-refactoring benchmark built on the audit finding that ~60% of unsolved SWE-bench Verified instances have flawed tests and gold patches can be verbatim memorized. Specs rewritten from scratch, test suites manually de-flawed.
- **[RynnValue](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09853)** (Huang, Zhang, Hou et al.) — Replaces preference supervision in robotic value models with temporal distance (cost-to-go to a language goal), scaling to 7,000+ hours and ~3M clips with no preference labels while beating fully preference-supervised SOTA on RBM-EVAL-OOD.
- **[Measuring the Wrong Thing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09624)** (Luo, Deng, Qiu, Cheng et al.) — Internal harmfulness scores anti-rank successful jailbreaks: AUROC falls 0.936 → 0.803 on wrapped prompts while actual harmful generation rises 0.05 → 0.27. Safety classifiers spend their false-positive budget on attacks that would have failed anyway.
- Also notable: **[Towards Expert-level Medical AI for Real-time Video Consultations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09861)** (AMIE Video rated at clinician level by evaluators), **[Rethinking Self-Evolving Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09629)** (open-ended optimization wins 12/14 comparisons at 34.3% of the token budget, but hits a capability floor for weaker optimizers), and **[Activation Probes Surface Code-Security Signals that the Model's Output Misses](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09643)** (a linear probe ranks vulnerable code above its fix on 61–67% of held-out CVEs, beating the model's own CoT verdicts).
