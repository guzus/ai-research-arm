## Breaking News

### OpenAI Reveals Astra, Its Next Major Model, After It Solves Ten Long-Standing Math Problems
**Date:** August 1-2, 2026 | **Sources:** [OpenAI — Ten advances in mathematics and theoretical computer science](https://openai.com/index/ten-advances-in-mathematics/), [SiliconANGLE — OpenAI's Astra solves 10 long-open math problems and publishes the proofs](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/02/openais-astra-solves-10-long-open-math-problems-publishes-proofs/), [BleepingComputer — OpenAI teases Astra, its next major AI model](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/openai-teases-astra-its-next-major-ai-model-after-it-solves-10-long-standing-math-problems/), [TheNextWeb — OpenAI says its next model, Astra, has solved ten open problems in mathematics](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-astra-model-ten-math-proofs-non-sofic-groups)
- OpenAI disclosed that an internal, unreleased version of its next major model, Astra, produced genuine resolutions — not incremental progress — on ten long-standing open problems spanning high-dimensional geometry, coding theory, arithmetic circuit complexity, group theory, quantum complexity, lattice cryptography, and extremal combinatorics, none of which had seen progress on their central result in at least a decade. The headline result is the first explicit construction of a non-sofic group, resolving a question in group theory open since Gromov introduced the concept of soficity in 1999; other results include new sphere-packing bounds down to the Cohn-Elkies threshold. OpenAI published a 249-page manuscript collection plus Lean 4 formal proof certificates for all ten results on GitHub under Apache 2.0, with a reported "sorry" count of zero (no unproven steps), and put the total token cost at roughly $2,000 at GPT-5.6 Sol API rates. Fields Medalist Timothy Gowers said he would recommend one of the proofs for a top journal without hesitation.
- **Why it matters, and the pushback:** It's the most concrete evidence yet of a frontier model doing formally-verified, novel mathematical research rather than reciting or lightly extending known results. AI critic Gary Marcus published a same-week rebuttal ("OpenAI's amazing — but vastly oversold — new model Astra") arguing OpenAI disclosed no methodology (verification process, human involvement, or failed attempts), that math's built-in symbolic verifiability and synthetic-data abundance make it a poor proxy for open-ended reasoning generally, and that framing this as possibly "the most significant day in the history of mathematics" is unsupported by one internal run.

## New Model Releases

### Microsoft Tests MAI-Realtime, a Full-Duplex Voice Model, in Hidden Preview
**Date:** August 2, 2026 | **Sources:** [TestingCatalog — Exclusive: Microsoft tests new MAI-Realtime voice model](https://www.testingcatalog.com/exclusive-microsoft-tests-new-mai-realtime-voice-model/), [Cryptobriefing — Microsoft previews MAI-Realtime bidirectional voice model](https://cryptobriefing.com/microsoft-mai-realtime-bidirectional-voice-model/)
- TestingCatalog reported an exclusive early preview of MAI-Realtime, an upcoming bidirectional (full-duplex) voice model that appeared as a hidden early-access entry in Microsoft's internal MAI Playground. A small partner group can test two voices ("Victoria" and "Grant") with configurable interruption handling, automatic mid-conversation language switching across 17 languages, and tool use including web search. Microsoft describes it as feeling "much more natural" than its existing MAI Voice 2 model. No public launch date or benchmark results have been disclosed; this is an internal/partner preview, not a general release.
