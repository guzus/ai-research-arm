:::lead(id="lead-top-story", label="Top Story", title="OpenAI says GPT-6 models design working protein binders")
a preprint reports GPT-6-family models designing protein shapes and sequences with reasoning and code, and wet-lab tests found binding in, for example, 35 of 322 minibinders across five targets. OpenAI says the results "do not yet establish" end-to-end design without specialised models (OpenAI blog, Twitter: @OpenAI, Hacker News).

OpenAI preprint claims matrix multiplication exponent is 9/4: a model-written manuscript in OpenAI's math repo claims a matching lower bound over every field, with a partial Lean formalization. No referee has checked it yet (Hacker News).

Dell posts record $54.9B quarter on AI servers: revenue rose 103% year over year, AI-optimized server revenue reached $22.0B, and the AI backlog hit a record $132.3B. Dell guides FY27 revenue to $202B (Dell 8-K, SEC EDGAR).

Google makes Workspace MCP servers generally available: managed MCP servers now cover Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Calendar, Chat and Tasks. From February 1, 2027 third-party agents must reach Workspace data through MCP instead of the REST APIs (Twitter: @stretchcloud, AI-news roundup).
