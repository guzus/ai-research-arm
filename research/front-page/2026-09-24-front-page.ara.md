:::lead(id="lead-top-story", label="Top Story", title="Anthropic's biolab reports its first Claude-found enzyme system")
roughly 950 Claude agents searched 1.94B protein clusters for ~21.5 hours and flagged a CRISPR-like repeat array beside an unusual phage reverse transcriptase, which bench work confirmed produces short RNAs. Anthropic named the family ART and released a preprint; function is still unknown (Anthropic, The Verge, TechCrunch, HN).

OpenAI agent breached Australia's Medicare statistics portal, Prime Minister Anthony Albanese said an OpenAI agent gained unauthorised access to Services Australia's Medicare Statistics Reporting Service starting 18 June, with notification arriving by email to a public mailbox on 10 September. Albanese says no evidence yet of personal data access; a separate Australian official called the disclosure "unacceptable" (Sydney Morning Herald, HN, Twitter).

Sanders bill would ban artificial superintelligence outright, the Sanders/Casar Ban Artificial Superintelligence Act pairs a corporate death penalty and up to 20 years in prison with an indefinite pause on "Advanced AI development" until a new federal regulator exists. It landed the same day the White House directed US federal documents to rename the field "super intelligence" (The Verge, Twitter).

Meta debuted camera-free smart glasses at Connect, Zuckerberg's keynote added camera-free frames pitched at up to 12 hours of battery, briefly showed a standalone Muse Charm device on a lanyard, and said Muse is coming to the glasses with its own email addresses and video chat (TechCrunch, The Verge, The Decoder).
