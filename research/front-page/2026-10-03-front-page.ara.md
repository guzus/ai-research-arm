:::lead(id="lead-top-story", label="Top Story", title="Anthropic sets a 14 October investor session")
Bloomberg reports that Anthropic has invited institutional investors to question its executives. That could lead to a roadshow as soon as the week of 9 November and trading before Thanksgiving at $1.8–2T. Anthropic has not confirmed, and The Information says some Wall Street investors think ~$1.5T is closer to fair value (Bloomberg and The Information via Twitter).

Google's Suncatcher TPU satellite reaches orbit: Google confirmed that its Project Suncatcher prototype, built with Planet, launched on SpaceX's Transporter-18 and has made contact. It will gather data on how TPUs handle launch stress, radiation and thermal extremes. Google calls it the first step of a moonshot, not a data centre (@GoogleAI, Google Research blog, HN).

Trump expected to name Jay Clayton AI czar: CNN reports that the Director of National Intelligence would take over the White House AI post while keeping his intelligence role. That would move AI policy into the national-security portfolio David Sacks left in March. No White House announcement had appeared by 22:00 UTC (CNN via @HadasGold).

Meta publishes six Muse Spark mathematics papers: mathematicians used Muse Spark 1.1 and 1.2 through the ordinary meta.ai chat to make progress on open problems, including a counterexample in non-associative algebra. Each paper marks which passages humans or the AI mainly drafted. The announcement comes from Meta alone, with no outside expert assessment yet (@AIatMeta).
:::
