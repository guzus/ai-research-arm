:::lead(id="lead-top-story", label="Top Story", title="OpenAI proposed shared perplexity limits for labs")
chief scientist Jakub Pachocki's essay "Perplexity and Intelligence" publishes OpenAI's pretraining log-perplexity curve. It asks frontier labs to agree a common limit on that metric and stay under it until monitoring reaches a concrete milestone, such as a successor to chain-of-thought monitors. Sam Altman backed it. No eval corpus and no numeric limit are named (Twitter: @merettm, @sama).

Meta's Alexandr Wang says superintelligence is arriving: Meta's Chief AI Officer published an essay saying intelligence is no longer the bottleneck to superhuman AI. He writes that humans are unlikely to keep control under "fully unconstrained recursive self improvement" and that the industry must leave the "default path." It names no mechanism or Meta commitment (Twitter: @alexandrwang).

Study puts Anthropic's 2028 power at 87% gas: a Better Data Center Project case study estimates 87% gas and 11% coal for the sites where Anthropic has contracted compute, and says Anthropic has no direct U.S. renewable commitments. The thread drew 522 Hacker News comments. These are the authors' own models, not Anthropic disclosures (HN, betterdatacenterproject.com).

OpenRouter dropped three providers after data incidents: the router off-boarded three inference providers and pulled 125 endpoints from Zero Data Retention eligibility after researchers' canary tests. Novita and Phala posted their own incident notices (HN, openrouter.ai).
