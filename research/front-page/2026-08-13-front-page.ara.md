:::lead(id="lead-top-story", label="Top Story", title="Three frontier models shipped inside 45 minutes")
xAI's Grok 4.6, Alibaba's open-weight Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B and DeepSeek-V4-Pro-0813 all landed in the same afternoon window, and every capability claim at launch traced back to the vendor publishing it (Twitter monitor 16:00 UTC, Hacker News, The Decoder).

Grok 4.6 reprices the frontier tier at $2/$6, versus $10/$50 for Claude Fable 5 Max, while scoring 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index against Fable 5 Max's 62 and taking first place on the separate Agentic Index (Artificial Analysis via Twitter monitor 19:00 UTC, The Decoder).

Frontier-grade work got 4.6× cheaper in 90 days, Parametric CAD Bench re-ran the same 100 tasks and found the cost to clear a 0.8 score fell from $170.00 to $36.59, with Claude Opus 5 taking the top score at 0.906 and Grok 4.6 reaching 96.9% of it for 61% of the cost (@gNucleusAI via Twitter monitor 21:00 UTC).

Nvidia will backstop 25% of a project's cost, and the WSJ reports Jensen Huang characterising that support inside the ~$500B financing package as residual-value guarantees — meaning Nvidia underwrites the resale floor of hardware whose obsolescence it schedules (WSJ via @rohanpaulai, @asharma).
:::
