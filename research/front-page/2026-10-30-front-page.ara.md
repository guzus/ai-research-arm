:::lead(id="lead-top-story", label="Top Story", title="Anthropic named as SpaceX's biggest customer")
Bloomberg identifies Anthropic as the anonymous "Customer B" behind 34.5% of SpaceX's $14.575B Q3 revenue, paid for rented Nvidia GPU capacity in Memphis. SpaceX's filing does not name the customer, but the figures match the up-to-$120.5B SpaceX commitment in Anthropic's S-1 (Bloomberg via @rohanpaulai, @AndrewCurran).

Mistral open-sources 1T-parameter Mistral Large 4: "Le Chonk" is a multimodal MoE with 52B active parameters, released on Hugging Face with day-0 transformers and llama.cpp support. Mistral claims #1 among open models on Cognition's FrontierCode; no closed-model comparison yet (@MistralAI, @mervenoyann, @UnslothAI).

AWS grows 39% to $184B run rate: Amazon's Q3 operating income rose to $28.1B, AWS operating income hit $17.8B (from $11.4B), and Andy Jassy projects AWS growth above 40% in both 2027 and 2028 (Amazon 8-K).

Samsung says 2027 HBM is sold out: Samsung's memory EVP said prices settled "at our target levels" and 60–70% of capacity will likely sit under long-term agreements through 2030, echoing SK hynix a day earlier (Samsung Q3 call via @jukan05).
