:::lead(id="lead-top-story", label="Top Story", title="DeepSeek nears a $12B-plus funding round")
Bloomberg reports the lab is close to raising at least 80B yuan (about $12B), well above its ~50B-yuan target, at a valuation of about 500B yuan (~$71B). The money would fund a 160,000-chip Huawei Ascend cluster ahead of a planned early-2027 IPO. The round has not closed (Bloomberg via @rohanpaulai, @jukan05, @Nusodinkelmi).

OpenAI courts UAE funds at $1.4T valuation: OpenAI is reportedly in talks with Abu Dhabi's MGX and other Gulf funds to anchor a $30B raise at $1.4T pre-money, with BlackRock also in discussions. These are talks, not signed terms (Bloomberg via @rohanpaulai).

Reflection AI unveils Beam, a 501B open model: the sparse MoE has 23B active parameters and was pretrained on 23.8T tokens on GB300s. Apache 2.0 weights are promised "later this month", so it is a preview, not a weight drop (Reflection, TechCrunch, MarkTechPost, Latent Space, Hacker News).

Meta and Microsoft cut back internal Claude use: The Information reports that Meta's Claude Code users fell from ~60K to ~30K and that Microsoft cut its expected internal Anthropic spend by more than a third. Both companies are pushing staff to their own coding tools (The Information, The Decoder).
:::
