reportedly taking most of Replit's AI spend at 30%+ off a $100M-a-year deal, while Rippling's coding mix moved from 57% Anthropic in May to 52% OpenAI now. The figures come from a single outlet, and a pricing timeline shows both labs cutting list prices by up to 80% in a few months (The Information via @markk, The Decoder, minimaxir.com).

Elvis rights holders sue Anthropic over 274 songs, seeking the $150,000 statutory maximum per work, about $41M. The case comes from the same lawyer who filed Reservoir Media's Oct 21 complaint, and it adds to music-publisher litigation that has run since 2023 (Billboard, Rolling Stone, @AndrewCurran).

Musk says lenders have shut out neoclouds, adding that SpaceX still closed $40B of GPU debt because it is investment grade. He predicts most of the 200+ neoclouds will fold or merge. He is talking his own book, and no independent credit data backs the claim (@elonmusk, @rohanpaulai).

Altman reportedly lists ways to slow dangerous AI, telling staff the options include international regulation, lab mergers, or letting one company dominate compute. This comes from one paywalled report, and he did not say which option he favours (The Information).
