"Le Chonk" is a natively multimodal MoE with 49B active parameters. It is on Mistral's API now, and open weights are promised for the end of October. Mistral pitches it at cybersecurity work that closed US models refuse, and it led Hacker News with 1,543 points and 944 comments (Mistral, TechCrunch, The Decoder, MarkTechPost, Simon Willison, Hacker News).

OpenAI publishes 722 math manuscripts from a private model: the 722 manuscripts fall into 372 result families. They came from about 4,000 problems posed to an internal frontier model, at roughly three hours of ChatGPT Pro thinking compute per result. Papers and Lean proofs are on GitHub, and the release followed advice from an Institute for Advanced Study advisory group. No outside mathematician has vetted the results yet (OpenAI, The Verge, WIRED via @AndrewCurran, Hacker News).

Anthropic opens Mythos 5.1 to verified security pros: an expanded Cyber Verification Program gives vetted professionals access to Claude Mythos 5.1, Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5. New tiers cover authorized penetration testing and red-teaming. Anthropic has not published the eligibility rules (Anthropic, @AndrewCurran).

Google ships Nano Banana 2.1 and EmbeddingGemma 2: Google says the new image model beats its old Pro model at about a quarter of the price ($0.034 vs $0.134 per image). EmbeddingGemma 2 is an Apache-2.0, 740M multimodal embedder built on Gemma 4 (@philschmid, The Decoder, MarkTechPost, Hacker News).
