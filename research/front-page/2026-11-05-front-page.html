An upcoming cyber model scanned about 7% of the Linux kernel, dynamically reproduced 1,724 findings, and generated 948 working local privilege-escalation exploits for $1.21 million in API spend, or $1,279 per LPE. Hundreds of reports are going to maintainers via Patch the Planet (OpenAI, HN).

Meta acquired coding startup Conductor: Founder Charlie Holtz will work with chief AI officer Alexandr Wang and Superintelligence Labs product head Nat Friedman. Conductor Cloud goes read-only on November 13 and shuts on November 20; terms were not disclosed (TechCrunch, The Verge, HN).

Moonshot published Kimi K3.1 Pro weights: The ungated Hugging Face checkpoint is a 2.8T MoE with 104B active parameters, MXFP4 weights, and a 1M-token window.
