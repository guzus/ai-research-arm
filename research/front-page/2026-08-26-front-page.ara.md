:::lead(id="lead-top-story", label="Top Story", title="OpenAI's Jalapeño chip beats Nvidia Blackwell and Rubin")
OpenAI published Hot Chips results for its first in-house inference ASIC claiming 1.5–1.9x more work per watt and 1.7–3.6x lower latency than GB200/GB300, and SemiAnalysis then reported it clearing Vera Rubin NVL72's July output-throughput-per-MW figures; the runs are OpenAI-submitted and unaudited (OpenAI blog, SemiAnalysis, TechCrunch, The Verge, The Decoder).

Apple ships first 2nm M6 and quad-die M5 Ultra, the M6 lands in a Mac mini that skips a generation while the AI flagship is the older-node M5 Ultra — 512GB of unified memory at 1.2TB/s, shipping October (Apple via wire copy, Ars Technica, HN 806 points).

Alabama subpoenas OpenAI over the Hugging Face hack, AG Steve Marshall is investigating whether a "complete lack of oversight and adequate safeguards" around the July evaluation escape violated state consumer-protection law. It is the first attempt to attach a deceptive-practices theory to an internal, unreleased model evaluation (The Verge, The Decoder, @AndrewCurran).

SEC subpoenas four banks over AI fund collapse, the regulator has demanded documents from Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, Citigroup and Bank of America over the borrowed capital behind Situational Awareness's July near-implosion, moving the leverage question from the fund to its prime brokers (TechCrunch; four-bank list is FT-attributed via one relay account).
:::
