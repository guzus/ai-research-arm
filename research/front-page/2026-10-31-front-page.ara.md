:::lead(id="lead-top-story", label="Top Story", title="Intel plunges 28.6% as AI hoards memory")
Intel's Q4 guide ($15–16B revenue, 38.0% non-GAAP gross margin vs 44.1%) tied to AI-driven memory prices dragged the chip index down about 7%. AMD fell 10.9% and Nvidia 4.2%, while Amazon recovered from about −7% premarket to close −1.4% (closing recaps via @DobbinsDownload, @alldaystocks, @WealthCoachMak).

Reddit falls 29% on lost Google traffic: Reddit's CEO said Google's AI Mode "does not drive traffic", with search referrals down more than 1M a week in October. It is the first hard publisher data showing AI answers cutting referrals; Reddit is making login the default (@kimmonismus, @rohanpaulai).

Anthropic renames Max 20x to Claude Ultra: The $200 tier drops the 5-hour rate limit for verified accounts, but Anthropic confirmed the weekly cap stays at the old 10x-Pro level. That changes how usage is paced, not how much you get (@alexalbert, @claudeai via @theo).

Google runs a prompt on an orbiting TPU: The Project Suncatcher prototype answered a prompt sent from Earth, which Sundar Pichai called a "hello world" for space-based compute. Thermal, radiation and downlink data have not been published (@testingcatalog, @rohanpaulai).
:::
