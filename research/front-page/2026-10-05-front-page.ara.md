:::lead(id="lead-top-story", label="Top Story", title="White House launches the \"Super Intelligence Force\"")
the official @WhiteHouse account posted Trump's statement creating the SIF to coordinate federal efforts so "America continues to lead the World in Super Intelligence". Per the WSJ and The Decoder, DNI Jay Clayton will lead it, but the statement itself names no officials and gives no budget or charter (Twitter/@WhiteHouse, WSJ via @AndrewCurran, TechCrunch, The Decoder).

OpenAI safety lead quits, calling its culture "broken": David Robinson wrote the safety reports that accompany OpenAI's major launches. His resignation essay in The Atlantic argues that "trial and error" deployment guarantees failures at growing scale. On HN it drew 711 comments (TechCrunch, The Verge, The Decoder, Hacker News).

Reflection AI nears an open-weight model aimed at Chinese rivals: Axios reports Reflection has paid $150M a month since July for compute on xAI's Colossus, on top of a $1B Nebius deal. No size, benchmarks or date have been disclosed (Axios via @AndrewCurran).

Altman says the world should "accept some bad things": he told Politico this was the price of AI's benefits, on the same day the SIF launched and Robinson's essay circulated (Politico via @AndrewCurran).
:::
