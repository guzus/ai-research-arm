"Recursive self improvement is here" was the most-repeated framing of the cycle — and it rests on a single sentence from one unnamed OpenAI employee in one outlet, describing automated sweeps, launches, and evaluation. That is an orchestration pipeline large labs have run for years, not a system directing its own research. The falsification test exists and nobody has run it. @teortaxesTex proposed the right one: publish the unambiguous problem statements plus hashes of the solutions from OpenAI's "more than 100 open mathematics problems" claim, so outsiders can attempt replication before disclosure. Until then it is an unaudited vendor count. Notably, OpenAI's own researcher Noam Brown told Dwarkesh Patel the same week that the lab's Navier-Stokes result came overwhelmingly from one strong model thinking for a long time — not the 10,000-agent swarm — and that recursive self-improvement is likelier to be a ~3× lab speedup than a 100× overnight explosion.
