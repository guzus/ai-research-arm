:::lead(id="lead-top-story", label="Top Story", title="OpenAI pipeline found 948 kernel exploits")
An upcoming cyber model scanned about 7% of the Linux kernel, dynamically reproduced 1,724 findings, and generated 948 working local privilege-escalation exploits for $1.21 million in API spend, or $1,279 per LPE. Hundreds of reports are going to maintainers via Patch the Planet (OpenAI, HN).

Meta acquired coding startup Conductor: Founder Charlie Holtz will work with chief AI officer Alexandr Wang and Superintelligence Labs product head Nat Friedman. Conductor Cloud goes read-only on November 13 and shuts on November 20; terms were not disclosed (TechCrunch, The Verge, HN).

Moonshot published Kimi K3.1 Pro weights: The ungated Hugging Face checkpoint is a 2.8T MoE with 104B active parameters, MXFP4 weights, and a 1M-token window. Moonshot's own table puts it at 65.7 on Terminal-Bench 4.0, one point behind Claude Opus 5.5; hosts earning over $20 million from model-as-a-service must negotiate a separate license (Hugging Face, @KimiMoonshot, HN).

Texas voters backed a data-center pause: Democrat Gina Hinojosa unseated Gov. Greg Abbott after pledging an immediate moratorium, and The Information counts incoming or leading governors in 12 states who have called for a pause. A national exit poll found 57% of voters think AI will do more harm than good and 76% oppose a data center nearby (CNBC, NBC/SSRS, The Information, Bluesky).
:::
