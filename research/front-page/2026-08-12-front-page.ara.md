:::lead(id="lead-top-story", label="Top Story", title="The AI buildout printed its first hard numbers")
CoreWeave, Supermicro and Lumentum all reported after the US close and every layer beat — yet the equity response was muted, with Supermicro moving just 5.3% on a guide $16B above consensus (Twitter pulse, SEC 8-K filings).

Nvidia assembled a $500B Wall Street financing package, partnering with BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs, Apollo and KKR to turn AI compute into an investable asset class — with an option to backstop up to 25% of each project (Bloomberg, CNBC, The Information via Twitter).

Anthropic will watermark all Claude text output worldwide, driven by EU AI Act Article 50, and staff confirmed a public text-detection API is coming; the chosen demo case, checking whether a pull request came from Claude Code, is the low-entropy case the technique handles worst (TechCrunch, The Verge, The Register).

OpenAI's COO of eight years is leaving, Brad Lightcap announced his departure the same day Bloomberg detailed a ~$7B employee tender at an $852B mark that OpenAI itself funded; the company's only dedicated ethicist also quit (TechCrunch, The Verge, FT via HN).
