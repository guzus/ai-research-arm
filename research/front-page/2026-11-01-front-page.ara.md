:::lead(id="lead-top-story", label="Top Story", title="Wiz counts nine AI platforms breached in \"Black October\"")
Northflank told customers to assume every stored secret was decrypted, and Wiz lists Fireworks AI, Railway, Warp, OpenCode and Lightning AI among nine platforms hit this month. Stolen AI keys are being resold, and two keys alone ran up more than $100K in Bedrock calls (Wiz via @2logics, @AndrewCurran; single secondhand summary of the Wiz numbers).

OpenAI's mega-round shrinks to about $20B: The FT reports the target fell from $30B at $1.4T to roughly $20B at $1.25T, with UAE investors including MGX expected to lead. A WSJ-sourced report says only about $11B has come in so far (FT via @noxflux, TechCrunch, The Decoder, WSJ via @techtactician).

Blackstone expects junk rating for Anthropic: The FT reports the anchor investor in Anthropic's $63B chip-financing deal now expects a double-B rating after the November listing, down from an earlier investment-grade view. No agency has published a rating yet (FT via @DailyStackHQ, TechCrunch).

xAI turns on Grok text watermarking: xAI announced invisible watermarking for Grok text on Oct 31, appearing to roll out globally from Nov 7 with an opt-out page. By @AndrewCurran's count, North American ChatGPT is now the only major text model without one (@AndrewCurran, @markk).
:::
