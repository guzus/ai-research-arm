It was HN's top story of the day at 620 points and 539 comments (@kimmonismus, @ThomWolf, MarkTechPost, The Decoder, HN).

OpenCode breach drains users' stored provider keys: unauthorized access to a legacy Console system exposed OpenAI, Anthropic and Google keys saved in workspaces, and users report drained balances. OpenCode says at least 1.4% of Console users were affected. The local CLI is not affected (OpenCode statement via @testingcatalog, HN 579 pts).

Replit makes OpenAI its default over Anthropic: CEO Amjad Masad walked back his "no longer working with Anthropic" remark, saying Claude stays selectable but OpenAI now powers Replit Agent by default (@amasad, TechCrunch).

Microsoft reportedly pays Anthropic $2B+ a year for Copilot: The Information says this holds even as Microsoft moves more Copilot traffic to OpenAI to protect margins. The same outlet reports Anthropic's growth is slowing ahead of its IPO (The Information).
