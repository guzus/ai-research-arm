:::lead(id="lead-top-story", label="Top Story", title="Google's Gemini 4 Carbon tops Artificial Analysis v5")
The new frontier model, released just 15 days after Argon, scores 59 on AA Intelligence Index v5 and #1 on Text Arena (1528). Access is limited to a "Fairwind" cohort of cyber defenders for now, at an intro price of $2/$10 per Mtok that matches Argon (Google DeepMind, Logan Kilpatrick, HN, The Verge, Ars Technica).

Google Cloud more than doubles to $30.7B: Alphabet Q3 revenue rose 25% to $127.6B and Cloud grew 103%. Search and YouTube ads missed estimates, and free cash flow reportedly went about −$9B on $51.4B of capex (Alphabet 8-K, @EmmanuelInvest, TechCrunch).

Microsoft discloses Azure revenue for the first time: Azure brought in $33.2B, up 48%. Microsoft Cloud reached $63.9B (+30%), total revenue was $92.3B (+19%), and Microsoft booked about $10B of revenue from OpenAI. The stock reportedly rose about 4% after hours (Microsoft 8-K, TechCrunch, @rohanpaulai).

SK hynix says memory is sold out through 2029: Q3 operating profit was KRW 83.3T on KRW 104.6T of revenue, beating consensus. Five-year deposit-backed contracts now cover more than half of 2027–28 capacity, and Samsung reportedly expects 2027 to be tighter still (SK hynix filing, @jukan05).
