:::lead(id="lead-top-story", label="Top Story", title="AMD data center sales nearly doubled")
Data Center segment revenue was a record $8.5B, up 97% year over year, on EPYC CPUs and the Instinct MI450 Series launch. Company revenue was a record $13.4B, up 45%, with non-GAAP EPS up 74% (AMD 8-K EX-99.1).

Weight-edited Qwen checkpoint is public: The small Qwen model GPT-6 Astra repaired by editing 0.016% of its weights is on Hugging Face, and a rebuild from the published logs scored 51.0% on MATH-500 versus the paper's 48.1%. Simon Willison separately found Qwen 3.8 27B can interpret model weights on a laptop (@rikiyatakehi, @akhaliq, simonwillison.net).

Replit tells firms to sandbox agents: After Bloomberg reported Goldman Sachs restricted most staff from AI coding tools, Replit published an advisory that agents should run in organization-controlled cloud environments, not on employee laptops. Replit also sells those environments, and the Bloomberg wording is a restriction on coding tools, not a blanket ban on bankers and traders (@lhchavez, @amasad, The Decoder).

OpenAI raises bug bounties to $172K: Exceptional findings can now earn up to $172,000 including bonuses, and critical-finding payouts more than quadrupled, including retroactively for the past 30 days. The company is also planning an internal security sprint with Cathedral Systems (OpenAI blog, The Decoder).
