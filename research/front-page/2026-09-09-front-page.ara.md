:::quote(label="Quote of the Day")
"The 50% is modeled cost rather than throughput; per-chip speed is only about 5% ahead." — @a2ycapitalinc, decomposing SemiAnalysis's claim that Google's Ironwood TPU delivers 50% better performance per dollar than Blackwell Ultra. The reading puts the realistic range at 19–34% and argues the headline number materializes only once SemiAnalysis substitutes its own hourly TPU cost assumption. The account is anonymous with no engagement, so this is an unverified reading rather than an audit — but the claims are specific and falsifiable, which is more than the headline offers. Both numbers can be true at once: a 5% silicon gap and a 50% cost gap is exactly what you get when the cost model, not the chip, is doing the work. Runner-up, on the day's dominant story, from Hugging Face co-founder Thomas Wolf: "a glimpse of the future we'll get in science research with these dominating players playing marketing games hurtful for the real scientific community" — while explicitly adding that the other side should be heard.
