:::lead(id="lead-top-story", label="Top Story", title="Google rewires its AI leadership in one hour")
Demis Hassabis moved from Google DeepMind CEO to Chair of GDM and Alphabet Chief Scientist while CTO Koray Kavukcuoglu took operational control reporting to Sundar Pichai. Minutes later Jeff Dean announced his departure after 27 years, taking Sanjay Ghemawat, Oriol Vinyals and Quoc Le to found Discovery Loop (The Verge, The Decoder, TechCrunch, @JeffDean).

Meta ships Muse Code, its first coding agent, on a new Muse Spark 1.2 priced at $1.25/$4.25 per million tokens with a cheaper tier for developers who let Meta train on their code. Independent readings three hours later put it behind Claude Opus 5 on both benchmarks it launched against (TechCrunch, @AIatMeta, @rohanpaulai).

The FT sizes Anthropic's chip financing at $150bn, describing a Google-arranged programme running through Broadcom, Apollo, Blackstone, Morgan Stanley and crypto miners. It is a financing structure rather than a purchase order, so the accelerators reach Anthropic without its balance sheet carrying them (FT via @trevornoren).

Anthropic starts hiring a chip design team, the last frontier lab renting all of its accelerators saying on the record that it intends to design its own silicon, at $320k–$485k per engineer. The company insists AWS, Google, NVIDIA and AMD remain central (TechCrunch).
