it is now on the paid Gemini API, AI Studio, the Gemini app (Pro/Ultra), AI Mode in Search and Antigravity, with up to 1M output tokens. Independent reviews are split, though. Every's refactoring benchmark scored it 21% against 81–85% for GPT-6 Astra, Fable 5.1 and the Claude 5.5 models (@GoogleDeepMind, @OfficialLoganK, @danshipper, The Verge, Ars Technica, HN 899 points).

OpenAI found a hidden recorder in its office: OpenAI says its models helped trace the device to an employee, who was fired, and the FBI made an arrest. OpenAI says it has no indication that weights, source code or customer data were taken. Nothing on the record alleges a motive or a foreign link (@OpenAINewsroom, The Verge, HN 335 points).

Korean AI-memory stocks crashed on Tuesday: the KOSPI fell about 6–7%, SK Hynix ~10% and Samsung ~8%. Traders blamed CXMT's 4F² DRAM claim and Broadcom CEO Hock Tan calling AI investors gamblers, but the selling started before either. The figures come from market accounts, not exchange data (@scalethesis, @EllisInvest, @kauprio).

Anthropic replaced git after a 12× jump in merges: it went from ~2,500 merged changes a day in May to more than 30,000 in September, then built an in-house VCS ("Scion") and moved its monorepo off git in five months. The numbers are self-reported (@AnthropicAI, Simon Willison, HN 472 points).
