:::lead(id="lead-top-story", label="Top Story", title="Anthropic also claims a Claude model refuted ETH")
a paper says an internal Claude research model found the core 3-SAT algorithm on October 16 using 14M output tokens and no human input. That makes Anthropic the second frontier lab within a day to claim the Exponential Time Hypothesis is false. Neither proof has passed outside review, and only OpenAI's is covered by the Williams/Impagliazzo/Paturi review so far (@alpoge via @AndrewCurran, TechCrunch, HN).

Anthropic becomes its own drug developer: Anthropic disclosed that in July it quietly bought Mithril Medicine, a biotech with one employee, and that it runs Claude-assisted programs with six life-sciences partners. The headline result is a preclinical mouse study, and Anthropic says human trials could come as early as late 2028 (@AnthropicAI, @AndrewCurran).

Nvidia puts $3B into Meta's record raise: Meta raised its fundraising target to $97.5B: $25B of common stock priced at a reported $625 a share, $12.5B of convertible preferred and up to $60B through an at-the-market program. Nvidia, AMD and Broadcom expressed nonbinding interest in up to $5B of the common combined, and Nvidia says Meta will expand its Vera Rubin deployments (CNBC, The Decoder, Nvidia via @testingcatalog).

Axiom machine-checks the Classification of Finite Simple Groups: Axiom says agent swarms produced about 40M lines of Lean in 24 days to formalize the proof, which runs to about 15,000 pages. No independent audit of the artifact has been published yet (@axiommathai, HN).
:::
