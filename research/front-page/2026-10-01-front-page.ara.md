:::lead(id="lead-top-story", label="Top Story", title="Google unveiled Gemini 4 Argon behind a gate")
its first frontier model in over seven months ships first to government and trusted cyber defenders via the Fairwind Program, with a 1M-token output limit (up from 64K) and $2/$10 per Mtok introductory pricing that rises to $4/$20 afterwards (Google DeepMind, The Verge, Ars Technica, TechCrunch, The Decoder, HN 381 pts).

Reuters says the FTC will probe frontier labs, the agency plans formal information demands and compelled executive testimony from Anthropic, OpenAI and the evaluator METR over consumer risks. The report rests on one unnamed source, and the FTC has not commented on the record (Reuters via Twitter, The Decoder).

OpenAI tied a distillation campaign to Moonshot, saying a core cluster of attempts to extract its models' hidden reasoning came from individuals associated with the Kimi developer. Relayed figures put it at 16,000 attempts from more than 4,000 users over two days in late July (OpenAI, Twitter relays).

Micron posted $54.2B quarterly revenue, nearly five times the year-earlier quarter, with $37.7B GAAP net income and $133.2B for fiscal 2026. CEO Sanjay Mehrotra says he expects "an even stronger fiscal 2027" (SEC 8-K EX-99.1).
