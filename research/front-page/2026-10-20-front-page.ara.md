:::lead(id="lead-top-story", label="Top Story", title="DHS orders agencies to purge DeepSeek-derived products")
A FASCSA order tells civilian federal agencies to remove and stop buying DeepSeek products and third-party services "developed using DeepSeek implementations," including self-hosted weights. The practical burden lands on government software vendors, who now have to prove which open-weight models are in their stacks (Twitter: @AndrewCurran, @rohanpaulai, @MartinSzerment).

MiniMax launches M3.1 Flash at $0.15 per million: The former anonymous "Space Bunny" is a 428B-total / ~23B-active MoE with 1M context, priced at $0.15/$0.60 per 1M tokens. Weights are promised for Oct 23. MiniMax's own chart shows it trailing DeepSeek V4.1 Flash on four of six shared benchmarks (Twitter, MarkTechPost, The Decoder, HN 279 pts).

Neocloud stress spreads from Nscale to CoreWeave: Reuters reports Microsoft ended its deal for Nscale's grid-delayed Essex site. CoreWeave bonds trade around +1,000 bps over Treasuries, and Gartner warns more than $3.5T of AI infrastructure could need write-downs by 2030. Yet Nscale is reportedly in talks with Anthropic on a second $45B compute deal (Twitter, The Decoder, The Verge, TechCrunch).

Anthropic's public-benefit fund draws a Musk counter-pledge: Anthropic is committing 5% of its shares to a company-owned LLC fund. Musk said he would instead give "one of my SpaceX shares to every kid in America," with no mechanism given, and the WSJ flagged an obscure trust over Anthropic's IPO (TechCrunch, The Decoder, @elonmusk, HN).
