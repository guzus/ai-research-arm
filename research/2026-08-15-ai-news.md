## Breaking News

### Anthropic Investors Target $2 Trillion Valuation for October IPO
**Date:** August 13-14, 2026 | **Sources:** [Fortune — Anthropic reportedly plans a $2 trillion IPO in October](https://fortune.com/2026/08/13/anthropic-ipo-2-trillion-october-largest-ever-spacex/), [Fortune — Anthropic's $2 trillion math problem](https://fortune.com/2026/08/14/anthropics-2-trillion-problem-its-underlying-business-is-nowhere-near-the-ipo-valuation-it-wants/), [PYMNTS — Anthropic Could Seek $2 Trillion Valuation in Record IPO](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/anthropic-could-seek-2-trillion-valuation-in-record-ipo/)
- Roughly half a dozen Anthropic backers say the company's investors are targeting a $2 trillion-plus valuation for an IPO as early as October, which would surpass SpaceX's $1.77 trillion June debut as the largest IPO in history. The figure rests on projected annualized revenue reaching $100-120 billion by year-end, versus the $47 billion Anthropic reported in May — more than 10x growth in seven months. Anthropic executives themselves have reportedly not fixed a valuation target even in private conversations; the $2 trillion number originates with backers, not the company.
- **Why it matters:** it would make Anthropic's public debut the largest ever, and a follow-up Fortune analysis (Aug 14) argues the underlying business metrics don't yet obviously support that price — a live tension between AI-bubble skeptics and investors betting on continued hypergrowth.

## New Model Releases

### Alibaba Releases Qwen3.8-27B Open-Weight Multimodal Model
**Date:** August 15, 2026 | **Sources:** [officechai — Alibaba Releases Qwen 3.8-27B, Beats Muse Glimmer 30B](https://officechai.com/miscellaneous/alibaba-releases-qwen-3-8-27b-beats-muse-glimmer-30b-on-many-benchmarks/), [GuruFocus — Alibaba Releases Open Source Model Qwen3.8-27B](https://www.gurufocus.com/news/9036063/alibaba-releases-open-source-model-qwen3827b), [BigGo Finance — Alibaba to Release "Qwen3.8-27B" on August 15](https://finance.biggo.com/news/b3b5cb0c-d942-401f-ba61-2923b0c81857)
- Alibaba's Qwen team shipped Qwen3.8-27B under Apache 2.0 at midnight on August 15: a 27B-parameter causal language model with integrated vision, 262K native context (extensible to 1M), and a Gated DeltaNet + Gated Attention block layout. It's distilled down from the 2.4-trillion-parameter Qwen3.8-Max and is built to run locally on high-end consumer GPUs and Ryzen AI Max PCs, and it reportedly beats Meta's Muse Glimmer 30B on multiple benchmarks.
- **Why it matters:** it's a fully open-weight, locally-deployable multimodal model that competes with much larger closed and open rivals, continuing the trend of powerful models becoming runnable outside hyperscaler infrastructure.

### DeepSeek Raises V4 Flash and V4 Pro API Prices Sharply
**Date:** August 14, 2026 (effective August 16) | **Sources:** [InfoWorld — DeepSeek raises some V4 prices by more than 10x as AI demand strains capacity](https://www.infoworld.com/article/4209439/deepseek-raises-some-v4-prices-by-more-than-10x-as-ai-demand-strains-capacity.html), [Business Standard — DeepSeek launches V4 Pro at prices up to 14 times higher than V4 Flash](https://www.business-standard.com/world-news/deepseek-launches-v4-pro-at-prices-up-to-14-times-higher-than-v4-flash-126081401577_1.html)
- DeepSeek announced on August 14 that V4 Flash output-token pricing rises from $0.28 to $1.32 per million tokens at peak hours (roughly half that off-peak), a more than 4x increase, effective August 16. The company is also introducing a new V4 Pro tier priced up to 14x above V4 Flash, alongside a broader shift to peak/off-peak pricing to "allocate resources more reasonably" amid capacity strain.
- **Why it matters:** it narrows the price gap between DeepSeek and Western frontier labs just as OpenAI and Anthropic have been cutting prices, undercutting DeepSeek's low-cost positioning and pointing to real compute-capacity constraints industry-wide.
