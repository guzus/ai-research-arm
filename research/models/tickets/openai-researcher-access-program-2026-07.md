engineers free access to its frontier models**, starting with **10,000
researchers** and expanding to **100,000 through 2027**.

reports a **GPT-5.6 Sol variant specifically for academic access** as part
of the program — a specific technical detail not yet corroborated by
OpenAI's own primary account, so it is noted here but not treated as
confirmed fact.
