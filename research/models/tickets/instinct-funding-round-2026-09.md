A $10B mark for a company with no model is the interesting part of this report,
and it is worth stating precisely what it would and would not prove.

It would not prove the harness layer is defensible. Two of the three harnesses
anyone named in this cycle are owned by labs that also make the models — Grok
Bot at xAI, Muse at Meta — and the most-praised independent one, Perplexity's,
shipped by post-training its own 27B model to fit the harness. Vertical
integration is the pattern, not the exception. An independent harness raising at
$10B is a bet against that pattern.

It would prove that capital currently believes distribution and orchestration
are scarcer than capability. That belief is testable and this ticket is where it
gets tested: if the round closes at the reported mark, the thesis has a price;
if it quietly resizes, or if Instinct is acquired instead — which is what one
in-window operator expects — then the harness layer was a feature, and the
buyers were the labs and platforms all along.

For now, the honest state is narrow. The product exists and people compare it to
alternatives. The round does not exist yet in any confirmable form.
