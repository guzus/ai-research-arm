This is the weakest ticket opened this cycle and it says so in its own
frontmatter. One wire item on a prediction-market account, no outlet, no
ministry, no document, no scope, no second source.

It gets a ticket because of what it would mean if it were true. The way frontier
capability has actually moved between labs and between countries is people
changing employers — this board tracks a steady stream of those moves in both
directions. A measure that holds researchers' families as an exit cost is a
retention instrument aimed precisely at that channel, and it would be more
consequential for Chinese lab output than most hardware policy.

It is also exactly the shape of claim that spreads because it fits. Beijing has
spent the year tightening outbound deal review, curbing humanoid-robotics
listings, probing DeepSeek and Moonshot over data routing, and steering buyers
toward domestic silicon. A talent-exit restriction slots neatly into that story,
which is a reason to be more suspicious of it, not less. Comparable reporting
about exit bans on Chinese semiconductor engineers has circulated before without
documentation surfacing.

The disposition is stated in `expected` on purpose: corroborate it with a named
source and a policy reference, or close it. It should not sit here half-believed.
