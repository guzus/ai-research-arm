**Amazon** is reportedly winding down most of its flagship **Nova**
models — Nova Premier, Nova Omni, Nova Reel, and Nova Canvas — as part
of a reorganization of its AI strategy around a smaller number of
frontier projects. Existing customers would continue receiving support,
but the products are described by unnamed employees as being in
"keep the lights on" mode rather than active development.

**The redirection.** Amazon is said to be redirecting engineering
talent and computing resources toward a new **Frontier Model Research**
team led by **Pieter Abbeel** (the well-known Berkeley AI/robotics
researcher), developing a new flagship foundation model expected to
debut at **re:Invent** later in 2026 — possibly still carrying the Nova
name. Nova 2 Lite, Nova 2 Sonic, Nova Act, and Nova Forge are reported
to remain active product lines, unaffected by the wind-down.

**Why `rumored` / `unverified`.** This rests on a single account with
no named outlet, no named sources, and no Amazon or AWS statement
confirming or denying it. Treat as a credible-sounding but unconfirmed
strategic shift.
