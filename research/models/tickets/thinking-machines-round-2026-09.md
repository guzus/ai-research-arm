The number that actually matters here is not $40 billion, it is NVIDIA's half.

If the reporting holds, NVIDIA would in a single fortnight have acquired Hugging
Face for $12.9B, licensed Poolside, and put ~$2.5B into the largest independent
open-weights lab in the US. Read together, that is not opportunistic investing;
it is a deliberate attempt to constitute an American open-weight frontier that
is not OpenAI and not Anthropic — and, not incidentally, one whose entire
compute demand routes through NVIDIA silicon. @GavinSBaker made the thesis
explicit on the Hugging Face deal: "Jensen is likely to bring American
open-weight AI to the frontier."

The valuation deserves a less flattering reading than the headline gives it.
$40B pre-money on "at least a few hundred million" of annualized revenue is a
step up from July 2025's $10B, but it is a step *down* from the $50B+ TML was
previously reported to want. A round that is half-funded by a hardware vendor
with a strategic interest in the company existing is also not a clean market
price.

Nothing is signed. Both The Information's original report and every relay say so
explicitly, which is why this sits at `rumored` rather than `confirmed` despite
the density of coverage.
