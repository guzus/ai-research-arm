In July, Caixin Global reported that China had cleared Apple Intelligence for launch through an Alibaba
partnership. That ticket, [[apple-alibaba-china-ai-2026-07]], closed as stale after no corroboration. It
stays closed. Today's signal adds a concrete schedule, so the deal gets a new ticket.

If the dates hold, Qwen would be the first Chinese model running system-level AI on iPhones. Outside China,
Apple's assistant runs on distilled and cloud Gemini ([[apple-wwdc-2026-siri]]).

`confirmed`; public rollout in China → `released`.
