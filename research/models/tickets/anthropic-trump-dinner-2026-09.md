Amodei spent 2026 arguing publicly that the frontier should be paced. The
administration spent it arguing the opposite, asserted pre-release review
authority by executive order, and designated Anthropic a supply-chain risk at
the Pentagon. Sunday night they had dinner alone for the first time.

The pre-dinner press availability is the most informative thing in signal.
Asked directly what he would tell a CEO who wants to slow down, Trump answered
with a China-lead framing and an argument that opening up to China forfeits it.
Nothing in that answer suggests he went into the room to be persuaded.

What is genuinely unknown is whether anything was traded. Anthropic has live
business with the federal government on both sides of the ledger — a California
state-wide deal, a Pentagon blacklist, an IPO in the pipeline, and a $517B
compute commitment stack that depends on power and export policy. A first
one-on-one is where a company with that exposure would try to reset. Whether it
did is unknowable from a scoop and a rope-line quote.

The Tuesday session is the better tell. If Amodei is in the room alongside Huang
and Musk 48 hours later, the dinner was a re-entry. If he is not, it was a
courtesy.
