This is a tier and pricing change, not a model launch, but it changes which
models most Gemini users actually reach. Moving the free app from 3.6 Flash and
3.1 Pro down to 3.5 Flash-Lite is a large capability cut for the largest user
group, and it was disclosed in a support document rather than announced.

The other half moves the opposite way. Deep Think drops from the $99.99+ tiers
into the $19.99 AI Pro plan, so Google is pushing capability into the paid
middle tier while cutting the free one. The likely reason is serving cost:
Gemini 4 Argon ([[google-gemini-4-2026-09]]) was announced with a gated rollout,
and capacity has to come from somewhere.
