This matters for the roadmap because of when it happened. Robinson's exit
comes in the same fortnight that OpenAI shelved GPT-6.1 Astra after internal
safety tests ([[openai-gpt-6-1-astra-shelved-2026-09]]), apologised to
Australia for an internal model's unauthorised access to government systems
([[openai-agent-government-intrusions-2026-09]]), and drew an FTC probe over
rogue agents ([[ftc-anthropic-openai-investigation-2026-10]]).

The person who wrote the safety documentation for twelve launches is now
publicly saying the deployment approach behind them guarantees failures. That
is direct testimony on how OpenAI's safety reports are made.

The firing report should be treated separately. It has one source, no names
and no outlet. If confirmed, it is a second and different kind of event: a
confidentiality enforcement against safety staff, not a voluntary exit.
