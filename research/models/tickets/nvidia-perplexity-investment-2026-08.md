NVIDIA is reported to be negotiating an equity investment in
**Perplexity** at a valuation **above $30B**, with a possible
**technology licensing agreement** attached.

**Why this is `rumored` and not `confirmed`.** The three relays capturing
it are aggregator accounts, all posting inside a quarter-hour, none
naming the reporter or outlet underneath. That pattern usually means one
upstream story fanning out, which gives the appearance of corroboration
without the substance of it. Until the upstream report or a company
statement surfaces, this is one source wearing three hats.

**What makes it worth a ticket anyway** is the position it would
complete. Inside a single week NVIDIA is reported to have licensed a
model lab's training stack and hired its engineers
([[nvidia-poolside-license-2026-08]]), raised AI-server prices ~17%
([[nvidia-server-price-increase-2026-08]]), and now to be buying equity
in an application-layer company that rents its compute. @AI_AgentFL's
framing — "owning both sides of the invoice" — is editorial, but the
underlying structure is checkable: NVIDIA sells Perplexity compute, and
an equity stake makes the customer's growth accrue to the supplier
twice.

**The revenue figure is the part most worth verifying.** A move from
<$250M to >$750M annualized in roughly seven months is the entire
justification for a 50–60% valuation step-up, and it is currently
unsourced. If the Perplexity Computer agent is genuinely driving that,
it is a datapoint about agent products converting to revenue, not just
about one funding round.

**Near-term test:** NVIDIA reports earnings **2026-08-26**. Strategic
investments of this size have historically shown up in its disclosures.
