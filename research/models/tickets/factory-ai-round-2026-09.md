**Factory AI** raised **$200M at a $5B valuation**, announced by its founder.

**What the number is competing with.** This is an enterprise coding-agent
company pricing at $5B in a window when the same category's incumbents are
being absorbed or repriced — Cognition ([[cognition-funding-round-2026-05]]),
Cursor/Anysphere under SpaceX ([[spacex-cursor-acquisition-2026-06]]), and
OpenAI and Anthropic both shipping first-party coding harnesses that compete
directly with the third-party layer. A $5B mark says private investors do not
believe the harness layer collapses into the model vendors.

**The logos are the substance.** RBC, Adobe, Nvidia, T-Mobile and Palo Alto
Networks are named on the record by the founder. Regulated-enterprise and
security-vendor deployments are the hardest sale in this category, and naming
five of them is a stronger signal than the "hundreds of thousands of
developers" reach figure next to it, which has no definition attached.

**What is missing.** No lead investor, no round letter, no prior valuation, no
revenue. Without revenue the $5B is unanchored, and this ticket does not
speculate about a multiple.
