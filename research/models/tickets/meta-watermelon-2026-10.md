Watermelon has been a codename in analyst and press reporting since the summer,
without its own ticket, because it only appeared as a pending item on the Muse
Spark 1.3 / Hatch ticket. That ticket is closed, and Zuckerberg now reportedly
names the model himself. The next foundation model is a distinct shipping
artifact, so it is tracked here.

Status stays `rumored` because the only CEO attribution is a third-party
podcast summary. A clip or transcript would make it `confirmed`.

Muse Spark 1.4 ([[meta-muse-spark-1-4-2026-09]]) could be an interim release on
the existing line. It should not be merged with this ticket unless Meta ships
Watermelon under that version number.
