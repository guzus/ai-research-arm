**Moonshot AI** — the Chinese lab behind the **Kimi** models — is reportedly
**targeting a ~$30B valuation** in a new raise, roughly **6× its late-2025
worth**. The signal this cycle is a valuation *target*, not a closed round:
no disclosed lead investor, instrument, raise size, or on-record confirmation.

**Why `rumored` / `partial`.** A reported valuation target with no closed
terms maps to `rumored`; `verification` is `partial` rather than `unverified`
because Moonshot's rapid re-rating is a recurring, credibly-sourced storyline
rather than a single anonymous tease — but it still lacks a primary.

It fits the wider **Chinese-frontier-lab capital wave**: DeepSeek's first
external round (~$7.4B, up to ~$59B) remains in advanced talks
([[deepseek-funding-round-2026-05]]), and US firms are increasingly trialing
cheaper Chinese models. Moonshot's jump signals investor appetite scaling with
Kimi's competitive standing.

**Transition triggers:**
- A confirmed lead / closed round / on-record valuation → `confirmed`, advance verification.
- A filing or official announcement → UPDATE with terms.
- The raise is denied or stalls ≥15 cycles with no corroboration → `closed: stale-rumor-unverified`.
