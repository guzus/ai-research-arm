A month ago SK hynix said a Japanese memory fab was under review and nothing had
been decided. One Korean outlet now says shovels are in the ground in Tohoku.

Both things can be true — companies say "under review" until the day they do not —
but the gap between them is the reason this opens at `rumored` and `unverified`
rather than higher. The evidence is unnamed industry sources in a single exclusive,
and the outlet itself writes "If true."

What makes it worth tracking rather than ignoring is the motive, which is
consistent with everything else on this board. Memory is the constraint: NVIDIA
trimmed HBM content on Rubin Ultra, is passing ~17% server price increases through
on memory inflation, and Chinese DRAM and NAND suppliers are listing into the
shortage. SK hynix has raised $26.5B on Nasdaq and is reportedly looking at
Intel's Ohio site. Greenfield capacity next to Japan's existing memory cluster is
the obvious next move, and Kioxia's Kitakami NAND fab sitting in the same
prefecture makes the JV question real rather than rhetorical.

The standalone-versus-JV answer will surface long before any output. So will a
denial, if there is one.
