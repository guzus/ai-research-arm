Jules was Google's asynchronous, cloud-hosted coding agent. Antigravity is Google's agentic IDE, which
already ships Gemini 4 Argon for Ultra subscribers ([[google-gemini-4-2026-09]]). Folding Jules into it
would leave Google with one coding-agent product instead of two.

This is a product consolidation, not a model release. It is tracked because it changes where Google's
coding models reach developers.
