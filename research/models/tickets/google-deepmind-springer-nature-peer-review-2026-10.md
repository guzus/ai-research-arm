**Springer Nature**, one of the largest scientific publishers, is partnering with **Google DeepMind** to co-design an AI assistant for peer review. Both parties are on the record: Springer Nature announced the partnership and @GoogleDeepMind amplified it.

The deal takes Google's review-tooling push from conference submissions, where Google Research's **Deep Review** ([[google-deep-review-2026-10]]) was used, to a major journal publisher. The announcement is exploratory ("exploring how AI can help"). No product, scope or date has been given.
