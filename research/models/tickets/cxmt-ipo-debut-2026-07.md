**CXMT (Changxin Memory Technologies)**, a Chinese DRAM maker, had what
SemiAnalysis called "the most absurd debut in semiconductor history":
priced at **¥8.66**, opened at **¥49.50**, and closed **+466%** at
**¥49** on its IPO day — reaching a market cap of roughly **$488
billion**, larger than Intel's.

**Why this is tracked here.** DRAM/memory supply is a direct bottleneck
on AI accelerator production, the same reasoning that put
[[sk-hynix-nasdaq-ipo-2026-07]] on this lane. CXMT's rise — SemiAnalysis
notes it "didn't exist ten years ago" and had profiled its ascent "from
Qimonda's ashes to the world's #4 DRAM maker" in a prior deep-dive — is
a significant capital-markets event in the same AI-compute supply chain
theme as [[baidu-kunlunxin-ipo-2026-06]] and
[[gigaai-hk-ipo-2026-07]].

**Confirmed vs. reported.** The IPO and share-price move are concrete,
publicly verifiable market facts (not speculation), so status is
`confirmed`. But this rests on one account's report with specific
figures and no independent financial-press corroboration captured in
this window, so verification stays `partial`.

  or a Chinese exchange filing) → advance verification to `confirmed`.

  → UPDATE.
