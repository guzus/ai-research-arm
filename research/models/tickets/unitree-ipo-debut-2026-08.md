**Unitree** — the most visible Chinese humanoid and quadruped maker —
listed in **Shanghai** and then fell roughly **45%**.

**The unresolved detail decides the meaning.** A 45% fall *from the debut
open* means the offering was mispriced or the float was thin. A 45% fall
*from a post-debut spike* is a first-day pop unwinding, which happens
routinely in hot Shanghai listings and says less. Neither relay
distinguishes them, so this ticket does not claim to know which happened.

**What is not ambiguous is the direction relative to private marks.**
Inside the same 24 hours, XPeng's Dogotix priced at $6.3B and Skild AI at
$14B ([[xpeng-dogotix-funding-2026-08]]) — private embodied-AI valuations
rising into a public market that just cut the sector's best-known name
roughly in half. Public markets reprice daily and private rounds reprice
annually, so a gap is expected; a gap this wide in one week is worth
tracking rather than explaining away.

**The capability picture is genuinely mixed, which is the point.** The
Beijing World Humanoid Robot Games produced a **7.97 m robot long jump** —
within a metre of a 35-year-old human world record, against a 1.25 m robot
best in 2025 — and, from the same event, a compilation of failures that
circulated just as widely. @tszzl's observation applies: what surfaces
from each country is promotional material, and both the triumph clips and
the blooper reels are selected. Locomotion is visibly improving; useful
manipulation in unstructured environments is not demonstrated by either
clip type.

**Why it belongs here at all.** Embodied AI is where several frontier
labs in this set are placing bets — [[google-gemini-robotics-2-2026-07]],
[[mistral-robostral-navigate-2026-07]], [[xiaomi-robotics-1-2026-07]],
[[figure-helix-02-2026-05]], [[openai-robotics-2026-05]]. A public
repricing of the category is a cost-of-capital input to every one of them.
