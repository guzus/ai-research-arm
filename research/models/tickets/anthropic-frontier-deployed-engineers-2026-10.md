This is a go-to-market commitment, not a model release. It is ticketed because
it shows where Anthropic thinks enterprise adoption is limited: by people who
can integrate agents into existing systems and evaluate them in production, not
by model capability.

The first cohort is consultancies plus a few large enterprises. That fits the
pre-IPO period ([[anthropic-ipo-2026-06]]): it builds a certified integrator
channel that sells Claude deployments.

Treat the numbers as targets. Entry is by nomination, and the first badges are
not due until early 2027.
