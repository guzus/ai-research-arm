XPeng's humanoid unit **Dogotix** has reportedly raised **$900M+ at a
$6.3B valuation**, led by **IDG Capital**, with **Tencent and Alibaba** in
the syndicate.

**The investors matter more than the amount.** A large China humanoid
round is not itself news in 2026. Tencent and Alibaba writing cheques into
one is: both run frontier model programmes, both already publish
open-weight families, and both are now buying exposure to the physical
side of the stack. That is the same reasoning behind Google shipping
Gemini Robotics 2 ([[google-gemini-robotics-2-2026-07]]) and Mistral
shipping Robostral Navigate ([[mistral-robostral-navigate-2026-07]]) — the
belief that action data is the next corpus, and that it cannot be scraped.

**Read against the same week's counter-signal.** Unitree's Shanghai debut
fell ~45% ([[unitree-ipo-debut-2026-08]]), and the World Humanoid Robot
Games produced both a 7.97 m robot long jump and a widely circulated
compilation of failures. Private embodied-AI valuations are rising into a
public market that just marked the sector down hard. Both facts are true
at once; this ticket does not resolve them.

**The sourcing is the weakest part and is not being smoothed over.** Two
aggregator accounts posting five minutes apart with identical figures is
consistent with two independent reads of one report — and equally
consistent with one report copied twice. Until a named outlet, an XPeng
statement or a filing lands, the numbers here are reported, not verified.

**What would settle it:** an XPeng or Dogotix statement, a named outlet,
or disclosure of whether Tencent's and Alibaba's participation carries any
model-licensing or data-sharing terms — which is what would make this a
model-lane event rather than a robotics-funding one.
