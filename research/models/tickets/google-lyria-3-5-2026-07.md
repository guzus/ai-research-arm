**Google DeepMind** released **Lyria 3.5**, the next generation of its
music-generation model, and integrated it directly into **Google Flow
Music** — Google's music-focused creative product — enabling
cover-generation and lip-sync-video features. A new dedicated **iOS app**
shipped alongside the integration.

**Corroboration.** Official @GoogleLabs primary announcement, an
independent (partial) relay from @GoogleDeepMind's own account, and
corroboration from @testingcatalog.

- Broader platform availability (Android, web, API access) → UPDATE.

  models → UPDATE.
