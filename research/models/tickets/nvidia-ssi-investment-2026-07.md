**NVIDIA** is making a "substantial" investment in **Ilya Sutskever's
Safe Superintelligence (SSI)**, and providing enough GPUs to increase
SSI's compute by roughly **10x**, including access to NVIDIA's
next-generation **Vera Rubin platform**.

**What's confirmed vs. reported.** A joint statement from both companies
confirms the investment and compute-access terms in principle; financial
terms were not disclosed publicly in that statement. Bloomberg separately
reported a figure around **$5B cash**, which neither company has
confirmed — treat that specific number as reported, not verified.

**Why this matters.** SSI, co-founded by former OpenAI chief scientist
Ilya Sutskever, has operated with unusually little public compute/product
detail since its founding. A 10x compute increase backed directly by
NVIDIA — rather than a cloud intermediary — is a significant scale-up and
a notable NVIDIA bet on a lab still pre-product.

- Official financial-terms disclosure (confirming or correcting the ~$5B
  figure) → UPDATE.
- First SSI research output, product, or model release on the expanded
  compute → UPDATE, or a new ticket if it's a discrete shipping artifact.
