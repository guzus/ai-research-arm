Griffin is a vertical model, not a frontier one: its claim is about the
nuance of live video conversation rather than reasoning or coding. It sits in
the same product space as Meta's realtime avatar work ([[meta-connect-2026-09]])
and Google's Live Avatar in Gemini Enterprise ([[google-gemini-3-8-live-2026-09]]).

The announcement is primary but thin on specifics. The ticket advances to
released when a usable access path is shown.
