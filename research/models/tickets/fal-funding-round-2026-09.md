fal serves image and video models for other people, and is reportedly being
offered about 19x revenue for it.

The multiple is defensible in a way its sibling report's is not, because there is
a revenue denominator. Whether it is *right* depends on the numbers nobody has
published: how fast $800M is growing, what it costs to serve, and how much of it
comes from a handful of media customers who could build in-house or move.

The structural case for a neutral generative-media host is the strongest in
inference. Text inference is being pulled toward the frontier labs, who sell
their own models directly and price aggressively. Image and video are the
opposite: dozens of interchangeable checkpoints, brutal latency requirements, and
customers whose preferred model changes on a weekly cadence. That is a business
where being model-agnostic is the product rather than a fallback.

Nothing has closed. "Has discussed" is the whole of the reported fact.
