A senior-researcher move is only a timeline event when the person is
load-bearing on a roadmap. Metz qualifies on the reported biography: the
research preview that became ChatGPT, then co-founding Thinking Machines
with Mira Murati, then returning to OpenAI in January 2026 — and now
leaving again seven months later.

**The signal is the churn rate, not the destination.** Someone who
returned to a lab in January and left by August is telling you something
about the lab, and the same window carried a second, independent OpenAI
departure story at the commercial layer
([[openai-sales-leadership-exodus-2026-08]]). Two departures do not make
an exodus, and the account reporting both is the same one — that is a
correlated-source problem worth naming rather than treating as
corroboration.

**Evidence quality is the weak point.** No first-person post from Metz,
nothing from Meta, nothing from OpenAI, no team or remit. The departure
half is the sturdier claim; "Meta" comes from a one-line follow-up. If
the destination is wrong, the ticket title changes and the history entry
stays — that is what history being append-only is for.
