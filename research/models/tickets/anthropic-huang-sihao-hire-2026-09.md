Anthropic created (or filled) a **Head of Frontier Compute Strategy** role and
Sihao Huang announced it himself. The hire is confirmed because the person
taking the job said so.

**Why this is a roadmap ticket and not gossip.** The stated mandate includes
"coalition building." Compute organizations do capacity planning, vendor
management and datacenter siting; they do not normally do coalition building.
That phrase points at the thing Anthropic has been visibly doing for months —
assembling compute through multi-party arrangements with Google, Nscale,
Micron, AMD and others rather than owning it outright — and it now has a named
owner reporting alongside Tom Brown.

**The honest limit.** A title and a three-clause mandate are all that exists.
It would be easy and wrong to read a specific deal into "coalition building";
nothing in-window names one. What this ticket buys is a name to attach to the
next compute announcement, and a falsifier: if the role produces no
counterparty activity over the next quarter, the reading above was
over-interpretation.

**Transition triggers:**
- Any Anthropic compute or financing announcement that names this role or
  this person → UPDATE here and on the relevant compute ticket.
- Departure, retitling, or an Anthropic org announcement clarifying the role →
  UPDATE.
- ≥4 weeks with the hire uneventful → this is normal coverage; close
  `released-and-aged` is not the right reason for a hire, so hold until there
  is either activity or a clear reason to retire it.
