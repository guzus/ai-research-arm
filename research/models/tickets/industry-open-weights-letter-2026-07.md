A cross-industry coalition published an open letter, **"Open Weights and
American AI Leadership,"** arguing that open-weight models are essential
to a healthy AI ecosystem and to US AI leadership. Signatories accumulated
rapidly over 2026-07-25 into 2026-07-26: **NVIDIA** (Jensen Huang posted
the letter directly), **Microsoft** (Satya Nadella), **Google** (Sundar
Pichai, Demis Hassabis — added officially partway through the day),
**Meta**, **OpenAI** (added late on 2026-07-24, without its own
announcement, spotted by trackers the next day), **Mistral**, **Cohere**,
**Hugging Face**, **GitHub**, **IBM**, **Nebius**, **Palantir**,
**CrowdStrike**, **Dell**, and **AMD** (confirmed signing the evening of
2026-07-25).

**Why this is ticket-worthy.** This is a rare instance of near-total
industry alignment across normally-competing labs and infrastructure
vendors on a single policy position, with primary-source confirmation
(company accounts and named executives posting directly, not third-hand
reporting).

**The Anthropic angle.** Anthropic's conspicuous absence became the
dominant framing in the discourse — described by multiple independent
commentators (@kimmonismus, @quxiaoyin, @francoisfleuret, @jeremyphoward,
@rohanpaul_ai) as "Silicon Valley vs. Anthropic." Critics point to
Anthropic's separate public positions on distillation risk and
open-weight safety (see [[anthropic-alibaba-distillation-2026-06]]) as
the likely reason. This is commentary/framing, not an official Anthropic
statement declining to sign — no such statement was captured in this
window.
