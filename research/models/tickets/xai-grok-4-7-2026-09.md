This ticket exists to hold a prediction accountable, not to endorse it.

The evidence is one well-followed aggregator naming a week, a second account
inferring the same thing from silence, and a third — @GavinSBaker — having
listed Grok 4.7 alongside two models that then actually shipped. That last point
is the strongest thing here and it is still circumstantial: knowing that Astra
and Fable 5.1 were imminent does not establish that the person also knew about
Grok 4.7.

Against it: xAI has said nothing. No model card, no API identifier, no
benchmark, no pricing, and — notably — no post from @SpaceXAI at all in this
cycle's fetch. Every previous ticket on this set that advanced on a
date-prediction from a relay account rather than on an artifact has needed
walking back.

The concrete falsifier arrives on its own: if Grok 4.7 does not appear by the
end of the week of 2026-09-06, the prediction is recorded as unfulfilled and
this ticket ages toward the stale-rumor close trigger. That is the useful thing
a ticket does that a timeline table did not.
