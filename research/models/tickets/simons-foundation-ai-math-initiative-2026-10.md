The two labs are funding the same community response to the same problem: AI is
producing mathematical results faster than mathematicians can check and absorb them.
OpenAI says so directly, pointing to the batch of results it published from an
unreleased model ([[openai-math-results-release-2026-10]]).

The foundation, not the labs, decides the awards. That separation is the point of
routing the money through Simons.
