This is a governance and capital-structure event, not a model release. It matters to
this desk because Anthropic's share structure is part of the IPO story tracked in
[[anthropic-ipo-2026-06]]. A large, formula-driven equity commitment to a
public-benefit vehicle changes what future shareholders are buying.

The mechanism is step-ups tied to valuation (up to +2% per 50% gain over a baseline).
The fund's stake therefore grows as Anthropic's value grows, rather than being a fixed
gift. The baseline value and the fund's governance were not in the announcement text.

**Watch for:** the baseline valuation, the fund's governance and board, and any
mention in IPO filings or reporting.
