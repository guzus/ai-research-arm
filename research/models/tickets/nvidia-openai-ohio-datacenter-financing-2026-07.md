**NVIDIA** is reported to be in talks to backstop a very large share of
the financing behind **OpenAI's planned 10-gigawatt datacenter in
southern Ohio**, part of a SoftBank-developed project that could
ultimately cost more than **$500 billion**.

**The reported shape.** Per WSJ (relayed by @AndrewCurran_):
- NVIDIA in talks to backstop **roughly $250B** of the lease financing.
- A further **$350B in financing** so OpenAI can buy **$350B of NVIDIA
  chips** for the same site — NVIDIA financing OpenAI's purchase of
  NVIDIA's own hardware, a structure that has drawn scrutiny elsewhere
  in the industry (circular-financing concerns around NVIDIA's other
  compute deals).

**Why `confirmed` / `partial`.** WSJ is a named, credible outlet and the
figures are specific and internally consistent across the relay chain,
clearing the bar for a real, substantive reported event (`confirmed`).
But this is explicitly "in talks," not a signed deal, and no primary
NVIDIA or OpenAI statement has been captured — so `verification: partial`.
