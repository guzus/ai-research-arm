**Taiwanese prosecutors** have **detained an NVIDIA employee** and
**searched NVIDIA offices** as part of a widening investigation into
**chip smuggling into China** that evades export controls — reported by
Bloomberg and relayed by @AndrewCurran_ as the **first time** Taiwan's
government has taken direct action against someone employed by NVIDIA.

**Why a separate ticket from [[china-nvidia-h200-import-2026-07]].** That
ticket tracks a *legal*, PRC-side policy shift reportedly preparing to
let Alibaba, ByteDance, and DeepSeek import limited quantities of H200
chips through sanctioned channels. This ticket is the opposite vector: a
**Taiwan-side criminal enforcement action** against alleged **illegal**
smuggling that circumvents export controls entirely. Related theme
(chips reaching China around US/allied export restrictions), distinct
shipping/legal artifact.

**Confirmed vs. reported.** A detention and an office search are
concrete, verifiable acts — not speculation — so status is `confirmed`.
But this rests on one relay of a Bloomberg report with no second
independent source or NVIDIA statement captured yet, so verification
stays `partial`.
