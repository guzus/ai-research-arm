Rosalind ([[openai-rosalind-biodefense-2026-05]]) has been OpenAI's life-sciences
model line. Buying Orchestra Bio adds workflow software for running drug programs,
which is where pharma budgets sit, rather than more research capability.

It is another acqui-hire-style product purchase in a year of them for OpenAI
([[openai-ona-acquisition-2026-06]], [[openai-instant-acquihire-2026-08]]).
