Genie 4 is the first Genie generation Google says will reach the Gemini API. Earlier Genie releases were
research previews. Putting a world model behind an API changes it from a demo into a tool for training
and evaluating agents and robots in simulation. Google DeepMind's thread says this directly.

A secondary digest (@clarityware) says Gemini 3.8 Flash acts as a guiding agent that watches frames and
checks each plan step. This is not in the first-party posts captured here.

**Transition triggers:** Ultra / API availability → `released`.
