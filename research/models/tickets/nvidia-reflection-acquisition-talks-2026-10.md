NVIDIA has been investing across the open-model layer — it licenses Poolside
([[nvidia-poolside-license-2026-08]]), backs SSI and others, and led the
industry open-weights letter ([[industry-open-weights-letter-2026-07]]).
Absorbing Reflection, one of the few US labs shipping frontier-scale open
weights (Beam), would fit that pattern.

The claim currently rests on one aggregator citing one outlet. "Acquire or
acqui-hire" covers two very different deals, and the difference decides
whether Beam continues as an open-weight line. Treat it as a rumor until a
primary outlet or either company confirms it.
