Sales leadership is not usually a model-timeline concern. It is here for
one reason: **the revenue org is where a lab's commercial roadmap gets
executed**, and OpenAI is currently running an aggressive pricing
strategy into an IPO process.

**Hold this one loosely.** It rests on one aggregator post. No names, no
OpenAI statement, no outlet pickup in the captured window, and the most
alarming line in it — that many others are considering resigning — is an
unattributed quote. `verification: unverified` is the honest reading, and
this ticket will close as a stale rumor if nothing corroborates it.

**What would make it real.** Named departures, an OpenAI comment, or
outlet confirmation. What would make it *matter*: whether it coincides
with a change in enterprise pricing or go-to-market posture. A 20% API
price cut announced within days of losing the CRO and VP of Sales
([[openai-gpt-5-6]]) is either coincidence or the same story told twice,
and nothing captured here distinguishes those.

The adjacent research-side departure ([[meta-luke-metz-hire-2026-08]])
is a separate event with a separate evidentiary basis; the two are
linked here only because @ns123abc frames them as one narrative about
OpenAI becoming a product company. That framing is opinion.
