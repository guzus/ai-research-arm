**Stability AI** closed its **Series B** on 2026-08-25 — **$76M** in new
money, **$232M** cumulative — and told the story deliberately as one about
investors rather than dollars: "This moment is more than the number, it's
about who's in the room."

**Who is in the room.** **EA**, **Sony Music**, **Universal Music Group** and
**Warner Music** — all three major labels — alongside **AMD Ventures** and
**Pacific Alliance Ventures**, with **Coatue**, **Greycroft**, **Kadmos
Capital**, **Sean Parker** and **Eric Schmidt** returning for a second
consecutive round. Variety adds James Cameron.

**Why it is a timeline event.** The AI-and-rights-holders story in this lane
has mostly been adversarial: copyright suits, licensing disputes, discovery
motions ([[midjourney-discovery-motion-2026-07]],
[[wikihow-openai-copyright-2026-08]],
[[nyt-openai-microsoft-copyright-2026-06]]). Three major labels writing
equity cheques into a generative-media company is the opposite posture, and
from an industry that has been among the most litigious. Whether that
translates into **licensed catalogue as training data** is the question the
round raises and does not answer.

**What is not disclosed.** No valuation in captured signal. No terms on what
the strategic investors get — licensing rights, board seats, content deals,
or none of the above. "Under new leadership" is Stability's own framing of a
turnaround; what that leadership has shipped is not in this signal either.
