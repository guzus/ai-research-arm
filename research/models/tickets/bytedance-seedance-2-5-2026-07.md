**ByteDance's Dreamina** shipped **Seedance 2.5**, its next-generation
video-generation model, live now via the Dreamina product with a **staged
regional rollout** (Southeast Asia, Middle East, Africa, Europe, South
America first). Headline capabilities: native **30-second single-shot
generation**, up to **3-minute long-form output with consistency**, and
support for up to **50 multimodal references** in a single generation.

**Enterprise access.** BytePlus (ByteDance's enterprise cloud arm)
describes **API access as "coming soon"** — not yet available at launch,
so this ticket's `released` status reflects the consumer Dreamina product,
not the enterprise API.

**Corroboration.** Official primary source (@BytePlusGlobal), with
independent same-day adoption/praise from @higgsfield_ai ("a major leap
over Seedance 2.0") and further relay from @AndrewCurran_ and
@kimmonismus.

**Transition triggers:**
- BytePlus enterprise API goes live → UPDATE.
- Broader regional rollout (beyond the initial launch regions) → UPDATE.
- ≥4 weeks past release, settled into normal coverage → `closed:
  released-and-aged`.
