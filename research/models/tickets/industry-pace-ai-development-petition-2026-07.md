A petition published on **2026-07-28** calls on the US government to
"support an international effort to develop the technical and
governance tools needed to deliberately pace the frontier of automated
AI development." It was signed **in a personal capacity** — not as an
official company position — by more than **1,100 employees and senior
researchers** across OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, Microsoft,
Mistral, Thinking Machines, and other frontier labs, including
high-profile names like **Dario Amodei** (Anthropic CEO), **Jakub
Pachocki** (OpenAI chief scientist), **Jared Kaplan**, **Shengjia
Zhao**, **Mark Chen**, **John Schulman**, and **Jasjeet Sekhon**.

**Why `confirmed`, not just a rumor.** Unlike most single-thread
petitions, this one drew same-evening, independent primary confirmation
from two rival companies' official accounts:

- **Anthropic** (22:17 UTC): "We support this petition, signed by our
  CEO, several co-founders, and senior staff. Our own research on
  recursive self-improvement, published last month, points to the need
  for tools to deliberately pace the frontier of AI development so
  society can prepare. We're glad to see broad agreement across the
  field."
- **OpenAI** (20:56 UTC): "We believe that, at some point in the
  future, AI acceleration for frontier model development may be so high
  that the world will need to pace the rate of AI advancement. We hope
  to contribute to work led by the U.S. government, alongside other
  labs and the open-source community, to develop the tools and
  mechanisms that could make that possible."

**The Anthropic-heavy signatory mix.** @scaling01's analysis of the
published signatory list found roughly **46% are Anthropic-affiliated**
— a striking concentration given Anthropic is one of the smaller
frontier labs by headcount — and that most signatories hold technical
or research roles rather than marketing/HR positions, suggesting the
petition reflects genuine researcher sentiment rather than a PR
exercise.

**Skeptical counter-framing.** Not everyone reads this at face value:
@scaling01 (the same account that surfaced the Anthropic-signatory
statistic) separately argued the petition is "more virtue signaling
than anything" absent concrete commitments, dubbing the signatories
"Larptrophic" and "LarpingAI" and predicting "the race will continue."
This is commentary, not a fact about the petition's contents, but is
worth tracking as the discourse's skeptical pole.

**Why a separate ticket from [[industry-open-weights-letter-2026-07]].**
That letter argues open-weight models are essential to US AI leadership
and is signed by companies (NVIDIA, Microsoft, Meta, etc.) as
institutional positions. This petition argues for pacing frontier
development broadly and is signed by individuals in a personal
capacity — a different mechanism (slow down vs. keep weights open) and
a different signing structure (company vs. person). It also connects
to the existing [[us-ai-model-review-eo-2026-06]] federal review
framework as a request to build further governance tooling on top of
it.
