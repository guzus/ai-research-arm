**Microsoft AI** shipped **MAI-Cyber-1-Flash**, its first in-house
cybersecurity model, announced directly by CEO **Satya Nadella**: "delivers
world-class performance at 50 percent of the cost," going to market through
a new initiative called **Project Perception**.

**The numbers.** Microsoft AI CEO **Mustafa Suleyman** followed up with
specifics: **96% on CyberGym**, a margin of **12 points above Anthropic's
Mythos** ([[mythos-public-release]]), at roughly half the inference cost.

**Why a separate ticket.** This is a distinct product launch from
Microsoft's existing MAI-* lineup — [[microsoft-mai-code-1-flash]] (code),
[[microsoft-mai-image-2-5]] / the 2.5 Pro variant (image), and
[[microsoft-mai-voice-2-flash-2026-07]] (voice) — and the first
cybersecurity-specific entry in that family, entering the same competitive
lane as Anthropic's Mythos (built for defenders) and the broader
NVIDIA-led Open Secure AI Alliance push
([[nvidia-open-secure-ai-alliance-2026-07]]).
