Microsoft AI's **Diagnostic Orchestrator** is a panel of AI agents that takes a history, orders tests and reaches a diagnosis. Its results are now peer-reviewed in **Science**. On NEJM case reports it reached about **83%** accuracy, against ~36% for generalist physicians with references and ~22% without. On Beth Israel Deaconess emergency-department cases it scored **80%** against 47%, and it got there at a lower estimated cost.

The system runs on GPT-5; the paper does not test current frontier models. Microsoft says later testing suggests better models do not erase the gain from the multi-agent setup. **SDBench**, the benchmark built for the study, will be open-sourced.

The next step is **clinical testing at Beth Israel Deaconess**, which Microsoft expects "in the next couple of months". Suleyman says the system "needs clinical validation". No commercial date has been given.
