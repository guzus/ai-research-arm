At its 2026-10-23 Q3 earnings, Meta announced a **non-voting Class C share class**, effective **2027-01-19**. The WSJ reports a plan to raise **up to $95B** through waves of stock offerings to pay for the AI buildout.

This changes how AI capex is financed. Until now the hyperscalers paid for the buildout from cash flow and debt. Meta is opening a permanent equity channel instead, and because the shares carry no votes, it does so without diluting Zuckerberg's control. The compute side is tracked at [[meta-compute-2026-07]].
