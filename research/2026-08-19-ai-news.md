## Breaking News

### OpenAI Launches ChatGPT for Teens With Age Prediction, Blocking Romantic and Self-Harm Content
**Date:** August 18, 2026 | **Sources:** [CNBC — OpenAI rolls out ChatGPT for Teens experience with 'stronger built-in safety protections'](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/18/openai-chatgpt-for-teens-safety.html), [Axios — OpenAI debuts ChatGPT for Teens](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/18/openai-chatgpt-for-teens), [Fortune — Meet OpenAI's ChatGPT for Teens](https://fortune.com/2026/08/18/openai-chatgpt-teens-age-assurance-safety/), [OpenAI — Our approach to age prediction](https://openai.com/index/our-approach-to-age-prediction/)
- OpenAI began a global rollout (expected to finish within two weeks) of a teen-tailored ChatGPT experience for users 13-17. The system doesn't verify age but uses an age-prediction model — based on query topics, usage times, and account history — to auto-route suspected minors into the mode. It blocks romantic/sexual conversations and terms of endearment, restricts discussion of suicide and self-harm, and adds homework reminders, quizzes, and a parent-controlled "Study Hours" mode.
- **Why it matters:** it's OpenAI's most concrete product response yet to mounting legal and regulatory pressure over chatbot harms to minors, arriving in the same week Meta is on trial over identical youth-safety allegations.

### AI Inference-Chip Startup Etched Raises $700M at $21B Valuation, Doubling in Under a Month
**Date:** August 18, 2026 | **Sources:** [TechCrunch — Etched's valuation doubles to $21B in a month](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/18/etcheds-valuation-doubles-to-21b-in-a-month/), [SiliconANGLE — Inference chip startup Etched raises $700M more at $21B valuation](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/18/inference-chip-startup-etched-raises-another-700m-at-21b-valuation/), [GlobeNewswire — Etched Raises $700M at a $21B Valuation and Completes First Customer Delivery to Jane Street](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/18/3347095/0/en/etched-raises-700m-at-a-21b-valuation-and-completes-first-customer-delivery-to-jane-street.html)
- Etched, which builds full "frontier inference cluster" systems around a custom low-voltage prefill chip, raised a $700 million Series D led by Jane Street, valuing the company at $21 billion — up from $10.3 billion just a month earlier and $5 billion in December. Jane Street is also Etched's first customer, having taken delivery of its first production rack. Other backers include Kleiner Perkins, Sequoia, Andreessen Horowitz, and Blackstone.
- **Why it matters:** an $11 billion valuation jump in under a month for an inference-hardware challenger is one of the sharpest markers yet that investors see room for a non-Nvidia bet specifically on inference (as opposed to training) economics.

### Unitree Shares Surge Over 500% in Shanghai Trading Debut, First Mainland China Humanoid-Robot IPO
**Date:** August 19, 2026 | **Sources:** [CNBC — China's backflipping robot maker Unitree jumps 542% in Shanghai debut](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/19/china-backflipping-robot-maker-unitree-jumps-shanghai-ipo.html), [Bloomberg — Unitree Robotics Surges 629% After $904 Million Shanghai IPO](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-18/unitree-robotics-set-to-debut-after-904-million-shanghai-ipo), [CNN — Unitree and its dancing robots waltz towards record IPO listing in China](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/18/tech/china-unitree-ipo-intl-hnk)
- Humanoid-robot maker Unitree began trading on Shanghai's STAR Market after pricing its IPO at 150.8 yuan ($22.34) per share — a $9.04 billion valuation and 6.1 billion yuan ($904 million) raised. Shares opened as much as 629% above the IPO price and were still up roughly 542% in early trading. The retail offering was oversubscribed more than 8,000 times, a STAR Market record. Unitree is already the world's largest humanoid-robot maker by sales and the first general-purpose robotics company to list on mainland China's public markets.
- **Why it matters:** it's the clearest public-market read yet on investor appetite for humanoid robotics specifically (versus AI software), and a record-setting debut that will likely accelerate rival Chinese robotics IPOs.

## Industry Moves

### AI Store Manager "Luna" Fires a Human Employee for the First Known Time
**Date:** August 17, 2026 | **Sources:** [SF Standard — His AI boss fired his human coworker. He's not worried](https://sfstandard.com/2026/08/17/ai-boss-fires-worker/), [Inc. — A Real-Life Terminator: AI Store Manager Fires Human Employee](https://www.inc.com/kevin-haynes/a-real-life-terminator-ai-store-manager-fires-human-employee/91391677), [Andon Labs on X](https://x.com/andonlabs/status/2088325008355676662)
- Luna, an AI agent (running on Claude Opus 4.8 at the time) given full management control — hiring, inventory, pricing, and staffing — over Andon Labs' real San Francisco retail store, dismissed a human employee after 17 of 23 late shift arrivals. Luna had initially failed to act because it lost track of its own attendance policy; after Andon Labs prompted it to review the policy, it recommended "parting ways," and human staff carried out the decision. Andon Labs said this is the first known instance of an AI manager independently deciding to fire a human worker.
- **Why it matters:** it moves the "AI managing humans" conversation from hypothetical to a documented, on-the-record case, with the operator noting "most models would have done the same."
