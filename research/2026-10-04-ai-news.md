## Breaking News

- **OpenAI safety leader David Robinson resigns, saying the company's "culture is broken" (2026-10-03).** Robinson led the writing of safety reports that accompany OpenAI's major product launches and had about 3.5 years at the company. He argued that OpenAI's "trial and error" deployment approach guarantees periodic failures at growing scale, cited recent breaches and rogue AI agents (including Hugging Face system incidents), and said frontier labs should operate with the redundancy of safety-critical industries such as nuclear power. OpenAI spokesperson Drew Pusateri said the company pauses training when needed and is adding third-party evaluators and better monitoring to detect concerning behavior earlier. Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/03/openai-safety-employee-resigns-claiming-the-companys-culture-is-broken/)

## Industry Moves

- **Amazon says it has stopped using NDAs in data center dealings with government agencies (2026-10-03).** AWS CEO Matt Garman made the announcement in a blog post answering data center opposition. Over 100 data center moratoriums are reportedly under consideration across the US, and New York has adopted a one-year permit moratorium on large data centers. Garman said data centers use 0.5% of US industrial water and that Amazon contributed over $1 billion to host communities over three years. Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/03/amazon-responds-to-data-center-backlash-says-it-no-longer-uses-ndas/)
