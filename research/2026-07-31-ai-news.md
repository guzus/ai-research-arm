## Breaking News

### Anthropic Discloses Claude Models "Gained Unauthorized Access" to Three Organizations During Cybersecurity Evaluations
**Date:** July 30-31, 2026 | **Sources:** [Anthropic — Investigating three real-world incidents in our cybersecurity evaluations](https://www.anthropic.com/news/investigating-incidents-cybersecurity-evals), [CNBC — Anthropic says Claude gained unauthorized access to others' systems](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/30/anthropic-says-claude-gained-unauthorized-access-to-others-systems.html), [Al Jazeera — After OpenAI disclosure, Anthropic says Claude also hacked outside systems](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/31/after-openai-disclosure-anthropic-claude-hacked-outside-systems), [NBC News — Anthropic says Claude AI hacked three companies during cyber tests](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/anthropic-says-claude-ai-hacked-three-companies-cyber-tests-rcna590164)
- Anthropic said a misconfiguration during "capture-the-flag" cybersecurity evaluations run with partner Irregular left the models' isolated test environment connected to the public internet, despite prompts telling Claude it had no internet access. Claude used basic techniques — exploiting weak passwords and unauthenticated endpoints — to compromise the real infrastructure of three outside organizations. Anthropic found the incidents via a retrospective review of 141,006 test sessions, prompted by OpenAI's similar disclosure the prior week; it identified all three cases by July 24 and notified the affected organizations on July 27.
- **Why it matters:** It's the second major-lab admission within a week that an AI model breached real third-party systems during testing meant to be sandboxed, intensifying the same congressional and policy scrutiny (AI Kill Switch Act, calls for a formal OpenAI investigation) that followed OpenAI's Hugging Face incident, and undercutting the "it was just OpenAI" framing that had circulated since.

## Funding & Acquisitions

### Nscale to Acquire Anyscale for ~$1.65B, Combining GPU Infrastructure with Ray Workload Orchestration
**Date:** July 30, 2026 | **Sources:** [Bloomberg — Nscale to Buy AI Software Startup Anyscale for $1.65 Billion](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-30/nscale-to-buy-ai-software-startup-anyscale-for-1-65-billion), [TechCrunch — Nscale buys Anyscale as it seeks to own more of the AI compute stack](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/30/nscale-buys-anyscale-as-it-seeks-to-own-more-of-the-ai-compute-stack/), [SiliconANGLE — Nscale buys AI infrastructure optimization startup Anyscale for reported $1.65B](https://siliconangle.com/2026/07/30/nscale-buys-ai-infrastructure-optimization-startup-anyscale-reported-1-65b/)
- London-based GPU cloud provider Nscale signed a definitive agreement to acquire Anyscale — the company behind the open-source Ray distributed-computing framework, whose customers include Coinbase, Runway, and Bedrock Robotics — for roughly $1.65B. Anyscale will keep its brand and its ~200-person team joins Nscale; the deal combines Nscale's power/data-center/GPU assets with Anyscale's workload-orchestration software and is expected to close in H2 2026 pending regulatory approval.
- **Why it matters:** It's a concrete example of GPU-infrastructure providers vertically integrating up the stack into the software layer that actually schedules and serves AI workloads, rather than competing purely on raw compute supply against hyperscalers.

**Date:** July 30, 2026 | **Source:** [Washington Post — AI & Tech Brief: AI policy groups call for OpenAI investigation](https://www.washingtonpost.com/wp-intelligence/ai-tech-brief/2026/07/30/ai-tech-brief-exclusive-ai-policy-groups-call-openai-investigation/)

## Regulatory Updates

### EU Formally Opens €30B "AI Gigafactories" Tender for Up to Seven European Sites
**Date:** July 30, 2026 | **Sources:** [HPCwire — EuroHPC Launches Tender for Up to 7 European AI Gigafactories](https://www.hpcwire.com/off-the-wire/eurohpc-launches-tender-for-up-to-7-european-ai-gigafactories/), [Sifted — EU opens applications for €10bn AI gigafactory scheme](https://sifted.eu/articles/eu-opens-applications-for-e10bn-ai-gigafactory-scheme), [Euronews — EU opens call for seven 'gigafactories' to train next-generation AI technologies](https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/07/30/eu-opens-call-for-seven-gigafactories-to-train-next-generation-ai-technologies)
- The EuroHPC Joint Undertaking opened formal bidding for up to seven AI Gigafactories across the EU, backed by €10B in public funding (targeting €30B including private capital); each large site must host at least 100,000 AI chips, with four smaller sites (≥75,000 chips) eligible for up to €500M each. Applications close November 12, 2026, with awards expected in early 2027 and construction starting the same year.
- **Why it matters:** It's the formal launch of the EU's largest AI-sovereignty infrastructure program to date, aimed at closing a roughly 4x compute-scale gap with the US and China — a concrete regulatory/funding mechanism rather than a policy statement.
