### Notable Discussions
- [Open-weight AI is having its Kubernetes moment](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49048034): Argues open-weight models are heading toward the same fate as Kubernetes — a commoditized substrate that a handful of platform/orchestration layers get built on top of, rather than a permanent point of differentiation for any one lab.
- [The new rules of context engineering for Claude 5 generation models](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49051361): Anthropic's own guidance on how prompting/context strategies should change for the Claude 5 model family, drawing heavy discussion on what's actually new versus repackaged best practice.
- [DeepSeek pause fundraise after comments on compute gap to US leaked](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49052912): Leaked investor-meeting transcript has Liang Wenfeng reportedly acknowledging a real compute gap versus US labs, coinciding with a paused fundraising round.
- [Running a 28.9M parameter LLM on an $8 microcontroller](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49050512): ESP32 project demonstrating a small language model running entirely on cheap embedded hardware — concrete edge-inference proof point.
- [LLM Usage in Debian: Three Proposals](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49050859): Debian's General Resolution process is voting on how (or whether) LLM-assisted contributions should be disclosed/permitted in the project, a bellwether for how open-source governance handles AI-authored patches.
