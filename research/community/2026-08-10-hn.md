### Notable Discussions

- **How I use LLMs to learn complex topics** (716 pts, the snapshot's biggest
  AI thread): A personal workflow for using LLMs as a learning scaffold —
  decomposing unfamiliar subjects, generating checkable practice problems, and
  treating the model as a tutor rather than an oracle. The 461-comment thread's
  sharpest counterpoint is the self-verification trap: without cross-checking
  against primary sources, the scaffold becomes a confidence machine; several
  commenters argue it works for breadth ("map the territory") and fails at
  depth — [link](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49234675)

- **Meta Muse Glimmer – open weights 30B local coding model** (358): Meta
  research's open-weights agentic coding model announcement — the day's actual
  model-release news. The 162-comment discussion centers on the agentic
  framing, local/inference cost at 30B, and how it slots against the open-
  vs-closed coding-model landscape —
  [link](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49241679)

- **Docker Sandboxes – Disposable, isolated sandboxes for AI agents** (348):
  Docker's disposable sandbox product aimed squarely at the agentic-compute
  market — ephemeral, isolated execution environments for AI agents. The
  216-comment thread argues over why agents need throwaway sandboxes (vs.
  persistent VMs/containers), cost profiles, and whether this is the default
  compute substrate agents will assume —
  [link](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49239751)

- **Everything you do is being recorded** (349): The Atlantic's survey of
  countermeasures against AI-wearable surveillance — detect-and-evade
  clothing, jammers, anti-wearable norms. Included as AI-adjacent commentary
  (wearable-AI surveillance policy), not model news —
  [link](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49230477)
