HN title stamps “385x more efficient inference”; the project page and abstract stamp 345× less inference compute than the next-best model. Energy-Based Reasoners (EBRs) learn an energy \(E_\theta(z;x)\) over latent reasoning states and treat looped updates as gradient descent on that energy: the value scores candidates (no external verifier), the gradient refines and stops, and the lowest-energy candidate is selected. Training: state-based Explorative Modeling (SXM). 1.8M parameters. Sudoku-Extreme single-sample 99.4% vs Looped flows 97.9 / FPRM 94.2 / EqR 93.0 / TRM 87.4 / GRAM 86.6 (those baselines 5–10M); 5 samples 99.9% vs EqR 99.8(128) / PTRM 98.8(100) / Looped flows 99.3(5) / GRAM 96.1(20). Adaptive stopping (stop when \(\|\nabla E\|<\tau\)) keeps 99.9% with 10.9× fewer FLOPs than a fixed-depth EBR and 345× fewer than EqR.
