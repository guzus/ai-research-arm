Solo nanochat-inspired pretrain: Llama-style 3.848B (RMSNorm, RoPE, GQA 24/8, relu², QK-norm, logit softcap, ResFormer value embeddings) on 65.3B ClimbMix tokens, 8× B200, 43 hours, CORE 0.384.
