Final best-of-15 (three trajectories × five candidates): **1.81×** geometric-mean speedup and **1.81×** total-workload speedup = **44.7%** summed latency cut, 68 wins / 0 regressions. Motifs that win: rewrite the join order with `Leading`, one scan fix, and `Parallel`; `Rows` corrections were rare (146 / 1,347 actions). Cost: ~$800 Lambda + ~$400 Astra API = **$1,200**. - [Dream-RSI: Recursive Self-Improvement through Evolving Worlds](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49726955): kept climbing (171/49 → 188/49). Tong Zheng, Xidong Wu, and Zheng Zhang with Zhankui He, Chaoyi Zhang, Benjamin Coleman, Ruoqiao Wei, Di Bai, Haolin Liu, Rui Liu, Xue Wang, Yue Zhuan, Wang-Cheng Kang, Renkai Xiang, Heng Huang, Xinwu Cheng, and Yunsong Guo / Google, University of Maryland, Google DeepMind, and University of Virginia, [arXiv 2609.14858](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14858) (submitted 14 September 2026 00:10 UTC; posted ~13:44 UTC yesterday). Load-bearing split vs r/MachineLearning's still-hot `RSI is not happening` paper and vs the Twitter headline that Google "cracked recursive self-improvement": the coding agent, evaluator, and execution interfaces stay fixed; what self-improves is the **exploration policy** (branching, parallelism, stopping). Completed discovery trees are treated as an exact replay simulator over the realized search space — not a learned world model — so thousands of candidate policies can be scored at zero new executions; the currently deployed policy is always in the candidate set, so the winner cannot score worse. Eight tasks / three domains. Lasso path: Dream-RSI on Gemini-3.1-Pro uses 317 discovery-agent calls vs 550 for Recursive Fixed Exploration and 51,200 for SimpleTES (gpt-oss-120b) — up to **162×** fewer calls than SimpleTES and **1.7×** fewer than the fixed-exploration baseline. Math: matches or beats the evolve family inside **<1k** generations vs SimpleTES's 51,200. KernelBench (Gemini-3.1 Pro): VGG16 / LayerNorm reach the target at **2.43×** / **1.79×** fewer generations. Semantic-guidance ablation loses. Code `github.com/zhengkid/Dream-RSI`; site `dream-rsi.com`. Remaining meta-exploration / "the weights do not change; the search policy does" thread — now sitting over Intel's ternary-packing paper.
- [Breaking the 1.58-bit Barrier for Ternary LLMs](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49732931): jumped (81/5 → 167/22) and is now the #6 AI story. Evangelos Georganas, Alexander Heinecke, and Pradeep Dubey / Intel, [arXiv 2609.16338](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16338) (posted ~20:59 UTC yesterday). Load-bearing measurement: 29 ternary checkpoints (BitNet b1.58, Bonsai, CAT-Q Qwen3, ParetoQ, TriLM, Maple, BitCPM-CANN) are **not** equiprobable \(\{-1,0,+1\}\); zeros reach **51.5%** (CAT-Q Qwen3-1.7B). Five-trit packing is 1.6 bits/weight in theory and **1.625** in practice (power-of-two groups of 128 need 26 bytes). **BITCOS** (bitmap + compacted signs) costs \(2-z\) bits/weight and beats five-trit once \(z>0.375\) — **26 of 29** models, down to **1.485** bits/weight on the sparsest, below \(\log_2 3 \approx 1.585\). Unpack sequences for AVX-512 / AVX2 / Intel Xe2; vs production ternary GEMV, up to **1.28×**. End-to-end decode: up to **1.18×** on CPUs and **1.27×** on discrete Xe2; an 8-core bandwidth-rich client is the named miss (unpack exposed). Remaining compression / "the 1.58-bit slogan assumed three equal symbols" thread — now sitting over the DeepMind Institute after the physics re-grade / AMD matrix cores left.

- [DeepSeek-v4.1 Flash: Pushing the Limits of KV Cache Compression](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49735410): new since 22:58 — zartbot architecture read of DeepSeek's *DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash: Pushing the Limits of KV Cache Compression* technical report (73/6, posted ~01:39 UTC). Load-bearing motive: long-horizon agent workflows make persistent KV — not sparse-attention FLOPs — the serving bottleneck. Reported shape: **552B** multimodal MoE, 1M context, **Causal Encoder-Decoder (CED)** (YoCo-style: 20 encoder layers produce reusable global KV, 20 decoder layers read it). Prefill activates **8B**; decode activates **16B**. At the same sequence length, runtime KV is about **1/4** of V4-Flash and persistent KV about **1/8**, with quality claimed above V4-Flash. **CSA2** compresses three ways: a 512-dim latent shared across heads (channel); encoder merges 2 adjacent positions (sequence); the network keeps 3 encoder cache copies + 1 decoder copy (layer). Combined with FP4 KV, global main KV + indexer grow at about **890 bytes/token**. Vision path: 32-layer ViT, patch 14, 3×3 pixel-unshuffle, 1024 visual tokens/image cap; per-modality MoE load-balancing biases. DSpark speculative decoding (3 SWA-128 draft blocks) and Engram conditional memory sit on top. Newest systems / "prefill is half the model if the cache is the product" thread under HarnessTax.
