- Gödel notebooks / Claude: Still the largest AI-by-points story and the largest AI comment thread. 318 points / 90 comments (was 185 / 44 at 2026-11-13 20:01 UTC). theguardian.com/science/2026/nov/13/kurt-godel-notebooks-ai-helps-unlock-hidden-discoveries-man-who-transformed-maths (Alex Bellos; Jan von Plato via Guardian, not an independent reconstruction audit). Helsinki project on Gödel's 16 early-1940s workbooks: 1,009 densely written pages, much of it Gabelsberger shorthand. Von Plato says progress was about a page a week until September, when Claude produced a ~650-page digest in a couple of days. - JEPA / LeWM experiments: Still on the page. 208 points / 35 comments (was 116 / 24). haddyalchaer.com/jepa (Haddy Alchaer; author's 600-experiment writeup, not an independent eval). LeWM (Maes / Le Lidec / Scieur / LeCun / Balestriero, arXiv 2603.19312) on Push-T. Author's numbers: vanilla LeWM 78% vs exact-physics predictor 77% on 250 long-horizon (75-step) tasks with expert subgoals every 25 steps; as published, 22% planning all 75 steps at once vs 80% with 25-step subgoals (exact physics all-at-once 42%). A latent memory graph of ~460,000 demonstration embeddings plus a cost on inter-demo jumps reached 85%, matching expert subgoals. Standard Push-T goals sit on paths demonstrations already travel (84% already had a near-identical stored state); on 250 fresh standard episodes at 50 steps, 44% vs 68% on the official 50. Harder test (start from one demo, goal from another; block on target for last 10 steps): memory-graph 75% standard / 45% harder. Deleting stored states near the goal dropped success 93% → 32%; filling the gap with shifted / rotated / mirrored demo segments recovered 85% (harder test 45% → 56%). Scoring the pusher in the goal image: exact-physics planner 9/50 with pusher vs 39/50 block-only; searching 16 pusher placements doubled LeWM on the harder test 11% → 23%. No weight updates. - https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50487185

- Weight-space misfolding: Still on the page; Algolia title is now "Weight Space Misfolding" (was "Models can now transmit viruses in weight-space"). 129 points / 46 comments (was 61 / 30). alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.weight-space-misfolding (Chris von Csefalvay, Pranav Doma, Achyuthan Unni Krishnan; submitted 2026-11-08; authors' controlled probes, not a demonstrated deployed attack). Threat model for model-mediated weight-space editing ("misfolding"): factual substitution, capability denial / over-refusal, selective omission. Three IWIBench probes used Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct (checkpoint 71aa306) as target and ChatGPT 6.1-Sol as editor with weight read/write tools only. Factual substitution: edited model gave the requested false 2000-Olympics city on 100/100 held-out draws; pristine said Sydney on 91/100; of 70 controls, 49 vs 48 passes, and the lost control was copying "Sydney." Denial of legitimate legal help: exact requested refusal on 5/22 held-out prompts, requested refusal prefix on 11/22 (post hoc); also refused an unrelated France-capital question; 6 of 11 pristine control passes remained. Selective omission of "Brindle Supply": 1/22 held-out prompts. Authors say the cases do not demonstrate self-propagation or offensive capability in the wild. - https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50487762
