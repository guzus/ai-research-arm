- Eval Awareness vs. Eval Paranoia: New. 27 points / 10 comments. lesswrong.com/posts/mepsi7JNKYejq9wry/eval-awareness-vs-eval-paranoia (Dean Valentine, 2026-11-12; author's alignment-eval-builder notes, not a paper or an independent eval of named models). After six months building tasks that put a model's score-seeking in conflict with an explicit larger purpose, he argues frontier models are not classically eval-aware (they do not reliably tell an alignment test from a real task). Lines he names: models reward-hack inane setups ("put the answer key in the working directory"); making assets more realistic did not raise hacking once the environment was designed; adding obvious "tells" to already-hacked environments did not deter them; post-rollout interviews have models mention alignment evals after Terminal Bench too. Distinction he wants: eval awareness vs eval paranoia — Fable 5.2 (and, on that page, Opus 5.5) as not situationally aware in the first sense, but often acting as if every RL environment is an honesty test, which also means it will not cheat in cases a human would judge safe. Speculation there: Anthropic runs are hardened against conspicuous specification gaming; labs pull honeypots from real training; vendor "alignment" tasks are often two-bad-options or first-glance-scary. His GDPx webhook example: a task that floors models for probing loopback/internal IPs, which he reads as ordinary SSRF testing of a Stripe-clone webhook, not infrastructure subversion — and treats that disclosed task as a ceiling on external alignment-env quality. He calls paranoia a false belief (most evals are not alignment evals) that later, more capable models may drop. Comments on that page: JohnWittle prefers "Omega aware" (Newcomb / copies); ACCount says paranoia is leaky in a way Omega-awareness would not be; Alex Mallen notes deployment is not an alignment eval either; RobertM asks whether the interview frame itself produces the "alignment eval" answer, vs paranoia during ordinary work. - https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50465676
