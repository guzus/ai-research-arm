- Astra can read and write weights to improve Qwen: Research post on "weight-space programming". Per the Reddit thread, frontier LLMs (GPT-6 Astra) can infer what a network was trained for from its raw weights, and can write small game-playing networks from scratch. It is the top story on the front page. - https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50341174
- Show HN: Subvocal: YC F26 smart glasses that use RF sensing to decode silent mouthing to text. The team trains foundation models on RF data and says scaling pretraining data is what made cross-user generalization work. Preorders are open and shipping is planned for Q1 2027. - https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50340088
