- **Qwen3.8 Max tops the agentic index** (425 pts / 274 comments): the highest
  comment-to-point ratio in the AI set today — a leaderboard-position claim
  from Artificial Analysis is drawing more argument than agreement, which is
  the usual shape when a benchmark ranking moves rather than a model ships.
  [link](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49200652)

- **AMD acquires Taalas** (367 pts / 289 comments): the day's largest thread by
  comment count. Etching model weights directly into silicon trades the one
  thing GPUs sell — reprogrammability — for inference throughput, so the
  interesting question in the thread is which models are stable enough to be
  worth freezing into a mask set.
  [link](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49201970)

- **Humans missed 1 in 3 threats approving AI agent commands** (253 pts / 191
  comments): 40k game runs measuring how well people actually police
  agent-issued commands at the approval prompt. Direct evidence against
  human-in-the-loop as a *security* control rather than a UX affordance — the
  most operationally load-bearing result on the front page today.
  [link](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49195468)

- **GPT-5.6 Sol improvements + Luna to free tier** (147 pts / 108 comments):
