- Energy-Based Reasoners: New, and the largest AI-by-points story. 243 points / 37 comments. energy-based-reasoners.github.io plus arXiv 2611.08977 (William Xingxu Chen and Zeyu Michael Li, Duke; Alexi Gladstone and Heng Ji, UIUC; Yilun Du, Harvard; authors’ benches vs TRM / EqR / FPRM / GRAM / Looped flows, not an independent eval). HN title stamps “385x more efficient inference”; the project page and abstract stamp 345× less inference compute than the next-best model. Energy-Based Reasoners (EBRs) learn an energy \(E_\theta(z;x)\) over latent reasoning states and treat looped updates as gradient descent on that energy: the value scores candidates (no external verifier), the gradient refines and stops, and the lowest-energy candidate is selected. Training: state-based Explorative Modeling (SXM). 1.8M parameters. Sudoku-Extreme single-sample 99.4% vs Looped flows 97.9 / FPRM 94.2 / EqR 93.0 / TRM 87.4 / GRAM 86.6 (those baselines 5–10M); 5 samples 99.9% vs EqR 99.8(128) / PTRM 98.8(100) / Looped flows 99.3(5) / GRAM 96.1(20). Maze-Hard single-sample 87.4 vs FPRM 87.0 / Looped flows 86.7 / TRM 85.3; 5 samples 88.3 vs Looped flows 86.9(5) / PTRM 86.7(100). Adaptive stopping (stop when \(\|\nabla E\|<\tau\)) keeps 99.9% with 10.9× fewer FLOPs than a fixed-depth EBR and 345× fewer than EqR. Test-time scaling chart: EqR’s best accuracy with 7× fewer iterations and 43× fewer samples (GRAM/TRM from GRAM’s paper; EqR from its released checkpoint; mean ± SD over three seeds). Multi-solution, 20 samples: N-Queens Cov@20 98.1% (8×8) / 92.0% (10×10) vs GRAM 89.7 / 57.5 vs TRM 22.6 / 2.0; pass@1 100 / 99.5 vs GRAM 99.5 / 89.7 vs TRM 66.8 / 17.5.
