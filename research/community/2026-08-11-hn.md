### Notable Discussions

- **As AI eats the web, the internet's collective memory is disappearing**
  (690 pts, the snapshot's top AI thread, up from 493 at 13:48 UTC): The
  Walrus piece on Google search quality argues LLM-generated content is
  flushing human-authored pages out of the index. The 742-comment thread is
  the loudest of the day — recurring themes are link rot, SEO collapse,
  platform paywalls, and the accelerating loss of the web as a durable
  archival corpus for both humans and future model training —
  [link](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49250836)

- **Mark Zuckerberg attacks 'closed' AI rivals as Meta returns to open models**
  (595, up from 554): Meta doubling down on the open-weights line while
  taking shots at OpenAI/Google. The 561-comment debate (one of the day's
  biggest) splits on whether this is genuine strategy or regulatory framing —
  weight-level openness vs. training-data transparency, and what a Meta
  pivot back toward open releases means for the competitive landscape —
  [link](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49243880)

- **Show HN: Needle2: 14MB agentic LLM for phones, wearables, smart home and
  robots** (472, up from 407): A remarkably small agentic LLM targeting edge
  devices. The 162-comment thread's core tension: whether a 14MB model can
  carry real agentic capability on constrained hardware, with the author
  fielding questions on quantization, context limits, and tool-use tradeoffs —
  [link](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49246804)

- **H3-metal – Native MiniMax-H3 inference for Apple Silicon** (391, up from
  347): antirez's native Metal implementation of MiniMax-H3 inference. The
  thread covers the H3 / hybrid-linear-attention architecture's memory
  advantages over dense transformers and how much of the speedup is kernel
  engineering versus architecture —
  [link](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49252179)

- **How Claude marks AI-generated content** (351, up from 262): Anthropic's
  explainer on labeling AI output. The 321-comment discussion centers on the
  practical limits of provenance: watermark robustness, metadata-based
  disclosure, and how easily markings can be stripped or evaded —
  [link](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49250109)

- **Stealing Reasoning Traces from Proprietary LLM APIs** (189): New security
  work on extracting hidden reasoning traces from closed-model APIs. The
  71-comment thread debates whether the disclosed attack is a real model
  leak or more of a side-channel curiosity, and what it implies for how
  providers hide their chain-of-thought —
  [link](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49257876)

- **Nvidia Nemotron 3.5 Lightning** (83) and **LFM2.5 2.6B** (133): two open
  model releases on HuggingFace this cycle — NVIDIA's 30B-A3B NVFP4 Lightning
  variant, and Liquid AI's LFM2.5-2.6B claiming competitiveness with models
  4x its size. Small-model benchmark skepticism is the running theme across
  both threads — [link](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49257947)
