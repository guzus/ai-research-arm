### Notable Discussions

- **Muse Glimmer – 30B-parameter open agentic model** (1078 pts, the
  snapshot's top story and the continuing top AI thread, up from 920 at
  21:03 UTC): Meta Research's open-weights model "optimized for always-on
  local agent workflows," published the same day Meta re-committed to open
  models. The 592-comment thread keeps arguing over the always-on/local
  framing, real-world inference costs at 30B, and how an open-weights model
  stacks up against API-hosted frontier coding agents —
  [link](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49241679)

- **Zuckerberg attacks 'closed' AI rivals as Meta returns to open models**
  (441, up from 223): Meta hitting OpenAI and Google as "closed" while
  doubling down on its open-weights Muse line. The 419-comment thread splits
  on whether the open push is substance or regulatory strategy, and pairs
  with the Muse Glimmer release to form the day's dominant theme —
  [link](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49243880)

- **Show HN: Needle2 – 14MB agentic LLM for phones, wearables, smart home
  and robots** (253, up from 12): a cactuscompute entry shipping a 14MB
  agentic LLM for constrained devices. The 98-comment thread debates what a
  sub-20MB agent can realistically do, quantization/tradeoffs at that size,
  and whether the "agentic" label holds up vs. a compact instruction-following
  model — [link](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49246804)

- **Humanising LLM Outputs Is Dumb** (187, up from 80): the counter-essay
  argues the vogue for stripping artifacts, hedging, and persona-y warmth out
  of model output is a regression. The 115-comment thread is a split take on
  whether fluency-without-flavor is a feature (native-instrument feel) or a
  misfeature (robotic sameness) — [link](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49243474)

- **H3-metal – Native MiniMax-H3 inference for Apple Silicon** (135): antirez
  ships native H3-inference on Apple Silicon. The 16-comment thread centers on
  linear-attention/state-space relevance to local inference and what "native"
  buys over existing backends — [link](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49252179)

- **What's the best programming language for coding agents?** (112): danluu
  looks at tokens-per-LOC across languages to pick agent-friendly languages.
  The 76-comment thread debates whether token density or tooling dominates
  agent effectiveness, and whether the answer generalizes past coding-agent
  benchmarks — [link](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49245936)

- **Letter to Governor Abbott on responsible AI infrastructure in Texas**
  (106): OpenAI's letter on siting AI infrastructure responsibly in Texas.
  The 190-comment thread is the day's policy argument: grid/power capacity,
  state-vs-local siting authority, and whether an AI-lab letter is a genuine
  proposal or positioning — [link](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49244308)
