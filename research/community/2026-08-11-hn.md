### Notable Discussions

- **As AI eats the web, the internet's collective memory is disappearing**
  (831 pts, up from 690 at 17:14 UTC — still the snapshot's top AI thread):
  The Walrus piece on Google search quality argues LLM-generated content is
  flushing human-authored pages out of the index. The 838-comment thread
  remains the loudest of the day — recurring themes are link rot, SEO
  collapse, platform paywalls, and the accelerating loss of the web as a
  durable archival corpus for both humans and future model training —
  [link](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49250836)

- **OpenAI's head of ethics leaves less than a year after joining** (142,
  up from 72): An FT-reported departure less than a year in. The 238-comment
  thread (one of the day's biggest) splits on whether the timing signals a
  substantive ethics-office rift or routine leadership churn, with a running
  debate on how much real authority an ethics function has inside an AI
  frontier lab — [link](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49257160)

- **Go is an ideal language for AI-assisted software engineering** (185):
  Google's argument that Go's simplicity and strong typing make it
  particularly well-suited to AI-assisted development. The 233-comment thread
  is skeptical and lively — defenders and detractors argue over whether the
  thesis generalizes or just flatters Go's tooling, and what "AI-assisted"
  workflows actually reward in a language —
  [link](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49261133)

- **Stealing Reasoning Traces from Proprietary LLM APIs** (381, up from 189):
  New security work on extracting hidden reasoning traces from closed-model
  APIs. The 151-comment thread debates whether the disclosed attack is a real
  model leak or more of a side-channel curiosity, and what it implies for how
  providers hide their chain-of-thought —
  [link](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49257876)

- **Show HN: Needle2: 14MB agentic LLM for phones, wearables, smart home and
  robots** (500, up from 472): A remarkably small agentic LLM targeting edge
  devices. The 169-comment thread's core tension: whether a 14MB model can
  carry real agentic capability on constrained hardware, with the author
  fielding questions on quantization, context limits, and tool-use tradeoffs —
  [link](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49246804)

- **Nvidia's Risky Business** (248, up from 156): Stratechery's analysis of
  Nvidia's position — the 109-comment thread weighs CUDA moat versus customer
  concentration, hyperscaler in-house silicon, and what a demand
  normalization does to the AI capex story —
  [link](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49255710)

- **Mojo 1.0** (178): Modular declaring Mojo 1.0. The 78-comment thread reads
  it against the backdrop of AI-native languages; the counterpoint — whether
  Mojo's promise on top of MLIR/LLVM has yet translated into broad practical
  adoption — [link](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49261128)
