Headline: Fable's total bill fell 5% but the equal-weighted per-task mean rose 1%; DeepSeek rose 5% overall and 17% per task. Terminal output was only ~11% of Fable input tokens (40% for DeepSeek); cache reads are 1/10 (Fable) and 1/30 (DeepSeek) of regular input; DeepSeek RTK runs had 7% less input per turn but 18% more turns. - [AI researchers debate how close we are to recursive self-improvement](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49665711): new since 22:37 — Dwarkesh Patel with John Schulman (Thinking Machines chief scientist; OpenAI co-founder / RLHF), Beren Millidge (Zyphra CTO), and Charlie O'Neill (Baseten head of model training) (74/61, posted ~21:35 UTC, newly on the front page). Steelmanning why 2036 is not a superintelligence world if politics is held fixed: Millidge's default is a persistent sim-to-real / no-meta-learning spark — models crush every benchmarked environment and still fail to generalize the next objective; Schulman expects the "this is AGI / then it feels dumb a month later" cycle to keep repeating because judgment and self-checking remain the bottleneck, so even a model that writes far more code does not 100× research; O'Neill's risk is that transformer + RL is not close enough to the global-optimum learner, and the next discontinuity (the kind that kept Moore's law and then pretrain-then-RL looking like a straight line) may be one the current recipe cannot discover. Shared load-bearing split: well-specified inner-loop RSI (lower pretrain loss, raise env reward) could still 10× the current paradigm — catching Kaplan annealing mistakes, muP width/LR, and similar bits that were sitting in the data — but proposing the *next* objective is a different skill, and that is the Moravec-style hard part. Newest RSI / "are we near the ceiling" thread.

Remaining genomics-model / "run it once for everyone" thread.

Static-analysis boundary is named: zero inbound edges means zero *observed* references, not runtime unreachability. Newest agent-tooling / semantic-index Show HN, sitting just above the 10-point floor.
