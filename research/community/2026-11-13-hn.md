- JEPA / LeWM experiments: New. 116 points / 24 comments. haddyalchaer.com/jepa (Haddy Alchaer; author's 600-experiment writeup, not an independent eval). LeWM (Maes / Le Lidec / Scieur / LeCun / Balestriero, arXiv 2603.19312) on Push-T. Author's numbers: vanilla LeWM 78% vs exact-physics predictor 77% on 250 long-horizon (75-step) tasks with expert subgoals every 25 steps; as published, 22% planning all 75 steps at once vs 80% with 25-step subgoals (exact physics all-at-once 42%). A latent memory graph of ~460,000 demonstration embeddings plus a cost on inter-demo jumps reached 85%, matching expert subgoals. Standard Push-T goals sit on paths demonstrations already travel (84% already had a near-identical stored state); on 250 fresh standard episodes at 50 steps, 44% vs 68% on the official 50. Harder test (start from one demo, goal from another; block on target for last 10 steps): memory-graph 75% standard / 45% harder. Deleting stored states near the goal dropped success 93% → 32%; filling the gap with shifted / rotated / mirrored demo segments recovered 85% (harder test 45% → 56%). Scoring the pusher in the goal image: exact-physics planner 9/50 with pusher vs 39/50 block-only; searching 16 pusher placements doubled LeWM on the harder test 11% → 23%. No weight updates. - https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50487185
