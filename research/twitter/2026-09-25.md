First, the headline mixes hardware generations: an H100 and a GB300 are not the same unit, so a "1 million GPUs" total that is substantially H100/H200 overstates delivered training capacity relative to what the GB300 share alone provides. Second, and more binding — on standard GB300 NVL72 rack power of roughly 130 kW per 72 GPUs, 440,000 GB300s alone imply on the order of 0.8 GW, and the full 1.44M-GPU fleet lands somewhere near 1.2 GW of continuously available power by our estimate. Chips can be shipped in 90 days; that much firm power and cooling generally cannot. "Online" is therefore the word carrying all the risk, since racked, energized, and training-ready are three different states and the guidance does not separate them.</p>
