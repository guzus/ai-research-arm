### Quick hits

V4-Pro is unchanged, making this a cheap-agent play rather than a frontier push. ([@WesRoth](https://x.com/WesRoth/status/2084096917269524504))
- @teortaxesTex reports V4-Flash now exposes four effort modes (nothink/low/high/max) but that low and high score about the same at roughly 2× the cost — the effort dial is not buying intelligence, and he's asked ArtificialAnalysis to bench the tiers separately. ### 🚩 Skeptic's corner
- The circulating "96% win rate vs Gemini 3.1 Pro, 94% vs Opus 4.8" figures for Qwen3.8-Max are vendor headline scores, not independent evaluation, and the comparison set excludes Opus 5 and Kimi-K3 entirely. Framing the result as beating Anthropic rests on Opus 4.8, a superseded model; against Fable 5 the same table drops to 37 of 49. ([@TeksEdge](https://x.com/TeksEdge/status/2084113671916343382), [@scaling01](https://x.com/scaling01/status/2084101963818365432))
