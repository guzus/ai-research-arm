## 00:00 UTC

**Cycle summary**: OpenAI now lets ChatGPT Pro and Business subscribers claim monthly API credits, up to $500. Microsoft reportedly grew Azure 48% to $33.2B while spending less on data centers than it had forecast, GitHub launched Copilot Fusion and four other agent features at Universe'26, and Samsung reportedly expects the memory shortage to be worse in 2027 than this year.

### Quick hits
- **OpenAI is giving ChatGPT Pro and Business subscribers monthly API credits.** Pro tiers get $100, $200 or $500 a month. Business plans get $20 per paid Standard seat or $100 per paid Premium seat, pooled up to $500 per workspace. @OpenAIDevs announced it ("up to $500 per month in API credits to build apps"), and @testingcatalog posted the tier details. The move links consumer subscriptions to API usage, which takes direct aim at developers who would otherwise pay for a separate API account. [@testingcatalog](https://x.com/testingcatalog/status/2115586101444329948), [@OpenAIDevs via RT](https://x.com/kimmonismus/status/2115584717374599595)
- **Microsoft's fiscal Q1: Azure revenue reportedly grew 48% YoY to $33.2B, ahead of the $32.3B estimate.** Reported data-center spending of $45.2B came in below Microsoft's own forecast of more than $50B, which it blamed on construction delays. Free cash flow reportedly fell 27% to $18.6B, and contracted future revenue was $677B, up 73% YoY but flat on the quarter. Microsoft did not give a new figure for paid Copilot seats; the last one was 30M in July. The stock reportedly rose about 4% after hours, the opposite of Alphabet's reaction a few hours earlier. These figures come from one detailed recap, and this run did not retrieve Microsoft's release. [@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2115554140764197143)
- **GitHub announced Copilot Fusion at Universe'26, which coordinates several models on one task, alongside Copilot Projects, Automations, a Security extension and "Copilot dreaming".** Dreaming proposes edits to Copilot's memories, repository instructions and agent skills. @kimmonismus says Fusion delivers "roughly the intelligence of Opus 5.5 at 50% less cost". GitHub has not published that comparison in this snapshot, so treat it as one person's reading of the launch. [@testingcatalog](https://x.com/testingcatalog/status/2115582968265670692), [@kimmonismus](https://x.com/kimmonismus/status/2115568792772432016)

The only source is a one-line headline from @jukan05 during Samsung's earnings window, with no numbers attached. [@jukan05](https://x.com/jukan05/status/2115605838656352630)
- **OpenAI says ChatGPT and ChatGPT Work will merge, but not yet.** Some users saw a pop-up announcing the merge. OpenAI's Tibo says it went out by mistake: the plan is to combine the two "at some point this year" and "proceed carefully". Earlier claims that the merge had already happened are wrong. [@testingcatalog](https://x.com/testingcatalog/status/2115599706843758927)

## 09:00 UTC

**Cycle summary**: On its Q3 call, Samsung said 2027 HBM is sold out at its target prices and that long-term agreements could cover 60–70% of its capacity through 2030. The Information reports that Chinese labs have moved their distillation of US models from Claude to OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra. ServiceNow fell 19% after hours even though it beat its targets.

### Quick hits
- **Samsung says 2027 HBM supply is already sold out and that prices were settled "at our target levels".** On the Q3 call, memory EVP Jaejune Kim also said Samsung has signed long-term supply agreements (LTAs) with 10 major customers and is negotiating with 10 more. He said 60–70% of total production capacity will likely be under LTAs through 2030, and that tight supply should last into 2028. This adds figures to the 00:00 "worse in 2027" headline and is similar to SK hynix's 14-customer LTA disclosure yesterday. @jukan05 reads "target levels" as NVIDIA accepting Samsung's HBM4 price, which earlier Korean reports put in the mid-to-high $4 per Gb range. That reading is an inference; Samsung did not say it. [@jukan05 (HBM)](https://x.com/jukan05/status/2115660598470222036), [@jukan05 (LTAs)](https://x.com/jukan05/status/2115624383750582414), [@jukan05 (2028)](https://x.com/jukan05/status/2115622336397443263)

The claim about Kimi comes from one outlet's reporting, passed on by two accounts. Moonshot has not responded in this snapshot. [@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2115643355522896283), [@kimmonismus](https://x.com/kimmonismus/status/2115610660470186480)

[@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2115645490352259252)
