      <p><strong>Counter / contradicting:</strong> $41 million is a demand, not a likely award: $150,000 is the U.S. statutory ceiling for willful infringement, and courts rarely land there on every work. Concord publishers have been litigating Claude lyrics since 2023, so this is another caption in an existing fight, not a new legal theory. A 2025 ruling in Bartz v. Anthropic treated training on lawfully purchased books as fair use while treating pirated library copies as a separate problem; Anthropic will lean on that split. Output claims — that Claude still rewrites songs "for a modern audience" after lyric filters — are the part that sits outside the training-fair-use debate, and those still have to be proved song by song.</p>
      <p><strong>Context:</strong> Music publishers and labels have already sued Anthropic, Suno and others over lyrics and training. An Elvis-branded caption raises the public profile more than it changes the doctrine.</p>
