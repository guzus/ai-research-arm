### 🚩 Skeptic's corner
- **"Unconditional Integer Factorization in Subexponential Time," described as a Google/Gemini 4 Cogentic paper and tagged #Cryptography.** The post doesn't support reading this as a threat to RSA. Rigorous, zero-error (Las Vegas) randomized factoring in subexponential time has been known since Lenstra and Pomerance in 1992, and the number field sieve used in practice is already subexponential. Any new contribution would have to be in what exactly is proven, possibly the Lean 4 proof itself, not a new attack. Until the paper is read, treat this as a formalization result. [@semasocial](https://x.com/semasocial/status/2125992267572027498)
