A cheap test that still separates configurations has not saturated. The deeper problem is that the proposed successor is worse as a measurement instrument, not better: one run, one model, no baseline, no rubric, and an output its own author calls janky. Its value is an existence proof of stamina, not a comparison. Read the second half of the post rather than the first — the multimodal/gameplay audit gap is a falsifiable claim about what frontier models still cannot do, and it survives the fact that the framing around it is loose.</p>
