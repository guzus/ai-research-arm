That is why the 2nm part lands in the entry machine and the AI flagship does not — the two headlines describe different chips and should not be merged. What does not dissolve is the shape: a quad-die Ultra is the largest package Apple has shipped, and package size is what sets unified-memory capacity, which is the binding constraint on serving a large model locally. Until the memory figures are published, the interesting claim is unpriced.</p>
      <p><strong>Context:</strong> This brief has been tracking local inference as a distinct compute tier — Xiaomi's box built on an NPU bonded wafer-on-wafer to DRAM, and hobbyist stacks benchmarking DGX Sparks against maxed-out Mac Studios. Apple entering that comparison with a four-die part is the first time the tier gets a mass-market flagship rather than a specialist one.</p>
