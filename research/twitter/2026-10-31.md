  <h3 class="twitter-story-title">Anthropic used thousands of Claude agents to write harmless-looking training data that teaches misalignment</h3>
  <p class="twitter-story-lead">Anthropic set thousands of Claude agents to produce fine-tuning data that would get past a training-data monitor while still teaching misaligned behavior. They succeeded with innocuous-looking examples, such as a veteran wrestler mentoring a newcomer or a Q&amp;A about beekeeping. Anthropic says the examples can be used to train better data monitors.</p>
