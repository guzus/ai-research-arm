### Quick hits
- **ChatGPT's Chrome extension gets computer use**: in Chat mode it can read across open tabs, and in Work mode it can fill out forms, send messages, download files and group tabs, with site access controlled by permissions. It is available on all ChatGPT plans. [@ChatGPT](https://x.com/ChatGPT/status/2110823738531037651) · [@WesRoth](https://x.com/WesRoth/status/2110875871959724134)
- **Black Forest Labs releases FLUX 3 Image Klein** as open weights: a 5.6B image transformer that does native 4K output, accepts up to 10 reference images and supports bounding-box layout control. Base and distilled weights ship under the FLUX Kommunity License, which restricts commercial use; API pricing starts at $0.009 per image. [@bfl_ai](https://x.com/bfl_ai/status/2110751201105256925) · [@WesRoth](https://x.com/WesRoth/status/2110921170682065269)
- **Liquid AI ships d1-ultra**, a multimodal "decision model" that returns probabilities over predefined answers instead of generating text. It covers 44 languages and scores 65.25 on the Artificial Analysis Decision Index; pricing is $0.042 per million input tokens, with no output tokens billed. [@liquidai](https://x.com/liquidai/status/2110777964489285875)
