## 11:00 UTC

**Cycle summary**: HojoAI released Hojo-ASR-Multi-V1, an Apache-2.0 multilingual speech recognizer that uses a Qwen3-4B decoder and reports 3.54 average WER across five languages. Separately, a new open-source FDE-Gym benchmark finds the best model solved only 14 of 95 real customer-delivery tasks when given raw requests.

### Quick hits
- **HojoAI open-sources Hojo-ASR-Multi-V1**, a multilingual conversational ASR model that pairs an acoustic encoder with a Qwen3-4B LLM decoder. It is Apache 2.0 on Hugging Face, installs as the `hojo-asr` Python package, runs locally, and claims 3.54 average WER across 5 languages. The number is self-reported and the test conditions aren't published yet, so treat it as unverified against existing ASR stacks ([@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2110995927716778128)).
- **FDE-Gym benchmark tests whether models can deliver real business outcomes, not just code**: 95 problems across 20 customer scenarios and 18 industries, with guidance levels running from L0 (detailed requirements) to L3 (raw customer request). 13 model configurations from 7 providers were tested. At L3 the best one averaged a 0.56 net score and solved only 14 of 95. Released open-source by DeepLethe with collaborators from 23 institutions ([@deepletheai](https://x.com/deepletheai/status/2111050388308898133)).
