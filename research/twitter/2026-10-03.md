## 05:00 UTC

**Cycle summary**: SemiAnalysis estimates Nvidia's custom HBM on Feynman cuts the GPU die area spent on memory controllers and PHYs from about 16% on Rubin to about 4%. Generality Labs results show ExploitBench scores swing widely with the agent harness, and xAI released an experimental TypeScript SDK.

### Quick hits
- **Nvidia custom HBM frees die area for compute.** SemiAnalysis estimates HBM controllers and PHYs take ~16% of the Rubin GPU die, falling to ~4% on Feynman with NVHBM, because the memory controller moves onto the HBM base die and the wide standard PHY gives way to Nvidia's NV-HBI die-to-die link. It says Samsung's custom HBM4 interface is ~60% smaller than its standard PHY. Nvidia's own claims are up to 25% more compute area, up to 30% more bandwidth and 15% lower HBM power than standard HBM4E. These are vendor and analyst estimates, not measured silicon. [@SemiAnalysis_](https://x.com/SemiAnalysis_/status/2106217837643575607)
- **ExploitBench scores depend heavily on the harness.** New Generality Labs results, shared by @teortaxesTex, show the same model scoring very differently across harnesses. DeepSeek V4.1 Flash trails GLM-5.3 Flash and does best inside Claude Code, with Codex second. Generality also reports GLM-5.3 Flash beating Mythos Preview's ExploitBench scores with enough test-time compute. Cross-lab cyber-capability comparisons that don't hold the harness fixed are not apples-to-apples. [@teortaxesTex](https://x.com/teortaxesTex/status/2106195529717714964)
- **xAI ships an experimental TypeScript SDK** (`npm install @xai-official/sdk`) covering text, voice, image and video generation, announced by @ericzakariasson and labelled experimental. [@ericzakariasson via RT](https://x.com/RayFernando1337/status/2106202423492829467)
- **TSMC reportedly in talks to join Musk's Terafab.** Culpium reports that TSMC is looking to participate in Terafab, that recent rumours of TSMC expanding in Texas are tied to it, and that collaboration with Intel is stalling. @jukan05 expects a dual-source arrangement rather than Intel dropping out. Neither company has confirmed. [@jukan05](https://x.com/jukan05/status/2106149596418470138)

### 🚩 Skeptic's corner
- **The viral "model nerfing" benchmark claims are being disputed.** @theo says BridgeMind's nerfbench does not document how its tests work. He also says BridgeMind's April Opus 4.6 "nerf" claim rested on 6 of 30 benchmark tests, with 2 failures. Theo has offered to match BridgeMind's $10,000 toward independent benching. Until the methodology and full runs are published, treat claims that a model was "nerfed" as unproven. Run-to-run variance, harness changes and cache behaviour can all explain the swings.
