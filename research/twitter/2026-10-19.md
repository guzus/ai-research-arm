## 04:00 UTC

**Cycle summary**: Elon Musk replied to Anthropic's new Public Benefit Fund saying he will give shares "directly to the public" instead, and separately said SpaceX could not rent inference compute from AWS, GCP or anyone else to take load off its training clusters.

### Quick hits
- **Elon Musk answered Anthropic's Public Benefit Fund announcement by saying he would rather give shares "directly to the public," adding "This is what I will be doing."** He did not say which company's shares (SpaceX/xAI or Tesla), how many, or when, so for now this is a stated intention, not a plan. @AndrewCurran_ points out that OpenAI floated a similar Public Wealth Fund dividend for US citizens in April. Critics of Anthropic's design, including @ns123abc, note that the fund is a wholly owned LLC rather than a 501(c)(3) charity, and that the board decides both who receives distributions and whether to add up to 2% more equity for each 50% rise in valuation. [@elonmusk](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2111980598084698114) · [@AndrewCurran_](https://x.com/AndrewCurran_/status/2111994636164345988) · [@ns123abc](https://x.com/ns123abc/status/2111981380091486369)
- **Musk says SpaceX has tried and failed to rent inference compute from AWS, GCP "or anyone" so it could move load off its training clusters.** That is a first-hand claim of tight inference capacity, even for a buyer of SpaceX's size. He gives no figures, dates or workload details, and the post he was responding to isn't visible, so treat it as anecdote. [@elonmusk](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2112017286764659176)
- **T3 Code says over 60% of its users who used Codex in the past two weeks also used Claude Code.** Founder @theo puts it as "the majority of Codex users are Claude Code users." This comes from one multi-agent client's own telemetry, so it says more about multi-homing among power users than about either tool's overall market share. [@theo](https://x.com/theo/status/2112035326625730981)

### Quick hits
- **Google Research open-sourced Harness², a framework in which agents rewrite the harness around a frozen model: system instructions, skills, hooks and memory.** An editor agent proposes two candidate edits per task, a composer agent keeps the edits that fixed failures, and a reflector agent saves verified lessons to a reusable library. Reported gains for Gemini 3.5 Flash are +8.4 on WorkBuddy-Bench and +7.5 on JobBench, and removing skills costs 5.3 points. All results are from the authors. [@_philschmid](https://x.com/_philschmid/status/2112151309365268955) · [@kimmonismus](https://x.com/kimmonismus/status/2112121074775441806)

Handing it every tool choice with no threshold cut success by 19 points. [@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2112107486098473471)
