      <p><strong>Counter / contradicting:</strong> The strongest counter is that this is not really industry-wide. That objection has force on enforcement but not on the record, since the Code of Practice signatures and OpenAI's own support-page language are the labs' documents rather than inference. A sharper technical objection also stands unanswered: a watermark robust enough to survive editing cannot be output-neutral, and nobody has said what happens when the model emits code, where token choice is semantically load-bearing. Treat OpenAI's position as stated intent, not shipped capability.</p>
      <p><strong>Context:</strong> Anthropic's move was read yesterday as a US frontier lab voluntarily going first on provenance. On this evidence the better frame is a compliance deadline that has already passed, with Anthropic the first to say so publicly.</p>
