Later in the cycle, <a href="https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2107914219408736463">@rohanpaul_ai</a> said Copilot will hand routine coding from Claude Haiku 4.5 to Microsoft's MAI-Code-1.1-Flash, a 137B-total / 6.8B-active model running on Nvidia-powered PCs with no inference charge. The relay gives a 256K context window at 3-bit quantization, claims parity with full precision on SWE-Bench Verified and Terminal-Bench 2.1, and names the Surface Laptop Ultra (from $2,599) and Surface RTX Spark Dev Box (from $5,999) as the hardware. It also says Microsoft Execution Containers (MXC) are now generally available on Windows 11.</p>
      <p><strong>Counter / contradicting:</strong> A 10× budget cut can be general cost control rather than an Anthropic-specific dump; the "non-Anthropic" steer is The Information's characterization. The Copilot details are one relay, and MAI-Code-1-Flash was already reaching GitHub Copilot earlier, so 1.1-Flash may be a point release bundled with a Windows-on-Arm hardware launch. Still, a $10K cap plus an on-device default for routine coding is a concrete step away from paying frontier APIs for everyday work.</p>
      <p><strong>Context:</strong> On Oct. 5 The Information reported that Microsoft had already cut expected internal Anthropic spend by more than a third. Today's numbers put a monthly dollar figure on that shift, and they land the same afternoon Anthropic cut Haiku's price.</p>

### Quick hits
- **Google opened SynthID Detector to everyone.** Anyone can now check an image, video or audio file for a SynthID watermark from Google, OpenAI, Nvidia or Kakao, with Apple listed as coming soon. Google says it has watermarked 180 billion images and videos and more than 240,000 years of audio, and already handles about 1 million verification requests a day. [@GoogleDeepMind](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2107834249680499136) · [@GoogleAI](https://x.com/GoogleAI/status/2107836750257107133)
- **ChatGPT for Teens now applies automatically to accounts identified as under 18**, with protections on by default. OpenAI also previewed College Planner, a U.S. four-year-college application dashboard for deadlines, tasks and financial-aid steps. [@OpenAI](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2107909792945832070)
- **Perplexity open-sourced pplx-embed-v2-late**, 9B and 0.6B late-interaction embedding models that share one text/image space so a large index can be queried on-device. Aravind Srinivas reports 92.4% on MADQA and 64% on BrowseComp+, plus PDF-page search without OCR. Weights are on Hugging Face. [@AravSrinivas](https://x.com/AravSrinivas/status/2107871834205196784)
- **National Compute launched subsidized on-demand GPUs for .edu and .gov emails:** MI355X at $3 per GPU-hour and Crusoe B300 at $4 per GPU-hour. SemiAnalysis says much of the public-research capacity is in Crusoe's Denver site. [@SemiAnalysis_](https://x.com/SemiAnalysis_/status/2107910452474786148)
- **Unsloth released an open recipe to turn small open models into local Decision models.** It reports lifting Qwen3.5 0.8B from 20.7% to 74.3% aggregate accuracy across three decision benchmarks on 4GB VRAM. [@UnslothAI](https://x.com/UnslothAI/status/2107868866361930236)
- **Computer-use and browser-use toolsets are now built into Claude's Python and TypeScript SDKs.** The API returns the click or keystroke Claude wants; the SDKs run the loop and can drive Browser Use, Browserbase, E2B or Daytona. [@ClaudeDevs](https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2107925762720326090)
