Early in development, 20 of 200 “successful” exploits reached root through a <em>different</em> 0-day than the one assigned — they tightened checks, but it is a reminder that a root shell is not a unique bug. The $1.2 million is API credits on OpenAI’s own models, an admitted upper bound, not cash spent by an attacker. Technique details are withheld, so “novel” cannot be independently audited from the post.</p>
