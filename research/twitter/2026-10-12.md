Falcon may roll out to other U.S. sites. This is a single relay of a paywalled report, with no Entergy or AWS statement in this window. [@kimmonismus](https://x.com/kimmonismus/status/2109557215900131721)

Splitting 700 SWE-Bench Pro issues into multi-turn flows with checkable code questions roughly **doubled agent cost** and produced no stable change in success rate. [@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2109558942594404854) · [arXiv 2610.09633](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09633)
