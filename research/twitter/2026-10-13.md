AI-hardware trade has roughly tripled in value since 2019, mostly from higher prices, not more units shipped. [@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2109866495790702696)

The gap rises to 4.7× for Taiwan-made chips, and domestic suppliers covered 23% of China’s chip demand in 2022, up from 12% in 2016.
