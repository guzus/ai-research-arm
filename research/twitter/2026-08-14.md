      <p><strong>Counter / contradicting:</strong> The two numbers are not measured on the same footing, and that is the weakness nobody in the window raised. Those answer different questions, and the second is partly an artifact of prompt fatigue that the first is not exposed to. Anthropic has also published recall without publishing the false-positive rate, which is the number that determines whether auto mode is usable — a classifier that blocks aggressively would score well on 89% and be intolerable in practice. The defence is that the direction survives anyway: even generously restated, human approval at 14% is not a control, and removing a control that does not work is defensible. But "89% vs 14%" is a comparison between a benchmark and a behaviour, and should be read that way.</p>
