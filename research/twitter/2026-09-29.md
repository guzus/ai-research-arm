This matters disproportionately right now because the week's model launches were sold almost entirely on agentic coding benchmarks — Sonnet 5.5's Terminal-Bench figure, the Sol and Luna capability claims — and every one of those numbers is only meaningful if the harness actually denies the model the ability to retrieve the answer.
