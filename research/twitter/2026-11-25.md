## 08:00 UTC

**Cycle summary**: Claude artifacts can now be installed as desktop apps on Mac and Windows, and Claude's MCP support adds Tasks (GA) for long-running server-side agent loops. Andrew Curran reports that OpenAI posted its own VP ≠ VNP proof, dated November 6, plus nine new manuscripts to openai/math, after Anthropic's Claude-authored proof. This is a single report and has not been confirmed.

### Quick hits
- **Claude artifacts become installable desktop apps.** On Mac and Windows, you click "Install as app" on an artifact Claude built. The app can use your connectors and, with permission, send notifications or add a menu-bar item. You don't package, sign, or provide an API key; usage counts toward plan limits. It's in beta on all plans, and on Enterprise an owner must switch it on first. [@ClaudeDevs](https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2125315491522650299) · [details](https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2125315493771059685)
- **MCP turns two: Tasks is now GA in Claude, with Skills over MCP.** With Tasks, MCP servers can run long operations or full agent loops while Claude keeps working and collects the result later. File Uploads, which sends PDFs, spreadsheets, and images to a server without passing them through the context window, is "rolling out soon." Anthropic says Claude now has 10,000+ MCP connectors. [@ClaudeDevs](https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2125289794871963935) · [Tasks](https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2125290306539253760)
- **⚠ OpenAI reportedly answers Anthropic's VP ≠ VNP proof with its own.** Andrew Curran says OpenAI uploaded an unannounced, unsigned proof of the same conjecture to its openai/math repo, stating the result "was obtained on November 6, 2026." He later reported nine new manuscripts across two result families. Neither proof has been checked by an outside complexity theorist, and OpenAI has made no statement about it. [@AndrewCurran_](https://x.com/AndrewCurran_/status/2125289527531245998) · [update](https://x.com/AndrewCurran_/status/2125403132427731308)
- **Anthropic Fellows argue for a return to individual neurons as the unit of interpretability.** Measured by what each neuron contributes to outputs, rather than what makes it fire, neurons rarely add two unrelated concepts, mean roughly the same thing across most layers, and keep facts readable. With no extra training, neurons in open-weight models helped detect harmful text, steer behavior, and find hidden behaviors in finetuned models. OpenAI's @tszzl (roon) criticized the work for not citing Tigges et al.'s "neurons as an interpretable basis." [@AnthropicAI](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2125365268381929637) · [critique](https://x.com/tszzl/status/2125406396334403967)
- **ShellBench: the best agents still fail roughly 4 in 10 everyday chores.** The benchmark has 100 short requests (median 43 words) run inside messy folders of about 188 files. The original top score is GPT-6 Astra at 62.5%, ahead of Claude Fable 5.2 (61.9%) and GPT-6.1 Sol (61.4%). A Kaggle rerun reportedly topped out at 64.3%. These figures come from small accounts and are not yet checked against the primary leaderboard. [@Yashpatil](https://x.com/Yashpatil/status/2125504306413531288) · [Kaggle rerun](https://x.com/mhdfaran/status/2125502933030867297)
