The circulating paragraph says that after Sept 1 rumours that two Millennium Prize problems had been resolved, OpenAI launched an *evaluation* of its internal model on all remaining Millennium problems and some unnamed high-impact ones. Riemann and P vs NP are on that list; a solution is not. Clay still lists both as open. The Pro-pause causal leap is Curran’s, not OpenAI’s ([@AndrewCurran_](https://x.com/AndrewCurran_/status/2098197758872289638)).
