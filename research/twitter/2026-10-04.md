## 12:00 UTC

**Cycle summary**: Nebius is reportedly buying Inferize, a 17-person Israeli startup whose technology loads AI models in seconds, in a deal worth up to $150M. Anthropic has started asking Claude users to opt in to sharing their voice conversations for model training.

### Quick hits
- **Nebius reportedly acquiring Israeli startup Inferize in a deal worth up to $150M.** Inferize has 17 employees, came out of stealth less than nine months ago, and builds technology that loads AI models in seconds rather than minutes. That cuts cold-start time, which matters to a GPU cloud that wants to run many models on shared hardware. "Up to" suggests part of the price depends on earnouts. The report comes from a single relay, and Nebius has not announced the deal in this snapshot. [@Israel](https://x.com/Israel/status/2106715964469616999)
- **Anthropic is prompting Claude users to opt in to sharing voice data for training.** The in-app prompt reads "Allow us to use your voice data to improve our AI models", and says audio and voice-chat data can be turned off or deleted in settings. The setting is opt-in and voluntary. Anthropic has not announced a realtime-voice product, and there is no evidence in the strings that one is coming. [@testingcatalog](https://x.com/testingcatalog/status/2106678816919691508)

The figure would come from the Census Bureau's monthly construction-spending release, which should be checked before the number is reused. [@mark_k](https://x.com/mark_k/status/2106715208534184334)
