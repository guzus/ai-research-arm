## 01:00 UTC

**Cycle summary**: Tesla's AI5 self-driving chip reportedly won't reach vehicles at scale until 2028, because its design partner GUC gave Google's Axion and Microsoft's in-house chips priority for scarce TSMC 3nm capacity. Several posts also say the FCC has conditionally approved SpaceX to launch up to 1 million satellites for orbital AI computing.

### Quick hits
- **⚠ Tesla's AI5 chip reportedly slips to 2028 for large-scale vehicle rollout.** @jukan05 relays a report that GUC, the Taiwanese firm co-designing AI5, gave Google's Axion and Microsoft's in-house chips priority for TSMC's limited N3 (3nm) capacity. This is one relayed report. Neither Tesla, GUC nor TSMC has commented in this snapshot, so the 2028 date is not confirmed. If it holds, it shows hyperscaler custom silicon pushing other customers out of the queue for leading-edge capacity. [@jukan05](https://x.com/jukan05/status/2126087266863120688)
- **⚠ Several posts say the FCC has conditionally approved SpaceX to launch up to 1 million satellites for orbital AI data centers.** They describe deployment milestones that include 100,000 satellites by 2035 and a full constellation by 2040. The posts come from small accounts, and this snapshot has no FCC order or SpaceX post, so the numbers are unconfirmed. Even if approved, a licence is permission to launch, not proof the economics of compute in orbit work. [@StockLong_com](https://x.com/StockLong_com/status/2126127008136769794) · [@akinnwpqv](https://x.com/akinnwpqv/status/2126128292482982334)

### Quick hits

This snapshot has no OpenAI post confirming the 40% figure. If it holds, it amounts to an unannounced price increase for Plus and Pro users on the new model. [@LanceUlanoff](https://x.com/LanceUlanoff/status/2126285180298006713)

The region has borrowed more than $49B for data centers since 2023. Only this relay of the Bloomberg piece is in the snapshot. [@Sallaokkk](https://x.com/Sallaokkk/status/2126282890803859624)

Chariot AI, one of the first four teams the mission picked to build sovereign models, trained them from scratch in India. This snapshot has no model sizes, benchmarks or licence terms. [@c_aashish](https://x.com/c_aashish/status/2126283645774438535)
