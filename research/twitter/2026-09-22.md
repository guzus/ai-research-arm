Against that, @teortaxesTex relays that <a href="https://x.com/teortaxesTex/status/2102244275652034739">Qwen is exploring RSI — the model identifying its own weaknesses from task feedback, designing experiments, constructing data, then continuing training</a> — and his own summary is the tell: "Everyone is doing RSI now." @alexandr_wang reported <a href="https://x.com/alexandr_wang/status/2102243160059752832">Scale AI progress on an RSI benchmark</a>, then joked about "outpacing the frontier" while adding a note about responsible frontier training.       <p><strong>Counter / contradicting:</strong> The bullish reading is that four organisations converging on self-improving research in one night is itself the signal. The falsification is available in the snapshot and it cuts the other way: the single entry that published a measured delta reports 2.56% on one model, and the gap between its generic-LLM score (8%) and its domain-equipped score (68%) shows the gain comes from hand-built domain scaffolding, not from a model improving itself. That is automation of the experiment loop — which is what the OpenAI sentence also literally describes, and what large labs have run for years. The stronger inference, that this cluster demonstrates recursive self-improvement, requires a system directing its own research agenda, and no entry here claims that on the record.</p>
