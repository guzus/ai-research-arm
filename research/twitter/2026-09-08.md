### 🚩 Skeptic's corner
- A widely shared reconstruction claims OpenAI searched a mathematician's Codex session logs, found his most promising Navier–Stokes attempt, spun up 10,000 agents to finish it, then pressured him into silence ([@ns123abc](https://x.com/ns123abc/status/2097423705240428932), 343 likes / 49 RT). No part of that sequence appears in Buckmaster's own published statement, which alleges an authorship condition rather than log access, and OpenAI has explicitly denied accessing specific user data. The narrative is built entirely by reading OpenAI's de-identified-data caveat as a confession. That caveat is worth pressing; this reconstruction is not evidence.
- Reactions treating the announcement as settled — "LLMs are now officially superhuman at mathematics" ([@scaling01](https://x.com/scaling01/status/2097392440604844168)) — run ahead of an unreviewed proof whose forced-versus-unforced status has not been independently confirmed against the Clay problem statement.
