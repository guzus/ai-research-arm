**Cycle summary**: The FT reports that Oracle will rent about 100,000 advanced AI chips to Tencent for five years in a roughly $7B deal, with the hardware in Southeast Asian data centers that sit outside US export controls. Also this cycle: Axios reports Salesforce is buying Listen Labs for about $2B, Sensor Tower puts Meta's Muse at 5M downloads, and Newsom signed a package of California workplace-AI bills.

### Quick hits
- Salesforce agreed to buy AI customer-research startup Listen Labs for about $2B, according to Axios. Listen Labs raised a $69M Series B at a $500M valuation in January — [@Techmeme](https://x.com/Techmeme/status/2105532153530577048).
- Sensor Tower data shows Meta's Muse passing **5 million downloads** while holding #1 on the US App Store for 12 straight days. By the same data, ChatGPT took 56 days to reach that mark, Grok 103 and Claude 492 — [@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2105473128621318596). This is a sourced replacement for yesterday's unsourced 1.1M-install claim. Note that downloads are not the same as active users, even though some reposts say "users."
- Gov. Newsom posted a mock executive order "permanently declaring Artificial Intelligence 'Artificial Intelligence'" in California, a jab at the federal "Super Intelligence" renaming. The real news is the bills he signed with it. @rohanpaul_ai's summary says AB 1883 bans workplace AI that infers employees' emotions or collects neural data, and the package bars AI-only firing or discipline, requires disclosure of AI-caused mass layoffs, bans removing AI watermarks, and requires gene-synthesis companies to screen customers — [@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2105503788995879098).
- a16z data: the share of US households paying for AI rose from about 1% to **2.2%** in a year, and average subscriber spending reached $31/month in May 2026 — [@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2105494428953739575).
- *Looped Diffusion Transformer* runs shared Transformer blocks several times within each denoising step. The authors report it beats a model 6.5× larger on text-to-image benchmarks while using 4.9× less inference compute — [@arankomatsuzaki](https://x.com/arankomatsuzaki/status/2105521688083620347).
