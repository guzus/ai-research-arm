- **⚠ A new OS-Omni computer-use benchmark reportedly shows agents failing on phones.** Gemini 4 Argon reportedly scored 95.8% on Linux, above the 74.6% human score, but only 18.8% on iOS. Claude Opus 5.5 led overall at 68.5%, against 77.3% for humans. Two small accounts give the same numbers, and this snapshot has no paper or leaderboard link. - **⚠ Xiaomi reportedly released World Model 2.0 for autonomous driving and robotics.** It combines multi-view video generation, physical-interaction modeling and action planning, and was trained on 12.6M video clips with 1–7 camera views. Reported results: collisions fell from 1.2% to 0.4% across 1,000 closed-loop simulations, task completion reached 96.3%, and LIBERO Plus success averaged 75.0%. Xiaomi says it has already supplied 200,000+ synthetic clips to train its perception models. Only one post gives these figures, and no Xiaomi post is in the snapshot. ### Quick hits
- **⚠ Sakana AI will start pre-training its own foundation model, Sawara, this month.** CEO David Ha told TechCrunch that a smaller version for on-premises enterprise use is due in early 2027, with a larger one later. He says the plan grew out of Sakana's government work, and that it aims at defense and cyber work that needs on-premises systems and, longer term, manufacturing and industry. He also argued that foreign models may refuse questions about Japan. Two small accounts relay the interview, and there is no Sakana post in this snapshot. - **⚠ Taiwan raised its 2026 GDP growth forecast from 11.05% to 13.28% on Friday, citing AI demand.** If it holds, it would be the island's fastest growth since 1978. The government expects real private fixed investment to rise 14.90% this year, and it also raised its 2027 forecast from 6.04% to 6.51%. It named US export restrictions, tariffs and financing costs as risks. One account gives these figures, and the statistics office release itself is not in the snapshot. [@Realxvox](https://x.com/Realxvox/status/2126697623868285147)
