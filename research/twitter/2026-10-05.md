### Quick hits
- **Custom GPTs reportedly go away in December, with private "plugins" as the migration path.** Mollick says he can move his most-used GPT to a private plugin. He points out that GPTs were built to be shared, and that companies and educators spent years training staff to build them, so a private-only replacement strands that use. His post is based on an OpenAI notice shown in a screenshot. OpenAI's own deprecation post is not in this snapshot, so the exact date and the sharing terms are still unconfirmed.
