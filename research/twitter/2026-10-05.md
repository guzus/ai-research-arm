# Twitter/X AI Pulse — 2026-10-05

## 05:00 UTC

**Cycle summary**: Ethan Mollick says OpenAI's custom GPTs are going away in December, and the migration route, private "plugins", doesn't cover GPTs built for other people to use. Semafor reports the Tarbell Foundation will spend $10M on AI journalism over the next year, and per The Wire China, YMTC expects the NAND shortage to last three more years.

### Quick hits
- **Custom GPTs reportedly go away in December, with private "plugins" as the migration path.** Mollick says he can move his most-used GPT to a private plugin. He points out that GPTs were built to be shared, and that companies and educators spent years training staff to build them, so a private-only replacement strands that use. His post is based on an OpenAI notice shown in a screenshot. OpenAI's own deprecation post is not in this snapshot, so the exact date and the sharing terms are still unconfirmed. [@emollick](https://x.com/emollick/status/2106958444196413670) · [@emollick](https://x.com/emollick/status/2106961322902745511)
- **Semafor: the Tarbell Foundation will spend $10M on AI journalism over the next year.** The money includes a grant to a new outlet, Machine Gods. This is a scoop by Max Tani, relayed by Semafor editor Ben Smith and @AndrewCurran_. [@semaforben](https://x.com/semaforben/status/2106912237415706882) · [@AndrewCurran_](https://x.com/AndrewCurran_/status/2106922631220072887)
- **The Wire China: YMTC expects the NAND shortage to last another three years.** That is the view of China's own NAND maker, and it cuts against sell-side models that show NAND oversupply by the end of 2027. The source is a single relay of a paywalled report. YMTC also benefits from talking up its pricing power. [@jukan05](https://x.com/jukan05/status/2106940458761089227)
- **SpaceX reportedly keeps about 60% of its 10GW of compute for its own use and offers 40% to outside customers.** If the figure holds, the cloud business The Information said yesterday books billions of dollars a month in commitments is running on less than half the fleet. The claim comes from a screenshot introduced with "apparently", and neither the source nor a date is given. Treat it as unconfirmed. [@jukan05](https://x.com/jukan05/status/2106907946890457304)
- **Microsoft paper "Agensh" scales coding-agent teams with no central manager.** Agents claim their own tasks, and average scores rose at every step from 1 to 128 agents, including on the 5 hardest of ProgramBench's 200 tasks. The paper's title claims scaling to 1,024 agents. The relay doesn't give the compute cost per point gained. [@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2106950188011258367) · [paper link](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2106950194587963431)
- **SemiAnalysis says NVIDIA's SLURM support for non-NVIDIA chips has got worse since it bought SchedMD.** As a result, AMD built its own scheduler, "spur". SemiAnalysis uses this to question NVIDIA's pledge that Hugging Face will stay compute-agnostic after the $12.9B acquisition. This is an analyst's assessment, and it gives no specific SLURM regressions. [@SemiAnalysis_](https://x.com/SemiAnalysis_/status/2106942597532963194)
