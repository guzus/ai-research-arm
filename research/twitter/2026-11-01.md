# Twitter/X AI Pulse — 2026-11-01

## 00:00 UTC

**Cycle summary**: xAI has announced text watermarking for Grok and appears to be rolling it out globally. Separately, vLLM's @mgoin_ reports fused Hopper decode kernels that run up to 38% faster, and a WSJ-sourced post says only about $11B of OpenAI's sought-after $30B has come in.

### Quick hits
- **xAI announced on Oct 31 that it is adding invisible text watermarking to Grok, and the rollout appears to be global.** This confirms the in-app notice reported in the previous cycle. @AndrewCurran_ notes that xAI did not sign the EU Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-Generated Content, while Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Meta, Microsoft and Mistral did. By his count, ChatGPT in North America is now the only major text model left unwatermarked. He also posted the opt-out page. Still unclear: whether xAI will offer a public detector. [@AndrewCurran_](https://x.com/AndrewCurran_/status/2116654648082022668), [opt-out](https://x.com/AndrewCurran_/status/2116667692208074942)
- **vLLM maintainer @mgoin_ used an agent swarm to write about 10,000 lines of ultra-fused Hopper kernels that speed up decode by as much as 38%, SemiAnalysis reports.** The work targets H100/H200-class GPUs. SemiAnalysis says NVIDIA's own decode-specialized software has mostly gone to Blackwell Ultra and Rubin. "Up to" marks a best case, and no per-model benchmark table appeared in the snapshot. [@SemiAnalysis_](https://x.com/SemiAnalysis_/status/2116666571728863431)
- **Google DeepMind's Gemini-based PlanAssist is going to 12 more UK councils.** DeepMind says it has cut the time planning officers spend assessing householder applications by up to 58%. [@GoogleDeepMind](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2116524000977285321)
- **The Wall Street Journal reports that only about $11B of the $30B OpenAI set out to raise has come in so far, according to a summary post.** This adds to yesterday's FT report that the target was cut to about $20B at a $1.25T valuation, with UAE investors leading. The post says the rest is expected to come from a later offering for wealthy individuals. The WSJ article itself was not checked. [@techtactician](https://x.com/techtactician/status/2116671768081633729)
