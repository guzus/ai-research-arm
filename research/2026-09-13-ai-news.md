## Research Papers

Three findings from Saturday’s build that were **not** in yesterday’s selected set cleared a concrete-result bar.

**NCP-ArchPreview, an 8.9B latent-space language model, hit OLMo-3-7B’s final pretrain loss after 51.3% of the tokens and finished +2.45 macro / +5.99 GSM8K.** The Intern-NCP team — Cao, Lin, Zhou and colleagues (`2609.10715`, submitted 2026-09-09) — train next-token prediction jointly with Next Concept Prediction: a product-quantized concept vocabulary from hidden states, a Concept Module that predicts multi-token concepts, and feedback of those concepts into token generation. Trained on 5.73T Dolma-3 tokens. At 85% of the compute of a matched 8.9B NTP baseline it approaches that baseline’s loss. After pretrain, a 17M VQ update is a domain-adaptation interface; injecting concepts into a DFlash2 drafter lifts mean accepted length 4.17%. First-party numbers; no independent 8.9B reproduction is in this window. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10715))

**A phase-decoupled, model-calibrated power controller delivered +20.4% tokens/J at +3.5% mean end-to-end latency on disaggregated B200 serving, versus NVIDIA Max-Q’s +8.6% tokens/J at +5.2%.** Kim, Yoo, Ryu, Ha, Lee and Ryu (`2609.11133`, submitted 2026-09-10) put an SM-clock floor on the prefill lane and a calibrated power cap just above a measured cliff on decode. On 8×B200 serving Qwen3-Coder-480B FP8 under agentic load, that balanced mode Pareto-beats Max-Q on both axes. On Qwen3-235B-A22B NVFP4 every operating mode meets ITL-p99 in every repetition; both vendor profiles miss. A three-day run saves 32.3% of a lane pair’s electricity. Both models are MoE; a dense model recovers about 5× less, so the authors scope the claim to MoE serving. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11133))

**Mr.LHDR, a multimodal long-horizon deep-research bench, has the strongest evaluated system at 43.1% overall accuracy and 34.3% strict accuracy.** Guo, Cao, Liang, Chang and colleagues (`2609.11318`, submitted 2026-09-10) build each question from a hidden node-relation graph: mean 12.1 necessary intermediate conclusions, dependency depth 10.4, plus at least one image, map, PDF, logo, chart, table, or video frame that changes the state. Grades final answers *and* intermediate conclusions (OA / SA / CS / DACS). Strip images: DACS −12.6. Strict accuracy falls as chains lengthen. Final-answer accuracy overstates complete research success; the bottleneck is dependency-consistent multimodal evidence. First-party board. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11318))
