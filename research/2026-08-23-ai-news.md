## Breaking News

- **OpenAI cuts GPT-5.6 "Sol" pricing by 20%+** — On August 21, 2026, OpenAI dropped API pricing for its GPT-5.6 Sol model by more than 20%, to $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens, with the promotional pricing holding through at least November 21, 2026. ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-6/))
- **Z.ai holds back GLM-5.3 open weights after the model found 1,000+ real security bugs** — Z.ai's open coding model GLM-5.3 uncovered more than a thousand genuine security vulnerabilities in widely used software during training/testing. The company says the model's cybersecurity capability grew faster than planned, and it is delaying the public weight release by roughly two weeks for extra safety review and hardening. ([unrot.co](https://unrot.co/blogs/today-top-ai-news-august-23-2026))
- **Nvidia research: the agent "harness," not the base model, is the deciding factor on long-horizon tasks** — In research published August 21, 2026, Nvidia showed that how an AI agent's surrounding tooling/harness is engineered matters more than the underlying model's raw capability for extended, multi-step tasks. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/21/nvidia-just-showed-that-the-harness-not-the-ai-model-is-now-the-real-hero/))
