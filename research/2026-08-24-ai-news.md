## Breaking News

- **Anthropic suffers global Claude outage across chat, API, and Claude Code** — Anthropic's status page moved to "Investigating" at 05:06 UTC on August 24, reporting elevated errors across Claude Mythos 5, Claude Fable 5, Claude Opus 5, and Claude Opus 4.8, with partial outages on claude.ai, the Claude API, Claude Code, and Claude Cowork. The company identified the cause by 05:27 UTC and continued remediation through at least 06:42 UTC, but did not disclose the underlying technical fault. Four distinct models degrading in the same short window points to a shared dependency (e.g., a routing layer or inference cluster) rather than a single model issue. ## Research Papers

- **Inherent's small "Faraday" agent claims to outperform Claude Opus 4.8 and GPT-5.5 at replicating published research** — London startup Inherent, founded by DeepMind alumni, published results (TechCrunch, August 22) claiming its Faraday agent — built on a 27-billion-parameter Qwen 3.6 base and trained with reinforcement learning — outperformed much larger systems, including Anthropic's Claude Opus 4.8 and OpenAI's GPT-5.5, at autonomously reproducing findings from published scientific papers without being told the answer in advance. The company says it is optimizing for "research taste" (judging which experiments are worth running), not just raw reproduction accuracy, and the claim follows Inherent's emergence from stealth with a $50 million seed round. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/22/inherent-founded-by-deepmind-alumni-says-its-ai-teammate-just-outperformed-anthropic-and-openai-at-replicating-research/))
