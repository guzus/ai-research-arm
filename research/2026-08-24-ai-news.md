## Breaking News

- **Anthropic suffers global Claude outage across chat, API, and Claude Code** — Anthropic's status page moved to "Investigating" at 05:06 UTC on August 24, reporting elevated errors across Claude Mythos 5, Claude Fable 5, Claude Opus 5, and Claude Opus 4.8, with partial outages on claude.ai, the Claude API, Claude Code, and Claude Cowork. The company identified the cause by 05:27 UTC and continued remediation through at least 06:42 UTC, but did not disclose the underlying technical fault. Four distinct models degrading in the same short window points to a shared dependency (e.g., a routing layer or inference cluster) rather than a single model issue. ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Claude-faces-another-outage-as-errors-hit-multiple-models.1376666.0.html), [Arabian Business](https://www.arabianbusiness.com/business/technology/anthropic-claude-down))
- **Nvidia in talks to invest in Perplexity at $30B+ valuation as revenue tops $750M** — The Information reported August 23–24 that Nvidia is discussing a financing round for Perplexity that would value the AI-search startup above $30 billion, more than 50% higher than its roughly $20 billion valuation about a year ago. Perplexity's annualized revenue has climbed above $750 million (up from under $250 million at the start of 2026), driven partly by Perplexity Computer, its cloud-based AI-agent product for professional users. Neither company has confirmed a deal. ([TechStartups](https://techstartups.com/2026/08/24/nvidia-in-talks-to-invest-in-perplexity-at-30-billion-valuation-as-revenue-tops-750-million/), [The Information](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/nvidia-discusses-perplexity-investment-30-billion-plus-valuation-considered-tech-licensing-deal))
- **Alibaba launches Wan3.0 video model a day after $10.2B AI-infrastructure share placement** — On August 24, Alibaba released Wan3.0, a 30-second AI video generation model, one day after raising roughly HK$80 billion (~$10.2 billion) in a Hong Kong share placement earmarked entirely for "full stack AI capabilities" — chips, infrastructure, and model development/deployment. VentureBeat reports Wan3.0 has risen to the No. 2 spot in global AI video model rankings as OpenAI's Sora and ByteDance's Seedance slip. ([TechNode](https://technode.com/2026/08/24/alibaba-raises-hk80-billion-through-a-share-placement-to-fund-ai-infrastructure/), [Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/08/24/alibaba-launches-wan30-a-30-second-ai-video-generation-model/), [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/alibabas-ai-video-model-rises-to-no-2-in-global-rankings-as-openais-sora-and-bytedances-seedance-fall-away))

## Funding & Acquisitions

- **XPeng's robotics unit (Dogotix) raises $900M+ at a $6.3B valuation** — Announced August 24, 2026: XPeng's humanoid-robot subsidiary raised more than $900 million (led by IDG Capital, with Gaorong Ventures, Alibaba, and Tencent participating — plus $200M from XPeng itself and $100M from executives), valuing the unit at over $6.3 billion post-money. ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-24/xpeng-robot-unit-to-raise-900-million-from-likes-of-alibaba), [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/08/24/xpeng-robotics-900m-iron-humanoid-robot-valuation/))

## Research Papers

- **Inherent's small "Faraday" agent claims to outperform Claude Opus 4.8 and GPT-5.5 at replicating published research** — London startup Inherent, founded by DeepMind alumni, published results (TechCrunch, August 22) claiming its Faraday agent — built on a 27-billion-parameter Qwen 3.6 base and trained with reinforcement learning — outperformed much larger systems, including Anthropic's Claude Opus 4.8 and OpenAI's GPT-5.5, at autonomously reproducing findings from published scientific papers without being told the answer in advance. The company says it is optimizing for "research taste" (judging which experiments are worth running), not just raw reproduction accuracy, and the claim follows Inherent's emergence from stealth with a $50 million seed round. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/22/inherent-founded-by-deepmind-alumni-says-its-ai-teammate-just-outperformed-anthropic-and-openai-at-replicating-research/))
