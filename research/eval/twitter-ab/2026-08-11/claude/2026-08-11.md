      <p><strong>Counter / contradicting:</strong> The strongest counter is that statistical text watermarks do not survive contact with an adversary, and practitioners said so in this window: @DeveloperAlly argued paraphrasing through a second model destroys the signal and that the scheme is not technically robust, and @GiulioRebuffo made the same point about a trivial local rewrite. That still has real value for the PR/code-attribution case Anthropic named, and none at all for catching someone who is trying.</p>
