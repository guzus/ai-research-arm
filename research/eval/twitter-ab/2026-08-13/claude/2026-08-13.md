# Twitter/X AI Pulse (A/B eval) — 2026-08-13

## 22:00 UTC

**Cycle summary**: Google shipped Gemini 3.7 Flash at $0.75/$3.75 per million tokens — half the price of a model three weeks old — while DeepSeek moved the other way, introducing peak/off-peak pricing that raises its cache-hit input rate roughly twelvefold at peak. OpenAI separately previewed Ultrafast mode running GPT-5.6 Sol on Cerebras silicon at 750 tokens/sec, shipped a ChatGPT desktop feature that records activity across your Mac's apps and websites, and X published its "For You" ranking code under Apache 2.0.
