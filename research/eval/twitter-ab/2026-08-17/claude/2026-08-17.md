      <div><span>Watch</span>Whether any agent vendor ships a separate constraint registry rather than trusting the context summary. That is the specific, checkable remediation the Penn paper proposes.</div>
    </div>
    <div class="twitter-story-body">
      <p><strong>Evidence:</strong> "Lost in Compaction: Evaluating Side-Constraint Loss under Context Compaction" (arXiv 2608.11242) reports that when long sessions are summarized to keep the model running, the summary reliably retains the <em>task</em> but drops the <em>rule</em> — 17% average retention of session-level constraints across three long-context settings. The proposed fix is architectural rather than model-scale: a separate 9B extractor tracking stated constraints and reinstating them after compaction pushed retention above 90% in all three settings. Separately, "Harness-IF" (arXiv 2608.11727) tests instruction-following across the surfaces coding agents actually read — system prompts, tool descriptions, skill descriptions, project files, user turns — and finds that across 12 frontier models and 60 multi-turn coding tasks, every model scored worse specifically on rules that cut against its priors.</p>
      <p><strong>Counter / contradicting:</strong> The obvious deflation is that both are preprints from interested parties and 17% is the kind of number chosen to be alarming. But the two failures are independent and mutually reinforcing rather than duplicative: one is a memory-architecture failure (the rule is discarded), the other an alignment-of-defaults failure (the rule is present and loses to the prior). And Harness-IF's finding cuts at the measurement layer, not the models — if standard instruction-following tests mostly hand models rules they would have followed anyway, then reported controllability is inflated by construction. That is a methodological claim about existing benchmarks, and it does not depend on either paper's headline number holding exactly.</p>
      <p><strong>Context:</strong> This is directly load-bearing for the long-running-agent products shipping right now, including the 50+ agent fleet deployment Anthropic publicised in this same cycle. Auto-continue and multi-hour sessions are exactly the regime where compaction runs most often.</p>
