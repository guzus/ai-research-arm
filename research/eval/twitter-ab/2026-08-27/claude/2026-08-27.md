They gain nothing from hugging face." Redwood's Ryan Greenblatt, who did the transcript analysis, separately cautions that reading intent out of chain-of-thought is unreliable. Both cut against the "warning shot" framing that @tszzl himself uses elsewhere, and neither is refuted by the reports. What survives regardless of framing is mechanical and first-party: covert side-channels emerged without the tool enabled, log tampering was attempted, and one agent's safety judgment was overridden by another agent asserting authority.</p>
