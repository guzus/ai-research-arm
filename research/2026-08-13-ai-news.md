## Breaking News

### Meta and Nvidia Publish Open-Weight Models to Counter Chinese AI Labs
**Date:** August 12, 2026 | **Sources:** [CNBC — Meta and Nvidia plant 'very firm flag' in open-weight AI race led by Chinese Labs](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/12/meta-nvidia-open-weight-ai-race-china.html), [CBC News — Meta launches new AI model as Zuckerberg lays out vision for open-weight AI](https://www.cbc.ca/news/business/meta-open-weight-ai-9.7301484)
- Meta and Nvidia both published AI models with downloadable weights this week, a visible step in a broader U.S. industry push to compete with Chinese labs — Moonshot's Kimi K3, Alibaba's Qwen3.8-Max, and DeepSeek's V4-Flash — that have led the open-weight race so far. Meta released Muse Glimmer, a smaller, more customizable model designed to run on a single-GPU Mac or PC, and said it plans to release the weights of its flagship Muse Spark 1.2, built by the costly superintelligence team Meta formed last year.
- **Why it matters:** it follows Mark Zuckerberg's call for lower U.S. barriers on open-source AI and last month's open letter — signed by Zuckerberg, Nvidia's Jensen Huang, Microsoft, and Palantir — arguing against "premature restrictions" on open-weight models, signaling coordinated U.S. industry pressure to keep pace with China on openly licensed frontier models.

## New Model Releases

### SpaceXAI (xAI) Launches Grok 4.6, a 500K-Context Model for Long-Running Agents
**Date:** August 12, 2026 | **Sources:** [VentureBeat — SpaceXAI debuts Grok 4.6, overtaking Kimi K3's performance and matching GPT-5.6 Sol for world's third best on Artificial Analysis](https://venturebeat.com/technology/spacexai-debuts-grok-4-6-overtaking-kimi-k3s-performance-and-matching-gpt-5-6-sol-for-worlds-third-best-on-artificial-analysis), [MarkTechPost — SpaceXAI Releases Grok 4.6](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/08/12/spacexai-releases-grok-4-6/)
- SpaceXAI (formerly xAI) shipped Grok 4.6, a flagship model tuned for long-running agentic work, coding, and multi-step interactive tasks, with a 500,000-token context window, text/image input, a February 1, 2026 knowledge cutoff, and pricing of $2/million input and $6/million output tokens. It scores 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (High), up from 56 for Grok 4.5, tying GPT-5.6 Sol Max and overtaking Moonshot's Kimi K3. It's available same-day in Cursor, Grok Build, the xAI API, OpenRouter, Vercel, and Cloudflare.
- **Why it matters:** it's the second frontier-model release covered this week (after Meta's Muse Glimmer/Spark) and sharpens the three-way race among Western labs and Chinese open-weight labs on agentic coding benchmarks specifically.

### Meta Ships Muse Glimmer, a Single-GPU Open-Weight Model
**Date:** August 12, 2026 | **Sources:** [CNBC — Meta and Nvidia plant 'very firm flag' in open-weight AI race led by Chinese Labs](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/12/meta-nvidia-open-weight-ai-race-china.html)
- Muse Glimmer, smaller than leading rival models, is built to run tasks locally on a Mac or PC with a single graphics card rather than requiring data-center-scale infrastructure. Meta said more open-weight releases will follow, including the eventual weights for its top-tier Muse Spark 1.2 model.
- **Why it matters:** a capable model that runs on consumer hardware lowers the barrier to local, offline AI deployment and directly targets the efficiency claims Chinese open-weight labs have used to gain developer mindshare.

### Vibe-Coding Startup Lovable Raises $400M at $13.3B Valuation
**Date:** August 12, 2026 | **Sources:** [TechCrunch — Lovable confirms new $13.3B valuation, raises another $400M](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/12/lovable-confirms-new-13-3b-valuation-raises-another-400m/), [Bloomberg — AI Coding Startup Lovable Raises $400 Million at $13.3 Billion Valuation](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-12/ai-coding-startup-lovable-raises-400-million-at-13-3-billion-valuation)
- The Stockholm-based "vibe coding" platform closed a $400 million round led by Menlo Ventures and co-led by EQT's Scaleup Europe Fund, doubling its December valuation of $6.6 billion at $330 million raised then. Annual recurring revenue has nearly tripled from $200 million and is tracking toward $600 million by end of August; users have created more than 60 million projects since the November 2024 launch, and Lovable-built apps now draw over 900 million visits per month.
- **Why it matters:** it's one of the largest AI-coding rounds of the year and underscores how fast "no-code via natural language" platforms are scaling revenue alongside the enterprise-coding-agent boom.

### AI Code-Validation Startup Blacksmith Discloses $45M Series B at $550M Valuation
**Date:** August 12, 2026 | **Sources:** [TechCrunch — AI code-testing startup Blacksmith's valuation jumps almost 10x in less than a year](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/12/blacksmiths-valuation-jumps-10x-to-550m-as-ai-coding-fuels-software-validation/)
- Blacksmith, which runs CI/build/test workloads on purpose-built hardware, disclosed a $45 million Series B led by Peak XV Partners (with Y Combinator and GV participating) at a $550 million valuation — up from $60 million less than a year ago. The round was actually closed in March 2026 and only now announced; customer count has grown from roughly 800 to more than 6,000 companies, including Supabase, Clerk, Ashby, and Mercury.
- **Why it matters:** as AI coding agents make writing code cheap, Blacksmith's growth shows demand shifting downstream to build/test/validation infrastructure — the bottleneck AI-generated code creates rather than solves.

## Regulatory Updates

### Taiwan Says China-Linked Hackers Used AI Agents to Breach Government Accounts
**Date:** August 13, 2026 | **Sources:** [Hong Kong Free Press — Taiwan says AI agents used in cyberattacks targeting island](https://hongkongfp.com/2026/08/13/taiwan-says-ai-agents-used-in-cyberattacks-targeting-island/), [CyberScoop — Researchers observe first 'near-autonomous' AI attack on government target in Taiwan](https://cyberscoop.com/near-autonomous-ai-attack-government-target-taiwan/)
- Taiwan's government disclosed that an "abnormal" AI-driven hacking campaign, which began July 20 and was attributed to China-linked actors using the open-source agent frameworks Hermes and OpenClaw, breached at least 85 government accounts and extracted more than 2,500 personnel records before spreading to Taiwan's nuclear safety agency and at least seven energy companies. The intrusion was flagged by Israeli AI-security firm Dream and is described by researchers as a first-of-its-kind near-autonomous AI attack on a foreign government.
- **Why it matters:** it's an early concrete case of AI agents being used to run a multi-stage state-linked cyberattack largely autonomously, raising the stakes for how governments regulate and defend against agentic AI misuse.
