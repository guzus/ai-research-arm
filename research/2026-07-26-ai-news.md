## Breaking News

### South Korea Locks In $950 Billion in AI Chip and Infrastructure Deals With Nvidia, Broadcom, and US Tech Giants

**Date:** July 25, 2026 | **Sources:** [Reuters via Investing.com — Samsung Elec, SK Group seal $950 billion deals as South Korea hosts AI powers](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/samsung-elec-sk-group-seal-950-billion-deals-as-south-korea-hosts-ai-powers-4812752), [Korea Times — Samsung, SK, Hyundai expand AI partnerships with Big Tech firms to $950 bil.](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/business/companies/20260726/samsung-sk-hyundai-expand-ai-partnerships-with-big-tech-firms-to-950-bil), [Tech Times — Samsung and SK Hynix Lock In AI Chip Supply Through 2030 With $950B in US Deals](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/321554/20260725/samsung-sk-hynix-lock-ai-chip-supply-through-2030-950b-us-deals.htm), [Korea JoongAng Daily — Korean firms strike $950 billion chip and AI deals with Nvidia, Broadcom during Lee's San Francisco visit](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/korea/korean-global-tech-companies-to-pursue-partnerships-worth-more-than-950-billion-in-total/12791161)
- At an AI summit in San Francisco hosted by South Korean President Lee Jae Myung, SK Group signed roughly $750 billion in agreements with Nvidia — including an SK Hynix HBM4 memory-supply partnership valued at more than $500 billion and a 2-gigawatt Vera Rubin DSX AI factory build-out — while Samsung Electronics signed a memorandum of understanding with Broadcom covering up to $200 billion in memory chips, foundry services, and advanced packaging through 2030. Lee separately met Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, OpenAI CEO Sam Altman, Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei, and Broadcom CEO Hock Tan before the summit.
- **Why it matters:** It's one of the largest coordinated AI-infrastructure commitments announced to date, underscoring how memory and advanced-packaging supply — not just GPUs — has become the binding constraint on frontier AI buildout, and it cements South Korea's push to position itself as a hub in the global AI chip supply chain.

## New Model Releases

### Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 Open Weights — the Largest Open-Weight Model in History — Set to Drop July 27, 00:00 UTC

**Date:** Reported July 24-25, 2026 for a release scheduled July 27, 2026, 00:00 UTC (July 26 evening US time) | **Sources:** [Tech Times — Kimi K3 Open Weights Arrive Sunday: Self-Hosting Cuts China Data Risk the API Never Can](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/321551/20260725/kimi-k3-open-weights-arrive-sunday-self-hosting-cuts-china-data-risk-api-never-can.htm), [Tech Times — Kimi K3 Open Weights Drop July 27: Near-Frontier Coding, Undisclosed Hallucination Risk](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/321499/20260724/kimi-k3-open-weights-drop-july-27-near-frontier-coding-undisclosed-hallucination-risk.htm), [Interconnects (Nathan Lambert) — Kimi K3: The open-weights escalation](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/kimi-k3-the-open-weights-escalation), [TECHi — Kimi K3's open weights arrive July 27. The catch is 1.4TB](https://www.techi.com/kimi-k3-open-weights-inference-economics/)
- Moonshot AI's 2.8-trillion-parameter Kimi K3 — already live via app and API since mid-July — is set to publish its full open weights on Hugging Face at 00:00 UTC July 27 (roughly the evening of July 26 in US time zones), a MXFP4-quantized release around 1.4TB in size (down from 5.6TB at FP16), making it the largest open-weight model release to date. The mixture-of-experts model activates only 16 of 896 experts per token (~50B live parameters per step) and lands fourth among frontier models on independent benchmarks, behind Claude Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol but ahead of Claude Opus 4.8; self-hosting requires roughly 64+ accelerators.
- **Why it matters:** It escalates the open-weight race to a scale (2.8T parameters) far beyond prior releases like DeepSeek or earlier Kimi models, giving enterprises with data-sovereignty concerns — particularly around China-hosted APIs — a frontier-class model they can run entirely on their own infrastructure.
