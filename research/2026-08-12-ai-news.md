## Breaking News

### OpenAI's Longtime COO Brad Lightcap Departs, Widening Executive Exodus Ahead of IPO
**Date:** August 11, 2026 | **Sources:** [TechCrunch — Brad Lightcap, OpenAI's longtime COO, is leaving to 'start something new'](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/11/brad-lightcap-openais-longtime-coo-is-leaving-to-start-something-new/), [CNBC — Longtime OpenAI executive Brad Lightcap leaves as shake-up at AI lab continues](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/11/longtime-openai-executive-brad-lightcap-leaves-as-shakeup-at-ai-lab-continues.html), [Axios — OpenAI executive Brad Lightcap is leaving](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/11/openai-executive-brad-lightcap-is-leaving)
- Brad Lightcap, an eight-year OpenAI veteran who served as COO before moving to a special-projects role reporting directly to Sam Altman in April, announced he is leaving to "start something new," saying he remains committed to the company's mission "from a different vantage point." His exit follows the earlier 2026 departures of Bill Peebles, Kevin Weil, Srinivas Narayanan, product/business chief Fidji Simo, CMO Kate Rouch, AI ethics lead Chloé Bakalar, safety systems chief Johannes Heidecke, and chief futurist Joshua Achiam.
- **Why it matters:** it's the latest in a monthslong C-suite exodus at OpenAI as the company prepares for a reported 2027 IPO, raising questions about executive continuity at a moment when Anthropic has been gaining ground in valuation and enterprise share.

## New Model Releases

### xAI Opens Public Beta of Grok Bot, Persistent AI Agents With Their Own Cloud Computers
**Date:** August 11, 2026 | **Sources:** [VentureBeat — xAI's Grok Bot turns agents into persistent digital coworkers that can operate your apps for $120-per-month](https://venturebeat.com/orchestration/spacexais-grok-bot-turns-agents-into-persistent-digital-coworkers-that-can-operate-your-apps-for-120-per-month), [Unite.AI — xAI Launches Grok Bot, Always-On AI Teammates With Their Own Cloud Computers](https://www.unite.ai/xai-launches-grok-bot-always-on-ai-teammates-with-their-own-cloud-computers/), [Techmeme — xAI rolls out Grok Bot AI agent app in beta on Mac, iOS, Windows, and Linux](https://www.techmeme.com/260811/p29)
- xAI launched an early beta of Grok Bot, which gives each agent a dedicated persistent cloud computer that stays logged into a user's existing tools and keeps working on multi-step tasks even after the laptop is closed. It uses computer-use (screen-reading, pointer control, typing) rather than APIs or connectors, and ships on desktop (including Linux), and iOS. Access is bundled into SuperGrok Heavy ($300/month), Cursor Ultra ($200/month), and Cursor Teams Premium ($120/seat/month); Elon Musk said a wider rollout tied to Grok 4.6 is coming later this week.
- **Why it matters:** it's xAI's entry into the "persistent AI coworker" category that Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google have all been racing toward, betting that always-on computer-use agents rather than chat interfaces are the next competitive front.
