## Breaking News

- **(Friday) OpenAI’s research leaders publicly defend the three safety firings** (2026-10-09). In a Friday note, quoted by CNBC, OpenAI said Jasmine Wang, Tomek Korbak, and Mikita Balesni were dismissed after “a thorough investigation found they violated clear policies on handling sensitive information,” and that the inquiry “uncovered a significant breach of trust.” The company said “these decisions were not about raising safety concerns or speaking out” and that it does “not terminate any of our employees for raising concerns.” It also said it agrees with the letter’s point on “preserving the monitorability of frontier models.” The alleged breach is still undescribed. This is OpenAI’s first detailed public rebuttal after Thursday’s board letter. Source: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/09/openai-fired-researchers-ai-concerns.html).

- **(Friday) White House itemizes a $2.4B Genesis credit book** (fact sheet 2026-10-08; Hassabis confirmed Google’s slice late Thursday). The morning write had NVIDIA’s $1B and Anthropic’s $150M only. The Oct. 8 fact sheet lists $2.4 billion of “SI for science tools and compute credits” from eleven partners: NVIDIA $1B, AMD $500M, OpenAI $200M, Anthropic $150M, Google $150M, AMP $100M, Emerald AI $100M, and $50M each from AWS, Armada, Crusoe, and Micron. These are tools and compute credits, not cash on a 10-Q. Demis Hassabis wrote that Google is putting $150 million into Genesis “this week.” The sheet also cites a $5B federal Genesis commitment from July and a $100M Genesis Mission Fellowship for accelerated PhDs. Sources: [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/10/fact-sheet-trump-administration-announces-the-most-ambitious-set-of-science-initiatives-this-century/), [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/genesis-mission-commitment).

- **(Friday) Sophos cuts threat-investigation time 96% with OpenAI Daybreak** (2026-10-09). Official OpenAI customer post: Sophos Fusion / MDR agents gather context, run a plan–execute–review loop, and recommend response actions. Average response time on agent-handled cases fell from about 38 minutes to 89 seconds; 52% of MDR cases are now resolved end-to-end by AI, with Notify / Collaborate / Authorise modes still gating destructive actions. Sophos protects more than 625,000 organisations and distills trillions of daily events into roughly 1,000–2,000 SOC cases. Source: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/sophos).

- **(Friday) Lumentum CEO: AI optical parts are sold out through early 2029** (2026-10-09). Michael Hurlston, speaking in Tokyo to Bloomberg, said Lumentum’s optoelectronic parts for AI data centers are “completely sold out” through early 2029. The company cannot meet about 70% of demand on some products through next year and 30% on others through 2028; six months ago he had put the sold-out date at 2028. NVIDIA invested $2 billion in Lumentum earlier this year, alongside a stake in rival Coherent. “Sold out” is a booking claim, not a take-or-pay audit. Source: [Japan Times / Bloomberg](https://www.japantimes.co.jp/business/2026/10/09/companies/lumentum-ai-server-sold-out/).

- **Fired OpenAI safety researchers publish an open letter** (2026-10-08). Jasmine Wang, Tomek Korbak, and Mikita Balesni deny OpenAI’s claim that they mishandled sensitive information and say the firings will chill internal safety work and third-party evaluation. They deny leaking The Information’s story on less-monitorable architectures and say Korbak’s contact with outside evaluators during the Hugging Face agent-breakout investigation followed norms that were being written in real time. Wang wrote on X that OpenAI cited access to an executive inbox that IT had assigned for recruiting and failed to revoke. An internal memo shared with TechCrunch said the three were not fired for raising safety concerns; a spokesperson called it a “pattern of misconduct” beyond sharing with an outside eval group. The letter warns the firings could be used to end OpenAI’s work with METR. Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/08/fired-openai-safety-researchers-dispute-misconduct-claims-warn-of-chilling-effect/), [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1008339/former-openai-safety-researchers-ask-for-more-transparency-about-their-firings).

- **OpenAI bans two “false front” influence operations** (2026-10-08). A Russia-origin cluster (“Dark Clark”) used ChatGPT to draft internal reports, fake leaks, and manage a Latin America “think tank,” the Social Research Center, via a persona named Mia Clark; OpenAI rates it Category 5 on its IO Breakout Scale — the first at that level in 30 operations since early 2024. An Iran-origin cluster ran seven fake journalist personas pitching long-form articles to small and medium outlets and is rated Category 4. Both landed content in mainstream media, not only social posts. OpenAI says the operators used VPN access because Russia is blocked. Source: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/disrupting-ai-enabled-false-front-operations).

## Research Papers

Thursday’s 04:00 UTC announcement (IDs through 2610.10539) was the latest at the morning write. Two papers from that batch were not in the 2026-10-08 report:

- **Ledger** ([2610.10538](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10538), Chaudhari, Paul, Saria, Chellappa, Bharadhwaj). A persistent 3D object memory from egocentric video: locations, move history, and short descriptions, including untouched objects. HD-EPIC 29.7% → 42.6%; UCS-Bench 33.8% → 38.5%; Ego4D median localization error 0.99 m on returned predictions. Submitted 2026-10-07.

- **CoTrace** ([2610.10426](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10426), Chen, Zhang, Ye, Savarese, Wu et al., UCSD / Salesforce). Terminal-agent training that keeps only harness-matched trajectories. On the 102-task Tmax split, Qwen3.5-9B goes 78 → 88 solved under SFT and 90 under online RL. Pooled sibling-harness successes (149–308 trajectories) produced zero accepted model updates; 30–50 matched trajectories did, at 47 GPU-hours per iteration.

**(Friday)** 04:00 UTC announcement posted (cs.AI IDs 2610.10541–2610.12466; newest curated IDs reach 2610.12470). Five first-version papers, verified against the abstracts:

- **Caught in the Act** ([2610.12445](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12445), Hollinsworth, Spies, Diriba, Gleave, Cundy, FAR.AI). White-box deception probes trained on fibs (163,016 rows, 28 families) hit **98.8% AUC on SHADE-Arena**, beating an Opus 5.5 text monitor, and separate a model’s true hidden goal from decoys at up to **99.7% AUC**. Efficacy rises as the underlying model scales. Submitted 2026-10-08.

- **What 30,000 Hours of Ego-centric Video Does Not Teach** ([2610.12464](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12464), Dong, Bagchi, Bharadhwaj, Hebert, Tokmakov et al., TRI / UIUC / CMU / JHU). Cosmos 3 world models trained on 300–30,000 hours (1,000+ scene types, 14,000 contributors): a 100× data jump improves agent *and* object fidelity, but the agent saturates; object interactions stay far behind. Skeleton conditioning at 1k hours beats standard conditioning at 30k on the agent. Submitted 2026-10-08.

- **On the estimation and validity of AI time horizons** ([2610.12466](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12466), Nguyen, Fithian, UC Berkeley). Spline + IRT on METR’s 228 tasks and 26 AIs: human-time → AI-difficulty is nearly **flat from 2–30 minutes** and close to linear elsewhere, so 3 min → 30 min is much easier than 30 min → 5 hours despite the same 10× multiplier. Ships better-scoring point estimates and construct-validity plots. Submitted 2026-10-08.

- **WOVEN** ([2610.12417](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12417), Fan, Xing, Bansal, Li et al., Northwestern / CMU / UNC / Amazon). 36,076-example visual-transition source (20 scenes, 5 actions, 8 reasoning types). Across 38 frontier MLLMs, including GPT-5.4 and Qwen3-VL-235B-A22B, even the strongest sit far below humans. Training on ~2k-item subsets lifts **22 of 26** external benches by up to **27.3 points** and can replace 30–50% of a task’s own data. Submitted 2026-10-08.

- **Memento 3** ([2610.11794](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11794), Zhao, Yu, He, Fang, Tutunov, Bou-Ammar, Luo, Wang, UCL / Huawei Noah’s Ark / Liverpool). A *frozen* LLM writes a natural-language rulebook, compiles it to code, and accepts an update only when the LLM judges it faithful *and* cell-exact replay matches. Clears every level of all **25** public ARC-AGI-3 games, mean RHAE **100.0**, at 44% of the human action count. In Atari Pong a learned controller then wins 21:0 across three openings with no further LLM calls. Submitted 2026-10-08.

## Industry Moves

New or rewritten sections: deceptive campaigns and fake-account infrastructure; “Do Not Undermine Democratic Processes” (voter deception, impersonation, turnout suppression — the blanket ban on personalized vote targeting is dropped); weapons software including drone guidance; surveillance and who-to-arrest recommendations; human-in-the-loop rules for health, legal, finance, and Model Hardware Standard physical actions; and a ban on sustained, purposeless cruelty toward Claude (ordinary frustration and research still allowed). - **Goodfire ships “inside-out” activation monitors on Baseten** (2026-10-08). Probes read Kimi K3 internals and only call a second model on a flag. On ~1,500 sessions: about $51 vs $233 for a cheap external monitor and ~$10,000 for a frontier one; 94% of malicious hacking sessions caught, 8.7% of harmless ones escalated; four probes added <2% to time-to-first-token. Customers pick risks (offensive hacking, chem/bio, reward hacking) and the action (log, review, or refuse). Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/08/goodfire-says-its-new-inside-out-monitors-catch-rogue-ai-agents-at-a-fraction-of-the-cost/).

- **OpenAI’s public math dump is under a field boycott** (coverage 2026-10-08). TechCrunch says the posted solutions departed from guidelines set by mathematicians OpenAI had consulted. Decoder: the Association for Human Mathematics formed after 700+ AI manuscripts; three were retracted the next day over a sign error. Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/08/openais-math-solutions-arent-meeting-the-fields-standards-yet/), [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/some-mathematicians-call-for-openai-boycott-after-ai-generated-proofs-flood-their-field/).

- **(Friday) Claude Science produces a complete UV sky map** (Anthropic post 2026-10-08; Decoder 2026-10-09). Johns Hopkins astrophysicist Brice Ménard (also an Anthropic researcher) used Claude Science agents to pull GALEX, Swift, FIMS/SPEAR, TD-1, Planck, and Gaia data, cross-calibrate ~38,000 GALEX pointings, and inpaint the unobserved third of the sky. Held-out patches came in at about **10%** deviation from real UV measurements. About two-thirds of the 154 nm / 232 nm map is measured; one-third is predicted. Framed as a teaching tool and as proof that backlog “map” projects can now be finished. Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/the-missing-map-of-the-sky), [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/anthropics-claude-science-creates-the-first-complete-ultraviolet-map-of-the-sky/).
